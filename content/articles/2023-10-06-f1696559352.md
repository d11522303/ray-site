This autumn days will shorten and grow cold.

The leaves will shake loose form the trees and fall.

Christmas will come, then the snowes of winter.

Winter will pass, the days will lengthen, the ice will melt in the pasture pond.

The song sparrow will return and sing, the frogs will awake, the warm wind will blow again.

All these sights and sounds and smells will be yours to enjoy, this lovely world, these precious days…
