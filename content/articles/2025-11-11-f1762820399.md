【每日英領單字】Day8 2025.11.11

plausible, (adj), 似乎合理的, appearing believable or reasonable

flak, (n), 抨擊；高射炮火, strong criticism or anti-aircraft fire

relentless, (adj), 毫不鬆懈的, persistent and unyielding

emissions, (n), 排放物, substances released into the air

theoretical, (adj), 理論上的, based on theory rather than practice

programme, (n), 計畫；節目, a planned series of actions or a broadcast show

carbon dioxide, (n), 二氧化碳, a gas produced by burning carbon and respiration

trajectories, (n), 軌跡, paths followed by moving objects

equivalent, (adj/n), 相等的；等值物, equal in value or a thing that is equal

pledges, (n/v), 承諾；保證, promises or to make a commitment
