【每日英領50字】Day4 2025.11.7

corridor, (n), 走廊, a long passage in a building

essential, (adj), 必要的, absolutely necessary or extremely important

provision, (n), 供應；條款, the act of supplying or a legal condition

reimbursement, (n), 報銷, repayment for expenses

draining, (adj/v), 使人疲憊的；排水, exhausting or removing liquid

flushing, (v), 沖洗, cleaning with a flow of water

enduce, (v), 引起；誘導, to bring about or cause (variant of induce)

evade, (v), 逃避, to avoid or escape from

exaggerate, (v), 誇張, to make something seem larger or more extreme

mopping, (v), 拖地, cleaning a floor with a mop

expedite, (v), 加快, to speed up a process

verification, (n), 驗證, confirmation of truth or accuracy

strict, (adj), 嚴格的, demanding adherence to rules

vandalism, (n), 故意破壞, deliberate destruction of property

vampire, (n), 吸血鬼, a mythical creature that drinks blood

vandor, (n), 小販, a person who sells goods (variant of vendor)

vanishment, (n), 消失, the act of disappearing

extinguish, (v), 撲滅, to put out (a fire or light)

ceramic, (adj/n), 陶瓷的；陶瓷製品, made of clay or a clay object
