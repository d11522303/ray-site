AI will be one of the most significant supports for for teaching and learning. Educators must embrace the future, as a new era of education is emerging.

embrace (vb)

1. to clasp in the arms

例：She embraced her friend warmly.

2. to enclose on all sides

例：Low hills embraced the valley.

3. to take up readily or gladly (embrace an opportunity)

例：He embraced the opportunity to study abroad.

emerging (vb)

1. to come out or into view (as from watch or a hole)

例：The sun was emerging from behind the clouds.

2. to become known especially as a result of study or questioning

例：A new pattern is emerging from the data.
