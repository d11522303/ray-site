【每日英領單字】Day6 2025.11.9

alienate, (v), 疏遠, to make someone feel isolated or estranged

relevant, (adj), 相關的, closely connected or appropriate to the matter

treaty, (n), 條約, a formal agreement between countries

perilous, (adj), 危險的, full of danger or risk

armoured, (adj), 裝甲的, protected by armor

brigade, (n), 部隊, a military unit or organized group

permanent, (adj), 永久的, lasting or intended to last indefinitely

deployment, (n), 部署, the movement of troops or equipment for action

perspective, (n), 觀點；視角, a particular point of view or way of seeing

recognize, (v), 認出；承認, to identify or acknowledge

ought to, (phr/modal), 應該, used to express duty or advisability

soaring, (adj/v), 高漲的；飛升, rising rapidly or flying high

oncology, (n), 腫瘤學, the study and treatment of cancer

immunology, (n), 免疫學, the study of the immune system

obesity, (n), 肥胖, excessive body fat

diabetes, (n), 糖尿病, a disease affecting blood sugar regulation

cardiovascular, (adj), 心血管的, relating to the heart and blood vessels

respiratory, (adj), 呼吸的, relating to breathing or the lungs
