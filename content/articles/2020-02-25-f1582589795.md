有看過2016的奧斯卡最佳影片關鍵少數Hidden Figures嗎?

真實世界的女主角凱薩琳·強森在昨天2/24離開人世，享年101歲。

Hidden Figures是我近年看過最感人的影片之一，也不斷地提醒著自己，身為人類不應該有國界、種族、膚色、性別及語言上的歧視與偏見。

雖然身處的台灣處處都是！！

Have you seen the "Hidden Figures", the best Oscar film in 2016?

The real-world heroine Catherine G. Johnson passed away yesterday at the age of 101.

Hidden Figures is one of the most touching films I've seen in recent years, and I keep reminding myself that as human beings, we should not have discrimination and prejudice on nations, race, color, gender, and language.

RIP. Katherine G. J.
