Here's an opportunity to share with the world and with other young people like YOU, just how the global employment crisis has affected you, your friends, your family or community.

1.You must be between the ages of 18-29 years;

2.Videos must be a maximum of two minutes in length;

3.Videos which are not in one of the three official languages of the ILO (English, French or Spanish), must include subtitles in one of the ILO’s official languages. Videos without narrations (silent videos) are also accepted.

4.You must send the link of your video via email toyouth@ilo.org and provide your name, age and nationality;

5.Deadline for submission is 10 April 2012 at 23:59 (GMT).
