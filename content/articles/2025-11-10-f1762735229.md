【每日英領單字】Day7 2025.11.10

abruptly, (adv), 突然地, suddenly and unexpectedly

bonvoy, (n), 萬豪旅享家（品牌名）, Marriott's loyalty program name

portfolio, (n), 投資組合；作品集, a collection of financial investments or creative work

never-ending, (adj), 永無止境的, continuing forever or seeming to have no end

terminate, (v), 終止；結束, to bring something to an end

be affiliated with, (v phr), 隸屬於；與...有關聯, to be officially connected to an organization or group

a third-party online travel agency, (n phr), 第三方線上旅行社, an independent company that sells travel services online

not much else out, (phr), 沒有其他選擇, few or no other options available

presumably, (adv), 大概；可能, used to say what you think is likely true

hook for, (v phr), 被迫承擔責任, to be held responsible or liable for something

screw-up, (n), 搞砸；失誤, a mistake or failure caused by someone

embarrass, (v), 使尷尬；使難堪, to make someone feel awkward or ashamed

inevitable, (adj), 無可避免的, certain to happen; unavoidable

dire, (adj), 嚴重的；可怕的, extremely serious or urgent

out of luck, (phr), 運氣不好；沒機會了, having no success or opportunity

integrated into, (v phr), 整合進；融入, combined or included as part of something

liquidation, (n), 清算；清盤, the process of closing a business and selling its assets

verge, (n), 邊緣；瀕臨, the edge or border of something; a point just before something happens

collapse, (v), 崩潰；倒塌, to fall down or fail suddenly and completely
