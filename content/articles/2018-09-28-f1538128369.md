新加坡教育部將減少國小至國中10年級的期中考試次數，以因應新時代的巨大改變。

未來將努力將學生導向對日常課程及學習活動有學習勤勉、好奇、合作、熱情等PLO積極學習為主，而非以學業成績為導向。

“On the ground, teachers are on a high-speed train ... rushing, assessments, and preparing the students for exams,” he said. “I think it’s time to take a pause.”

The selection of both awards at these levels will no longer be based on academic scores, said MOE. Instead, the eligibility criteria will be adjusted to recognize “positive learning orientations” such as diligence, curiosity, collaboration and enthusiasm in daily lessons and learning activities.
