【每日英領單字】Day9 2025.11.12

spontaneous, (adj), 自發的；自然的, arising naturally; not planned or forced

addiction, (n), 上癮；沉溺, the state of being unable to stop doing or using something, especially something harmful

particularly, (adv), 特別地；尤其, especially; more than usual or more than others

synthetic, (adj), 合成的；人造的, made by chemical synthesis; not natural

opioid, (n), 類鴉片藥物, a substance that acts on the nervous system to relieve pain and can cause addiction

truce, (n), 停戰；休戰協定, an agreement between enemies to stop fighting for a certain time

momentum, (n), 動力；勢頭, the force or speed gained by a moving object or by a developing process

reassess (v), 重新評估, to think about something again in order to decide if you should change your opinion or judgment

stability, (n), 穩定；平衡, the state of being steady and not changing or likely to fall

assess, (v), 評估；評定, to evaluate or estimate the nature, ability, or quality of something
