This approach helps people stay focused on a single task, Many people tend to make excuses when facing difficult thing, especially when the task requires sustained concentration and patience.

Writing, for example, is often postponed because it demands both clear thinking and continuous effort.

For this reason, having an online writing parner for weekly practice might be helpful. A writing partner can provide encouragement, feedback, and a sense of accountability. When someone knows that

another person is also writing at the same time, it becomes easier to stay committed to the task.

In addition, regular writing sessions can gradually build confidence and improve writing skills over time. What begins as a small weekly habit may eventually develop into a productive and sustainable writing routine.
