#UCLA

環球影城與UCLA都在洛城的北邊，這個最原生的影城有著所有知名電影的場景與設施。孩子向媽媽說他有去嘗試了聽說最可怕的設施，他覺得沒有以前當小孩時那麼可怕，並非是長大了，而是他覺得美國娛樂文化不是以最極端的工具性來呈現緊張的情境，反而是將整個設施的情緒做到滿讓所有遊客去體會，這與台灣的樂園設計很不同，應該是民族性的不同。

Universal Studios and UCLA are both located in the northern part of Los Angeles. Universal Studios is a theme park that features famous Hollywood film studios and a variety of immersive attractions. After spending the entire day there, Hans shared his thoughts with his mother. He said that he had tried the most thrilling ride and realized that it was not as frightening as he had remembered from childhood.

Hans also concluded that American entertainment culture does not rely solely on extreme rides to create excitement. Instead, it places greater emphasis on storytelling, immersive experiences, and emotional engagement for visitors of all ages. In contrast, many theme parks in Taiwan focus more on the intensity of the rides themselves. This difference may reflect the distinct cultural values and approaches to entertainment in the two societies.
