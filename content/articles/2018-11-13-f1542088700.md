義大利Fincantieri造船廠將郵輪技術轉移給中國造船，中國制造又更進一步。以基隆為夏季母港的盛世公主號就是2017年Fincantieri造船廠的產品。

其實空中巴士在1994年之後中國也有造A330及A320飛機的工廠，而且市佔率也達到50%！

Italian Fincantieri transferred cruise ship technology to China State Shipbuilding, and China manufacturing went further. The Majestic Princess, with Keelung as its home port in summer, is the product of Fincantieri in 2017.

In fact, since 1994, China also has the factory of A330 and A320 aircraft, and the market share will be more than 50%!
