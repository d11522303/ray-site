【每日英領單字】Day10 2025.11.13

pledged, (v), 保證；發誓, formally promised or committed to do something

distortion, (n), 扭曲；失真, a change that makes something appear different from the truth or its original form

deceit, (n), 欺騙；詭計, the act of deceiving someone by concealing or misrepresenting the truth

outright, (adv/adj), 完全地；徹底的, complete and total; without any doubt or hesitation

accumulated, (v/adj), 累積的；逐漸增加的, gathered or collected over time

stench, (n), 惡臭；難聞的氣味, a very strong and unpleasant smell

prediction, (n), 預測；預言, a statement about what is likely to happen in the future based on evidence or reasoning

speculation, (n), 推測；臆測, the act of forming opinions or guesses without firm evidence
