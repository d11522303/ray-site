28/672 "Unknown Realm: Team Communication Repair Mission"

Many communication problems are not caused by a lack of ability, but by differences in each other's thinking patterns and communication preferences. Through this game-based learning, you will quickly get to know your own communication traits in real interaction scenarios, understand how different roles think, and learn to adjust your expression and cooperation strategies to make communication more efficient.

In this experience, you will gain:

✨ See your own communication style

Understand your thinking patterns, ways of expression and decision-making habits in different situations.

✨ Understand different types of people

Learn to recognise the needs, strengths and blind spots of partners with different traits, reducing misunderstandings and conflict.

✨ Improve team cooperation efficiency

Experience the challenges of cooperating across different roles, and find the best way to communicate through interaction.

✨ Bring what you learn straight back to work

Turn the reflections from the game into communication skills and cooperation abilities in the workplace.

Date and time: 20 November 2026 (ROC Year 115), 13:00 - 15:00

Venue: Online meeting

Who can join: Open to all. Anyone interested in this topic is welcome (minors must be accompanied by a parent or relative)

Tools needed: A computer or laptop with a stable internet connection
