Sheng-Tsung Hou: "Exploring knowledge and truth is the essence of a university; but accompanying students in finding themselves is its real value. Although a university cannot simply hand students a clear purpose in life, teachers can still do a great deal. For example, through project-based work, industry placements, overseas exchanges, and hands-on lab guidance, we can give students opportunities to think independently, room for diverse conversations, deep sharing of life experience, and resources at the right moment. All of this aims at nurturing a sense of purpose in young people, helping them find their own calling in life, awaken their inner motivation to exist, and set out on their own hero's journey."
