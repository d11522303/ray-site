12/688 Laboratory Safety and Health

This was the first session yesterday of a required course in the Graduate Institute of Applied Science and Technology. I was very well behaved and sat in the second row by the door!

In the Ministry of Education's statistics, the Graduate Institute of Applied Science and Technology is classified under the materials engineering field of study, so the institute has many laboratories. The first class started from "safety and health". In the professor's slides yesterday there were labs on fire, a scalp pulled into a machine, professional shower equipment, and so on.

Sitting behind me to the left were two young master's students. When I asked, they said they are in the materials engineering track, learning semiconductor materials. According to Ministry of Education statistics, in Academic Year 114 the Graduate Institute of Applied Science and Technology at NTUST had 266 enrolled students: 165 in the doctoral programme and 101 in the master's programme. There is no undergraduate programme; all are graduate students.

And the director said that lab experiment content differs from year to year now. If your experiments are the same as those of the seniors, your lab may fall far behind others. Wow! Everyone is grinding.
