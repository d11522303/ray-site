#61DaysToTheTourLeaderExam

27 The Xiao Family Ancestral House in Jiadong Township, Pingtung is a large double "回"-shaped house with five halls and six courtyards. It is over 200 years old and is a complete, enclosed Hakka encircling-dragon house (weilong wu). Which of the following statements about the Xiao Family Ancestral House is correct? ① It is located in a Hoklo (Minnan) village ② It has an architectural landscape blending Chinese and Western styles ③ It is the traditional dwelling form of the early Pingpu peoples ④ Its structure emphasises defensive function

Explanation: the answer is in the second picture 🤪

The architect Lee Chien-lang once wrote a book ranking the Lin An Tai Historical House, the Maxing Chen Residence, the Lin Family Mansion and Garden of Banqiao, the Li Tengfang Old House, Xiaoyun Mountain Villa, the Wufeng Lin Family Mansion and Garden, Zhaixing Mountain Villa, the Shekou Dafu Residence, Yu San Guan and the Jiadong Xiao Residence as his ten great Taiwanese residences. How many of them have you visited?

Actually I have only been to 3. I feel we really do not know enough about our own homeland 😅
