Mickey Mouse Degrees

Have you heard of "Mickey Mouse degrees"?

The Telegraph in the UK ran an article (link in the comments) listing the ten subjects whose graduates earn the lowest average salaries five years after graduating. Graduates of these subjects can hardly earn a reasonable wage, simply because they studied Mickey Mouse degrees, that is, the subjects with the worst value for money.

1. Photography

2. Translation

3. Film

4. Fine art

5. Criminology

6. Music

7. English

8. Event management

9. Fashion

10. Architecture

UK graduates in the subjects above earn an average of less than £27K a year, which is less than one million Taiwan dollars. A Big Mac in the UK costs £3.79, about NT$140. The Taiwan General Scholastic Ability Test has just ended, and many high school students are now thinking about what to study and how to choose a department and a school. If you think about the future in terms of earning power, quite a few subjects might really be Mickey Mouse degrees.

At the singularity of AI development, university coursework, written and illustrated reports, and even statistical papers could be replaced in an instant.
