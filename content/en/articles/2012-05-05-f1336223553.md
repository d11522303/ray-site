2012-05-05 Gengsheng Daily

Photo: Aboriginal-style dishes made of paper, using the colours of paper and natural materials to recreate indigenous cuisine. (Photo by reporter Jung Jen-shun / Reported by reporter Cheng Ke-hsin)

The graduation exhibition of the Department of Travel Management at Taiwan Hospitality & Tourism College is being held yesterday and today from 1:30 pm to 5:00 pm at the Heping Plaza of Far Eastern Department Store in Hualien. This year's exhibition takes green tourism as its concept: green tourism not only protects the environment but also serves as a way of learning. All the works on display were created by students after months of careful thought and effort, and the exhibition is lively and fun. The public is warmly invited to come and see it.

The exhibition also features interactive games and a dance performance by the college's Indigenous Peoples Club, giving visitors a first-hand experience of indigenous culture and a deeper understanding of its richness. The event is lively and varied: besides static guided displays, there are dynamic activities, including DIY handmade souvenirs, a low-carbon food feast, an eco-friendly fashion hair show, charity haircuts, a plant adoption drive and band performances.
