#TourLeaderExamCountdown3Days

36 Europe's Danube River often suffers flooding. Which of the following is the main cause? (A) Snowstorms (B) Typhoons (C) Snowmelt (D) A riverbed that is too steep

The answer is (C)

Explanation: The Danube River is the second-longest river in Europe, about 2,850 kilometres long, flowing through 10 countries, the most of any river in the world.

#1 One of the Danube's most famous features is that it flows directly through four national capitals. Which of the following pairings is wrong?

(A) Austria - Vienna

(B) Slovenia - Bratislava

(C) Hungary - Budapest

(D) Serbia - Belgrade

Answer: (B) is wrong. Bratislava is the capital of Slovakia.

#2 A travel agency is planning a "Romantic Danube River Cruise" that departs from Passau, Germany, and sails downstream to the final stop, Budapest, Hungary. In order, which countries' capitals will the tour leader pass on the voyage?

(A) Berlin → Vienna → Bratislava

(B) Vienna → Bratislava → Budapest

(C) Munich → Vienna → Budapest

(D) Vienna → Zagreb → Budapest

Correct answer: (B)
