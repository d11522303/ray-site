Before the Tour Guide and Tour Leader Exams, EP58

#41DaysLeftToTheTourLeaderExam

26 Sports and athletic events can energise a city's economy. In 2024, in which of the following cities was the 33rd Summer Olympic Games held? (A) Los Angeles (B) Paris (C) Qatar (D) Jakarta

The answer is (B)

Questions aimed directly at important upcoming world sporting events:

#1 The 23rd FIFA World Cup in 2026 will be jointly hosted by three countries. Which of the following countries is NOT a host of that tournament? (A) United States (B) Mexico (C) Canada (D) Brazil

Answer (D)

#2 Which of the following statements about the 2028 Summer Olympic Games is correct? (A) The host city is Brisbane, Australia (B) It is the first time the city has hosted the Summer Olympics (C) The host city is Los Angeles, USA (D) The opening ceremony is expected to be held in December to avoid the summer heat

Answer (C)

#3 The 2030 FIFA World Cup coincides with the tournament's "centenary", and FIFA has announced that countries on three continents will take part together. In which region will the first three commemorative matches be held, to pay tribute to the first host nation? (A) North America (B) South America (Uruguay, Argentina, Paraguay) (C) Europe (Spain, Portugal) (D) Africa (Morocco)

Answer (B)

#4 Regarding the host places of the Winter Olympic Games, which of the following pairings is correct? (A) 2026: Milano Cortina, Italy (B) 2030: Sapporo, Japan (C) 2034: Oslo, Norway (D) 2026: Salt Lake City, USA

Answer (A)

#5 The International Olympic Committee (IOC) has announced the host city of the 2032 Summer Olympics, located in Oceania. Which city is it? (A) Sydney (B) Melbourne (C) Brisbane (D) Auckland

Answer (C)

And a little general knowledge:

🔰 The modern Olympic Games were first held in #1896 in #Athens, Greece

🔰 The attached picture is a medal from the 1896 Olympics. The front of the medal shows the head of #Zeus, chief god of Greek mythology. In his right hand Zeus holds a globe, on which stands #Nike, goddess of victory, with the Greek inscription ΟΛΥΜΠΙΑ (Olympia). The back of the medal shows the #AthensAcropolis site.

🔰 The city that has hosted both the Summer and Winter Olympic Games is #Beijing

🔰 In the 1972 #Munich Olympics a terrorist attack occurred, in which many members of the #Israeli delegation were killed

🔰 The Olympics of 1916, 1940 and 1944 were all #cancelled because of the World Wars

🔰 The 2020 Tokyo, Japan Olympics were #postponed to 2021 because of the #pandemic
