Slowly the sea is receding......

They say this is what happens before a tsunami......

Some time ago I shared this thirty-year-old photo with my Vietnamese students, and a Vietnamese classmate in the class actually recognised family members in it.

Many of the schools below have been visited for recruitment, and some of our students graduated from them. The impact of Taiwan's falling birth rate is enormous. Although Taiwan faces more problems than just this one, and neighbouring countries such as Japan and Korea are not much different, it looks as if the government has studied other countries' approaches and decided to let school corporations find their own way out within the scope of laws and regulations.
