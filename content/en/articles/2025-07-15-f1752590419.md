title: "A Stomach in Revolt and a Thoughtful Daughter"
excerpt: "Stomach trouble, a pile of prescriptions and a sense of helplessness, brightened by my child's safe journey home from two weeks of mountain service."
published: true
---

2025.7.15

I got up this morning feeling unwell. My stomach has been acting up badly these past few days and I have suffered a lot. I glanced at the table full of prescription slips: since mid-June until today I have taken more and more medicine, yet my body feels less and less my own. I find it very hard to accept the way my body reacts to my inability to cope with stress. At this age, nobody expects much from a father or husband who is unable to earn money.

My child finished two weeks of mountain service team and came home. Last night they had already told the family they wanted to ride the scooter home themselves. Early this morning they even phoned me personally to discuss the route for getting home safely, and talked with the family to put their mind at ease. All along the way they followed the route and took photos to report progress from time to time. After returning to the Linkou plateau, the first thing they asked was whether they should bring lunch home. What a considerate child.

These reactions of my body leave me feeling powerless. Health really is the source of all good things. I envy my dad even more for a constitution that has never given him headaches, constipation or insomnia. It is a pity he never made good use of it in studying or working hard; he lived most of his life peacefully, as his nature and expectations led him. My younger brother also said in the car that day that Dad has done nothing right and nothing wrong in his life. Perhaps Heaven really has already designed the script for a lifetime, and we mortals have no reason to fight with Heaven.
