42. Taiwan's traditional festivals are rich and colourful, reflecting diverse cultural backgrounds and deep religious traditions. Which of the following lists them in the correct order by month?

① Yanshui Fireworks, Neimen Song Jiang Battle Formation, Donggang Welcoming the Royal Lords Peace Festival, Baosheng Cultural Festival

② Neimen Song Jiang Battle Formation, Baosheng Cultural Festival, Yanshui Fireworks, Donggang Welcoming the Royal Lords Peace Festival

③ Neimen Song Jiang Battle Formation, Baosheng Cultural Festival, Donggang Welcoming the Royal Lords Peace Festival, Yanshui Fireworks

④ Yanshui Fireworks, Neimen Song Jiang Battle Formation, Baosheng Cultural Festival, Donggang Welcoming the Royal Lords Peace Festival

Explanation:

In time order, first is the Yanshui Fireworks, held on the Lantern Festival, one of the most important religious celebrations of Taiwan's early spring. The Neimen Song Jiang Battle Formation is usually held on the 19th of the second lunar month, the birthday of Guanyin. The Baosheng Cultural Festival celebrates the birthday of Emperor Baosheng on the 15th of the third lunar month. Last is the Donggang Welcoming the Royal Lords Peace Festival, held mainly in Donggang Township, Pingtung County, centred on Donglong Temple. Held once every three years, its core is welcoming the five Royal Lords who patrol on behalf of Heaven, and burning the king boat to send off the plague gods and pray for peace. Traditionally it is mostly held in autumn after the harvest.
