2025.6.27

Summer vacation has really started, and I am going to learn how to spend it with a peaceful mind!

Before elementary school, my summers were always spent in the Five Villages of National Chung Hsing University in Taichung. Every day passed gently and quietly, and at night I fell asleep to the sound of insects. When I got up in the morning, Grandpa and Grandma were actually already out, but they would leave a rich breakfast in the cupboard, and I could enjoy a delicious handmade Chinese breakfast all to myself.

But after I returned to Taipei for junior high school, my summers changed. They were not about the study and cram schools that 13- and 14-year-olds care about. The summer between sixth grade and seventh grade I still spent in Taichung, and I innocently started junior high, only to find out at the start of term that there was an English subject you had to prepare for in advance, and I could not even recognise the 26 letters. What I remember is that the summer when I moved from the B-track class in seventh grade up to the A- class in eighth grade, I did odd jobs at an electronics factory. After eighth grade, because I had been promoted to the A+ class, there were full-day classes all week and no summer at all. The summer after I got into the Affiliated Senior High School of National Taiwan Normal University I also worked part-time. The proportion of earning money naturally grew with age, so naturally that I felt it was perfectly normal and just how things were. This is a lesson of life at the bottom, and only in recent years have I slowly come to understand that this mindset can keep you from ever turning things around.

Now my child is at university, and the school has given us a full sixty days of summer break. After being busy for half my life, I am suddenly at a loss. It turns out that spending a summer vacation also has to be learned. These past few days I have first planned language exams and a self-study plan, plus an evening travel sketching and watercolour class. Of course my ongoing exercise will not stop, and I am even considering switching to morning workouts and study, so that while the daytime heat fills the air I have a regular routine of peaceful rest, swimming and fitness.

At the same time I am still hoping that the industry college project is approved, and I want to use it to add more fuel to my doctoral studies and open up more opportunities.
