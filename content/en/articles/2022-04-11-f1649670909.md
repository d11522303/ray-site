What have I been busy with lately?

Of course, daily teaching, preparing online classes, recruiting for senior high and vocational schools, and internship visits. I also worry about the never-ending institutional research, master's thesis supervision and writing of journal papers.

But what I am using all my strength on is this: during the spread of the pandemic, helping third-year students in the Department of Travel Management to win rare and precious workplace opportunities in aviation and the travel industry.

Students in the Department of Travel Management at Hsing Wu University have a full year of internship credits. When students enter their third year they have to start preparing their résumés, settling themselves, organising what they have learned and reviewing what they can do. During a process of submitting applications that lasts for months, they learn how to look for jobs, interview and approach companies.

From February to April, here is what is in progress:

I am grateful for all of these internship opportunities: the best leaders at Hsing Wu pulled strings to win places at well-known companies, and the teachers fully supported students with teaching, training and certification guidance. This is why the students of Hsing Wu's Department of Travel Management, which takes only one class a year, have such promising preparation for entering the workplace and such promising futures.
