Do You Dream of Working on a Cruise Ship?

The cruise service staff interviews that many people have been curious about are finally here, a cruise internship opportunity that Professor Chi-Wen Wu worked hard to win. Resorts World Cruises will first give an introduction to the job next Friday, 2024/3/29, with a Q&A session, and then arrange online interviews in March and April.

Interview time: See the login link for details (in the comments)

Interview format: Online interview

Eligible candidates: Alumni, graduating students and those about to start internships.

Note: Even if you have not yet been trained or received a seafarer's book, you can still sign up if you intend to work on a cruise ship this year. After the interview, you will be notified of the next training course.

The full-time recruitment is looking for fourth-year students and graduates who are willing. If you have already graduated and are working but still want to work on cruise ships, or if you have friends who are interested, this is a rare opportunity. Please join, spread the word, and give yourself a chance to make your dream come true.
