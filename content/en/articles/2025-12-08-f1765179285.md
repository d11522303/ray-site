Before the Tour Guide and Tour Leader Exams, EP3

#2025 (ROC Year 114) Tourism Administration Tour Leader Exam Question

40. Which of the following famous local tourist attractions became widely known after being mistaken by the US military for missile launch silos?

① Hakka tulou ② Dong ethnic drum towers ③ Northern Shaanxi cave dwellings ④ Longmen Grottoes

This question is easy, practically a free point for Taiwanese students. If you don't know it, you might go and stand in front of Taiwan High Speed Rail (THSR) Miaoli Station, or shop at Big City in Zhubei, and you will see what a Hakka tulou is. Of course, I even more strongly recommend taking the Mini Three Links to Xiamen for a real self-guided day trip to the Hakka tulou, where you can see and feel with your own eyes what kind of distinctive buildings some of the Taiwanese Hakka and Hoklo ancestors lived in hundreds of years ago.

As usual, here are a few derived questions for you to try.

1. The World Heritage Hakka tulou, also called Hakka enclosed houses, are mainly located in which province of China? ① Jiangxi ② Fujian ③ Guangdong ④ Guangxi

2. China's three great grotto treasure houses listed as World Heritage, the Mogao Caves in Dunhuang, the Yungang Grottoes and the Longmen Grottoes, are treasuries of stone carvings and murals of Buddhist culture from the 4th to the 14th century, and are world-famous tourist sites. When a travel agency plans an itinerary, it must follow China's current provinces. Which of the following pairs of site and province is correct? ① Mogao Caves - Xinjiang, Yungang Grottoes - Shanxi, Longmen Grottoes - Henan ② Mogao Caves - Gansu, Yungang Grottoes - Shanxi, Longmen Grottoes - Henan ③ Mogao Caves - Gansu, Yungang Grottoes - Shaanxi, Longmen Grottoes - Henan ④ Mogao Caves - Gansu, Yungang Grottoes - Shanxi, Longmen Grottoes - Shaanxi
