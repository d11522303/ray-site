Have you been to New York? I haven't, but everyone knows the Statue of Liberty stands there, and it's common knowledge that it was a gift from France to the US. #1 Regarding the historical background of the Statue of Liberty, which of the following statements is incorrect?

(A) The Statue of Liberty was a gift from the French people to the American people to commemorate the centennial of American independence.

(B) The statue was designed by French sculptor Frédéric Auguste Bartholdi.

(C) The statue's internal steel framework was built by Gustave Eiffel, the engineer of the Eiffel Tower in Paris.

(D) Since its completion, administrative authority over the Statue of Liberty has always belonged to the US Department of Commerce's Lighthouse Board.

Explanation: (D) The Statue of Liberty was initially managed by the Lighthouse Board, but in 1901 it was transferred to the War Department, and from 1933 it has been maintained and managed by the National Park Service.

#2 As a tour leader taking a group to visit the Statue of Liberty in New York, which of the following pieces of guide information is incorrect? (A) The Statue of Liberty stands on Liberty Island, the first stop for early immigrants landing. (B) For safety reasons, the balcony around the statue's torch has been closed to the public since 1916. (C) At the 1886 dedication ceremony, it was presided over by the then US President Abraham Lincoln. (D) The statue is made of copper, and has turned green through long exposure to oxidation in the air.

Explanation: (C) The dedication ceremony was presided over by President Grover Cleveland. Abraham Lincoln (1809-1865) was the 16th US president, whose life is closely tied to the bloodiest war in American history, the Civil War, and to the abolition of slavery.

#3 The design details of the Statue of Liberty contain many symbolic meanings. Which of the following statements is correct?

(A) The seven rays on the goddess's crown represent the seven continents and four oceans of the world.

(B) The goddess holds a torch high in her right hand, and the tablet in her left hand is inscribed with the full text of the US Constitution.

(C) Broken chains lie at the goddess's feet, in a prominent position, intended as a strong protest against slavery at the time.

(D) The goddess's facial design is said to be modelled on Bartholdi's wife.

Explanation: (A) is correct. The tablet in her left hand is inscribed with the date of the signing of the Declaration of Independence, "JULY IV MDCCLXXVI". The broken chains at her feet are at the goddess's ankles, largely covered by her robe with only a part exposed, symbolising humanity's liberation from servitude, tyranny and oppression. This signifies that freedom is no longer just a dream but an action already realised. And the Statue of Liberty's serene yet resolute face was based on Bartholdi's "mother," Charlotte.
