0/700 START GAME

I have just registered a new identity, logging into a learning game full of anticipation of knowledge and professional adventure.

After paying the half-year fee of over NT$20,000, I could activate my login to the student player's campus information system, where I can download an electronic certificate myself (as in the picture). It really feels ceremonial, especially when I see the hexagonal-bolt school emblem watermark on the certificate. I know that as I clear levels in this learning game, I must check that the bolt at every link is working properly, and keep the bolts of every ability series' weapons tightened at all times, because within 700 days I must defeat the first big boss (Note 1).

Wishing myself a smooth run, with plenty of ammo and a full health bar at all times!

Note 1. Article 2 of the Regulations on Doctoral Candidacy Qualification Examination: Doctoral students of the Graduate Institute of Applied Science and Technology must pass the qualifying examination within two years of enrolment; those who fail to pass by then shall be dismissed.
