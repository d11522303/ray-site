#TourLeaderExam16DaysToGo

48 Many famous tourist attractions in Zhejiang, China depend on local government for upkeep. Which of the following is NOT located in Zhejiang Province? (A) Qiandao Lake Scenic Area (B) Wuzhen (C) West Lake in Hangzhou (D) Hanshan Temple

The answer is (D). The Tang dynasty poet Zhang Ji's famous poem "Mooring at Maple Bridge at Night" goes: "The moon sets, crows caw, frost fills the sky; river maples and fishing lights face my troubled sleep. Outside Gusu city stands Hanshan Temple; its midnight bell reaches the traveller's boat." The "Gusu" in it is the ancient name of Suzhou in Jiangsu.

One of the options in this question refers to a tragedy that once happened in the travel industry. Students who want to know more can ask AI themselves. Based on it, here are 2 practical tour leader questions for reference.

#1 If a tour group led by a tour leader encounters a major safety incident in mainland China, such as the Qiandao Lake incident, resulting in missing or dead group members, when should the tour leader report to the Tourism Administration, MOTC (or the organisation it commissions)?

(A) After all investigations are over and the local public security report has been obtained.

(B) Immediately after learning of the incident.

(C) A written report within three days after returning home is enough.

(D) Only the dispatching travel agency needs to be notified; there is no need to notify the Tourism Administration.

Answer: (B)

Explanation: Under the Regulations Governing the Management of Travel Agencies, when a major travel safety incident occurs, it must be reported to the Tourism Administration "immediately". Cross-strait matters are especially sensitive and need coordination through bodies such as the Straits Exchange Foundation, so timeliness is critical.

#2 The area around West Lake in Hangzhou is rich in local products. When arranging afternoon tea or shopping stops, which famous green tea does a tour leader most often recommend? (A) Biluochun (B) Tieguanyin (C) Longjing tea (D) Da Hong Pao

Answer: (C)
