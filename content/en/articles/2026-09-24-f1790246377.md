24/676 "Survival Countdown: 10 Minutes"

Can you escape? I have already signed up. If you're interested in playing the game with me to learn fire escape common sense, registration is in the comments.

"Survival Countdown: 10 Minutes" is a decision-making training game centred on fire scenarios and multiple escape routes. After entering a simulated fire scene, players must observe the fire, smoke, exits, obstacles and other clues in the environment, choose the most appropriate way to escape under time pressure, and keep making judgements as the situation changes.

During the game, players may face choices such as "keep going forward, change route, go back, or look for another exit", and different decisions lead to different results. This is not simply memorising fire safety knowledge, but a fire survival challenge combining observation, risk recognition, information integration, problem-solving and emergency decision-making.
