How Singapore's Ministry of Manpower Views Global Academic Standing

Only four Taiwanese schools and programmes are listed. The aim is to ensure that high-level foreign talent coming to Singapore is of high quality and complements the local Singaporean workforce.

When assessing Employment Pass applications, Singapore's Ministry of Manpower (MOM) uses the COMPASS points system. Every year it reviews the world's important schools and programmes against national talent needs to identify the human resources Singapore needs most. An applicant must score at least 40 points in total across four core criteria and two bonus criteria to pass the assessment.

Under the qualifications criterion, MOM awards points according to school rankings. Top institutions can earn 20 points and are divided into two categories, A and B:

Group A: graduates of any department in the whole school earn 20 points.

Group B: only graduates of specific designated departments earn 20 points.

The Taiwanese qualifications listed as earning 20 points are, in alphabetical order:

Group A: National Cheng Kung University, National Taiwan University, National Tsing Hua University

Group B: National Taiwan Ocean University, Bachelor's Degree Program in Ocean Engineering Technology (ESG)
