[The Bullied Flight Attendant]

Working at a vocational university, I also feel there's a problem with exam-based education further up the line, because most of the students we receive are students who never learned how to solve exam problems, not bad students.

Luckily, most of them want to do something, and we have designed a scaffolded learning process of certificates and career awareness. As long as they are at a university of technology, or even earlier at a vocational high school, they won't be troubled by competitive learning in Chinese, maths and so on. Instead they can grow in the area they're interested in and become well-rounded people who adapt to their environment.

In Taiwan, I think there's still a chance to shape the environment. But if in future salary decides how we look at people, and that becomes the view of most of society, with incidents of disrespect towards service workers (like the XX Airlines flight attendant these past few days), then the kindness of a certain group of Taiwanese will gradually be withdrawn and become another kind of protective colouring.
