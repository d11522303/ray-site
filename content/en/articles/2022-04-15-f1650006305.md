Good Listening Share No. 2: 49 minutes

Taiwan's local COVID cases passed a thousand in April, so I'm making a note of it.

The photo is also from today: students from the HWU Department of Travel Management being interviewed on site for jobs at Starlux Airlines' ground services.

So I still want to share some information about when we might travel abroad again. I recommend the "King's Travel Ears" series from the Pan-Travel Guests Club podcast, in which General Manager Lin of Jiguang Travel is interviewed and shares his latest observations with unique insight. After listening, I really felt how admirable a professional manager who can run a travel agency is, both in professional ability and in the way he speaks and explains things.

First, a disclaimer: we must not make any value judgement about catching the virus, and we must never use numbers to label the people or the region of a place as something bad, because a virus does not choose its people. Where there is a host, it infects; where there is space, it survives and spreads. For ourselves, the only thing to do is look after our own health and immunity, to improve our chances of staying well, and so reduce our social responsibility for spreading it after infection.

In fact, I have been browsing flights abroad online lately. I just look around and don't dare book. But then I see classmates and friends in Europe, the US and Japan, who have long since reopened and are travelling everywhere, like a parallel universe, and it is hard to bear. In class I also share with students that since 2022, the share prices of major global cruise companies such as RCCI and CCL have already rebounded, and passenger numbers and bookings are gradually returning to pre-pandemic levels. So what I care about most is when we can travel abroad and return home without quarantine! My personal hope is that as long as vaccination is effective in real-world prevention and in confidence about disease control, we can open the borders and stop locking ourselves in. I hope that day comes soon, whether July or September, the sooner the better.

So what does General Manager Domingo Lin of Jiguang Travel think? Working on the front line, he gives us his most exclusive observations and insights, and shares what he expects and what he is preparing and doing now. If you want to travel abroad, give it a listen.
