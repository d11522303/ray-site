I Almost Became a Private School Student

Long ago in high school I didn't study seriously. I spent all my time on clubs and part-time jobs, and pushed most of my schoolwork aside, saying I couldn't understand it. Thinking back now, the truth is that I simply didn't study hard enough. The result was that I failed three subjects, English 44, physics 57 and chemistry 58, and was held back a year. In the Group 2 (science) class, 16 classmates failed together. I remember being scolded by my parents several times when I got home. They truly never expected that a child who had never given them a worry about studying would become a repeater at the turn from second to third year of high school.

At the time, my mother asked an older boy from next door, who was at university, to talk with me. I've forgotten what he said. I only remember that he suggested I transfer straight to Yanping High School next to the Affiliated Senior High School of National Taiwan Normal University. Back then the Affiliated High School only wanted its own repeaters, while Yanping accepted them all, so I wouldn't have to study an extra year and could still get into a good university.

Did I choose that?

Once an Affiliated High School person, always an Affiliated High School soul. How could a young, headstrong Affiliated High School student make such a decision? So my parents could do nothing with their stubborn son and still let me stay at the Affiliated High School. Useless as I was, I even switched from the science track to the humanities and business Group 1, and in the end I still only got into Fu Jen Catholic University on the university entrance exam, and into the Sociology Department, something I regretted for the rest of my life.

Sometimes I still think of those summer nights when I was persuaded again and again. If I had listened to the advice then and gone to Yanping, what would my life have been like?
