2025.6.29

In Taiwanese society, saying "I don't have enough money" is a kind of protective colouring. After looking up the data and proving that I really don't have enough money, I find it rather sad.

The British newspaper The Independent writes (Note 1) that America's Generation X is the generation with the worst financial security, and that most Americans believe they need an income of over US$100,000 a year to live comfortably. Converted to Taiwan, that is NT$3 million a year.

Generation X refers to Taiwanese people born between the 54th and 69th years of the Republic (1965-1980). According to statistics from the Ministry of the Interior (Note 2), there are 5.47 million of them across Taiwan, the largest group among the six generations, though not far from the generations before and after.

Jean M. Twenge (Note 3) has written that Generation X began entering society in the early 1990s. Its traits include wearing black clothes, widespread cynicism and embracing individualism. Gen X also married later and began having children while unmarried. Gen X is more sceptical of authority and clearly more distrustful of others.
