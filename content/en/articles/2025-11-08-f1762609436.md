[Daily English Vocabulary for Tour Leaders] Day5 2025.11.8

abide, (v), 遵守, to follow or accept rules or decisions

assess, (v), 評估, to evaluate or estimate the value of something

inflation, (n), 通貨膨脹, a general increase in prices

circulating, (v/adj), 流通中的, moving around or being passed from person to person

stagnating, (v/adj), 停滯的, not growing or progressing

interchangeably, (adv), 可互換地, in a way that can be used in place of each other

a stop en route, (phr), 途中停留, a pause or break during a journey

refueling, (n/v), 加油, the act of filling with fuel

dropping off, (phr/v), 下車；送達, letting someone out or delivering something

sought-after, (adj), 搶手的, in high demand or desired

essentially, (adv), 本質上, basically or fundamentally

even longer layover, (phr), 更長的轉機時間, an extended waiting period between flights

en route, (adv/phr), 在途中, on the way to a destination

layover, (n), 中途停留, a short stay between parts of a journey
