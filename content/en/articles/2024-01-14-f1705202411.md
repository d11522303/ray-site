Thirty Years of Taiwan's Elections

CommonWealth Magazine used AI to describe the results of each local race, which is a very distinctive analysis tool, and I also love LINE's chart of legislative seats, which shows the shape of the coming parliament at a glance. If you're interested, go to the comments, type in the district you care about, and see who your neighbours voted for.

I remember that in 1994, as a third-year sociology student at Fu Jen, I worked every evening as a telephone interviewer at a party office and followed the Taipei mayoral election closely. It was a three-way race between Chen Shui-bian of the DPP, Huang Ta-chou of the KMT and Jaw Shaw-kong of the New Party. On election night, my then girlfriend, now my wife, and I rode around on a scooter to see each camp's election night. We finally reached the ground floor of the Hsing Wu building at the end of Qingdao East Road on Hangzhou South Road, and came face to face with Mayor Huang Ta-chou speaking one by one to the supporters who were already leaving. The scene was deserted, and we still remember the dejected look on his face.
