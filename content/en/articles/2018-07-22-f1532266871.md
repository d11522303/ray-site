---
title: "Riding the Wind to Hangzhou: Flights from Xiamen to Shanghai Cancelled One by One"
excerpt: "Visiting Taiwanese interns at a five-star hotel in Hangzhou on a typhoon day, and hearing how highly the hotel's management rates Taiwan's hospitality education."
published: true
---

On 22 July I flew to Hangzhou on the wind. Early that morning, the flights from Xiamen to Shanghai were being cancelled one after another at the boarding gates! Amid the tension of typhoon warnings we still heard our boarding call, and with only a half-hour delay we walked through the jet bridge and set off for our next stop, Hangzhou.

Waiting to pick us up were the trainees from the five-star Kaiyuan Mingdu Hotel and HR specialist Li Shilei. Shilei graduated from the tourism department of Henan Institute of Science and Technology; thanks to an outstanding internship performance he stayed on to work in Hangzhou, so he arranged the hotel's mentoring for this visit. From the students' reactions you could tell they get along well with their colleagues at the hotel! And in every part of the arrangements, of course, you could also feel how much he loves his job.

Four students at the hotel come from National Ilan University in the east, Providence University in central Taiwan, National Kaohsiung University of Hospitality and Tourism in the south, and National Penghu University of Science and Technology on the outlying islands, working in the concierge and reception department and at the bar. They have come to understand deeply the cultural differences across the strait and the differences in management style. They also made a special point of reminding future trainees to lose some weight, because the hotel uniforms are all very small, and if you can't fit into one you won't get the chance to stand at the counter.

After the discussion session, when we talked with the hotel's senior management, both General Manager Jin and HR Director Lyu praised Taiwan's hospitality education a great deal because of the students' attentive service and wholehearted enthusiasm. They also said they would warmly welcome any student who wants to join Kaiyuan in the future!

Tomorrow two of the bar-department students will take part in the group-wide service competition, which the hotel group has held for 13 years. I wish them good results for all their hard practice!
