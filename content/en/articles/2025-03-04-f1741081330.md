2025.3.4

Tuesday has seven classes in one day, which I taught until dizzy, just like today's changeable weather.

But there are several things I want to record.

In the morning I took on a graduate student from Uzbekistan. She wants to study negative comments on Instagram. I find it interesting and think we can do it, although the research will have to be written in English, which is a challenge for both her and me. In the licensing class that started at noon, there was a working alumnus who graduated in 2019 who came back to class. He said such courses are expensive outside, so he feels he is getting a bargain coming back to study. Later, after class ended at five, I found a student I knew from the Applied English Department sitting in on our licensing class. She said the Travel Management students are very fortunate to have so many good courses to learn from. The school pays me NT$800 per hour for the licensing class, so teaching well is only what I should do, and I hope such feedback can become one of the foundations of the school's sustainability.

Also, yesterday I decided to deepen and toughen the Vietnamese class. Today's response and results were good. While organising the Introduction to Tourism materials, I found that the proportion of students in Vietnam who can attend university is not high. If I don't make the course content heavier, they will surely say after returning home that they learned nothing in Taiwan, and I, having once been their teacher, would deeply regret it many years later.

After getting home, while discussing the air conditioner with Yen-Ling, the plumber and electrician suddenly called to help replace the exhaust fan. After it was replaced, the fan was still very loud and Yen-Ling was very dissatisfied. I felt it too but didn't want to trouble anyone again. As it happened, the plumber had left his tools at our home, so on his return trip I asked him to adjust it for a while, and in exchange we got a very quiet bathroom.

So my personality problem is really a big problem. And then I kept waking up and falling asleep all night, unable to rest fully because of a small event the next day. That is also a big problem.
