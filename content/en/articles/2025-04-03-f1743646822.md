2025.4.3

The news is all about Trump, but I don't know why I should spend time studying tariffs.

Is it because they will affect my income? Actually, no. My main income is my salary from Hsing Wu University. The few hundred thousand NT dollars in the stock market is part of an investment game. My work income is what matters most, and over the years most of my earnings have gone into the home I live in. I hope not to touch my cash savings and retirement funds for over a decade, because I still hope to walk healthily into old age. So where should I spend my time?

1. Spend time adding value to my current profession. At noon I chatted with Yen-Ling about difficulties at work. The most important thing, I thought, is to use my own expertise to create new income. With nearly 20 years of teaching, there is no turning back. Adding value and depth to my research is the only direction.

2. Right now, health and maintenance of my body come first. My writing and my image both depend on good looks and confidence. Daily running and a sense of satisfaction in body and mind are the top priority, and a healthy diet adds to that.

3. Financial planning is hard to decide on a short-term basis. Cashing out investments would also be a wash because of inflation, and I have no spare money to invest anyway. On top of that, I am still considering a research plan for a doctoral programme, and between the two, the time cost allows only one.

Sorting out my mindset and current situation, I realise tariffs and the cross-strait political struggle don't have much to do with me. What matters is continuing my work at Hsing Wu University and adding value to myself. Putting all my energy there is the key work of my life, and it is also the guarantee for my family and my future.
