Before the Tour Guide and Tour Leader Exams, EP52

#47DaysLeftToTheTourLeaderExam

14 Which of the following regions was NOT within the scope of "British India" under the British Empire? (A) Pakistan (B) Myanmar (C) Bangladesh (D) Nepal (2024 foreign-language tour leader exam)

The answer is (D)

By the end of the 19th century almost all of Asia had been divided up by the great powers of the time. The attached map is one often used in class: Britain, France, the Netherlands, Spain and Japan divided up almost all the land, and only a very few countries in Asia kept their independence through diplomatic skill, geography or military reform.

#Thailand (Siam): its location lies between French Indochina and British India. King Rama V (Chulalongkorn) used its position as a "buffer state" to seek a balance between the two great powers, Britain and France, and actively carried out modernising reforms.

#Nepal, #Bhutan: located in the Himalayas, although under British protection, they kept their royal houses and autonomy.

Besides the British India mentioned in this question, the Dutch East Indies and French Indochina are also directions often tested. If you are interested, you can ask AI to understand how the colonial policies of that time influenced the later political systems and the founding of the nations!

#1 When a tour leader presents the cultural tourism resources of 19th-century French Indochina, which group of countries best embodies the blend of French colonial architecture, Catholic faith and coffee culture? (A) India, Pakistan, Bangladesh (B) Philippines, Indonesia, Malaysia (C) Vietnam, Laos, Cambodia (D) Thailand, Myanmar, Nepal

The answer is (C)

#2 The "Dutch East Indies" was the predecessor of which present-day country, whose colonial-era territorial extent and administrative framework directly shaped the country's territorial composition today? (A) Philippines (B) Indonesia (C) Malaysia (D) Sri Lanka

The answer is (B)
