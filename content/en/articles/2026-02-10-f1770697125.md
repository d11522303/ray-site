43 Which of the following cities is not on Germany's famous Romantic Road (Romantische Straße)? (A) Würzburg (B) Augsburg (C) Füssen (D) Heidelberg

Answer: (D)

The Romantic Road was Germany's first officially named themed tourist route. It links the wine-growing region in the north (Würzburg) with the snowy Alpine scenery in the south (Füssen). The main reason it was created is that in 1950, after the world wars ended, Germany lay in ruins, its economy had collapsed, and its international image was very poor. To attract international tourists and the American troops stationed in Germany and their families to spend money, the German government and local tourism operators put together a historic route named "Romantic" to shift the stereotype of wartime Germany. Most of this route follows the military road of Roman times (Via Claudia Augusta), linking historic old towns so that tourists can experience old German culture like travellers of the past.
