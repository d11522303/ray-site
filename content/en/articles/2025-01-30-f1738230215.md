30 January 2025

Little moments on the journey back north. It really is a joy to travel together as a family!

Along Highway 11 we parked by the roadside and queued to try the Hualien Route 055 Yanliao lobster, waiting an hour with the other Lunar New Year travellers. Apart from the lobster, the seafood was reasonably fresh, and the stir-fried wild fern and ocean sunfish were good, but everything else was not. We were also limited to an hour for the meal, and the restaurant had made the process very complicated. Online ordering would fix that completely, though then the sour-faced aunties on site would probably lose their jobs.

Compared with my experiences of travelling overseas, or even in mainland China, this Hualien restaurant was simply not up to standard, and I don't even want to make the comparison. The dining environment and the restrooms were also very hard to accept. I know the Pacific Ocean has no lid, but this is my hometown, and I sincerely hope every kind of business will raise its quality while making money, and not live only for money.

Travelling around Taiwan with family, without having to haul big bags onto a plane to go abroad, is a very happy and blessed thing.
