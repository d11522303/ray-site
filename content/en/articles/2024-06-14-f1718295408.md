University Results Are Out? It's Actually Not Over Yet!

The university application results are out. I posted the admission scores from the 1991 joint entrance exam, and looking closely, after so many years the popular departments in the science and engineering groups are still about the same, and the groupings are just old wine in new bottles! But 33 years ago, within a month after our exams in early July, our scores and placements came out, and most classmates settled on their departments.

Today's high school students are different. After sitting the GSAT before the Lunar New Year, five months have passed. They have been through the first-stage registration, the second-stage tests and interviews at various campuses (many with fees), and application choices, until today's placement results. After repeatedly weighing and preparing for one trial after another, only some students have settled on their university department.

Take my own child's class of 38 high school students: as of today only 6 have secured a place. Excluding those going to study across the strait or overseas, nearly thirty classmates still have to sit the subject tests in mid-July, which is a real ordeal for this group of energetic boys.

In a Taiwan where everyone can go to university, is it really good to design a system that wears students out like this?

At the very least, as a parent, I am really annoyed.
