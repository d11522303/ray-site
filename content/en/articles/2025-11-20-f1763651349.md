Middle-Aged Man Takes the PhD Exams: Update 2025.12.2

National Taiwan University of Science and Technology, Graduate Institute of Applied Science and Technology: admitted, 4th place

National Taiwan University, Department of Library and Information Science, PhD programme selection: waitlist, 2nd place

National Tsing Hua University, Department of Education and Learning Technology, PhD programme selection: waitlist, 5th place

Fu Jen Catholic University, Graduate Institute of Business Administration, PhD programme: waitlist, 2nd place

This is my first attempt at doctoral entrance exams in the twenty-five years since I finished my master's, and the results are as above. I plan to enrol at National Taiwan University of Science and Technology next September. I applied to four schools in total. The two I most hoped to attend, National Taiwan University and National Tsing Hua University, both put me on the waitlist, and with my alma mater Fu Jen's business PhD also a waitlist, I can't help feeling a sense of loss, feeling that my effort has still not been fully recognised. But perhaps in life you can't have everything, and I'm thinking about whether to fight another round next year.

In this process I did the very best I could, and the whole experience was very informative and educational: the struggle of revising and re-revising my application documents, preparing for the interviews round by round, and then facing the professors' questions alone, session after session. I could feel the professors' expertise and expectations, and they scrutinised the materials I submitted in concrete detail. It was more nerve-racking and exciting than reading the written review comments of journal papers. The real lessons about organising and arranging my mindset only came after I took action, but all of it carried very high expectations and hopes for myself.

At noon on 2025/11/20 I opened the registration system online and saw the National Taiwan University waitlist, and before bed I saw that Tsing Hua was also a waitlist, with an even lower ranking. The sense of loss is hard to describe in words. On 2025/11/26, after all the admitted candidates at NTU's library and information science programme had registered, it was confirmed that I could not be admitted. But on the same day NTUST also announced its results, with me admitted in 4th place. The tangled feelings of disappointment and anticipation were hard for me to digest, and as I probed around inside myself I could not find a happy feeling.

I took some time to message and thank the teachers and classmates who had helped me greatly along the way of the exams. Although I have long held the Ministry's associate professor qualification, the state of mind I am in right now really lets me appreciate the true meaning of "the sea of learning has no shore". If you have expectations of yourself, you should discipline yourself to set some challenges, and prepare and face them well, so that you don't waste your time.

Going back to study for a PhD only when life takes a turn backward? My feelings are very mixed, and rather childish. Earlier I only wanted to do a PhD abroad and did not want to study in Taiwan. Over the years I studied doctoral programmes in the US, UK, Australia, and even Japan and Singapore with English-taught programmes on their websites in detail, and I childishly imagined the day I would enrol, but that day never really appeared before me. Of course I could use the excuse that life's dreams are full but reality is lean, or family pressure, financial needs, and a malnourished mindset that topples in the wind, as countless reasons. But right now I truly feel that it was simply that I didn't prepare well and let time flow away like water, always rationalising the wishes I never fulfilled in order to forgive myself.

Over the past two years, the passing of close family and a student, my beloved partner's illness, and my parents ageing ever faster, all the excuses I made up for myself would become real obstacles. I really don't want to resign myself to fate. If I want my life to be different, the only way is to act and put it into practice. You only know what the scenery along the road is like once you take a big step out.
