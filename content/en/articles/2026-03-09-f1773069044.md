Before the Tour Guide and Tour Leader Exams, EP93

#TourLeaderExamCountdown6Days

46. In 2021, which European World Cultural Heritage site was struck off the World Heritage list because of over-development? (A) Historic Centre of Florence (B) Liverpool Maritime Mercantile City (C) Paris, Banks of the Seine (D) Historic Centre of Vienna

Answer (B)

Historically, only three World Heritage sites have been formally delisted:

1. Arabian Oryx Sanctuary (2007): because the Omani government reduced the sanctuary's area by 90% to extract oil.

2. Dresden Elbe Valley, Germany (2009): because it insisted on building the modern Waldschlösschen Bridge across the valley, spoiling the landscape.

3. Liverpool Maritime Mercantile City, UK (2021): as the question says, because of over-modernisation of the port area.

Below are questions on recent well-known World Heritage sites:

#1 Which building among the World Cultural Heritage sites was listed even though it is still unfinished? (A) Notre-Dame de Paris (B) Sagrada Familia in Barcelona (C) Milan Cathedral (D) Sydney Opera House

Answer (B)

#2 Which of the following heritage sites is currently on UNESCO's endangered list because of "landscape fragmentation" and "high-rise building plans", and is most likely to become the next European historic city at risk of delisting?

(A) Mont-Saint-Michel

(B) Acropolis of Athens

(C) Historic Centre of Vienna

(D) Historic Centre of Prague

Answer (C)
