Is studying useless?

Then you chose the wrong school and department.

1. In this age of diversity and competition, some departments are already changing, so before enrolling, those with ambition should look at what graduates of that department are doing. Is it work you want to do yourself?

2. Next, go online and look at the curriculum map. Does it repeat what you learned in three years of high school? Are there subjects you want to learn, or fields that at least don't look off-putting? Otherwise, you will complain constantly for the next four years, or strive in vain for a high GPA only to open your transcript and find nothing but easy filler courses, four years of your life wasted.

3. Finally, look at whether the professors' experience and publications relate to the department's development, because this affects the content and quality of teaching. Is there anything about the adjunct industry instructors that you admire, someone you'd like to take as a role model in the future?

4. One more: what matters most when studying in Taiwan is the environment. If you have the chance, look at the classrooms and campus and feel whether the classmates give off an atmosphere you like and that is positive. Otherwise, after four years you will have met a group of classmates who cannot encourage or help each other, just lifelong drinking buddies, or your abilities and horizons will be about the same as four years ago, or even weaker and narrower. Then university will truly have been turned by you into "four years of play."
