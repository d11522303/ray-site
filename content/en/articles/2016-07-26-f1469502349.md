[Le Cordon Bleu Grand Diplôme Programme: Chinese-Interpreted Class Starts 2016/09/23!]

Registration for the 08/05 information session is open: http://goo.gl/forms/gahK0RIyXYPNcac93

The Le Cordon Bleu Grand Diplôme programme is divided into French cuisine and French pastry courses. It runs for 15 months in total, with 16 students per class, and includes hands-on culinary practice and food and beverage management courses. French Le Cordon Bleu chefs teach on site in person, so you don't need to fly abroad, saving airfare and accommodation costs!

Register for the 2016-17 programme before 2016/11/30 and enjoy a waiver of the screening fee.

Chinese-interpreted class start dates

2016: Autumn intake 09/23

2017: Winter intake 01/06, Spring intake 04/07, Summer intake 07/14, Autumn intake 09/29

Brochure for the Chinese-interpreted Le Cordon Bleu Grand Diplôme programme: https://goo.gl/kyh8v1

You can book a visit to see the facilities in advance!
