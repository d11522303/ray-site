My Running Mentor, Teacher Wu Chin-Yun, RIP

Even today, university PE electives always seem to be popular, and everyone tries every way to avoid ending up in a sport they dislike. I honestly cannot remember why I signed up for Teacher Wu Chin-Yun's jogging class back then. As I recall, none of my classmates took the same session as me. I was just one of the thousands of nameless students in Teacher Wu's PE electives, but of all the PE classes I have taken since childhood, I remember only Teacher Wu Chin-Yun's, and I still benefit from it enormously today.

Teacher Wu's classes were very substantial. She taught with a strong, full voice, and as I remember it she joined us herself even for the warm-up and shuttle runs before class, and we had to keep up. In the first lesson, before we had even started jogging, our legs were weak and our hands were shaking. In the second lesson we had to run 10 laps of the track on Guizi Road before cooling down and ending class. Students with poor stamina could give all they had and still not satisfy Teacher Wu enough to be dismissed, and for the final exam we had to run three laps around the outermost edge of the campus.

I knew back then that Teacher Wu had been a sports star in her youth, but I really never expected that in her class, university students who were not PE majors would be drilled like this. Only after seeing the news today did I learn that Teacher Wu had been the "Queen of Triathlon." No wonder she shook her head the whole time as she trained alongside us. Compared with her fitness, we useless university students were truly just feed chickens.

This summer I will find time to go back to the school and run those three laps, in gratitude for your guidance and teaching back then!

Beloved Teacher Wu, rest in peace. R.I.P.
