Tour Guide and Tour Leader Exam Prep EP43

#Tour Leader Exam Countdown: 56 Days

In the history of Taiwan's tea industry in the late Qing dynasty, the British merchant John Dodd played an important role, and above all his direct shipment of Taiwan oolong tea to New York in the United States in 1869 drew attention. Who was it that worked together with John Dodd at that time to promote oolong tea exports? (A) Chen Tien-lai (B) Kuo Chun-yang (C) Chen Ching-po (D) Li Chun-sheng

Explanation:

The answer to this question is #LiChunSheng, a native of Xiamen, Fujian, Taiwanese industrialist and philosopher, and a Dadaocheng merchant-gentleman of Taipei. He was both a wealthy merchant who made his fortune in the tea trade and a founder of the Presbyterian Church in Taiwan, and is known as the "Father of Taiwan's Tea" and "Taiwan's First Thinker."

#ChenTienLai and #ChenChingPo, among the options for the same question, were father and son. Chen Tien-lai's former residence, the "Chin Chi Tea Firm" on Guide Street in Dadaocheng, has been designated a Taipei City historic site. Restoration work began in 2024 under Taipei's cultural bureau and it is expected to open to the public in early 2026, when you will be able to feel the splendid past of the family home of Taiwan's top tea merchant of the time.

#KuoChunYang was also a tea merchant. In 1888 he moved from Java to Taiwan to run a tea production and marketing business, and set up the "Chin Mao Tea Firm" in Dadaocheng, with his nephew Kuo Po-jung as general manager. He exported Pouchong tea to Southeast Asia and also took part in founding the Taiwan Tea Merchants Association, so all four people listed among the options were key figures in Taiwan's tea industry.

＃1 Regarding the contribution of the English merchant John Dodd to Taiwan's tea industry, which of the following statements is FALSE?

A. He set up Dodd & Co. in Tamsui and promoted tea exports

B. He introduced Anxi tea varieties from Fujian and tea-making techniques from Fuzhou and Xiamen

C. He was the first to export Taiwan tea in large quantities to the American market

D. During the Japanese colonial period he served as director of the tea experiment station and institutionalised tea production

Explanation: D is not the correct answer.

＃2 Regarding the development of Taiwan's tea industry and export ports, which of the following is correct?

A. In the Qing dynasty, Taiwan tea was mainly exported from Anping Port to the European market

B. The rise of Taiwan's tea industry was closely tied to the opening of Tamsui to trade in 1860

C. In the Qing dynasty, Taiwan tea first had to be shipped to Xiamen or Fuzhou for refining before export

D. Dadaocheng was mainly known for the sugar and rice trade and had little to do with tea

Explanation: B is the correct answer.
