Amazing Thailand

I once read a passage in which Yukio Mishima describes the Bangkok royal court: "In a land where temples glitter and flowers and fruit cover the fields, people under the languid sun devote themselves wholeheartedly to worshipping the Buddha and firmly believe in reincarnation, yet they shun and evade any orderly system of theory, possessing an indolence as heavy as gold, and a spirit that sways unmoving in the breeze beneath the trees." - Yukio Mishima, *The Temple of Dawn*

As I sort through each day's itinerary and photos, I use words to savour the air of Siam once more, recalling the brilliant, scorching sun, Wat Arun (the Temple of Dawn) glowing like gold in the evening breeze along the Chao Phraya River, and people on a different wavelength from mine. Together with travellers from all over the world met along the way, I was a little amazed by Thailand.
