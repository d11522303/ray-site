When the Cart Reaches the Hill, a Road Will Appear: Simply Providence

I left home at first light to attend a conference I had long looked forward to, the 2023 conference of the Taiwan Society of Tourism Management. It was not because the host was the TSSCI-indexed Journal of Tourism and Leisure Studies; I actually went to find something again.

The society's conference is one of the most important gatherings of research for Taiwan's tourism and hospitality academics. When it was founded 31 years ago, it was formed by a few senior professors from the handful of universities with tourism-related departments and graduate institutes at the time: Chinese Culture, Shih Hsin, Providence, Hsing Wu and Ming Chuan. I was required to join as a student in 1999 when I was studying in the tourism graduate institute, but my first real paper presentation was at the 2000 Kaohsiung Hospitality College conference.

By happenstance I later went to work at the Hualien tourism college, and began another round of organising academic conferences. Which of these events have my friends attended?

2010 Asia Tourism Forum @ Hualien Ideal Land

2011 World Culinary Forum @ Chientan (Youth Activity Center), Taipei

2012 APTA Asia Pacific Tourism Association International Conference @ Yuanshan, Taipei

2014 Cross-Strait Tourism Conference @ Fuzhou, China

2016 Tourism Industry Innovation and Quality Service Workshop @ Hsing Wu, Linkou

2018 Global Chinese Tourism and Hospitality Summit @ Hsing Wu, Linkou

Compiling this list reminded me how many friends and peers I annoyed with my frantic emails. It also reminded me of how fast I walked across the Shoufeng campus, answering everything immediately, strange behaviour that really did not fit the relaxed life of Hualien or its quiet campus. I remember several times being so busy that my car ran out of fuel and stopped by the highway, and I had to phone a colleague to pick me up and then go fetch my child to our rented home. Once I even drove my little dog to the school by mistake, and only discovered him sleeping in the back seat when I stopped and closed the car door. I then drove him back to the daycare in Jian.

The next ten thousand words are omitted......

Back in Taichung, basking in the warm sun, I thought of the peaceful time of my childhood with my grandparents in the Chung Hsing University dormitory. My last training event at Providence University was the 1992 HTML campus camp on information networks, and I still remember the computer science teachers teaching simple lessons on editing code to make web pages. As the car reached the gate I saw the English name Providence University. What a simply providence. It was surely heaven's will, asking me to come to the conference with a simple heart and role: to chat with teachers and senior colleagues, to present a paper on institutional research, to accompany a lovely Hsing Wu advisee, now a graduate student in hospitality at Fu Jen, as she presented her master's thesis, and to serve as session chair commenting on six very interesting studies. Thank you to the organising team of the Department of Tourism at Providence University, and thank you to the society's team.

A wonderful day.

I thought of my teachers' guidance, of the details of organising events, of the air in Hualien, and of myself as a student.
