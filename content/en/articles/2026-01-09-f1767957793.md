Before the Tour Guide and Tour Leader Exams, EP35

#TourLeaderExamCountdown64Days

48 Taiwan's local culture and industries are rich and diverse, reflecting varied historical backgrounds and natural environments. Comparing the following local cultural features, which description is correct?

① Lukang: known for indigo dyeing crafts ② Meinong: famous for wood carving art

③ Sanxia: famous for making Hakka floral cloth ④ Daxi: known for spinning tops

This kind of question is easy to solve, but you need to understand the different parts of Taiwan to get it right. These days I see many colleagues at private schools rushing around on admissions trips, with endless reports and administrative work at the end of the semester too. Having just finished discussing the internship contract with STARLUX Airlines, let's end the day with 5 questions.

#1 Regarding Taiwan's local crafts and industry features, which of the following statements is FALSE?

① Yingge, thanks to its clay resources and the development of kilns, formed a community famous for its ceramics industry

② Sanyi, because of the development of its wood carving industry, is regarded as an important centre of wood carving art in Taiwan

③ Lukang, because of its commercial prosperity in the Qing dynasty, preserves many traditional street houses and religious buildings

④ Beipu, because of its concentration of ceramic workshops, is known as northern Taiwan's pottery town

Correct answer: ④

#2 Which of the following descriptions of Taiwan's local ethnic cultures is FALSE?

① Meinong is a Hakka settlement, known for its irrigation canal system and paper umbrella culture

② Neipu is an important Hakka village in Pingtung, preserving Hakka settlements and rituals

③ Because Sanxia was originally home to Pingpu peoples, its local core remains Indigenous culture to this day

④ Beipu, developed by Hakka immigrants, formed a settlement characterised by its old street and ancestral halls

Correct answer: ③

#3 Regarding Taiwan's local development and industrial history, which of the following statements is FALSE?

① Jinguashi formed a mining settlement and related cultural landscape because of gold mining

② Chishang, thanks to rice cultivation, became an agricultural township famous for rice food culture

③ Beigang, because of the flourishing Mazu belief, became an important religious and commercial centre

④ Wuqi, because of the rise of camphor export trade, developed into a mountain forestry settlement

Correct answer: ④

#4 Which of the following statements about Taiwan's local religious culture is FALSE?

① The Dajia Jenn Lann Temple Mazu procession is an important religious event across Taiwan

② Beigang Chaotian Temple is one of the important centres of Mazu belief in Taiwan

③ The Yanshui Beehive Fireworks is closely related to the Wangye belief and local folk customs

④ Checheng Fu'an Temple mainly enshrines Mazu and is a major centre of Mazu belief in southern Taiwan

#5 Regarding the relationship between Taiwan's local natural environment and cultural development, which of the following is FALSE?

① Zhushan, with abundant bamboo forest resources, developed bamboo crafts and the bamboo industry

② Donggang, thanks to its coastal conditions and the influence of the Kuroshio Current, formed a fishing industry and bluefin tuna culture

③ Ruifang, with flat terrain and abundant water, became the main rice-producing area of northern Taiwan

④ Guanshan, thanks to its rift valley plain terrain, developed a settlement pattern based mainly on agriculture

Correct answer: ③
