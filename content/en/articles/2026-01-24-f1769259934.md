#TourLeaderExamCountdown49Days

11 Ancient Egyptian temples retain a strong religious atmosphere and a spirit of realism. One of these temples has a magnificent grandeur, with a total area of 5,000 square metres. The temple is supported by more than 130 giant stone columns, up to 120 metres high, and the shafts bear many kinds of relief carvings. Which of the following is correct?

(A) Philae Temple

(B) Luxor Temple

(C) Karnak Temple

(D) Abu Simbel Temple

The correct answer is (C) Karnak Temple.

Explanation:

The key features described in the question include:

• A magnificent ancient Egyptian temple

• A total area of about 5,000 square metres

• Supported by more than 130 giant stone columns

• Shafts covered with all kinds of religious and royal reliefs

These features clearly point to the "Great Hypostyle Hall" at Karnak Temple. The hall has 134 stone columns and is the largest and most representative hypostyle hall space in ancient Egypt, concentrating the display of religious rituals, the authority of the pharaoh and a realist sculptural style.

Additional note: the "120 metres" column height mentioned in the question should be a typo; the actual tallest is about 21 metres 🤣
