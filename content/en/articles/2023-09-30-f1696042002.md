Have you ever had this experience?

Within just a few months.

A colleague, friend or subordinate has already argued with you face to face and made a scene, leaving you shaking with anger and incoherent, your mind full of how to respond or refute them, so that for a whole day your work or studies are distracted and absent-minded, and then you can't sleep all night, seeking help from a psychiatrist and a Chinese-medicine doctor, begging for peace of mind;

But when you look back at your phone, suddenly there is an even more serious notice in the family group chat, telling you that your mother has been sued for a serious crime and has gone to the police station for questioning. The whole family is at a loss and has no clue, so you can only set aside the unpleasantness in front of you, urgently contact everyone to handle things and comfort your family, organise all the documents, and keep them company as the days pass.

But within a few days, your grandmother, who loved you most, perhaps because of worry amid the family's panic, passes away, free of suffering and gone to the west, leaving you in regret and deep despair about your own life.

Turning back to face your own work, life and family, everything is out of order in this chaos. You no longer have the strength to strive for good performance in work projects or to take initiative in your duties; you feel helpless about the endless obligations and sense of responsibility toward your family of origin; the salary in your account is not enough to cover living costs, and you feel you have let down your wife and children, who quietly stay beside you and put up with an unhappy father.

At this very moment, I even feel that if my own body suddenly warned me that illness was coming, and that I had little time left in this life and was reaching the final stop, it would not seem impossible.

How will you face your life?

How will you measure your life?
