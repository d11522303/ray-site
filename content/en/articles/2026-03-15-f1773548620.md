1. Which of the following is NOT a purpose for which the Development of Tourism Act was enacted?

(A) Balancing urban-rural gaps (B) Promoting international friendship (C) Promoting traditional culture (D) Improving the physical and mental health of citizens

Correct answer: (A)

Explanation: According to Article 1 of the Development of Tourism Act, the purposes include (B), (C) and (D), as well as developing the tourism industry and opening up national revenue sources.

2. Regarding the assessment tests for tour guides, which of the following statements is incorrect?

(A) For the Mandarin tour guide category, an average score of 60 across all subjects is a pass

(B) For the written test of the foreign-language tour guide category, an average score of 60 across all subjects and a score of 50 in the foreign language subject is a pass. For those exempted from some subjects, a score of 60 in the foreign language subject is a pass

(C) For the oral test of the foreign-language tour guide category, a pass requires every oral examiner's score to be 60 or above

(D) A score of zero in any one subject is not a pass

Correct answer: (C)

Explanation: According to the Regulations Governing Tour Guides, for the oral test of the foreign-language tour guide category, a pass requires the average of the oral examiners' scores to be 60 or above.

3. In which Asian cities does the Tourism Administration, MOTC maintain overseas offices?

(A) Tokyo, Osaka, Busan, Singapore, Kuala Lumpur, Bangkok, Hong Kong, Beijing, Shanghai (Fuzhou), Hanoi, etc.

(B) Tokyo, Osaka, Seoul, Singapore, Kuala Lumpur, Bangkok, Hong Kong, Beijing, Shanghai (Fuzhou), Macau, etc.

(C) Tokyo, Osaka, Busan, Singapore, Kuala Lumpur, Bangkok, Hong Kong, Beijing, Shanghai (Fuzhou), Ho Chi Minh City, etc.

(D) Tokyo, Osaka, Seoul, Singapore, Kuala Lumpur, Bangkok, Hong Kong, Beijing, Shanghai (Fuzhou), Fukuoka, etc.

Correct answer: (C)

Explanation: This question tests the Tourism Administration's current overseas locations, which include Ho Chi Minh City in Vietnam and Busan in Korea.

4. According to the Regulations Governing Travel Agencies, which of the following statements about persons who may not be employed as a travel agency manager is incorrect?

(A) Having committed an offence under the Organized Crime Prevention Act, with a final guilty verdict, where the sentence has not yet been served, or less than five years have passed since completion of the sentence, expiry of probation or pardon

(B) Having committed fraud, breach of trust or embezzlement and been sentenced to a fixed-term imprisonment of one year or more by a final judgment, where the sentence has not yet been served, or less than three years have passed since completion of the sentence, expiry of probation or pardon

(C) Having committed fraud, breach of trust or embezzlement and been sentenced to a fixed-term imprisonment of one year or more by a final judgment, where the sentence has not yet been served

(D) Passports of travellers should be held collectively throughout the trip to avoid loss

Correct answer: (D)

Explanation: The disqualification conditions for managers are in Article 14 of the Regulations Governing Travel Agencies. Option (D) has nothing to do with manager qualifications, and tour guides and tour leaders should not "compel" collective safekeeping of documents throughout the trip; travellers' wishes should be respected.

5. According to the Development of Tourism Act, in order to delimit the scope of a scenic area, the competent authority may send personnel onto public or private land to survey or measure it. But which method must be used first to notify the landowner or user?

(A) Notification in person (B) Public announcement (C) Notification by telephone (D) Written notification

Correct answer: (D)

Explanation: According to Article 16 of the Development of Tourism Act, "written" notice must be given first.

6. Which of the following is incorrect regarding the rules that tour escorts dispatched by a travel agency must follow when performing their duties?

(A) Must not change the itinerary without travellers' request (B) Must disclose the itinerary information (C) Must not say or do anything harmful to the nation (D) Must hold travellers' passports collectively throughout the trip to avoid loss

Correct answer: (D)

Explanation: Article 36 of the Regulations Governing Travel Agencies: service personnel must not change the itinerary without authorisation or harm national interests.

7. According to the Development of Tourism Act, when a travel agency fails to take out performance guarantee insurance or liability insurance as required, which of the following actions by the competent authority is incorrect?

(A) A fine of NT$30,000 to NT$500,000

(B) A fine of NT$100,000 to NT$500,000

(C) May immediately suspend its outbound and domestic travel business

(D) Must complete the insurance within three months, and if it does not do so by the deadline, its travel agency licence may be revoked

Correct answer: (B)

Explanation: According to Article 55 of the Act, the fine range is NT$30,000 to NT$500,000.

8. According to the Regulations Governing Travel Agencies, which of the following statements about travel agencies handling group tour business is incorrect?

(A) When Class A or Class B travel agencies act as agents for general travel agencies in handling group tour business, they must be commissioned by the general travel agency, and sign the travel contract with the travellers in the name of the general travel agency, with the selling travel agency countersigning

(B) A travel agency running its own group-organising business may not transfer that business to another travel agency without the traveller's written consent

(C) When a travel agency accepts the transfer of travel business, it must sign a new travel contract with the traveller

(D) Class A and Class B travel agencies running their own group-organising business may place their promotional materials with other travel agencies and commission them to sell and solicit on their behalf

Correct answer: (C)

Explanation: A transfer requires the traveller's written consent, but no new contract is needed. Instead, the receiving travel agency assumes the rights and obligations of the original contract.

9. According to the Regulations Governing Travel Agencies, the travel agency manager training test is scored out of 100. What is the passing score?

(A) 60 (B) 65 (C) 70 (D) 75

Correct answer: (A)

Explanation: The passing score for manager, tour guide and tour leader training tests is 60.

10. According to the Regulations Governing Tour Guides, how is it handled when a candidate is absent from a required subject of the foreign-language tour guide assessment test?

(A) A make-up test may be arranged on one's own depending on the missed subject (B) It is treated as zero (C) The missed subject is not counted in the average (D) It only affects the foreign language subject score

Correct answer: (B)

Explanation: A missed subject is scored as zero.

11. According to the Foreign Exchange Control Act, what is the fine range for deliberately failing to declare or falsely declaring foreign exchange?

(A) NT$30,000 to NT$600,000 (B) NT$30,000 to NT$1,000,000 (C) Up to twice the transaction amount (D) Confiscation of the foreign exchange

Correct answer: (B)

12. According to the Regulations Governing Travel Agencies, when a travel agency conducts travel business through its own or other online platforms, mobile applications (apps) or other electronic means, which of the following essential items must be disclosed on its pages and interfaces?

① Company name, type and address ② Company capital and guarantee deposit ③ Company's trade association membership number ④ Company registration number and name of representative ⑤ Company telephone, fax, email account and contact person

(A) ①②③ (B) ①②④ (C) ①③⑤ (D) ①④⑤

Correct answer: (D)

Explanation: According to Article 21-1 of the Regulations, contact information such as name, type, address, registration number, representative and telephone must be disclosed.

13. At a tax-refund designated shop, a foreign traveller whose cumulative tax-inclusive purchases from the same designated shop on the same day reach what amount in NT$ may, on the day of purchase, present their entry travel document to the shop to apply for a tax refund form?

(A) NT$3,000 or more; within 60 days (B) NT$3,000 or more; within 90 days (C) NT$2,000 or more; within 60 days (D) NT$2,000 or more; within 90 days

Correct answer: (D)

Explanation: The shopping tax-refund threshold is NT$2,000 including tax at a single shop on the same day, and the goods must be taken out of the country within 90 days.

14. When international travellers at most join a domestic group tour run by a Taiwanese travel agency, the agency must assign or hire tour guides to receive or guide tourists. If it carries out business without assigning a lawfully qualified tour guide as required, under the Development of Tourism Act, what fine in NT$ is imposed, and its practice is prohibited?

(A) NT$10,000 to NT$30,000 (B) NT$10,000 to NT$50,000 (C) NT$10,000 to NT$100,000 (D) NT$20,000 to NT$100,000

Correct answer: (B)

Explanation: Article 55 of the Development of Tourism Act: failing to assign a tour guide or tour leader is fined NT$10,000 to NT$50,000.

15. After graduating from senior high school, Mr. Wang wants to receive non-Mandarin-speaking tourists from Japan, Europe and America. Which assessment test should he take, and which practice licence should he obtain?

(A) Obtaining a Mandarin tour guide practice licence is enough, working together with a foreign-language interpreter

(B) He should take the foreign-language tour guide category assessment test and obtain a foreign-language tour guide practice licence

(C) Take the Mandarin tour guide assessment test, because all visitors to Taiwan should have Mandarin communication ability

(D) He must pass both the Mandarin tour guide and foreign-language tour guide categories

Correct answer: (B)

16. According to the Passport Act, in which of the following situations may one apply for a replacement passport?

(A) The remaining validity of the passport is less than two years (B) The passport held is not of the latest current design (C) The holder's appearance has changed and no longer matches the passport photo (D) The chip in the passport cannot be read

Correct answer: (C)

Explanation: Article 15 of the Passport Act: if the appearance has changed and does not match the photo, one should apply for a replacement.

17. Among the definitions of terms in the Development of Tourism Act, which of the following is a scenic area of natural beauty delimited according to the prescribed procedure?

(A) Tourist area (B) National park (C) Natural and cultural ecological landscape area (D) Specific scenic area

Correct answer: (D)

Explanation: This is the legal definition in Article 2 of the Development of Tourism Act.

18. Regarding tourism and recreation areas and their competent authorities, which of the following is incorrect?

(A) University experimental forests: Ministry of Education (B) Natural landscapes: Ministry of Agriculture (C) National parks: Ministry of Transportation and Communications (D) Reservoirs: Ministry of Economic Affairs

Correct answer: (C)

Explanation: The competent authority for national parks is the Ministry of the Interior (National Land Management Agency).

19. According to the Organizational Regulations of the Tourism Administration, MOTC, the "development planning of the tourism and recreation industry" falls under which unit of the Administration?

(A) International Affairs Division (B) Planning Division (C) Operations Division (D) Travel Promotion Division

Correct answer: (B)

20. According to the Development of Tourism Act, what should the tourism competent authority do about local special products and handicrafts?

① Jointly investigate and compile statistics with relevant agencies ② Guide improvements to their production and manufacturing techniques, raise quality, and mark prices ③ Ask travel agencies to help promote and sell them ④ They should be sold in a concentrated way in business districts of tourist areas

(A) ①②③ (B) ①②④ (C) ①③④ (D) ②③④

Correct answer: (B)

21. According to the Regulations Governing Tour Guides, which of the following is correct regarding dismissal from tour guide pre-service training?

① Missing more than one-fifteenth of class sessions ② Using violence or coercion against lecturers, counsellors or other training staff during training ③ Having someone else attend the training under a false name ④ After dismissal, training fees already paid cannot be refunded

(A) ①②③ (B) ①②④ (C) ①③④ (D) ②③④

Correct answer: (D)

Explanation: The absence rule is usually dismissal for missing more than one-tenth (Article 21).

22. According to the Regulations Governing Declaration of Foreign Exchange Receipts and Disbursements or Transactions, what is the limit per transaction for which a non-resident may directly handle NT$ foreign exchange conversion or sale with a bank?

(A) US$100,000 (B) US$150,000 (C) US$200,000 (D) US$250,000

Correct answer: (A)

23. According to the Development of Tourism Act, when a traveller enters a natural and cultural ecological landscape area without applying for a professional guide to accompany them as required, how much may the competent authority fine the person in NT$?

(A) Up to NT$10,000 (B) Up to NT$20,000 (C) Up to NT$30,000 (D) Up to NT$40,000

Correct answer: (C)

Explanation: Article 62: a fine of up to NT$30,000.

24. Which of the following English abbreviations of major international organisations is incorrect?

(A) International Air Transport Association: IATA (B) International Congress and Convention Association: ICCA (C) Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation: APTA (D) United Nations Educational, Scientific and Cultural Organization: UNESCO

Correct answer: (C)

Explanation: Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation should be APEC.

25. According to the Regulations Governing Travel Agencies, which of the following is not within the scope of business of a Class B travel agency?

(A) Soliciting or receiving domestic travel, accommodation, transport and related services for local travellers, or for foreigners, Hong Kong and Macau residents and mainland Chinese holding legal residence documents

(B) Collecting, incidentally when travellers buy travel products or services, insurance premiums for travel-related insurance products promoted in cooperation with insurers through its own online platform or mobile app

(C) Acting as an agent for general travel agencies in soliciting domestic group tour business

(D) Acting as an agent for foreign travel agencies in liaison, promotion, quotation and similar business

Correct answer: (D)

Explanation: Acting as an agent for foreign travel agencies is exclusive business of general travel agencies.

26. According to the Regulations Governing Travel Agencies, when a travel agency serves travellers under a trademark, the agency must apply for trademark registration according to law and report it to the Tourism Administration, MOTC for the record. How many trademarks may be reported for the record at most?

(A) One (B) Two (C) Three (D) Four

Correct answer: (A)

27. According to the Development of Tourism Act, in order to raise the quality of high-level tourism services in Taiwan and use foreign languages to help interpret the island's distinctive ecological and cultural tourism resources, what are the foreign-language-capable personnel that the competent authorities place in natural and cultural ecological landscape areas called?

(A) Tour guides (B) Tour leaders (C) Professional guides (D) Foreign-language tourism guides

Correct answer: (D)

28. According to the Regulations Governing Travel Agencies, the total paid-in capital of a Class B travel agency may not be less than how much in NT$?

(A) NT$30 million (B) NT$6 million (C) NT$1.2 million (D) NT$1 million

Correct answer: (C)

Explanation: General agencies NT$25 million, Class A NT$6 million, Class B NT$1.2 million.

29. According to the Regulations Governing Tour Guides, eligibility for registering for the foreign-language tour guide assessment test can be divided into which two types?

(A) General and specialised (B) Basic and advanced (C) Ordinary system and special system (D) Domestic system and international system

Correct answer: (A)

30. When a foreign traveller in Taiwan goes to a bank to exchange euro banknotes for NT$, which euro exchange rate is used?

(A) Spot buying rate (B) Spot selling rate (C) Cash buying rate (D) Cash selling rate

Correct answer: (C)

Explanation: When a bank buys foreign currency banknotes from a customer, it uses the "cash buying rate".

31. According to the entry and exit customs clearance guidelines, departing travellers carrying foreign currency cash above the limit must declare it to customs. Which of the following is incorrect?

(A) Declaration may only be made for the amount above the limit (B) The amount is capped at US$20,000

(C) If the declaration is false, the excess portion is confiscated

(D) If the declaration is false, the entire declared amount is confiscated

Correct answer: (D)

Explanation: Only the portion that is "over the limit and undeclared" or "falsely declared" is confiscated.

32. According to the Development of Tourism Act, travel products must be sold exclusively by travel agencies, but which of the following travel products can be bought at a convenience store?

(A) Tour itineraries for foreigners visiting Taiwan (B) Tickets of Taiwan High Speed Rail or Taiwan Railway

(C) Group tours abroad for Taiwanese (D) Domestic package tours for Taiwanese

Correct answer: (B)

33. Regarding the rules for a foreign travel agency setting up a branch in Taiwan, which of the following is incorrect?

(A) It must apply to the Tourism Administration, MOTC for approval (B) It must complete recognition and branch registration according to law

(C) The scope of business and paid-in capital follow the rules for branches of Taiwanese travel agencies (D) The guarantee deposit follows the rules for Taiwanese travel agencies

Correct answer: (C)

Explanation: For a foreign travel agency's branch, the capital and guarantee deposit follow the rules for the "head office", not for branches.

34. (Case question) Wang Xiao-ming's home is within the East Coast National Scenic Area. Recently his father tore down the old single-storey house and rebuilt it on the same site as a brightly coloured, gaudy three-storey Western-style house. Xiao-ming said to his father: "Dad, you have to apply to the National Scenic Area Administration first to see if this is allowed!" His father said: "No need, the building permit is applied for from the county government, not the Administration." Which of the following statements about this issue is correct?

(A) Regardless of renovating the building's structure, form or colour, Xiao-ming's father only needs to apply to the county government directly under the Building Act

(B) After the county government approves and everything is completed, to maintain the beauty of the National Scenic Area, he applies to the National Scenic Area Administration to review and determine the building's form, structure and colour before it is issued

(C) To maintain the beauty of the National Scenic Area, the Administration may impose planning restrictions on the form, structure and colour of buildings within the area

(D) To protect people's freedom of residence, the Administration may not impose planning restrictions on the form, structure and colour of buildings within the area

Correct answer: (C)

Explanation: According to Article 19, the Administration has the power to impose planning restrictions on the form, structure and colour of buildings.

35. According to the Development of Tourism Act, which of the following is not issued a special tourism emblem by the competent authority?

(A) Travel agencies (B) Homestay operators (C) Hotel businesses (D) Tourist recreation businesses

Correct answer: (A)

Explanation: Travel agencies have no special tourism emblem.

36. When a tourist recreation business, hotel business, travel agency, tourism and recreation business or homestay operator is found by the competent authority's regular or irregular inspection to be non-compliant, besides handling under relevant laws, what actions does the Development of Tourism Act prescribe?

① Order improvement within a time limit; if not improved by the deadline, a fine of NT$30,000 to NT$300,000

② In serious cases, business may also be suspended in whole or in part for a fixed period

③ If it continues operating, a fine of NT$50,000 to NT$300,000

④ If it continues operating after being ordered to suspend business, its business licence or registration certificate is revoked

(A) ①②③ (B) ①②④ (C) ①③④ (D) ②③④

Explanation: According to Article 54.

37. Which of the following is Taiwan's current tourism CIS brand slogan?

(A) Heart of Asia (B) Waves of Wonder (C) Touch Your Heart (D) Time for Taiwan

Correct answer: (B)

Explanation: The latest brand is Waves of Wonder (Taiwan's charm, endless surprises).

38. According to the Development of Tourism Act, which of the following acts in a scenic area or tourist area is subject to a fine of NT$10,000 to NT$50,000 by the competent authority?

① Operating fixed or mobile stalls without permission ② Discarding or burning garbage or waste at will ③ Driving vehicles into areas where vehicles are prohibited or parking in no-parking areas ④ Setting up signs or advertisements without permission ⑤ Forcibly photographing tourists and charging a fee

(A) ①④⑤ (B) ①②③ (C) ③④⑤ (D) ①③④

Correct answer: (D)

Explanation: According to Article 64. ② falls under environmental protection or has a different amount, and ⑤ forced photography carries a higher fine and involves the Criminal Code.

39. According to the Development of Tourism Act, tourist recreation businesses, tourist hotel businesses and hotel businesses that cooperate with tourism policy to raise service quality, as approved by the central competent authority, may enjoy land value tax and house tax reductions on the real estate they use directly. Which of the following is correct about this tax incentive?

(A) The reduction period may not exceed one year (B) The reduction period is five years, and may be extended once before expiry, with the extension limited to five years (C) The reduction period may not exceed three years (D) The reduction period is ten years, and may be extended once before expiry, with the extension limited to ten years

Correct answer: (B)

Explanation: According to Article 49, the 5+5 rule.

40. According to the Development of Tourism Act, how much is the fine, in NT$, for operating a homestay without obtaining a registration certificate under the Act, and the operator is also ordered to cease business?

(A) NT$30,000 to NT$150,000 (B) NT$50,000 to NT$300,000 (C) NT$150,000 to NT$500,000 (D) NT$100,000 to NT$1,000,000

Correct answer: (A)

Explanation: According to Article 55, paragraph 6, homestays are fined NT$60,000 to NT$300,000 (the regulations were recently amended, and option A for this question is closer to the old system or a specific distinction).

42. Mr. Zhang is a civil servant of the 11th grade at the Ministry of Economic Affairs and plans to use a long holiday to travel to mainland China. Which of the following departments must he apply to for permission before going?

(A) National Security Bureau (B) Mainland Affairs Council (C) Ministry of the Interior (D) Ministry of Justice

43. To strengthen the protection of the travel and personal safety of Taiwanese travelling to mainland China, Hong Kong and Macau, which is the competent authority that issues travel warnings for mainland China, Hong Kong and Macau on behalf of our government?

① They must be organised by travel agencies, entering and leaving as a group, with each group of four to fifty people

② A travel agency receiving mainland Chinese for individual travel to Taiwan may not accept individual-travel tourist groups formed by travel agencies, other organisations or individuals from Hong Kong, Macau or the mainland

③ An "individual-travel tourist group" means a whole group made up of tourists coming to Taiwan for individual travel, or made up of tourists coming for individual travel together with mainland Chinese coming for purposes other than tourism

④ If an individual-travel tourist group is accompanied by a mainland Chinese or Hong Kong or Macau resident performing tour leader duties, it is presumed to have been formed by a travel agency, other organisation or individual from Hong Kong, Macau or the mainland
