28. Drinking water in the United Arab Emirates relies mainly on seawater desalination. This is because of which of the following geographic constraints? (A) Strong sunshine all year (B) Too little total rainfall (C) Serious river pollution (D) High salinity of groundwater

The answer is (B)

The United Arab Emirates, the UAE (The Emirates), is a federal monarchy in southwestern Asia, in the southeast of the Arabian Peninsula, with a total area of about 83,600 square kilometres. It is made up of seven emirates: Abu Dhabi, Sharjah, Dubai, Ajman, Fujairah, Umm Al Quwain and Ras Al Khaimah. In aviation, Etihad Airways has Abu Dhabi as its main hub, while Emirates has Dubai as its main hub, and both fly directly to Taiwan.
