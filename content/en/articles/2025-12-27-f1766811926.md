Tour Guide and Tour Leader Exam Prep EP22

#TourLeaderExamCountdown77Days

10. Which of the following is said to be the "twin brother" of Tokyo Station in Japan, in the "Tatsuno style" of architecture?

① Taiwan Governor-General's Office ② Taiwan Governor-General's Residence ③ Taiwan Governor-General's Museum ④ Hsinchu Railway Station

Solution:

The Taiwan Governor-General's Office was one of the most important centres of power built in the Japanese colonial period, and it is often described as being in the Tatsuno architectural style: red-brick exterior walls, symmetrical or asymmetrical but harmonious design, and European elements such as arched windows. Tokyo Station, designed by Tatsuno Kingo, is a classic "Tatsuno-style" building, characterised by red brick with grey-white bands, a clock tower and domes.

But today's Presidential Office Building was actually given its final form and detailed design by Moriyama Matsunosuke, in a style deeply influenced by Tatsuno Kingo, the Japanese architectural authority of the time, and it has a very strong sense of authority.

Moriyama Matsunosuke has many representative works in Taiwan, listed below:

# Tainan Prefectural Hall (today's National Museum of Taiwan Literature): similar in style to the Taichung Prefectural Hall, with a graceful mansard roof.

# Taichung Prefectural Hall (today's old offices of the Taichung City Government): a magnificent Baroque building, the administrative centre of the central region at the time.

# Taipei Prefectural Hall (today's Control Yuan): at the corner of Zhongxiao East Road and Zhongshan South Road, most distinctive for its dome and red-brick decoration.

# Monopoly Bureau (today's Taiwan Tobacco and Liquor Corporation headquarters): red brick alternating with white stone stripes, with a very recognisable tower shape.

# Waterworks Bureau Pump House (today's Museum of Drinking Water): located in Gongguan, Taipei, with an elegant semicircular colonnade in Baroque style.
