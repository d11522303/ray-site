FIT is the travel industry's common abbreviation for independent tickets, but what does it actually stand for?

In class today I wrote Free Individual Ticket

After class I felt it was wrong, and after searching online I got even more dizzy. Here are the various versions:

Free Individual Tourist

Flight Individual Travel

Foreign Independent Tour

Foreign Individual Tourist

Full Independent Tour

Frequent Individual Tourist

Flexible Independent Tour

Free Independent Traveller

Well, anyway, an independent traveller is just FIT. Overthinking it only makes things more troublesome.
