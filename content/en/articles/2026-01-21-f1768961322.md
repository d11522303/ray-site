Before the Tour Guide and Tour Leader Exam, EP47

#TourLeaderExamCountdown52Days

The Frankish Kingdom broke up in 843 AD. This was caused by the signing of which of the following treaties? (A) Treaty of Paris (B) Treaty of Milan (C) Treaty of Cologne (D) Treaty of Verdun

Explanation: When I saw this question I still didn't know the answer, but I just guessed D. And the result?

It was correct, because the question writer asked something I had no idea about, and I guessed the answer I had also never heard of. It is also reasonable to infer that in the ninth century many of today's famous big cities may not yet have taken shape, so you wouldn't pick Paris, Milan, Cologne and so on.

I suggest that when studying history, you turn place names and country names into three-dimensional thinking, compare them with today's administrative maps, and line up the important world events of the same period across Central and West Asia and other major peoples. Guessing may even become a pleasure.

#Verdun is located in present-day northeastern France on the Meuse River. Its location is very important and it has been a strategic fortress of Western Europe since ancient times.

Verdun was chosen as the signing place because in the ninth century it was a relatively central city of significant standing within the Frankish Kingdom's territory. The treaty was not only signed there; Verdun later also played key roles several times in European history:

Verdun is most widely known for the "#BattleOfVerdun" (1916) in the First World War. It is one of the longest and bloodiest battles in human history. The French army successfully blocked the German offensive here, so Verdun symbolises courage and resistance in the hearts of the French. Because of the extremely brutal war it experienced, in 1984 French President Mitterrand and German Chancellor Kohl stood there hand in hand to mourn the fallen soldiers, one of the most iconic images of peace in the 20th century, and the World Peace Center (Centre Mondial de la Paix) was established.

# Question set 39-42: After World War II, the US and the Soviet Union stood in confrontation, and Europe, caught in between, considered how to find its own way. In 1950 a political cartoon titled "Europe under the Magnifying Glass: The Third Force" (Figure 4) drew the European countries as children being examined by two adults with a magnifying glass, with some children pinned under one adult's knee.

In 1952, six countries, #Germany, France, Italy, the Netherlands, Luxembourg and Belgium, established the #EuropeanCoalAndSteelCommunity. The idea came from a French plan for Western European countries to jointly manage important industrial resources, stipulating that member states could obtain coal and steel raw materials directly without paying tariffs. In 1958 the six countries established the "European Economic Community" and the "European Parliament"; in 1967 the six integrated by treaty into the "European Communities"; in 1993 the "European Communities" was formally renamed the "European Union" (EU). From 2004, many Eastern European countries joined the EU one after another; the UK, because of many domestic problems, decided by referendum in 2016 to leave, and negotiated with the EU on immigration, international trade, cross-border finance and more. Question:

39. Which of the following best describes the international situation reflected in Figure 4?

(A) The period of four-power occupation of Germany; the two adults represent the Allies and the Axis, and the one pinned down is Central Europe

(B) The Cold War period; the two adults represent the democratic bloc and the communist bloc, and the one pinned down is Eastern Europe

(C) The period of colonial liberation; the two adults represent US and Soviet imperialism, and the one pinned down is Southwestern Europe

(D) The period of Western European reconstruction; the two adults represent a free economy and a planned economy, and the one pinned down is Northern Europe

40. According to the passage, the idea implied by the stipulations of the plan France proposed in the 1950s is best explained by which of the following concepts? (A) core-periphery (B) geopolitical pivot (C) regional interdependence (D) central place system

41. According to the passage, which of the following impacts is the 2004 change in the EU organisation most likely to bring to member states?

(A) Begin Western European countries' use of the euro currency

(B) Expand the sources of industrial funding for Eastern European countries

(C) Open visa-free exit for Western European countries

(D) Stabilise Eastern European oil and energy exports

42. According to the information in the passage, which best interprets the main purpose of the UK's negotiations with the EU? (A) Address governance issues in which the UK is intertwined with globalisation

(B) Confirm that the UK will establish bilateral free trade with European countries

(C) Protect the UK's local culture from the impact of globalisation

(D) Seek to let the UK continue to apply the EU's immigration policy
