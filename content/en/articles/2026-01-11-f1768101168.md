(AI) Three Tips for Learning a New Language

1. Imagine yourself in a specific real-life setting and associate vocabulary with visual or contextual cues in that environment.

2. Watching videos or listening to podcasts is often more effective than relying solely on paper-based examinations.

3. Practising for one hour a day is far more effective than studying intensively on just one day a week.
