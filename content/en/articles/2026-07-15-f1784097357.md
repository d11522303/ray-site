2026.7.15

Facebook's Memories feature brought up my review of my health from last year. A year has passed and my body is still not good. Slowly I've worked out the main cause: poor sleep affects my weak points, and almost daily headaches bring daytime drowsiness and low spirits, along with catching colds and feeling unwell more easily. I have quite a few expectations of my own abilities, but I feel deeply frustrated because my body can't keep up.

This year my child is in the United States, and in two weeks we will travel to the US and Canada. Because my body still hasn't returned to normal, I feel little enthusiasm for overseas travel.

Maybe Taipei is just too hot. Summer is always a test for me, in emotions, body and willpower. I can't even summon the energy to write, which is my favourite thing. No wonder you rarely see famous writers who come from tropical places; much thinking and writing simply can't be sustained.

I'm recording here how it feels in the most exhausting month of the year, and hope even more that things will improve day by day, so that this time next year I'll be refreshed and full of energy.
