I have only a little more than ten years left in my teaching career. How to make a splendid transformation, it seems, is not going to come from this job alone. How can I create a new peak in life?

Lately a frozen shoulder, psychosomatic symptoms and insomnia have been troubling me endlessly, but my mood is good. In teaching and work, I can always find interesting points to keep going.
