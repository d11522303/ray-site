2025.2.7

I slept badly last night. Yen-Ling said the child also slept badly camping in the mountains, so the whole family slept badly.

I felt groggy until about 2 pm and only felt a bit better after I had lunch early and went to the toilet to settle my body and mind.

Lately it has been hard to write properly, so I record my life on FB first. Putting the thoughts in my head into words pauses the endless rumination and worry. It seems to really work, and slowly my typing hands are warming up.

Last night Yen-Ling said she would give me a birthday gift. I'm grateful, and I thank her for sponsoring the family trip to Japan, so I will pay for this one myself. After fifty, I bought myself a new scooter again, a Yamaha JOG 125, paid by transfer from my own account. The previous one was a Suzuki 125 that my mother bought me when I left the military. After using it just a few years, while working at the Taipei Youth Recreation Center, I passed it cheaply to a colleague. Thinking about it, almost 30 years have passed.

The plate number assigned by the Banqiao Motor Vehicles Office, PAZ8136, suits me well. I hope that when my child and I ride this scooter, every journey is safe and fulfilling.
