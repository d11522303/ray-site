[Highly Recommended] Dad Play Time: Papa Hou's Parent-Child Inspiration Library

My teacher is truly remarkable. He is accomplished in both research and teaching, a global leader in his research field, and also a fun and playful "Papa Hou." He has launched a parent-child education website, and both its purpose and its execution are spot on. Parents with children at home can take a look at his new parent-child website, and I'd also like to borrow his prompts to get families with children thinking together.

Why play with children?

How do you play with children?

How can playing build memories?

How can playing bring learning?

As I browsed my teacher's website, what I saw reflected was the practice field of the "Mom of Our Dog-Boy's Parenting and Learning" at home. Every one of Professor Hou's parent-child articles reminded me of a stage in our little Dog-Boy's upbringing, education and cultivation, and the support behind it. All of that was thanks to Dog-Boy's mum, who threw herself wholeheartedly into parent-teacher interaction, learning guidance, gathering resources in and out of school, and planning time. Our home is full of marks of all kinds of learning and traces of growth. Although our child is now studying and growing ten thousand miles away, the accumulated memories and affection remain in our family space and in each other's hearts.

My teacher's sharing moved me, and it also made me reflect and look ahead. I hope everyone will click in and see how positive learning education can be put into practice, and you are very welcome to spread and share it.
