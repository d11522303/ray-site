30 In which of the following countries can one visit the sites of all three ancient cultures: the Maya, Aztec and Inca? (A) Mexico (B) Indonesia (C) Turkey (D) Iran (113 Foreign Tour Leader Tourism Exam)

Explanation: This question is a giveaway. With no options like Peru, Guatemala or Belize, it is almost a freebie, but I think the question is wrong and I must point it out, because the Inca civilisation has nothing at all to do with Mexico.

My favourite is the Maya civilisation (from about 2000 BCE to the 16th century CE), and I look forward to the day I can go and see the pyramids and the Maya script. Within an agricultural society, the Maya developed an extremely complex hieroglyphic script and a base-20 numeral system, and accurately calculated the Earth's orbital period. Their architecture is characterised by stepped pyramids hidden in the jungle, and by loose cities made up of many autonomous city-states, rather than a monarchical centralised system.

Apart from the Maya, the demise of the three ancient civilisations above was all related to the Spanish discovery of the New World. So history has different meanings for different peoples, and the competition and development among humans seem in the long run to be the essence, with conflict hard to escape and only responded to, by strengthening one's own abilities.

Below are simple questions on Maya culture. You can also take a look at the cute Maya script. The structure of its glyphs is similar to Chinese characters, but Maya glyphs can be composed of syllabic letters, which is closer to Korean.
