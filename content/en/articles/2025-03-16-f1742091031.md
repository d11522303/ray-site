2025.3.16

I finished reading the text of 《失樂園》 (Lost Paradise) before bed, and so that is how it ends. I have seen the film version. The film is far more restrained, mainly discussing the psychological effects and the impact on those around them, rather than being written about two people chasing pleasure. Dr. Watanabe from Hokkaido University writes in a way that cuts to the bone, taking a man and a woman in middle age, indulging in desire and ignoring life and relationships, to the very extreme. I learned that this novel began as a long serial published in the Nihon Keizai Shimbun on 1 September 1995. I really wonder what the Japanese who read the newspaper every day felt when reading it in the office or any public place!

I have also noticed that when I write about unhappy things in my life, the people I attract are others who also complain. But when I express happiness, a group of positive people often rejoice with me. Isn't that good? Why am I so tragic, always raising the things I'm dissatisfied with, forgetting that a person's life is limited? If I don't go looking now, the future will not be attainable, because what you lose is the most precious time.

I have been writing a doctoral research proposal for how many years now, without once actually sitting the exam. These past few days I looked over what I wrote before, and I truly shook my head at myself. If I were a professor, I would think twice about the author of such a proposal, because the content has far too many personal statements, gilding the lily and defeating its purpose.
