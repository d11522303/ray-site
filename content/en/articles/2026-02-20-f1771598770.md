Tour Guide and Tour Leader Exam Prep, EP79

#TourLeaderExamCountdown20Days

40 The tour leader informs us that the country we are about to visit is a high-risk area for yellow fever, and recommends getting the yellow fever vaccine before going. The country we are going to may be: (A) Laos (B) Kiribati (C) Brazil (D) Croatia

The answer is C.

Yellow fever is a mosquito-borne disease, and most affected areas are in Central and South America and Africa. So Panama, which Taiwanese visit on cruises, Peru of the Inca Empire, and Ecuador, the equatorial country mentioned in EP73, are all yellow fever areas. If you travel there, you will be required to be vaccinated beforehand and show proof before entry, and some transit countries also have restrictions.

In addition, I still want to suggest that travel questions about Central and South American countries without diplomatic ties to Taiwan be set less often. Apart from the high cost and small number of travellers, in recent years Taiwanese passports have gone from visa-free to requiring visas for entering and leaving Latin American countries, and the fees are not low. You get tired just sitting on the plane, and the sights and transport all require extra arrangements. Apart from a few specialised travel agencies that run tours, the common sense and knowledge a tour leader really needs cannot be measured by an exam, and operating such tours depends on experience and connections.

Let's ask questions about countries that have diplomatic ties with Taiwan.

#1 A tour group plans to go to Guatemala, an important Central American ally of Taiwan, for an in-depth cultural tour. While introducing it, the tour leader mentions a site in the country listed by UNESCO as a "dual natural and cultural heritage" site, with spectacular stepped pyramids, and once one of the most powerful Maya city-states. What is this site?

(A) Chichén Itzá

(B) Tikal National Park

(C) Copán Ruinas

(D) Antigua

Answer: (B)

#2 A tour leader takes a group of diving-loving travellers to Belize. The itinerary includes a round, deep-blue underwater cave which, seen from the air, looks like the "eye of the ocean". It is not only a world-famous diving mecca but also the largest underwater cave in the world. What is the name of this attraction?

(A) The Great Blue Hole

(B) Blue Mountains

(C) Brimstone Hill

(D) Pigeon Island

Answer: (A)

#3 When arranging itineraries to Taiwan's allies in Central America, the tour leader mentions a country that is geographically adjacent to Mexico and Guatemala, yet is the only country in Central America with English as its official language, which makes it unique in Maya cultural tourism. Which country is it?

(A) Saint Lucia

(B) Saint Kitts and Nevis

(C) Belize

(D) Guatemala

Answer: (C)
