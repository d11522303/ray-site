2015 Global Chinese Tourism and Hospitality Industry-Academia Summit Forum Workshop

Date: 16-17 November 2015

Venue: Hsing Wu University (HWU) (No. 101, Sec. 1, Fenliao Rd., Linkou District, New Taipei City)

Keynote speakers on Day 2 (17 November):

Professor Cheng-Hung Yen, Chair of the Department of Hospitality Management, San Jose State University, USA

Professor Gang Li, University of Surrey, UK

Intended participants: University and college faculty, senior high and vocational high school teachers, and master's and doctoral students in tourism and hospitality programmes

[Workshop Fees]

One-day workshop: NT$2,000;

Two-day workshop: NT$3,500. The fee includes:

1. The summit forum and professional paper presentation sessions

2. Lunch for one or both days

3. Workshop certificate (proof of 8 or 16 training hours)
