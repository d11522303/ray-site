[Tour guide or tour leader? Which one is easier to pass?]

The conclusion first:

"Begin with the end in mind" and start preparing from now, and this exam is not hard. The NT$2,000 registration fee is still affordable, so I suggest registering for both exams. The one thing to consider is the choice between foreign language and Mandarin. If your foreign language skills are good, register directly for the foreign-language tour guide or tour leader exam. The foreign-language exemption standards will be announced before long! Don't wait until next year to sit it just once.

If you are still hesitating, keep reading.

#WhatAreTheBenefitsOfTheTourLeaderLicence

Every year-end many students in our department ask about the annual national tour guide and tour leader exam (renamed an "assessment" starting next year), especially now that vocational colleges are led by licences in teaching. This is the time when students in travel, tourism, and leisure departments make their decision. With the post-pandemic travel boom, preparing for this assessment is a major annual event for tourism and leisure students at vocational colleges. Some well-known national universities even make passing it a graduation requirement, and the sight of students posting their red-banner passes as an honour for themselves and their schools is everywhere.

#LetsNotTalkAboutPassionOnlyPay

In the early days, whenever students asked me this, my answer was always: "It depends on your interests! If you don't plan to enter the travel industry, and if you're shy and don't dare to lead groups, you don't need to take it." For myself, because I love overseas travel, and my image of domestic travel was just traditional spots such as Kenting, Hualien and Taitung, I never thought of the tour guide job and only prepared for the English tour leader exam.

I attach the 2023 salary data for tour leaders and tour guides from 104 Job Bank for students and friends who want to enter this industry. The numbers suggest that the average monthly income of tour guides can reach nearly NT$50,000, about NT$10,000 more than tour leaders. Perhaps I have been away from the industry too long, or perhaps it is the survey method, but it does not seem close to my understanding. I would welcome corrections from people in the field.

#HowLongToPrepare

Apart from English and Japanese language exams, which need long accumulation and cannot be rushed, many domestic qualification exams can show results in a short time. As long as you master the answering techniques, a single prep book you can read thoroughly is also important. Taking registration in December and the exam the following March, preparation time is about 3 months. If you read 10 pages a day, after subtracting the fun time of Lunar New Year, winter break, New Year's Eve and Christmas, you have about 70 days left, so your study book should be within 700 pages.

#IllTakeItNextYearIfImNotReadyThisYear

This is a somewhat paradoxical question. The scope of the exam is very large and it is based on practical content, so the moment you meet the eligibility requirement (a high school or vocational school graduate) is the moment you can start preparing.

From the pass rates of past years we can infer that in recent years the pass rate for the Mandarin tour leader and tour guide exams has gradually converged toward 20% to 30%. Still, it is sometimes high and sometimes low and hard to predict. Moreover, 2024 is the first year of the reform, and at least this time the question style is not much changed from before, which makes it easier to grasp. If you delay another year, you may meet a year with harder questions and a lower pass rate, and wouldn't that waste a chance to cram properly...

Moreover, students hope to fill in their familiarity with the exam through teachers' classes, but from my experience teaching at a university of science and technology, the pass rate does not rise as students' grade level rises. We often joke that the pass rate is probably negatively correlated with grade level, that is, the younger you are, the higher the pass rate.

Why? That is another topic 😅

#IOnlyWantToTakeTheEasyOne

The Mandarin tour guide exam has been sometimes hard and sometimes easy over the years, while the Mandarin tour leader pass rate has been more stable. I remember ten years ago, when cross-strait tourism opened up, the question style and difficulty increased greatly to limit the number of passers, and that year's pass rate was extremely low, so there was no question of whether tour leader or tour guide was easier. In recent years many universities have set it as a graduation requirement, so when many under-prepared students who just paid the fee all sign up for the exam, the overall pass rate clearly falls.

Finally, I'd still like to remind everyone: the tour guide and tour leader exam on March 16 and 17, 2024, should open registration in early December this year. This is a lot of text and tiring to read, right? How does it feel to read a 700-page textbook? Think carefully, students: do you want to spend the NT$2,000 registration fee and textbook costs? Or is a buffet at a five-star hotel more satisfying?

Good luck to everyone! May you top the list!
