Tour Guide and Tour Leader Exam Prep EP91

#Tour Leader Exam Countdown: 8 Days

40 At tourist hotels approved to set up a foreign currency exchange point, what is the maximum amount of foreign banknotes a foreign visitor can exchange, in US dollar equivalent? (A) US$1,000 (B) US$2,000 (C) US$3,000 (D) US$4,000

The answer is (C) US$3,000. According to Article 3 of the Central Bank's amended Regulations Governing the Establishment and Management of Foreign Currency Exchange Counters, when foreign currency exchange counters (such as tourist hotels and department stores) handle foreign currency exchange, the amount per person per transaction is limited to the equivalent of US$3,000.

#1 When a foreign currency exchange counter exchanges RMB banknotes, what is the limit per person per transaction?

(A) RMB 10,000

(B) RMB 20,000

(C) RMB 50,000

(D) RMB equivalent to US$3,000

Answer: (B)

#2 Under current regulations, which of the following categories of person is not eligible for foreign currency exchange at a foreign currency exchange counter?

(A) Inbound travellers holding a foreign passport

(B) Mainland China travellers holding an entry-exit permit

(C) Hong Kong and Macau travellers holding an entry-exit permit

(D) Citizens holding a Republic of China identity card

Answer: (D)
