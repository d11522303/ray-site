Venezuela: world records

😱1. Angel Falls is the tallest waterfall in the world, with a drop of 807 metres. The animated film Up was inspired by it.

😱2. Lake Maracaibo (Spanish: Lago de Maracaibo) is a lagoon in northwestern Venezuela and the largest lake in South America, rich in oil and gas resources. It is 210 kilometres long from north to south, with a shoreline of about 1,000 kilometres. The lake area is the place on Earth with the most frequent lightning. The famous Catatumbo lightning appears about 140 to 160 days a year, and at its peak in September the lake can see up to 280 lightning strikes per hour, lasting up to nine hours, bright enough to light the way for night navigation.

😱3. Venezuela is a country in the northern hemisphere of South America. Near the equator, it has a tropical climate throughout. Its capital is Caracas. According to reports, Venezuela and its capital Caracas are among the countries and cities with the highest per capita murder rates in the world.
