34 The portrait on Serbia's 500-dinar banknote, Jovan Cvijić, was a geographer known as the "Father of Karst Topography". His earliest study area was the Dinaric Alps. From this we can infer that which kind of rock is widely exposed in this region? (A) mudstone (B) limestone (C) granite (D) basalt

The answer is (B). This question is very easy, but because the interior of the Balkan Peninsula is not a main tourist destination for Taiwanese, many people may not even know where Serbia is.

But speaking of world-famous athletes, tennis star Novak Đoković and the Denver Nuggets' NBA centre Nikola Jokić both come from Serbia.

Master Travel, an internship partner travel agency of HWU's Department of Travel Management, is Taiwan's expert in tours to Eastern Europe and the Balkan Peninsula. In terms of local scenery and character, the region is also one of the holy lands for those who study geography and travel. If you become a tour leader, you must get the following questions right.

#1 In geography, the technical term "karst (Karst/Kras)" describing limestone dissolution landforms originates from the border plateau of which present-day country?

(A) the Dalmatian coast of Croatia

(B) the southwestern plateau of Slovenia

(C) the Montenegrin mountains of Montenegro

(D) the Neretva River valley of Bosnia and Herzegovina

Answer (B)

#2 Croatia's famous Plitvice Lakes National Park (the Sixteen Lakes) is known for its terraced lakes. The physical and chemical mechanism behind its special geological landscape depends mainly on which of the following processes?

(A) water pooling in U-shaped valleys left by glacial erosion

(B) seawater intrusion and salt crystallisation caused by crustal movement

(C) deposition and barriers of "travertine" formed with the help of moss and algae

(D) cooling and contraction of basalt after volcanic eruptions

Answer (C)
