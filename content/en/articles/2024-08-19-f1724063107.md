2024.8.19

The College of Tourism and Hospitality held its Ghost Festival offering today. When the time came, everyone lined up to pay respects. This year's offerings were chosen with some expert advice, and on this important annual folk festival, the teachers lit incense and prayed.

The head of the Department of Hospitality, who led the ceremony, prayed: "May all the students and colleagues of the College of Tourism and Hospitality come and go safely." I didn't hear anything like "may enrollment be full"! Instead, the prayer was all about blessings for the thousands of students in the whole college.

During summer vacation, vocational students are mostly working hard in the service industry, part-time or on internships, leaving early and coming home late to strive for their studies and lives. When everyone else is out having fun in summer, it is the busiest time for our tourism and hospitality students.

That one sentence, "May the students come and go safely", says it all about the days and nights, and the constant worry, of us as vocational educators. I was very moved, deeply touched, and jotted this down quickly. Otherwise it would all be forgotten once work gets busy.

By the way, does anyone know why the basin at the very front holds water spinach?
