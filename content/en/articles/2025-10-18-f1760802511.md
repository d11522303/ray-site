[Selflessness, Simplicity, No Excess]

Dr Fang offered a few reminders. When you enter the second-life stage of middle age, don't copy the highlights of your first life, and don't try to make up for its regrets. So what should you do?

1 Selflessness: help things succeed rather than seeking credit; be someone else's cheerleader and supporter; experience all of life's feelings; accept people and things as they are without judging; become a tool that gathers information for the universe's AI.

2 Wise simplicity: try to turn the information you take in into writing or video, and organise it into layered, orderly, systematic knowledge.

3 Everyone worries or feels anxious when facing something new. Don't over-prepare or over-imagine; it drains the brain's energy, and you may even be swallowed by anxiety.

On how to improve point 3, Dr Fang said medicine can offer solutions. But I think middle-aged people, caught between the generations above and below, face many events and situations, and how to meet them positively with a strong will and mindset is everyone's challenge. I find this part very hard, and every family has its own troubles. As human beings in this world, there seem to be only two roads: face it or run from it. All the other worrying and rehearsing is bad for our middle-aged bodies and minds. Taking good care of yourself matters most.
