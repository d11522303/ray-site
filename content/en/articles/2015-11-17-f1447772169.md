【2016 Tourism Elite Cup National Itinerary Design Competition】

So that you can prepare early~ here are the dates and venues of the competition:

Competition dates and venues:

Southern round: Monday, 16 May 2016, Tajen University

Central round: Tuesday, 17 May 2016, Chienkuo Technology University

Northern round: Wednesday, 18 May 2016, Chihlee University of Technology

Grand final: Tuesday, 31 May 2016, Chung Hua University

Themes:

Domestic travel category (3 days, 2 nights): Travelling for Local Produce

(Use ingredients from around Taiwan as the central thread of the itinerary; the region and ingredient are up to you. For example, with Yujing mango from Tainan, link up the attractions around Yujing, Tainan, and include meals or experience activities built around the local produce.)

Inbound visitor category (5 days, 4 nights): Family Theme Park Holiday

(The itinerary must include at least one theme park, with at least one full day spent inside the park. The park chosen must be legally registered; please refer to the Taiwan theme park website http://themepark.net.tw/)
