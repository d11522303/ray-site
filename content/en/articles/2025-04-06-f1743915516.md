2025.4.6

These past few days I have been sorting out my doctoral research direction, and the only principle in my mind is this: how to move the doctoral dissertation research forward within my existing profession and work, achieving a win-win life strategy for myself.

My current income comes mainly from full-time teaching and after-class certificate classes. I also hope to increase my income in the field by winning project funding. In addition, internship mentoring is voluntary work, and by combining it with projects I have now made internship mentoring part of the projects' results.

＃APhDIsAGoalNotADream

So when I ask myself whether it matters which school I get a doctorate from, and why not a good top university, it is because Brother Liu, speaking as someone who has been through it, advised that the best doctoral programme is one where you don't have to take courses and mainly write research, so you don't waste extra time attending class and doing homework. I strongly agreed when I heard that, and that is why I later prepared with the Fu Jen psychology doctoral programme as my only direction.

＃KeepUpAcademicEnglishWriting

How can I persevere in academic writing in English? I still struggle with my limitations in this area. I truly wish I could express my thoughts and points of view fluently in English, and be able to discuss with colleagues at academic conferences. How can I maintain my passion? I wonder.

Should the vehicle for writing a dissertation be Chinese or English? I am still agonising over this. I very much look forward to writing entirely in English in the future, using Chinese writing for relaxed everyday life, and doing all academic writing and publishing in English. I still want to stick with this.

＃IncreasingIncomeIsTheOnlyPurpose

How can I make money while doing a PhD? The way I thought of is self-media: an old guy taking the doctoral exam, an old guy doing a PhD. This is my own interest, so the most important thing is to start shooting and editing videos. The first video is "Going Back to Fu Jen to Take the PhD Exam".
