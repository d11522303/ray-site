#FullYearWorkplaceInternship is not training, nor a course experience, and certainly not some kind of childcare or nursery, but the most practical arena for vocational students. Starting July 1, these students will prepare their mindset and their professional skills for a professional hands-on process lasting 11 to 13 months. The process includes assessments, a master-apprentice system, business trips and customer service, and may also include the realities of work such as not fitting in, being unhappy, not getting enough sleep, standing all day, and even being dismissed from training or transferred to other units.

#VocationalEducation cannot be shown by the subjects on a timetable; the same course gives different results under different teaching models. Students and parents must think about whether their 20-year-old darling has a chance to turn into a mature adult.
