Have you seen the 2016 Oscar-nominated film Hidden Figures?

The real-world heroine, Katherine Johnson, passed away yesterday, 24 February, at the age of 101.

Hidden Figures is one of the most touching films I have seen in recent years, and it keeps reminding me that, as human beings, we should not hold discrimination or prejudice over nationality, race, skin colour, gender or language.

Even though it is everywhere in Taiwan, where I live!!

Have you seen the "Hidden Figures", the best Oscar film in 2016?

The real-world heroine Catherine G. Johnson passed away yesterday at the age of 101.

Hidden Figures is one of the most touching films I've seen in recent years, and I keep reminding myself that as human beings, we should not have discrimination and prejudice on nations, race, color, gender, and language.

RIP. Katherine G. J.
