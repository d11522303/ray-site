[The Nanking Safety Zone vs the Gaza Strip]

The Israeli–Palestinian conflict goes on. When will the war between Ukraine and Russia end?

What perspective should we in Taiwan take on international conflicts? I'm no expert and can hardly offer an opinion. But whenever I see maps of the Gaza Strip and news of how things are developing, I keep comparing it with the map of the 1937 Nanking Safety Zone, hoping that such problems between peoples will never happen again.

The Nanking Safety Zone was also called the Nanking Refugee Zone. Historical records say that early in the Sino-Japanese War, Nanking became a main target of attack. Hang Li-wu, then chairman of the board of the University of Nanking, worked with European and American residents of the city to plan it, following the example of the Shanghai concessions, and marked out the Nanking Safety Zone to protect civilians from the fighting. It ran east to Zhongshan Road, north to Shanxi Road, south to Hanzhong Road and west to Xikang Road: a long, narrow area of only 4 square kilometres in the northwest corner of the city. The University of Nanking, Ginling College, Gulou Hospital, the American, German, British and Japanese embassies, and many government offices, upmarket apartments and private Western-style houses were all inside the zone. It is said that hundreds of thousands of people crowded into the area to escape the war.

I can't bring myself to write about what happened in Nanking afterwards; I've put it in the comments for reference.

I feel that Taiwan is a safety zone the whole world left behind after the Second World War. Over many years, economic development and social security have raised generation after generation of happy Taiwanese, and how lucky I am to be born among them. But at this moment we should also face what history has shown, recognise that selfish human nature is capable of anything, and hope events don't repeat themselves. It depends on the wisdom of statesmen and diplomats never to repeat the tragedies of history, whether between Israel and Palestine, Ukraine and Russia, the two Koreas, or across the Taiwan Strait.
