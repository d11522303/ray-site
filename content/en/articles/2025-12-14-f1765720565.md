25 There is a saying: "See the mountains at Zhangjiajie, see the waters at Jiuzhaigou." Which of the following landforms does Jiuzhaigou in China have?

① Danxia landform ② Yardang landform ③ Aeolian landform ④ Karst landform

Explanation: Jiuzhaigou, named after nine Tibetan villages, is a karst landform area. Many scenic spots around the world are this kind of limestone terrain. In Taiwan, Kenting National Park is the most typical example, with its many limestone caves (Fairy Cave, Big Cave), limestone hills and sea-erosion gullies, and a well-developed underground water system that forms abundant stalactites, stalagmites and so on.

Zhangjiajie, however, is a landform of quartz sandstone peak forest, a very special geological environment. Elsewhere in the world, places with similar geological features are also generally called Zhangjiajie-type landforms. The two are different!
