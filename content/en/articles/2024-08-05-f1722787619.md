#ParisOlympics

At last we got to hear the National Flag Anthem. So moving.

Here is the English that ChatGPT helped translate, so foreign friends know what the Chinese means.

National Flag Anthem of Taiwan, ROC:

Rugged mountains and majestic rivers,

Abundant harvests and fertile lands,

Descendants of the Flame Emperor,

Dominating East Asia.

Never give up in despair,

Never remain stagnant in old ways,

Illuminate our nation,

Promote harmony for all.

Embarking on ventures is arduous,

Remembering our ancestors,

Maintaining achievements is not easy,

Not merely seeking immediate success.

With one heart and one mind,

Persisting steadfastly,

Under the blue sky, under the bright sun,

The land is filled with crimson.
