There are 50 questions below. If you're tired, no need to read them, just look at the photos. The questions are worked through as a reference for my students.

1. Which of the following definitions of interpretation is incorrect?

(A) An educational activity that reveals meanings and relationships through the use of original objects

(B) Helping visitors feel what the environment and people present, and improving their understanding of and attitude toward the environment

(C) A translation skill designed specifically for tour guides, aimed at improving communication ability

(D) A way for humans to interact with cultural and natural resources

Correct answer: (C)

Explanation: Interpretation is not just a translation skill but a communication process of conveying information, educating and inspiring.

2. Which of the following is not a type of non-personal guiding?

(A) Interpretive signs (B) Self-guided trail systems (C) Ecological models and display cases (D) Interpreting and translation services

Correct answer: (D)

Explanation: Interpreting involves real-time service by a "person", so it counts as personal guiding.

3. In an outdoor guided tour, which sense, when included in the interpretation design as a medium for connecting with the environment, more easily raises safety concerns?

(A) Sight (B) Hearing (C) Touch (D) Taste

Correct answer: (D)

Explanation: Wild plants or resources may be poisonous or unclean, so casually trying the "taste" experience carries the highest risk.

4. During an ecological guided tour, the weather suddenly changes, and visitors become anxious and eager for guidance. What is the most appropriate response for the interpreter?

(A) Immediately stay calm and soothe the group's emotions (B) Explain in detail the scientific knowledge of climate change (C) Continue to complete the tour content according to the original schedule (D) Suggest that visitors disperse and find shelter on their own

Correct answer: (A)

Explanation: In an emergency, safety and calming emotions come first, not academic explanations or scattering.

5. In traditional Taiwanese old streets, the front of a building has a raised, covered space at ground-floor level where pedestrians can walk and cool off, avoiding sun and rain. In Hokkien, this building feature is called:

(A) Miao cheng (temple square) (B) Yi tiao tong (one-lane thoroughfare) (C) Hi-chhiu-kha (opera stage foot) (D) Teng-a-kha (pavilion foot)

Correct answer: (D)

Explanation: "Teng-a-kha" is the arcade walkway (qilou), a common architectural feature in Taiwan.

6. Which of the following is not a main interpretive purpose of smart multimedia interactive guide equipment?

(A) Reducing the burden on interpreters (B) Lowering the organisation's operating costs (C) Strengthening the way interpretation is presented (D) Improving visitors' guided tour experience

Correct answer: (B)

Explanation: Interactive equipment usually has high maintenance and installation costs, and its purpose is mainly to improve quality and assist staff, not simply to save money.

7. According to UNESCO's four evaluation elements for the quality of heritage interpretation, which of the following is not included?

(A) Providing access to deeper meaning (B) Turning phenomena into experiences (C) Promoting stewardship responsibility for all heritage (D) Emphasising the benefits heritage brings to the locality

Correct answer: (D)

Explanation: The core of heritage interpretation is conveying and protecting cultural and natural values, not simply economic benefits.

8. Which of the following statements about the guided interpretation of the Matsu National Scenic Area is incorrect?

(A) It lies to the northwest-west of the Taiwan Strait and consists of four townships and five islands: Nangan, Beigan, Juguang and Dongyin (B) It has the "three treasures" of yellow wine, white wine and medicinal wine (C) Its cultural resources include the life, customs and religion of eastern Fujian (D) Its terrain is mainly basalt, with island reefs and cliffs

Correct answer: (D)

Explanation: Matsu's geology is mainly "granite"; basalt is a feature of Penghu.

9. To help visitors understand easily and hear clearly, what is the most suitable length for each unit of a tour guide's interpretation?

(A) 100 words (B) 200 words (C) 400 words (D) 600 words

Correct answer: (B)

Explanation: Content of around 200 words can be delivered in 1-2 minutes, which best holds visitors' attention.

10. What is the highest guiding principle in the process of outdoor guided interpretation?

(A) Visitor interest (B) Attire and appearance (C) Revenue (D) Visitor safety

Correct answer: (D)

Explanation: For any travel activity, "safety" is always the first consideration.

11. When a tour guide has many visitors, which of the following ways of interpreting is correct?

(A) Standing in the north position (B) Interpreting while moving (C) Standing at a fixed point facing the visitors (D) Forbidding visitors to ask questions

Correct answer: (C)

Explanation: Interpreting from a fixed point ensures the voice carries and makes it easy to control the group, and you should have your back to the scenery and face the visitors.

12. To prevent group members from getting lost while visiting a museum, the tour leader must tell members in advance what to do if they get lost. Which of the following responses is least appropriate?

(A) Stay put and wait for the tour leader to come find you (B) Call the tour leader's mobile phone directly (C) Try to find the group on your own (D) Ask museum staff for help

Correct answer: (C)

Explanation: When lost, "finding the group on your own" easily leads to missing each other; you should wait in place or contact the tour leader.

13. When a travel agency organises a domestic (overseas) group tour and an emergency occurs, within how many hours must it report to the Tourism Administration, MOTC?

(A) 12 hours (B) 24 hours (C) 36 hours (D) 48 hours

Correct answer: (B)

Explanation: According to regulations, emergencies must be reported within 24 hours.

14. Each year in Taiwan there are hornet attacks on mountain-climbing travellers. Which of the following months carries the highest risk?

(A) January to April (B) May to July (C) August to November (D) December to February of the following year

Correct answer: (C)

Explanation: Autumn (August to November) is the peak period for hornets building nests and foraging, when they are most aggressive.

15. Generally speaking, how long does it take to recover from jet lag for each time zone crossed?

(A) About half a day (B) About 1 day (C) About 2 days (D) About 3 days

Correct answer: (B)

Explanation: The general rule for adjusting the body clock is about one day of adaptation per one-hour time zone crossed.

16. When group tour members go out at night, which of the following ways of keeping passports is correct?

(A) The tour guide keeps them all together (B) Each member keeps their own (C) Entrusted to a fellow group member (D) Handed to the hotel front desk staff

Correct answer: (B)

Explanation: At present, the common practice is to recommend that members keep their own personal documents on them, in case of need (or adjusted according to each tour leader's habits, but B is the basic principle).

17. Regarding safety rules when a tour guide takes a group on a tour coach, which of the following is incorrect?

(A) Confirm in advance that the traffic route is clear (B) Pay attention to the coach driver's driving behaviour at all times (C) Remind members at all times not to put their hands out of the windows (D) Seat the elderly and members prone to motion sickness in the back seats

Correct answer: (D)

Explanation: People prone to motion sickness should be seated at the "front" or "middle"; the rear seats have more vibration.

18. Which of the following about hot spring bathing precautions is correct?

(A) A single soak should not exceed 15 minutes, and the total time should not exceed 1 hour (B) Pets can be brought into the bath (C) People with heart disease had best alternate between cold and hot water (D) In a full-body bath, the water level should be above the heart

Correct answer: (A)

Explanation: People with heart disease should not alternate between hot and cold, and the soaking depth should not exceed the heart to reduce pressure.

19. When an emergency occurs on a tour, what should the tour guide do first?

(A) Notify the travel agency head office (B) Rescue and stabilise the injured at the scene (C) Release information to the media (D) Arrange the subsequent itinerary

Correct answer: (B)

Explanation: Human life is at stake, so first aid and rescue are the first priority.

20. According to the "Regulations Governing Travel Agencies", travel agencies must take out liability insurance for group tours. What is the minimum insured amount for accidental death, in NT$?

(A) NT$1 million (B) NT$1.5 million (C) NT$2.5 million (D) NT$5 million

Correct answer: (C)

Explanation: Current regulations set the minimum death coverage for travel agency liability insurance at NT$2.5 million.

21. When a passenger arrives at a Taiwan airport, which of the following items can be carried through without declaration?

(A) Beef jerky (B) Fresh oranges (C) Pork floss egg rolls (D) Chocolate

Correct answer: (D)

Explanation: Meat products and fresh fruit and vegetables are strictly prohibited or must be declared for quarantine. Chocolate is not subject to this.

22. Which of the following is the largest national scenic area in Taiwan by area (including land and sea)?

(A) East Coast National Scenic Area (B) Penghu National Scenic Area (C) Yunchianan Coastal National Scenic Area (D) East Rift Valley National Scenic Area

Correct answer: (B)

Explanation: If the vast sea area is included, Penghu National Scenic Area has the largest jurisdiction.

23. Seniors who spend freely during their trips and share the experience with friends and relatives are acting on which of the following travel motivations?

(A) Promoting health (B) Pleasure and showing off (C) Escape and relaxation (D) Expanding social circles

Correct answer: (B)

Explanation: Spending and sharing experiences (conspicuous consumption) match the psychological motive of "pleasure and showing off".

24. Visitors tend to revisit attractions they like and buy souvenirs to remind themselves they have been there. Which personality type is this?

(A) Dependable type (B) Adventurous type (C) Mid-centric type (D) Self-centred type

Correct answer: (A)

Explanation: Preferring familiar environments and seeking security and keepsakes corresponds to the "dependable/conservative (Psychocentric)" type in Plog's theory.

25. Which of the following is not a travel type preferred by seniors?

(A) Railway tourism (B) Hands-on craft experiences (C) River tracing adventures (D) Forest healing

Correct answer: (C)

Explanation: River tracing is physically demanding and highly dangerous, which does not suit seniors' physical needs.

26. Which of the following is not an external environmental factor of tourism demand?

(A) Natural resources (B) Low-cost airlines (C) Tourism motivation (D) Foreign exchange rates

Correct answer: (C)

Explanation: Tourism motivation is an internal psychological factor of the visitor.

27. Which of the following statements about tourism behaviour is incorrect?

(A) Tourism activities can be seen as a commodity (B) A travel itinerary is a tangible product (C) Buying a tourism product is consumption (D) Tourism behaviour is a kind of consumer behaviour

Correct answer: (B)

Explanation: A travel itinerary is intangible and cannot be stored, so it is a "service product", not a "tangible product".

28. An airline gives out member VIP cards and sends greetings or gifts on special holidays. This corresponds to which of Maslow's needs?

(A) Belonging needs (B) Physiological needs (C) Esteem needs (D) Safety needs

Correct answer: (A)

Explanation: Member VIP cards and holiday greetings aim to build group identity and social relationships, which belong to the "love and belonging" needs.

29. "Choosing to stay at a certain B&B because a friend recommended it" belongs to which of the following influencing factors?

(A) Personal factors (B) Cultural factors (C) Social factors (D) Psychological factors

Correct answer: (C)

Explanation: The influence of reference groups (friends, family) is a social factor.

30. Which of the following is not a source from which consumers gather information?

(A) Personal sources (B) Commercial sources (C) Motivation sources (D) Public sources

Correct answer: (C)

Explanation: "Motivation" is the reason that triggers an information search, not a channel through which information comes.

31. A traveller's gap between expectations of a tourism product and the actual experience is which of the following phenomena?

(A) Social exchange (B) Motivation reinforcement (C) Recency effect (D) Cognitive dissonance

Correct answer: (D)

Explanation: The psychological discomfort that arises when reality does not match expectations is called "cognitive dissonance".

32. A traveller thinks a tour guide must be very professionally capable because he interacts warmly and speaks properly. Which psychological effect is this?

(A) Projection effect (B) Contrast effect (C) Recency effect (D) Halo effect

Correct answer: (D)

Explanation: The "Halo Effect" refers to one good characteristic extending into a positive evaluation of the whole.

33. In tourism sales, explaining the content and value of the product honestly, without exaggeration or concealment, is mainly meant to:

(A) Build a professional image and trust (B) Increase impulse buying (C) Make the trip more entertaining (D) Speed up sales

Correct answer: (A)

34. Among Taiwan's indigenous peoples, which of the following has the custom of tattooing (the face)?

(A) Saisiyat (B) Bunun (C) Atayal (D) Amis

Correct answer: (C)

Explanation: The Atayal, Truku and Seediq are known for their facial tattooing tradition.

35. A traveller holds a one-year full-fare economy class ticket. Which of the following is the standard rule for changing it?

(A) A high change fee must be paid (B) The fare difference between low and peak season classes must be paid (C) The date can usually be changed free of charge within the ticket's validity (D) The change must be completed seven days before the original departure date, otherwise it becomes invalid

Correct answer: (C)

Explanation: Full fare tickets are usually the most flexible, and the date can be changed free of charge within the validity period.

36. A traveller bought a non-refundable ticket but cannot travel because the airline cancelled the flight. How should the airline handle it?

(A) Refund only the taxes, not the fare (B) It may charge the traveller a high refund fee (C) The airline must give a full refund or arrange an alternative flight (D) It can only refund part of the fare

Correct answer: (C)

Explanation: If the cancellation is due to the airline, even a non-refundable ticket must be fully refunded or properly handled.

37. A passenger carrying an oversized camera equipment case must check it in. Which of the following is the correct code?

(A) BSCT (B) BULK (C) CBBG (D) XBAG

Correct answer: (D)

Explanation: XBAG stands for excess/oversized baggage. CBBG is cabin baggage (seat-occupying baggage).

38. Delta Air Lines is a member of which alliance?

(A) Star Alliance (B) SkyTeam (C) Value Alliance (D) oneworld

Correct answer: (B)

Explanation: Delta and China Airlines both belong to SkyTeam.

39. Regarding aviation organisations' Chinese names and English abbreviations, which of the following is incorrect?

(A) International Air Transport Association (IATA) (B) International Civil Aviation Organization (ICAO) (C) Aviation Reporting Company (ARC) (D) US Federal Aviation Administration (FAA)

Correct answer: (C)

Explanation: In the travel industry, ARC usually refers to the Airlines Reporting Corporation. Exam questions often test basic recognition using such abbreviations (option (C) here is a more obscure one, but A, B and D are all correct).

40. If a wound dressing and elevating the limb do not stop the bleeding, which of the following haemostatic measures should be taken?

(A) Hot compress with local pressure (B) Use a tourniquet on the heart side of the wound (C) Apply ice over a large area of the affected part (D) Just apply a hot compress

Correct answer: (B)

Explanation: When pressure fails to stop bleeding and life is at risk, the last resort is a tourniquet on the proximal (heart) side.

41. A patient with slurred speech and paralysis on one side of the body may have which of the following conditions?

(A) Hypoglycaemia (B) Stroke (C) Myocardial infarction (D) Cervical spine injury

Correct answer: (B)

Explanation: A drooping face, a weak arm and unclear speech are the three major signs of a stroke.

42. If someone is found collapsed, unconscious and not breathing, which of the following actions is correct?

(A) First check the ID on them (B) Move the body (C) Call their family (D) After confirming the scene is safe, perform CPR

Correct answer: (D)

Explanation: The first step of first aid is to confirm the environment is safe, then activate EMS and perform CPR.

43. Regarding the check items of the "Cincinnati" Prehospital Stroke Scale, which of the following is incorrect?

(A) Ask the patient to raise the corners of the mouth and smile showing teeth, and observe whether the face is symmetrical (B) Ask the patient to close their eyes and hold both arms out level for 10 seconds, and observe whether they droop (C) Ask the patient to say a simple sentence, and observe whether it is correct and clear (D) Ask the patient to stand on one leg for 10 seconds, and observe whether they fall

Correct answer: (D)

Explanation: The Cincinnati Stroke Scale (FAST) includes Face, Arm and Speech, and does not include standing on one leg.

44. When a single rescuer performs adult CPR, the ratio of chest compressions to rescue breaths is:

(A) 15:2 (B) 20:2 (C) 25:2 (D) 30:2

Correct answer: (D)

Explanation: For adult CPR (by one or two rescuers) the compression-to-breath ratio is 30:2.

45. At a Chinese banquet with a lazy Susan, what should you do if you need to take a dish that is far from you?

(A) Stand up to pick up the food (B) Turn the lazy Susan clockwise (C) Quickly turn the lazy Susan anticlockwise (D) Look at whether the dish is on the left or right, then turn it

Correct answer: (B)

Explanation: The etiquette for the lazy Susan is usually to turn it clockwise, taking care not to compete with others.

46. Which of the following statements about brunch is correct?

(A) A more lavish breakfast than usual (B) Popular in poorer countries to reduce food consumption (C) Mostly on holiday mornings, when everyone wakes up later, so breakfast and lunch are combined (D) Its content is simply milk and bread

Correct answer: (C)

Explanation: Brunch is a combination of breakfast and lunch.

47. Which of the following banquet etiquette practices is incorrect?

(A) The accompanying guests should be of higher status than the guest of honour to show respect (B) Once invitations are sent out, they should not be changed or cancelled lightly (C) The R.S.V.P. note at the lower left corner of an English invitation means to reply whether you will attend (D) If you have promised to attend a banquet, you should arrive on time

Correct answer: (A)

Explanation: The accompanying guests' status should not be higher than the guest of honour's, to avoid upstaging the guest.

48. Which of the following table manners to observe while dining is incorrect?

(A) You should bring food to your mouth rather than your mouth to the food, and chew quietly with your mouth closed, not talking loudly (B) If there is a fish bone, other bone or fruit pit in your mouth, you can spit it out into a loosely closed fist and then place it on the plate (C) If you want to take a condiment that is far away, you can ask the guest next to you to pass it (D) If a lady needs to touch up her make-up while dining, she may do so at her seat with some cover

Correct answer: (D)

Explanation: You should not touch up make-up at the table; go to the restroom to do so.

49. Which of the following about car riding etiquette is correct?

(A) In a car with the steering wheel on the left and a chauffeur driving, the left back seat is the seat of honour, the right back seat is second, and the seat beside the driver is the lowest (B) If the host drives the car personally and rides with elders or superiors, the elders or superiors should be seated in the back (C) If the host drives personally, the guest beside the host gets out first, and the guests in the back can simply stay in their seats (D) For a car with a chauffeur, according to international custom, female guests should not sit in the front seat

Correct answer: (D)

Explanation: (A) The seat of honour should be the right back seat; (B) when the host drives personally, the seat of honour is the front passenger seat, and elders should sit in front; (C) when the host drives personally and the front seat is vacated, guests in the back should move to the front seat as a courtesy.

50. Regarding chopstick etiquette, which of the following is considered impolite?

(A) Placing chopsticks side by side on a chopstick rest (B) Sticking chopsticks upright into rice (C) Resting chopsticks on the edge of the bowl (D) Using serving chopsticks when dining in a group

Correct answer: (B)

Explanation: Sticking chopsticks upright into rice resembles offerings to the dead (called "foot-end rice"), and is a major taboo.
