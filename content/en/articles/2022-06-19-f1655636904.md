During the pandemic, one travel agency said in an interview that it seems to be getting busier and busier, so busy that it has opened a branch. If you are curious what kind of travel agency can keep its business going at a time like this, click the link in the comments and listen. I'd love to know what you think after listening.

Not long ago my family wanted to join a trip on the Ming Ri Hao train. When I checked the website and saw the price of three days and two nights, it was quite different from what I knew of domestic rail tour products. If you really had the means, you would want to experience that top-tier design, with exquisite meals and local travel flavour.

But as dinner time approached, I was still typing and wondering how, with prices soaring, to once again reluctantly weigh the bento shops, set-meal restaurants and little noodle stalls we had already eaten at again and again, or go to the evening market to see whether today's three bunches of vegetables for NT$50 and a plate of cooked dishes for NT$100 could take care of the family's meal. I recalled every stall in the Nanshijiao evening market in Zhonghe, Guting Market, the Sanhe morning market and night market in Sanchong, Muxin Market in Muzha, Zhongxing Market in Xindian, Ji'an evening market, the Zhixue student street, and the Linkou Wenyi, Wener and Fude evening markets. For me, perhaps that is my local-style travel at the end of each week or each working day.
