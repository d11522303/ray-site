Prompt: Several campus suicide incidents have occurred this year, drawing social media discussion and public attention to university students' study pressure and emotional management. Please write an essay:

(1) Explaining your views on the frequent campus suicide incidents in recent years

(2) Offering positive suggestions and methods for young people on dealing with pressure and managing their emotions

【Campus Suicide Prevention and Emotional Health】

Nowadays, campus suicide cases have drawn much attention in society. Many people are worried about the pressure university students face in their daily lives.

In my opinion, these tragedies remind us that emotional problems should never be ignored. Academic stress, family expectations, and worries about the future can make young people feel overwhelmed.

When facing pressure, students should learn healthy ways to manage their emotions. For example, they can talk with friends, family members, or teachers they trust. Exercising regularly, keeping a balanced schedule, and seeking professional counseling are also helpful methods.

Life is precious, and asking for help is a sign of courage, not weakness. By supporting one another and caring about mental health, we can create a safer and warmer campus environment for everyone.
