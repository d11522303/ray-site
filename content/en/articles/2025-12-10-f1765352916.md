Before the Tour Guide and Tour Leader Exams, EP5

#2025TourLeaderExamQuestionFromTourismAdministration

49 Which of the following is the largest World Heritage site in China by area, and is located at the upper reaches of major rivers of Southeast Asian countries?

① Three Parallel Rivers protected area

② Hoh Xil protected area

③ Altai Mountains Kanas protected area

④ Shennongjia protected area

Explanation: Actually, if you have no idea at all how to answer this question, using the keyword 【river】 to guess may also get you the right answer. The "Three Rivers" refer to the Nu River (upper Salween) of the Qinghai-Tibet Plateau, the Lancang River (upper Mekong) and the Jinsha River (upper Yangtze). When I travelled in Yunnan last year, I also specially took the plateau express train toward Shangri-La, where you can see the magnificent scenery of the Jinsha River gorge. I highly recommend going to see it, but because the terrain is over 3,000 metres, pay particular attention to the symptoms of altitude sickness, and in Tibetan areas respect local culture and religion even more.

Below, using five famous World Heritage resources of Yunnan Province, here are two more questions for you to try:

1. Which of the following World Heritage scenic areas in Yunnan, China was once regarded by UNESCO as an example of over-tourism, and needs more attention to the preservation of local cultural resources?

① South China Karst

② Old Town of Lijiang

③ Cultural Landscape of Honghe Hani Rice Terraces

④ Cultural Landscape of Old Tea Forests of Jingmai Mountain in Pu'er

2. The Old Town of Lijiang in Yunnan still preserves a pictographic script that is one of the few still in use today, a unique Naxi culture, and it often appears alongside Chinese characters on all the local signs and road markers. What is this script called?

① Dongba script ② Nüshu ③ Tangut script ④ Shui script
