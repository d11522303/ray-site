[Job Opening] Hsing Wu Office of Information and Library Services: Full-Time Information Systems Technician

1. A bachelor's degree or above from a domestic or overseas institution recognised by the Ministry of Education.

2. Good work attitude, resilience under pressure, good communication and coordination skills, and the ability to learn independently and solve problems.

3. Familiar with SQL.

4. Familiar with programming languages.

5. Work experience in information system maintenance.

6. Experience in information system analysis and planning, and in project management and execution, is a plus.

Applicants please send:

1. CV and autobiography (with photo, date of birth, address, phone number, email, education and experience, and a description of skills)

2. Other: proof of relevant work experience, professional certificates, a copy of your highest degree certificate, etc.

Deadline: before 15 March 2016 (ROC year 105), by email to the university's Personnel Office at h054@mail.hwu.edu.tw

or by post to: No. 101, Sec. 1, Fenliao Rd., Linkou District, New Taipei City 24452

Attn: Personnel Office, Hsing Wu University

Please mark the envelope "Application for Full-Time Information Systems Technician". Applicants who pass the first review will be invited to an interview. (Applications that do not meet the requirements will not be returned.)
