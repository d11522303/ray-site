Before the Tour Guide and Leader Exam, EP51

#TourLeaderExamCountdown48Days

12 Besides buying Alaska from Russia in 1867, for sovereignty over which of the following places did the United States also pay money as compensation to the original owner? (A) The Philippines (B) Puerto Rico (C) Guam (D) The Hawaiian Islands (113 Tour Leader Exam)

Recently US President Trump has kept wanting to buy Greenland, because the more than 200 years of US territorial expansion are full of examples of diplomatic manoeuvring and exchanging money for land.

Starting with the 1803 purchase of Louisiana from France, the US then acquired Florida from Spain through the Adams-Onis Treaty, and annexed Mexican territory (Arizona and southern New Mexico) through the Gadsden Purchase. Better known is Alaska, bought from Russia in 1867; after winning the Spanish-American War in 1898 it obtained Puerto Rico and Guam, and also paid Spain US$20 million as compensation for sovereignty over the Philippines; and in 1917 it also bought today's US Virgin Islands from Denmark.

#1 Which of the following statements about the territories the US obtained after the 1898 Spanish-American War is incorrect?

(A) Under the Treaty of Paris, the US paid Spain US$20 million to obtain sovereignty over the Philippines

(B) Although Cuba gained nominal independence after the war, it was under substantial political interference from the US for a long time

(C) Guam and Puerto Rico became US territory after this war and remain so today

(D) To compensate Mexico for its losses in the war, the US additionally paid US$15 million to buy Louisiana

The answer is that (B) is incorrect; today's Cuba is a communist country.

#2 Regarding the "purchase expansion" in US history, which of the following pairings of "trading partner" and "territory purchased" is incorrect?

(A) Florida bought from Mexico

(B) Alaska bought from Russia

(C) The Philippines bought from Spain

(D) Louisiana bought from France (Napoleon)

The answer is that (A) is incorrect.
