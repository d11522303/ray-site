#ReissueOfTranscriptsAndDiplomas

Quite a few homeroom classes of the Hualien Taiwan Hospitality & Tourism College ask: now that the school no longer exists, how can a graduation certificate be reissued, and can transcripts be reissued?

Of course they can. Before, some devoted teachers who stayed at the school helped, and now there is an official channel for applications. Thanks to the teachers' painstaking work, all the student records of Taiwan Hospitality & Tourism College in Hualien over several decades were sorted out (those teachers who stayed behind deserve immense credit), and on 29 September 2022 they were all handed over to National Yunlin University of Science and Technology. To apply for reissued transcripts and diplomas now, you can go to the dedicated unit.

NYUST contact number: 05-5342601 ext. 2203 or 5373
