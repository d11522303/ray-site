2025.5.14

Congratulations to Zhu Zi-Yu, a second-year student in the HWU Department of Travel Management, on winning second place in the live-creation category of the National Tourism Elite Cup.

This year the finals of the Tourism Elite Cup were held at Hsiuping University of Science and Technology in Taichung. In fact, the student, who is also the president of the student association, was frantically busy with the school's three-in-one election. When I notified her in the LINE group at 11 p.m. the night before that we would leave early the next morning, she had only just finished her part-time job and was getting ready to go back to the dormitory. Like a superhero, she still appeared full of energy in the morning light, right on time at 6:28 at the agreed meeting point. Before Linkou's crazy commuter rush had even begun, the four of us, teachers and students, headed south toward the rising sun. On the way, she, her classmate and I still found time to review aloud the locations, content, features and accessibility of each of Taiwan's 100 Tourism Highlights, and with a cheerful yet careful mood we arrived at the warm Hsiuping University of Science and Technology in Taichung, host school of this year's national finals.

The Tourism Elite Cup is the most important annual tour-design competition in Taiwan, guided by the Tourism Administration. Winning teams from each school's preliminaries, with their teachers and students, even arrived at the venue the day before, and some schools even chartered coaches to take whole groups along. Our four teachers and students from Travel Management, by contrast, went in alone, and a banner hung up in the plaza gave a charmingly out-of-place feeling. I, the teacher, was the driver and local guide. I arranged the insurance and got the students' official leave approved, and the mental preparation before departure was to let the students feel that taking on a competition is a fun chance to discover their own abilities, and that being brave enough to enter is something worth recognising. I first added points and health to the students' hearts, cheering and encouraging them.

After the live competition at noon, the temperature in Taichung had already reached 36 degrees. We wanted to get back to school before the evening commuter traffic in each city, so we decided to drive right away from Dali to nearby Wufeng to have lunch at Chaoyang University of Technology, and to visit Caohu Meifang Taro Ice, my childhood favourite, to reward ourselves for working so hard and cool down at the same time.

The news that Zi-Yu had won came from Teacher Kong Ling-Yang's live text updates at Hsiuping. Besides coaching Zi-Yu outside class, Teacher Kong was also named an outstanding tour-planning teacher by the Tourism Administration. We were all overjoyed, and also grateful to Director Huang Ming-Yi, who kept caring that our journey would be safe, to Teacher Xiao-Hua for the course requirements and support, and to homeroom Teacher Fan for the invisible encouragement. On the freeway, the students were still rushing to finish Teacher Xiao-Jie's Abacus online booking assignment, and even as we neared campus they were still trying to use the radar roll-call in the car for the last class. It was a truly full day.

The four of us, teachers and students, used the competition as training and gained precious experience in learning, training, competing and watching others compete. We hope this small experience and encouragement will become nourishment for growth.

On site we saw the hard work and support of busy Chairman Chung Jen-Jung, Chief Judge Associate Professor Huang Yung-Chuan, Associate Professor Tseng Hsi-Peng of National Chi Nan University, Director Yu Hsiao-Ling of Aletheia University, Director Kuo Chung-Ling of Lee-Ming Institute of Technology, Secretary-General Tu Hung-An of the Tour Leader Association, and other heavyweight teachers and industry experts. It is their contributions that have built the most credible professional stage for a national tour-design competition. Thank you to the organiser, the Republic of China Tour Planning Association. See you next year.
