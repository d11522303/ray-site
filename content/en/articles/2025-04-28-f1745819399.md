Turn the sound on for the music!

Doesn't it sound familiar? When you hear this piece, what does it remind you of?

Frequent travellers will always think of the music that plays softly in the cabin of an EVA Air flight when it returns to Taiwan. For the girl in the video, it was the daily atmosphere accompanying her work in passenger services at Songshan Airport. Every bit of her internship, the sweet, sour, bitter and spicy learning experiences, nourished the student's growth.

The Department of Travel Management at Hsing Wu has cooperated with EVA Air and UNI Air on internships for many years. The department's aviation teachers all come from this great company, rooted in Taiwan and flying to the world, and of course students now studying in the department already regard the cabin crew teachers as role models for growth, hoping that one day they can put on the green uniform and serve.

Hui-Hsin did it, because while at school she ranked at the top in every subject, always among the top three in her class. She also took an active part in department activities, serving as class leader of my homeroom class, president of the department student association, and representative of the graduating-class union. She is a teacher's best helper and prized student. I wish this mature and considerate young woman a bright future after graduation, smooth sailing in everything, and a great career.

Class of 2021, Zhongli Commercial High School, foreign languages

Class representative, 2022 department student association

2024 EVA Air Songshan Airport ground staff internship
