From Munich Central Station to the Deutsches Museum

With the development of AI, trip planning is quite different from the old way of looking up information. Take my 2016 independent-travel transport plan for Munich as an example. The main itinerary that day was to set out from a hotel near the central station and visit the Deutsches Museum. Because it was independent travel, every leg had to be checked. I remember first consulting the transport suggestions on the museum's website, then using Google Maps to plan travel times and walking routes. Confirming the whole route took at least ten-odd minutes.

Now I just open the app and ask ChatGPT directly: "Please help plan the public transport options from Munich Central Station to the Deutsches Museum, and present the travel time and cost in a table." The result comes back in milliseconds. I then ask it, "Can you provide a map?", and the result is as shown in the attached picture: rich, accurate and complete.

So I use Google Maps with its suggestions, and within one minute I have a clear route map. Put it in my phone and just follow along. Six years ago the internet wasn't as well developed as today, and I wrote everything in an itinerary sheet and travelled by the sheet.

The attached pictures are the 2016 illustrated record of the trip from Munich Central Station to the Deutsches Museum, for anyone interested in independent travel in Germany. Because we rode the underground and walked ourselves, we could better feel the summer city atmosphere of Munich.
