Tour Guide and Tour Leader Exam Prep EP12

#2025TourismAdministrationTourLeaderExamQuestion

34. In Malaysia, ethnic Chinese are one of the important minority groups. Which Malaysian city is listed as a World Cultural Heritage site for its well-preserved colonial-era architecture and rich Chinese cultural heritage? ① Kuching ② Penang ③ Kuala Lumpur ④ Johor Bahru

Solution:

Penang in Malaysia, also known as George Town, and Malacca (Melaka) were inscribed by UNESCO in 2008 as a World Cultural Heritage property under the name "Melaka and George Town, Historic Cities of the Straits of Malacca." Both are long-established trading ports that blend diverse cultures, are famous for their distinctive architecture and cultural landscapes, and are typical cities where Chinese, Malay, Indian and European colonial cultures mingle. Here are two more Malaysia-related questions for you to try.

#1 A Malaysian city was an important transshipment port for Eurasian maritime trade from the 15th to the 17th century and was ruled successively by the Portuguese, the Dutch and the British. It still preserves intact colonial-era building districts, and a cityscape where Chinese, Malay and European cultures blend can still be seen, and so it was listed as a World Cultural Heritage site. Which of the following cities best fits the description?

① Kuching ② Malacca ③ Penang ④ Johor Bahru

#2 A Malaysian city is located on the island of Borneo and is the capital of Sarawak. Chinese are one of its important ethnic groups. The city's tourism features are mainly local indigenous cultures, tropical rainforest ecotourism and river-port scenery, and because the city's name sounds the same as the word for cat, "kucing," in Malay, it is also known as the City of Cats. Which of the following cities best fits the description? ① Kuala Lumpur ② Kota Kinabalu ③ Kuching ④ George Town
