HWU Travel Management Fully Enrolled for 2026 🏆🎯

It is a happy thing to be a teacher in the Department of Travel Management at Hsing Wu University. My colleagues are all experts from the travel and aviation industries who teach and encourage students as if they were their own disciples, and we see the students grow and flourish. Through internships and professional training, year after year they find their footing and move forward, and as teachers we feel a real sense of achievement.

August, the height of summer at the start of the 2026 academic year (ROC year 115), is really the month when the results of our whole year of teaching and guidance are announced, so of course we look forward to the enrolment results. The department's teaching team has taught, studied and mentored diligently and steadily, and treasured the bond of being teachers and students. Seeing the figures on registration day, we felt happy and moved!

Falling birth rate! It has not dampened our passion for teaching. The 2026 enrolment results have once again rewarded us with over a hundred new students in a single cohort of three classes, so each of us teachers has the chance to know more students and work to turn them into more fresh blood for travel and new recruits for airport ground services!

Welcome, freshmen, to HWU, Hsing Wu University
