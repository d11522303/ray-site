Eight Habits of Happy People

1. Don’t worry about what others think about you.

2. Always be friendly and grateful.

3. Always look for the positives in every situation.

4. Accept that not everything in this world is perfect.

5. Smile as often as possible, and stay positive.

6. Make your contribution to the world around you.

7. Live in the present.

8. Keep learning.
