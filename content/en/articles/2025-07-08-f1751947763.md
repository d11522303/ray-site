2025.7.8

A Roundup of Personnel News Heard at HWU This Summer

Many names were mentioned at the college evaluation meeting, but most had nothing to do with the College of Tourism and Hospitality: Lin Tien-Sheng, Kuo Shih-Cheng, Kuo Ying-Chih, Kang Po-Kai and others. Several colleagues shared a similar professional identity with me, and I had exchanged more views with them. But at this moment I can also feel that these people are all trying to leave HWU or are being purged by the school, which makes me wonder whether I should think more about how I interact and socialise with others, since the colleagues and friends in my own circle have all left the school.

The teachers who successfully moved to better schools all cultivated outside connections, and were still at the stage of having just earned their doctorates and being assistant professors. They bravely published articles and competed for all kinds of projects, using their ability to prove they could change jobs.

Are the teachers who were purged or transferred out of their departments really as difficult to get along with as the managers present said? I feel instead that managers found them hard to control, or that they had too many opinions, and, combined with their unfamiliarity with the system, were dismissed by the department and re-hired as administrative staff, or chose to retire early and leave this environment.

Recording a few colleagues who are about to leave: Wu I-Fang, Hsiao Chen-Chen, Yang Wen-Jen, Sister Mei-Lan and Sister Hui-Ling are all retiring early, before reaching retirement age. In short, at this moment it is important to keep a low profile, find ways to stay at the school, actively compete for projects, and work to widen your outside connections.
