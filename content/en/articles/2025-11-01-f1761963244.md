【The Most Unsatisfactory Airlines?!】

Click Intelligence, a UK data research company, used the internet and AI analysis in a report that steers its audience. The company says it developed an airline dissatisfaction index by analysing four key quality indicators for the world's major airlines. The researchers examined passenger experience scores (1 to 10), the star ratings (1 to 5 stars) from the airline rating agency Skytrax, standardised complaint search volumes, and safety records documenting major incidents and operational failures. Airlines score from 0 to 100, with a higher score meaning lower passenger satisfaction. The analysed data includes each airline's annual passenger volume, route destinations, and specific indicators related to customer complaints and safety incidents.

But after the article, Click Intelligence puts in a disclaimer, saying that individual passengers' experiences may differ. Airlines and their services may change at any time, and readers should treat it only as a reference when making travel decisions, with the company accepting no related rights or obligations. This disclaimer is interesting, and the comments on the original article also pointed out that a European airline may have bought a reverse-spin article through the survey, so I add the same disclaimer in this repost.

Because students are less familiar with British and American airlines, I used AI to make a chart (for reference only; I tidied up the text, but the picture part is all ChatGPT's work and it always makes mistakes).

See whether you have flown any of the following airlines. Below are the ten most unsatisfactory, ranked first to tenth. I also looked up some recent reports on the airlines. I don't think the ranking is reasonable; most are British and American airlines. Do those who have flown them feel the same?

#1 American Airlines

According to reports from the Associated Press, The Washington Post and ABC News, on 29 January 2025, American Airlines Flight 5342 collided in mid-air with a Black Hawk helicopter over the Potomac River in Washington, D.C., killing 67 people, the first major commercial flight accident in the US in 16 years.

#2 Frontier Airlines

According to reports from Newsweek and The Times of India, between 2024 and 2025 Frontier Airlines received the most customer complaints in the US for consecutive years, mainly due to flight cancellations, lost baggage and delayed refunds, and in 2025 an airport employee was fired after a video of a conflict with a passenger went viral on social media.

#3 United Airlines

According to reports from Reuters and AP News, between 2024 and 2025 United Airlines had multiple incidents including engine fires and cabin panels coming loose, leading the US Federal Aviation Administration (FAA) to increase oversight, and the CEO also wrote publicly to passengers promising to improve safety.

#4 Air France

According to reports from SecurityWeek, Bleeping Computer and Forbes, in August 2025 Air France and its group subsidiary KLM suffered a hacker intrusion into a third-party system, leaking some customer data (such as names, contact details and loyalty programme numbers). Although no credit card or passport data was involved, it raised passengers' privacy concerns.

#5 Ryanair

According to the company's official press release and The Guardian, from 12 June 2025 Ryanair has imposed a minimum fine of 500 euros on passengers who cause an aircraft diversion or offloading, in order to deter unruly onboard behaviour.

#6 AirAsia

According to AirAsia's official news and the Malay Mail, in July 2024, during a large-scale IT outage across global aviation systems, AirAsia carried more than 500,000 passengers on about 3,000 flights, with an operating rate above 99%, showing its operational continuity.

#7 Aeroméxico

According to Mexico Business News, on 28 July 2025, an Aeroméxico AM190 aircraft returned because of a cabin pressure system failure; the oxygen masks dropped, the passengers landed safely, and it was the company's third major safety incident recently. Mexico's civil aviation authority (AFAC) has launched an investigation.

#8 Scandinavian Airlines (SAS)

I think it is somewhat unreasonable that Scandinavian Airlines was included in the ranking, because after checking I found that recent public reports do not clearly point to any major customer harm or operational disruption at SAS in 2024-2025.

#9 Wizz Air

I hadn't heard of this regional airline. According to an investigative report in The Independent, on 31 July 2024 Wizz Air was rated the airline with the worst customer service in the UK. Its passengers complained of slow responses and poor attitudes from customer service, and 57% of respondents said they had an unsatisfactory customer service experience.

#10 British Airways

According to The Guardian, the Financial Times and AirwaysMag, during 2024-2025 British Airways repeatedly caused flight delays and cancellations at Heathrow Airport due to technical system failures, mainly because of complicated crew scheduling, increased fleet maintenance needs and budget pressure. Safety incidents were fewer, but the customer experience was poor and was affected.

To say it once more, this ranking is the view reported by Click Intelligence and has nothing to do with the person who compiled this content. The above content and images were produced with AI collaboration, and the main article sources are in the comments section.
