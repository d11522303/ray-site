【Sending Ronaldo and Portugal Home】

The home of Portuguese heart-throb #Ronaldo, the island of #Madeira, is actually very close to Morocco

Portugal's football star Ronaldo did not manage to show his true worth at the 2022 World Cup before retirement, as he had hoped. A newspaper headline read: "Sending Ronaldo and Portugal home".

In fact, Ronaldo's home is a paradise on earth, the island of Madeira #Madeira, in the Atlantic Ocean west of Morocco in Africa. It is one of the two autonomous regions of Portugal, and Portugal began to rule it in the fifteenth century. The island's largest port and capital, #Funchal, is the birthplace of the handsome Ronaldo. If you join a cruise in the Mediterranean and the Atlantic, most cruises will include this paradise-like and beautiful port, where prices and scenery are very friendly and pleasant.

The next stop for the cruise is the garden city of North Africa, #Morocco, for a visit to Casablanca, to see the real locations where #IngridBergman, with the most beautiful smile in Casablanca, once left her mark.

Morocco is a monarchy in Africa, and the hereditary succession of the present kingdom has continued since the 17th century. During the period of Western expansion it was declared a protectorate by France and Spain, but it kept the continuity of its sovereignty, culture and religion. The culture of the Kingdom of Morocco blends Arab, Moorish, Western European and other cultures. The former king Hassan II once described it: "Morocco is a tree whose roots are planted in Africa, but whose leaves breathe European air."
