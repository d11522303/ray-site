Tour Guide and Tour Leader Exam Prep EP4

#2025TourismAdministrationTourLeaderExamQuestion

4. The famous historic site "Qingjing Temple" in Quanzhou, China, is what kind of religious building?

① Buddhist temple ② Mosque ③ Christian church ④ Taoist temple

You can guess the answer above from the sound of the name......

China has 60 World Heritage Sites, second in the world in number, only behind Italy with 61, and the [Silk Roads] are a transnational World Cultural Heritage property. Quanzhou, mentioned in this question, was both the starting point and the end point of the Maritime Silk Road. In the Yuan dynasty it was one of the busiest ports in the world, and businesspeople from all over the world settled in the city, even living there for generations. Quite a few Taiwanese people have ancestors from Quanzhou.

Here are two more questions based on Quanzhou's World Heritage for you to try.

1. Between the 10th and 14th centuries, Quanzhou was a centre of world maritime trade, and so it was inscribed as a World Cultural Heritage property in 2021. Which two dynasties mainly make up the historical background of that time? ① Ming and Qing ② Yuan and Ming ③ Song and Yuan ④ Southern Song

2. The Silk Roads: the Routes Network of Chang'an-Tianshan Corridor was inscribed as a World Cultural Heritage property in 2014. It is an overland trade route crossing countries and cultures. According to UNESCO's clear definition, which of the following modern countries is not included? ① Kazakhstan ② India ③ China ④ Kyrgyzstan
