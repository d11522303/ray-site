2025.2.9

A person without roots?

How long have you lived in your current home?

I have lived in this house I bought for myself for almost ten years now. It is a second-hand home that we bought 11 years ago, after moving from Hualien to be near our work. The house came with help from family and our own effort. If you divide the price into ten parts, three parts were paid by the elders and the other seven by us. We have lived here happily and peacefully.

From birth until now, because of my own or my family's work, I have moved many times. Over half a century I have moved house a dozen-plus times for various reasons.

Because I could never stay long in one place to nurture body and mind, I always feel uneasy, feeling that one day we will again be packing up the whole household and searching everywhere for a place to settle. It's the same with work: go where the money is, and when the work environment is at risk, switch to the next job early, with the result that as the years pass, my expertise and abilities have been hard to accumulate.

Truly, a rolling stone gathers no moss.

Now life is on the return journey. Looking back at midnight on the road I've come, I always feel dissatisfied, though it was due to family circumstances and the times. Reflecting on the present, a stable career that builds up the wealth and property I already have is the most crucial thing for my family and the next generation.

By the time I had written this far, the reason I picked up my pen and my discontent had gradually vanished. Instead I recognised the impractical, overly romantic side of my character. Living down to earth, striving to put down roots, and building up energy, doing what the resources I have allow, is what matters most.
