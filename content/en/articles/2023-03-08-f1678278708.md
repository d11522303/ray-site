Three years ago, a twelfth-grade student from Jinou Girls' High School, wearing a yellow sports uniform, took a two-hour bus ride after school to visit our department and see the learning environment. She asked quite a few questions on her own and looked at the department's courses and facilities. I remember that when she and her classmate headed home, the Linkou campus was already completely dark. Right then she told me firmly: Teacher, I want to study in the Department of Travel Management at Hsing Wu.

Today the students ran into the classroom with delighted steps, jumping and shouting, pushed a small phone screen right in front of me and shouted: Teacher! I got the job! This was the best job in her heart. She told me to look carefully.

I looked, but I couldn't see clearly. Was it my old-age eyesight, or was it tears?
