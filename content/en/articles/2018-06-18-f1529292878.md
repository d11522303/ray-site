Hsing Wu University (HWU) President Tsan-Te Chou said that the students of the College of Tourism and Hospitality took the initiative to join the training, spending a full three to four months preparing seriously. Their attitude is moving, and the care the teachers in charge showed in coaching them in their spare time is just as admirable.

Szu-Jou Chen, a third-year student in the Department of Travel Management, said she is grateful for the university's abundant software and hardware support and the teachers' tireless, careful guidance in helping her earn both the Mandarin tour guide and tour leader licences. Her goal is to join the travel industry and realise a lifelong dream of exploring the world. Passing the national exams is only the first step. In the future she hopes to use the university's full-time fourth-year internship programme to choose a good partner company for her placement, and to keep moving steadily towards her plans.
