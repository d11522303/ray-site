【2017 Ordinary Examination for Professional and Technical Personnel: Tour Guides and Tour Leaders】

Students, it is that time of year again, the annual registration rush.

You only have 10 days to register, and if you miss it, you wait another winter!

Registration period: from 22 November 2016 until 5:00 p.m. on 1 December.

Examination levels and categories:

(1) Foreign-language tour guides (English, Japanese, French, German, Spanish, Korean, Thai, Arabic, Russian, Italian, Vietnamese, Indonesian, Malay).

(2) Mandarin Chinese tour guides.

(3) Foreign-language tour leaders (English, Japanese, French, German, Spanish).

(4) Mandarin Chinese tour leaders.

Registration website: http://register.moex.gov.tw/
