【Last call: registration for the 2nd-semester 2015-16 TOEIC campus course at Hsing Wu University】

To raise and sharpen the English ability of all students and meet the English graduation requirement, the university has set up a TOEIC TA elite seed tutoring class for English certification. Through professional, intensive training, students will master the methods and tricks of English certification exams, raise their test scores and gain confidence in earning an English certificate. They can then spread this to every department and class, sharing their experience and helping classmates in the same class or department prepare for English exams. The target is mainly TOEIC CEFR B1, a score of 550 (275 in listening and 275 in reading).

Fee: NT$800 for books (you can find out what it costs elsewhere with a quick Google)

Eligibility: Department of Travel Management students at Hsing Wu University, years 2 and 3

Instructors:
Tourism & Hospitality Class A: Tue periods 6-7, Thu periods 8-9, industry instructor Kelly
Tourism & Hospitality Class B: Tue periods 6-7, Wed periods 6-7, industry instructor David
