#On-the-Spot Solutions (answer options assisted by AI, please use for reference first)

1. After Japan took over Taiwan, it devoted itself to suppressing the anti-Japanese activities of Han Chinese on the plains and had no time to deal with indigenous resistance. So which Qing-dynasty system did it inherit in order to block indigenous attacks?

(A) Interpreter (tongshi) system (B) Military craftsmen system (C) Aboriginal garrison-farming (fantun) system (D) Aiyong (border guard) system

Answer: (D)

Explanation: The aiyong system was an armed guard system used from the Qing period to the early Japanese colonial period to guard against indigenous peoples, demarcate the boundary between Han and indigenous areas, and protect camphor harvesting.

2. The construction of the North-Link Railway had a major impact on eastern Taiwan. Which government programme promoted this transportation project?

(A) The Ten Major Construction Projects (B) The Twelve Major Construction Projects (C) The Fourteen Major Construction Projects (D) The Six-Year National Development Plan

Answer: (A)

Explanation: The Ten Major Construction Projects included six transportation projects, of which the North-Link Railway was one (construction began in 1973 and it opened in 1980).

3. What is the historical name of the Han Chinese farmers' uprising that took place in Taiwan during the Japanese colonial period?

(A) The Guo Huai-yi Incident (B) The Hamada Yahyoe Incident (C) The Mado Stream Incident (D) The Erlin Incident

Answer: (D)

Explanation: The Erlin Incident (1925) was a farmers' movement triggered by a dispute over sugarcane purchase prices during the Japanese colonial period, and is regarded as the forerunner of Taiwan's farmers' movements.

4. Taiwan is a multi-ethnic society, but there have been many cross-ethnic conflicts in its history. Which of the following involved indigenous people, Han Chinese and Westerners?

(A) The Chu Yi-kuei Incident (B) The Tai Chao-chun Incident (C) The Rover Incident (D) The Kalyawan (Jialiwan) Incident

Answer: (C)

Explanation: The Rover Incident (1867) involved an American ship, Paiwan people and the Qing court (with Western diplomat Charles Le Gendre intervening in negotiations), and led to the Convention of South Cape.

5. In which period did Taiwan begin to introduce the "seven-day week" and adopt Greenwich Mean Time?

(A) Republic period (B) Japanese colonial period (C) Qing period (D) Dutch period

Answer: (B)

Explanation: After Japan took over Taiwan, for ease of rule and modernisation, it introduced the Western calendar, concept of time and the seven-day week.

6. Which Qing official erected the "Tiger Character Stele" on the Caoling Historic Trail?

(A) Hsu Tsung-kan (B) Liu Ming-chuan (C) Shen Pao-chen (D) Liu Ming-teng

Answer: (D)

Explanation: Both the Tiger Character Stele and the "Xiong Zhen Man Yan" stele were inscribed by Taiwan Regional Commander Liu Ming-teng when he inspected the trail during the Tongzhi reign of the Qing dynasty.

7. The "Tomb of Wang Te-lu" is a nationally designated historic site. Due to the outbreak of which war was Wang Te-lu ordered by the Qing court to defend Penghu, only to die of illness during the defence?

(A) The Opium War (B) The Anglo-French Allied Forces (C) The Sino-French War (D) The Mudan Incident

Answer: (A)

Explanation: Wang Te-lu (the highest-ranking official from Taiwan in the Qing period) was ordered to defend Penghu during the Opium War in 1841, and died of illness from overwork at the age of 72.

8. In many places in Taiwan there is a similar legend that when war broke out, the goddess Mazu would appear and catch bombs to protect the people. Which war does this legend refer to?

(A) The Japanese air raids on indigenous villages during the Wushe Incident (B) The Communist bombardment of Kinmen during the August 23 Artillery Bombardment (C) The Allied bombing of Taiwan during the Pacific War (D) The Japanese bombing of Chinese cities during the War of Resistance against Japan

Answer: (C)

Explanation: At the end of World War II (the Pacific War), the US military carried out large-scale bombing of Japanese-ruled Taiwan, and Mazu temples across Taiwan (such as Dajia and Puzi) all have folk legends of Mazu catching bombs.

9. The Sinkang (Xingang) Manuscripts are written records left by indigenous people with the help of Dutch missionaries during the Dutch rule of Taiwan, written in the Roman alphabet. Which ethnic group is this document associated with?

(A) Ketagalan (B) Siraya (C) Taokas (D) Pazeh

Answer: (B)

Explanation: The Sinkang Manuscripts (Sinkan script) are records of the language used by the Siraya, a plains indigenous group in the Tainan area.

10. The Qing court governed Taiwan with a policy of "governing Taiwan to prevent rebellion," which changed only after the stimulus of which incident?

(A) The Lin Shuang-wen Incident (B) The Dananao Incident (C) The Tai Chao-chun Incident (D) The Mudan Incident

Answer: (D)

Explanation: The Mudan Incident of 1874 made the Qing court realise the importance of sovereignty over Taiwan, shifting from "passive governance of Taiwan" to "active civilising."

11. The National Museum of Prehistory is Taiwan's first national-level archaeological museum. Which site lies around its "Kangle Main Museum"?

(A) Niumatou site (B) Shihsanhang site (C) Chihshanyen site (D) Peinan site

Answer: (D)

Explanation: The Kangle Main Museum of the National Museum of Prehistory is in Taitung. It was decided to build it precisely because the Peinan site was discovered during construction of the Peinan Station on the South-Link Railway.

12. The Ting Jiao (Upper Guild) was one of the most important trading guilds in the Monga (Wanhua) area. Which place was the Ting Jiao's main trading region?

(A) Jiayingzhou (B) Guangzhou (C) Hainan (D) Quanzhou

Answer: (D)

Explanation: In the Qing period, the trading guilds (jiao) were divided into "upper and lower." The Upper Guild was mainly made up of merchants from Jinjiang, Hui'an and Nan'an in Quanzhou, and its trade was mostly with Quanzhou.

13. Neither the Lingxing Gate nor the Dacheng Gate of the Tainan Confucius Temple is painted with door gods. They are decorated with door studs instead. How many door studs are there on both sides of one gate in total?

(A) 36 (B) 72 (C) 108 (D) 144

Answer: (C)

Explanation: A Confucius temple is of the highest grade of architecture, and its number of door studs is a "multiple of nine." Each door leaf has 54, so the two leaves of a gate total 108.

14. Camphor was a world-class commodity of Taiwanese industry in the nineteenth century. Which is the correct combination of the following statements? (1) Whether camphor production went smoothly depended on relations with indigenous people (2) Dadaocheng merchant Li Chun-sheng became rich because of it (3) It drove the development of towns in the foothills (4) The camphor issue was the trigger for disputes between Taiwan and foreign countries in the late Qing period

(A) 1 and 3 (B) 3 and 4 (C) 2 and 4 (D) 2 and 3

Answer: (A)

Explanation: Li Chun-sheng became rich from "oolong tea," not camphor. Although camphor disputes did occur in the Qing period (such as the Camphor War), they were not central to the definition of the trigger for foreign disputes. Statements 1 and 3 best match the social and economic situation of the time.

15. In the 43rd year of the Republic (1954), under which legal instrument did the United States assist Taiwan and continue to support our representation in the United Nations?

(A) The Taiwan Relations Act (B) The Sino-American Mutual Defense Treaty (C) The Charter of the United Nations (D) The Korean War Armistice Agreement

Answer: (B)

Explanation: The Sino-American Mutual Defense Treaty was signed in 1954 (the 43rd year of the Republic), establishing the Cold War military alliance between the US and Taiwan.

16. Which of the following statements about the development of education in Taiwan is correct?

Answer: (C)

Explanation: (A) In the Dutch period, the indigenous people were taught the Sinkan script. (B) The Confucius Temple was established during the Zheng (Koxinga) period on the suggestion of Chen Yung-hua. (D) Taihoku Imperial University already existed in the Japanese period. In 1943, during the Japanese colonial period, six-year compulsory national education did indeed begin to be implemented.

17. The "Battle of Hobe (Tamsui) in the Sino-French War" is celebrated with a historical banquet marking the French attack on Tamsui on October 8, 1884. One dish is named "Nine Excellencies Crispy Taro," commemorating the defending general of Tamsui at the time. Which general was it?

(A) Chang Li-cheng (B) Liu Ming-chuan (C) Sun Kai-hua (D) Liao Kao-yuan

Explanation: The commander of the Battle of Hobe in the Sino-French War was General Sun Kai-hua, who led Qing troops and local militia in successfully repelling the French landing.

18. Which of the following was not among the institutional tools Japan used to control local society in the early period of its rule over Taiwan?

(A) The street-and-village (jiezhuang) system (B) The police system (C) The baojia system (D) The conscription system

Answer: (D)

Explanation: The conscription system was implemented only at the end of the Japanese colonial period (in 1945) to meet manpower needs in World War II, and was not an early tool of control.

19. What is the correct chronological order of developments in the 228 Incident, which had a major impact on Taiwanese society? (1) Improper handling of the official crackdown on contraband cigarettes sparked unrest across the island; Taiwanese elites came forward to maintain order by organising the "228 Incident Settlement Committee," but ended up killed or imprisoned (2) The Taiwanese people looked forward joyfully to leaving Japanese colonial rule and returning to the motherland, but soon became widely dissatisfied with the new postwar regime in Taiwan (3) Some Taiwanese went overseas to promote the Taiwan independence movement (4) The government carried out large-scale arrests under the name of "rural pacification"

Answer: (A)

Explanation: Background dissatisfaction (2) -> outbreak of the contraband cigarette crackdown and formation of the Settlement Committee (1) -> rural pacification after the troops arrived in Taiwan (4) -> rise of the overseas movement (3).

20. Which combination correctly describes the contributions of Christian missionaries? (1) James Laidlaw Maxwell was the first to come to Taiwan to preach (2) George Leslie Mackay and Hugh Ritchie jointly established Taiwan's first Western-style hospital (3) Thomas Barclay published the Tâi-oân-hú-siâⁿ Kàu-hōe-pò (Taiwan Prefectural City Church News) (4) William Campbell devoted himself to education for the blind

24. In northern Taiwan, because of monsoons, there is a saying, "Hsinchu wind, Ilan rain," and tourism activities are easily affected. In which season does this climatic feature mainly occur?

(A) Spring (B) Summer (C) Autumn (D) Winter

Answer: (D)

Explanation: In winter the northeast monsoon prevails. Terrain creates strong winds in Hsinchu, while on the Lanyang Plain (Yilan), terrain uplift causes days of continuous overcast rain.

25. At Kuibishan, a famous scenic spot in Penghu, the "Parting of the Red Sea (Moses' parting of the sea)" occurs when the tide changes and a path connecting Chiyu, out in the sea, to the main island of Penghu appears. In terms of coastal landforms, where does this phenomenon occur?

Answer: (A)

Explanation: The path of the "Parting of the Red Sea" is submerged under seawater normally and is exposed only at low tide, in the zone called the intertidal zone.

29. Flights from Taiwan to North America often take off late at night, mainly in consideration of adjusting for the time difference. Xiao-ming takes a China Airlines flight to Vancouver (W123°) to attend a conference. He departs Taoyuan Airport at 23:00 on November 30, with a flight time of about 10 hours. After the conference he takes a flight at 02:00 local time on December 6 back to Taoyuan Airport, with a flight time of about 12 hours. What are the local times of Xiao-ming's arrival in Vancouver and return to Taoyuan Airport, respectively?

(A) Arrives in Vancouver Dec. 1, 17:00; returns to Taoyuan Airport Dec. 6, 06:00

(B) Arrives in Vancouver Nov. 30, 17:00; returns to Taoyuan Airport Dec. 7, 06:00

(C) Arrives in Vancouver Dec. 1, 05:00; returns to Taoyuan Airport Dec. 7, 18:00

(D) Arrives in Vancouver Nov. 30, 05:00; returns to Taoyuan Airport Dec. 6, 06:00

Answer: (B)

Explanation:

1. Time difference: Taoyuan (E120) and Vancouver (W120 standard) differ by 16 hours (Taoyuan is 16 hours ahead).

2. Outbound: 11/30 23:00 + 10 hr = 12/1 09:00 (Taiwan time). Subtract the 16-hour difference = 11/30 17:00 (Vancouver time).

3. Return: 12/6 02:00 (Vancouver time) + 12 hr = 12/6 14:00 (Vancouver time). Add the 16-hour difference = 12/7 06:00 (Taiwan time).

Explanation: Taiwan's five Longshan Temples (Tainan, Lukang, Fengshan, Monga and Tamsui) were mostly built in the Qing period. Generally, Tainan Longshan Temple (Kangxi reign) is the earliest, and Tamsui Longshan Temple (Xianfeng reign) is later.
