#1 A farmer plans to grow crops on the central-southern plains, hoping for "plenty of winter sunshine and little rain, to reduce pests and disease and improve crop quality". Which of the following crop arrangements best fits this climate?

A. Spring tender tea leaves

B. Winter tomatoes and onions

C. Summer first-crop rice

D. Autumn high-mountain cabbage

#2 A local government promotes an "autumn persimmon-drying, wine-making and farm-product processing festival", choosing October to November as the main event period. Which of the following climate factors was the main consideration in choosing this time?

B. The northeast monsoon is strongest

C. Monsoon transition, with stable, crisp and dry weather

D. Summer heat favours fermentation
