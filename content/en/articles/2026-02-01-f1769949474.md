27 Which of the following countries has aurora as a tourism resource? (A) Norway (B) Germany (C) Switzerland (D) Brazil (2024 overseas tour leader tourism resources exam)

Explanation: In tourism geography, aurora tourism resources are usually related to latitude and climate. Among the countries above, only Norway's territory extends north into the Arctic Circle, making it one of the world's most famous aurora-viewing destinations. In addition, because Norway's harbours are also affected by the North Atlantic Current, the northern harbours are mostly ice-free, and the climate is milder than other regions at the same latitude, making it suitable for leading tour groups.

The following places also have aurora resources and are popular choices for tour leaders leading aurora tours:

Northern Europe: Norway, Sweden, Finland, Iceland.

North America: Alaska in the United States, Canada (such as Yellowknife and Whitehorse).

Southern Hemisphere (aurora australis): South Island of New Zealand, Tasmania in Australia.
