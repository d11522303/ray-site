2025.8.17

The 12 Zodiac Animals and the 12 Double-Hours

Every day has 12 double-hours, but the productive ones may be fewer than four, that is, eight hours. My values have always been to live well, not to pursue productivity. But over the years I have slowly realised that in Chinese society people have so much contact with one another that if you do not build up ability through time and habits, and just live by desire and instinct, you eventually stagnate.

In Mr. Yen Chang-shou's book "You Are the Starting Point of Change", he writes: you hope your simple days can carry on peacefully, but when you choose to stay where you are while others strive forward, your competitiveness falls relatively. Even if you are content with being "plain" and "ordinary", in an age of such rapid change, if you do not strive, it will be hard to resist all the forces pulling you down. This is not a question of willingness or ambition; rather, you must work very hard and have big dreams to find a foothold in global competition. If you choose stability from the beginning and choose to stay in the greenhouse, your future choices may no longer be yours to make.

Do you feel the force pulling you down?

Or have you found that the choices you have now are no longer yours to make?

Since junior high school, I have felt that endless downward pull: can't learn it! Can't keep up! The fear of punishment! The anxiety of being left behind! No peace at any moment. What to do? When your survival resources are insufficient, you can only face it with effort and not give up your dreams; otherwise, when the years close in, your choices are no longer yours to make.

The year 114 of the Republic (2025) is the Year of the Snake. The next Year of the Snake is year 126 (2037), the year I must retire from my current job. If, unfortunately, my private school cannot hold out, I may have to face the day of retirement even earlier. Year after year, day after day, the 12 double-hours of each day are, from this angle, very precious. From the Chen (Dragon) hour when I wake at seven in the morning to the You (Rooster) hour when I get home tired after work, the time I can control is limited, and most of it is governed by physiology: I get sleepy, tired, vexed and anxious; I get lazy, forgetful, greedy and want to lie flat.

So I will tune the channel of life back to myself: record my daily exercise and running again, connect with people through likes and little hearts, and write down the bits of effort I put into teaching and community involvement. That will be the focus of my daily life for the next twelve years.
