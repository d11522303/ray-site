Jonathan Lee (Lo Ta-yu) wandered the world, romantically searching for himself. Unwilling to be labelled, he left his familiar hometown and drifted around New York, Hong Kong and Beijing, moving 19 times in all.

I, a nobody, moved 18 times in the first part of my short 40 years of life. But I was forced to face reality, a life like a sesame seed (a "daughter's fate" like the oil-hemp seed, as the Taiwanese saying goes), hoping to live every moment as much like a human being as I could.
