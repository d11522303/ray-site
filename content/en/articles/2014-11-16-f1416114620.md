Bonus question:

1. Who won our country's first gold medal in an official Olympic event?

(A) Yang Chuan-kwang (B) Chi Cheng (C) Chen Yi-an (D) Chen Shih-hsin

Explanation

(A) Mr Yang Chuan-kwang won our country's first Olympic medal, a silver in the decathlon at the 1960 Rome Olympics.

(B) Ms Chi Cheng won bronze in the women's 80m hurdles at the 1968 Mexico City Olympics. She was the first Asian woman to win a medal in a sprint event at an international athletics competition, and the first Olympic medal won by a Taiwanese female athlete.

(C) Ms Chen Yi-an beat a Korean opponent in the finweight taekwondo demonstration event at the 1988 Seoul Olympics, becoming the first athlete in the Republic of China's sporting history to win an Olympic gold medal (a demonstration event, not an official Olympic sport).

(D) Coach Chen Shih-hsin won gold in women's taekwondo at the 2004 Athens Olympics. That medal was also the Republic of China's first gold in an official event in 72 years of Olympic history.
