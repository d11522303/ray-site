A few weeks ago, a publisher phoned the department to invite a manuscript, and to my surprise they wanted a textbook on MICE tourism. I casually asked the editor why they would publish about an industry that is currently badly affected during the pandemic. The editor could not answer and said it was the editor-in-chief's idea.

Nothing has come of it since, but the plague of the century is still spreading, and the related national certification exams are still being held, oblivious to the suffering of the world. Should you sign up?

If you have taken related courses and are interested in exhibition promotion, this is a qualification certified by the Bureau of Foreign Trade, Ministry of Economic Affairs. It is not very easy to pass, but you can give it a try! After all, you cannot go job hunting upon graduation with just a single university diploma and compete with the army of unemployed for fewer and fewer jobs.
