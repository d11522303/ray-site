2025.7.9

Quiet Mind

Today I made up my mind to do what I like, planning a trip.

I organised the videos I had seen online lately onto paper in the way I am used to. It gives me a sense of reassurance and a feeling of anticipation for the coming trip, rather than the feeling of being forced to escape from life.

In midsummer July, because of a typhoon, the campus was quiet, accompanied by the sound of rain and a cool breeze, and the summer heat was gone. For several hours I quietly wrote station names on paper and marked sights and restaurants on Google Maps, which was truly delightful.

I planned a trip around Bangkok's "dirty" canal boats and Hua Lamphong Railway Station, which is about to be retired and turned into a railway museum, and I am really looking forward to the experience. https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=94yJ9NrbAgw

I also want to try the Michelin Bib Gourmand-recommended Bangkok food along the river, Thipsamai Pad Thai (the "Ghost Gate" fried rice noodles) and Go-Ang Pratunam Chicken Rice (the "Big Brother Red" chicken rice).
