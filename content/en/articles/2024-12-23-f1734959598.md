2024.12.23

At year's end, I just calculated my evaluation points, and for academic year 113 I may get a grade B.

It's really sad. I feel I put enormous effort into teaching and internship coaching this year, and the department also worked hard on administration and admissions. But looking at the faculty evaluation form: preparing lesson plans and teaching hard (+0), coaching students for certificates outside class (+3), serving as internship secretary outside class and actively arranging student internships (+1 to 3), and visiting industry for employment guidance (+0). The above took most of my time and energy, yet it added only 4 points.

To get back to grade A in 2025, besides the above I also have to do the following:

Teaching

(1) Coach students to a top-three finish in a national competition +3 to 6

(2) Publish with an ISBN +2

(3) Digital teaching materials +6

(4) Obtain a TOEIC gold certificate +8

(5) Obtain a Level 2 professional certificate +5

(6) Pass a teaching practice research project +10

(7) After completing all of the above, I can apply for Excellent Teacher +10

Research

(1) Submit to a Tier I journal +10

(2) Submit to a good journal +6

(3) Publish with an ISBN +5

(4) Pass an NSTC project of NT$100,000 or more +2

(5) Pass a utility model patent +5
