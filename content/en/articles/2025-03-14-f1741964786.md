2025.3.14

The last time I installed an air conditioner for our own home was probably in 1997, when we lived on Chang'an Street in Sanchong, and it was still a window unit back then. When we were forced to move in 2001, I remember we took the air conditioner with us, and later installed it on the iron-barred balcony extension of our Muzha apartment. My memory is hazy; I only remember the feeling of a young newly married couple working hard to improve their life in the face of reality.

Today the two of us bought another split-type air conditioner for our home. We can afford the cost, but our long-accumulated cautious mindset is just the same. Though we could have replaced everything at once, we still tentatively asked a technician someone recommended to do the installation, checked the unit's specifications again and again, and still hope that the one-to-three air conditioner, which has been running for 17 years, can be repaired one day.

After the installation, I shared a message on LINE with our child, hoping he would see it when he comes home tomorrow. And sitting in the living room looking at the brand-new air conditioner, I thought that a simple life is actually not so simple.
