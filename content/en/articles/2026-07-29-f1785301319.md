【Participants are being recruited for the latest work of NTUST MEG's classic online puzzle game series, "Dream Seeking Teacher Li"!!!】‼️

About the activity:

One day, Teacher Xiaoqi suddenly lost contact! In the familiar campus, many unsolved clues were left behind. Step together into this unknown dream journey, moving between fragments of events and memories, and gradually uncover a teacher's balance between teaching and their inner world, public opinion, and a silent cry from an emotional predicament. By piecing together clues, you will search together for the truth behind Teacher Xiaoqi's disappearance, and along the way feel what others are going through, building your empathy and your awareness of the psychological phenomena behind social issues!

🕵️Project organiser: Mini Educational Games (MEG) team, National Taiwan University of Science and Technology

🕵️Who can join: age 18 and over

🕵️Game location: online (single player)

🕵️Session time: you can tick it on the registration form. (Feel free to invite friends and family to take the challenge)

🕵️Duration: 60-70 minutes per session

🕵️Equipment: To give you the best game experience, please prepare a computer with a stable internet connection and power for the study (tablets and phones are not included, sorry!)

Participation prize: a NT$50 voucher for each player

Online puzzle-solving x realistic scenarios x gamified challenges, to improve your puzzle-solving and empathy!
