#37DaysToTheTourLeaderExam

31 Victoria Falls, Niagara Falls and Iguazu Falls are known as the world's three great waterfalls. Victoria Falls was named after the British queen by the first European explorer to see it, in the mid-19th century. In which region of the world is the waterfall located?

(A) Northern Europe (B) Southern Africa (C) Eastern Americas (D) Western Asia (2024 Overseas Tour Leader, Tourism Resources)

Explanation: This question is not hard either, but its wording still contains an error. "Niagara Falls" and "the Republic of Nicaragua" look similar in Chinese translation, but in the original language they are two completely different words with different etymologies. Also, questions about "the world's top three" or "first in the world" are the most dangerous kind. For example, Angel Falls in Venezuela is on record as the world's tallest waterfall, and Khone Falls in Laos is also huge and wide. I have put the rankings of other, less famous waterfalls in the comments for your reference. Question writers really have to be careful when consulting all sorts of information.

#1 Regarding the geography and hydrology of Taiwan's waterfalls, which of the following statements is incorrect?

(A) The area with the highest density of waterfalls in Taiwan is mainly distributed along the upper Keelung River.

(B) The waterfall with the greatest water volume in Taiwan is generally considered to be Shifen Waterfall in New Taipei City.

(C) Waterfalls are mainly formed by differential erosion of rivers.

(D) All of Taiwan's waterfalls dry up completely in winter as they enter the dry season.

The answer is (D)

#2 When a tour guide takes tourists to Wulai Waterfall, which of the following pieces of guide information is incorrect?

(A) The Wulai push-cart line was originally built during the Japanese colonial period to transport timber.

(B) "Wulai" in the Atayal language originally means hot spring.

(C) Wulai Waterfall is a typical pothole-type waterfall.

(D) Visitors can now take the only commercial aerial cable car in Taiwan across the waterfall area to reach it.

The answer is (C)
