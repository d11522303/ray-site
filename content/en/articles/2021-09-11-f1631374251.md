【Universities Exit, Students Do Not】

Taiwan's high school graduates are expected to fall below 200,000 in academic year 111 (2022), and the number is dropping by about 10,000 a year. By academic year 118 (2029) there will be fewer than 160,000 graduates. With the current 69 general universities alone providing more than 100,000 places for new students every year, and the universities of technology in the separate vocational system admitting almost as many as the universities, every student who wants to go to university will certainly find a place. Students will have more choices, but teaching work in universities will surely become harder and more complicated.

Thanks to the policy of expanding universities in the 1990s (the early 2000s), I, who only had a master's degree, was able to move from industry into a vocational college in a remote area. But because of that same mistaken policy, we will soon face a great ebbing of the education environment.

Facebook popped up reports about Taiwan Hospitality & Tourism College closing down. I have always been proud of having worked at Taiwan Hospitality & Tourism College in Hualien, where I formed lifelong bonds with many thoughtful students. I saw in the Hualien Gengsheng Daily that the last 123 students had transferred smoothly to Tzu Chi University of Science and Technology, and will complete their university studies step by step under the original curriculum and system. The transition must be full of helplessness and anxiety, but I still wish this group of students a smoother and smoother road ahead.
