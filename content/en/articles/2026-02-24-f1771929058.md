Daily Morning Reading-3 / 2026.2.24

D179

Free and Romantic Travel

Today I kept reading the article on finding travel companions in the International Journal of Tourism Research (IJTR). The last author is Professor Songshan Huang of the Western Australian business school, whose field is tourism research. Looking carefully at his publications, almost all are in SSCI Q1 and Q2 professional journals in tourism and travel, on broad topics, with statistical methods that don't show off, and research content that keeps getting more interesting. I always feel his travel research has practical value. On his Google Scholar you can see 319 journal papers published since 1994, with 12 already out in the first weeks of 2026. The most cited topic is negative issues and information in travel, followed by food destination marketing and others.

Back to this article on young Chinese people looking for travel partners online. The study also finds that "gender" is a dynamic and complex moderating factor in finding companions for travel. For partners of different genders, the most important factor is whether they are harmonious and compatible with each other. Expectations of a partner of the opposite sex start out lower, and when you find things in common while travelling together, it exceeds your expectations and builds a closer relationship. In addition, opposite-sex partners often bring complementary abilities because of their gender traits, which increases the opportunities and room for cooperation on the journey.

While writing up this passage I was reminded of the Chinese drama "Meet Yourself" (去有風的地方). Perhaps it's because I really like Liu Yifei's temperament: the love that sort-of-exists between her, having fled the city, and the local young man Li Xian, in the windy courtyard in Dali, Yunnan, is just like the faint, now-you-see-it relationship of attraction between opposite-sex travel companions, drawn to each other by their differences, adding even more charm to an already romantic journey. But can there be love between travel companions?

I think there can, but with the difficulties of real life, staying together for a lifetime bathed in love is probably not so simple!

Article source: Fang, X., Yu, J., Zhang, K., & Huang, S. (2025). Who Would You Travel With? Identifying the Key Attributes of a Desirable Travel Partner. International Journal of Tourism Research, 27(5), https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1002/jtr.70124
