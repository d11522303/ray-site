SMEA Entrepreneurs' Forum in Hualien, Tuesday 9/24 at 10:00

The Small and Medium Enterprise Administration, Ministry of Economic Affairs, is holding the Entrepreneurs' Forum <Hualien Session> at the Information Building of Hualien Senior Commercial High School on 9/24 (Tuesday) at 10:00.

This session has invited successful entrepreneurs and business leaders: Chairman Chang Ching-Lai of the Hualien Shangri-La, Chairman Hsu Chao-Lin of Ming Li Travel Service, Founder Fan Chi-Ming of Ji Zheng Ranch, and Founder Chiang Kuan-Ming of PASA Space. They will share on the theme [Leisure Tourism with a Cultural Flavour Is More Appealing]. The hosts are Chairwoman Chen Hui-Tzu of the Hualien County Tourist Hotel Association and Associate Vice President Yen Hsin-Chun of Hotung Venture Capital!

Students and teachers are both welcome to sign up. It is a rare opportunity and places are limited, so register early!

The forum provides a boxed lunch and refined refreshments, and the event is completely free!

Register online now at the Startup Incubation Learning Network (http://www.527188.com.tw/Web/RC.aspx)!
