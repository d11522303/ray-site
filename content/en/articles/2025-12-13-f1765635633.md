Before the Tour Guide and Tour Leader Exams, EP8

#2025 (ROC Year 114) Tourism Administration Tour Leader Exam Question

12. The design on Cambodia's national flag is a pride of the country's history. Which dynasty does it refer to? ① Ayutthaya Dynasty ② Toungoo Dynasty ③ Angkor Dynasty ④ Lan Na Dynasty

Solution: The temple on Cambodia's flag is Angkor Wat. This question is a free point, so let me add two slightly harder ones for you to try.

#1 In 1296, Zhou Daguan of the Yuan dynasty accompanied an envoy mission sent by the Yuan emperor Temür to the Khmer Empire to deliver an imperial edict, and stayed a full year in the city where Angkor Wat stands. After returning home, which book did he write recording his first-hand experience of the Khmer Empire's history, culture and daily life?

① A Record of Cambodia: The Land and Its People (Zhenla Fengtu Ji)

② Records of Strange Things in Funan (Funan Yiwu Zhi)

③ Travels Around the Sea (Bihai Jiyou)

④ Overall Survey of the Ocean's Shores (Yingya Shenglan)

#2 Cambodia and Thailand have been fighting again these past few days. In fact, the two countries share a World Heritage site, an ancient Hindu temple whose ownership has often caused a long-running sovereignty dispute between them and has led to border clashes many times. Which temple is it?

① Sambor Prei Kuk

② Angkor Wat

③ Preah Vihear Temple

④ The ancient city of Ayutthaya
