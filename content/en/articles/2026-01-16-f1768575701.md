Tour Guide and Tour Leader Exam Prep EP42

#TourLeaderExam57DaysToGo

2 Tokyo, Japan is a metropolis that many Taiwanese visit. Which of the following has nothing to do with the history of Tokyo's urban development? (A) It was once the seat of the Tokugawa shogunate (B) Its former name was Edo (C) In the early Meiji period the Emperor moved here from Kyoto (D) It was the main battlefield of the Satsuma Rebellion

Explanation: The Satsuma Rebellion (Seinan War) was a civil war that took place in the Kyushu region of Japan, led by Saigo Takamori's uprising, so it did not happen in Tokyo and has nothing to do with the city's development.

Travel to Japan is still the main product of Taiwan's travel industry, so it makes perfect sense that the tour leader exam includes Japanese history and geography. Having some knowledge of Japan is a key point before the exam, and this question is a super basic freebie. Let's try a few more basic questions on Japan, focusing on everyone's favourite, the yen.

#1 In July 2024 Japan officially issued new banknotes, with figures all closely tied to Japan's modern social progress. Which of the following descriptions of the people on the new banknotes is correct?

(A) 10,000-yen note: Shibusawa Eiichi, known as the "father of Japanese capitalism", founded Japan's first modern bank and contributed greatly to industrial modernisation.

(B) 5,000-yen note: Tsuda Umeko. She founded Tsuda University and was a pioneer of higher education for women in Japan; her father was the last shogun of the Edo shogunate.

(C) 1,000-yen note: Kitasato Shibasaburo. He discovered the plague bacillus and developed a serum therapy for tetanus, and his portrait replaced that of Higuchi Ichiyo.

(D) 2,000-yen note: Murasaki Shikibu. Although this denomination is little circulated, its front-side figure, Murasaki Shikibu, author of The Tale of Genji, is still the only female portrait on Japanese banknotes today.

#2 As a tour leader introducing Japanese banknotes to your group, being able to tell the stories of the buildings or sights on the back will greatly raise your professionalism. Which of the following descriptions of the designs on the back of the notes currently in circulation is incorrect?

(A) Old 10,000-yen note: the back is printed with the phoenix statue of the Phoenix Hall of Byodo-in in Uji, Kyoto, symbolising the cultural peak of the Heian period.

(B) New 10,000-yen note: the back design is the Marunouchi side station building of Tokyo Station in Tokyo, a Baroque-style building whose restoration was completed in 2012.

(C) New 1,000-yen note: the back design is "The Great Wave off Kanagawa" from "Thirty-six Views of Mount Fuji", the famous work of ukiyo-e artist Katsushika Hokusai.

(D) Old 1,000-yen note: the back design is Shureimon Gate in Okinawa.
