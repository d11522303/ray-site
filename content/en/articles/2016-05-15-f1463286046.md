It's been so hot these days, and everyone is starting to plan summer activities. If you follow international news, the hottest and most frightening topic lately is the Zika virus, for which there is no cure.

Taiwan's Ministry of Health and Welfare website also lists a number of countries under a Level 2 Alert. If you are considering any of the following countries for summer travel, women who may be pregnant should avoid going there.

Countries under the Level 2 Alert for Zika virus

1. Southeast Asia: the Philippines, Thailand, Vietnam

2. South Asia: Maldives

3. Oceania: American Samoa, Fiji, Republic of the Marshall Islands, New Caledonia, Papua New Guinea, Samoa, Solomon Islands, Tonga, Vanuatu

4. South America: Brazil, Bolivia, Colombia, Ecuador, French Guiana, Guyana, Paraguay, Peru, Suriname, Venezuela

5. Central America and the Caribbean: Barbados, Bonaire (Netherlands), Belize, Costa Rica, Cuba, Curaçao (Netherlands), Dominica, Dominican Republic, El Salvador, Grenada, Guatemala, Guadeloupe (France), Haiti, Honduras, Jamaica, Martinique (France), Mexico, Nicaragua, Panama, Puerto Rico (US), Saint Barthélemy (France), Saint Lucia, Saint Vincent and the Grenadines, Sint Maarten, Saint Martin (France), Trinidad and Tobago, US Virgin Islands, Aruba (Netherlands)
