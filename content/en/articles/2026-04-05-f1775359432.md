1995.4.5 @ Ho Chi Minh City University of Agriculture and Forestry Academic Exchange, Day 2

On the second day of an academic exchange there is usually a pre-meeting tour, and it was a really relaxing day. The Vietnamese students took us to see the history museum, the cathedral and the central post office. Riding through the streets of Ho Chi Minh City was thrilling. I hear it is much the same as now, with no traffic rules to speak of, and pedestrians, motorbikes and cars all go as they please.

After the city tour we went back to the University of Agriculture and Forestry for a small gathering with fellow university students. We sat in a classroom exchanging ideas, all interacting in not-very-fluent English. In class we watched the Vietnamese students perform local dances. Although I couldn't understand them or quite grasp them, it was still a relaxed and happy day.

As I recall, at noon that day we ate at an authentic Vietnamese pho restaurant. I remember the soup was clear and sweet, with lots of fresh greens and grilled meat. The wall hangings beside the restaurant were quite risque. It is a real pity that no photos of it remain at all, only the "I was here" photos in front of the important buildings in Ho Chi Minh City. Looking at the old photos, I search among memories from 31 years ago for my youthful days.
