HWU Department of Travel Management

The Travel Information Technology Application AR Short Video Online Competition has begun

Please help your favourite video gain popularity (links in the comments)

Competition schedule: from 8 December 2023 (ROC 112/12/08) to Wednesday 20 December (112/12/20)

Results announced: Wednesday 20 December (112/12/20), with the list of winners announced during class

Prize collection: Wednesday 20 December (112/12/20), 3:00 to 4:00 pm

Competition details:

The Travel Information Application AR Video Competition is mainly an online contest. Participating students use phones or other devices to record a 1-minute video on a signature guided-tour theme of the Department of Travel Management; sample content is in the attachment. Industry mentors will score each video on its content, supplemented by popularity scoring based on online likes. The top three places are chosen by the professional judges, and two additional popularity awards are given.

Judging criteria:

1. Video content (70%)

2. Popularity by online likes (30%)

Prizes:

Award / Number / Prize

First place / 1 / Uni-President Group voucher NT$1,000

Second place / 1 / Uni-President Group voucher NT$800

Third place / 1 / Uni-President Group voucher NT$500

Popularity award / 1-3 / Uni-President Group voucher NT$300

Note: Six students will also be selected for interviews, with an interview fee of a NT$100 voucher. For popularity awards with more than 300 likes, an additional bonus will be given according to the count, at NT$1 per like.
