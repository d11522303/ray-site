An Example of AI Applied to Airline Reservation Systems

The Department of Travel Management at Hsing Wu University has a professional travel information classroom equipped with Sabre, Amadeus, Travelport and Kewei. To train the ticketing skills of Travel Management students, we pay a considerable amount in royalties every year, but the students gain and improve even more.

Sabre recently launched a new airfare technology that uses artificial intelligence (AI) to improve how airlines price tickets. The main goal of this technology is to let airlines offer more reasonable ticket prices based on real-time market conditions and passenger demand. This change is gradually turning the traditional fixed-fare approach into a more flexible and diverse pricing model.

The system continuously observes how passengers search for tickets online and actually make bookings, and adjusts fares in real time based on this data. Unlike the past use of fixed fare classes or preset prices, the new pricing method can generate prices that better match market conditions according to changes in current demand. At the same time, the system also refers to other airlines' fares and travel demand in different seasons, bringing fares closer to reality.

Sabre is not the only company developing this kind of technology. Now many airlines and airline technology companies are working to make fare pricing smarter and more flexible. Companies including Fetcherr, PROS, FLYR and Amadeus have all begun to apply AI to airline revenue management. According to Sabre, some airlines saw their overall revenue increase by about 3% to 6% after using this AI pricing system. This is because fares can match passenger demand more accurately, reducing cases of prices that are too high or too low, so that both airlines and passengers get better results.

Overall, the way airfares are priced is changing rapidly. The fixed fare-class system used for a long time is slowly being replaced by new models based on customer feedback data and AI. This means ticket prices are no longer fixed but can be adjusted in real time as the market changes.
