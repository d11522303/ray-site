45 The traditional territory of China's Tibetan people is not all within the Tibet Autonomous Region. For example, one of the three great ancient Tibetan cultural centres is located in Kangding, in Sichuan Province's autonomous prefecture, where the "Khampa" people mainly live, with many sacred mountains such as Gongga Mountain nearby. Which of the following is this cultural centre?

(A) Lhasa (B) Yushu (C) Garze (D) Turpan

The answer is (C)

This question is not hard for those of us who were made to memorise Chinese geography as children, with a beating from the teacher thrown in. But for the new generation of students it is not easy; they may not even know there is a Kangding Road in Taipei. Kangding Road is in Taipei's Wanhua District, running from Ximending in the north to Monga Boulevard in the south, through the heart of Monga. Along it are historic sites such as the Bopiliao historic street houses, Monga Qingshui Temple and the Red House Theater in Ximen. It is commercially prosperous and has a strong old-Taipei-city atmosphere.

#1 The logic for naming Taipei's streets is based on "Chinese geography". Taking Taipei Main Station as the reference point, which of the following roads is most likely located in the southwest of Taipei? (A) Longjiang Road (B) Jilin Road (C) Guangzhou Street (D) Dihua Street

The answer is (C)

#2 As a tour guide, when passing through Taipei's Datong District you will find that many streets are named after cities in which area of China (under today's administrative divisions)?

(A) The Northeast (such as Changchun, Songjiang)

(B) The Northwest (such as Dihua, Guisui, Lanzhou)

(C) South China (such as Chaozhou, Wenzhou)

(D) Shandong Province (such as Qingdao, Jinan)

The answer is (B)
