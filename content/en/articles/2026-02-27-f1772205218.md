#14DaysToTheTourLeaderExam

43 The ancient temple city of Machu Picchu has been designated by UNESCO as a mixed World Cultural and Natural Heritage site. Which of the following statements about it is incorrect?

(A) Machu Picchu is the ruins of an Inca Empire city

(B) It is located in the mountains of southern Peru, at an altitude of more than 2,400 metres

(C) Archaeologists believe Machu Picchu was first built in the 11th century

(D) The Intihuatana stone, the Temple of the Sun and the Temple of the Three Windows are the three main well-preserved sights

The answer is C, because according to research, Machu Picchu was ordered to be built by the Inca ruler Pachacuti in the 15th century.
