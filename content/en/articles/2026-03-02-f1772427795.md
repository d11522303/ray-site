#TourLeaderExamCountdown11Days

#1 Taiwan has rich biodiversity, and many endemic animals and plants are printed on the backs of its banknotes. Regarding the pairing of the "theme creatures" on the backs of the different denominations, which of the following is incorrect?

(A) NT$500 note: Formosan sika deer

(B) NT$1,000 note: Mikado pheasant

(C) NT$2,000 note: Formosan landlocked salmon

(D) NT$100 note: Formosan black bear

The answer is D

Besides creatures, the backs of New Taiwan Dollar banknotes also include representative geographic landscapes or buildings of Taiwan. Which of the following combinations is described incorrectly?

(A) NT$200 note: the landscape on the back is "Yangmingshan"

(B) NT$1,000 note: the picture on the back is Taiwan's highest peak "Yushan"

(C) NT$500 note: the landscape on the back is "Dabajian Mountain"

(D) NT$2,000 note: the landscape on the back is "Nanhu Mountain"

The answer is A
