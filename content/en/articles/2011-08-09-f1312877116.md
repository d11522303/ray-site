To My THT Graduates

Now that you've graduated, do you ever think back on the days when you left home and came from all over the island to live on the east coast, going through the adjustment and all the ups and downs of learning? I'm sure that amid your busy lives wherever you are now, the beauty of Hualien and the easy pace of campus life still linger deep in your hearts. Besides the mountains, the fields and the starry skies, I believe you also found lifelong friends or partners here. I did too. Because you walked this road with me, my life in Hualien has been so rich and wonderful.

If you feel nostalgic and are willing to recommend us: if the people and things at Taiwan Hospitality & Tourism College still mean something to you, I hope you can help recommend to your junior schoolmates the joys of life in Hualien. On 8/28 the college will hold its independent admissions exam. You are welcome to recommend it to your younger schoolmates, relatives or friends who want to study in Hualien. Tell them this is a little taste of studying abroad, right here in Taiwan. There will be challenges, and plenty of rewards too.

You can contact me via FB or email. I can help fill out the application form, pay the registration fee and so on. Feel free to get in touch~

Teacher Ray

8/28 exam venues:

East: Taiwan Hospitality & Tourism College

North: National Taipei University of Technology

Central: National Taichung Institute of Technology

South: National Kaohsiung University of Applied Sciences
