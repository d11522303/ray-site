The 2015 (ROC year 104) Ordinary Examination for Professionals and Technicians: Tour Guides and Tour Leaders.

Registration period: from November 25, 2014 to 5:00 p.m. on December 4.

Registration website: http://register.moex.gov.tw

Exam dates:

(1) Foreign-language tour leaders and Mandarin tour leaders: Saturday, March 14, 2015.

(2) Foreign-language tour guides (Stage 1 written exam) and Mandarin tour guides: Sunday, March 15, 2015. The Stage 2 oral exam for foreign-language tour guides is tentatively scheduled for Saturday, May 23 to Sunday, May 24, 2015, in several sessions; the actual oral exam dates will be announced separately.

3. Exam venues: The exam is held in 12 exam districts: Taipei, Taoyuan, Hsinchu, Taichung, Chiayi, Tainan, Kaohsiung, Hualien, Taitung, Penghu, Kinmen and Matsu. (Detailed venues are printed on the admission ticket that will be sent to you.)

4. Registration rules:

(1) Registration period: from November 25, 2014 to 5:00 p.m. on December 4.

(2) Deadline for receipt of application documents: Applicants must send their registration forms and documents by registered mail on or before December 5, 2014 (the postmark counts) to complete registration. Late applications will not be accepted and the original documents will be returned.

(3) Mailing address: Section 1, Department of Professional and Technical Examinations, Ministry of Examination, No. 1-1 Shiyuan Road, Wenshan District, Taipei City 11602.

(4) Registration method: Registration is online only. Applicants should log in to the Ministry's website at www.moex.gov.tw and click the main or new online registration site to enter the online registration portal, or go directly to register.moex.gov.tw (main site) or register.moex2.nat.gov.tw (new site). Download and print the registration forms, then send them by registered mail to the address above together with proof of eligibility and proof of payment.
