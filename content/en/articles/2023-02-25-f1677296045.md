MSC Cruises' world cruise is expected to call at Keelung Port on Wednesday, March 15, from 09:00 to 18:00. Today, Feb 25, the Poesia is sailing across the South Pacific toward Sydney, Australia, and I really want to go to Keelung Port to take photos! The cruise class on Wednesday, March 15, periods 8 and 9, should simply move to the port's passenger terminal (the teacher is talking in his sleep).

MSC Poesia set out from Milan, Italy on Jan 5 this year on a 119-day round-the-world voyage, passing through 33 countries and 53 ports. Surely someone is wondering how much it costs. According to comments I found on Cruise Critic, it starts at US$15,750 per person for two people sharing a cabin, roughly NT$500,000, though of course the price differs by cabin type.
