In fact, strong typhoons in late October are nothing new. It is just that reporters and editors want to boost ratings quickly, so they build programmes around high-traffic videos from the internet. On such a dangerous typhoon day, many pilots who risked their lives to fly home have become internet heroes.

What the news and the high-traffic videos all show is footage of EVA, China Airlines and STARLUX pilots forced to land and take off in "roller-coaster" fashion against Typhoon Kong-rey.

Does no one really remember that 24 years ago on the very same day, 31 October, Typhoon Xangsane struck Taiwan, and Singapore Airlines flight SQ006 suffered the worst air disaster in that company's history at Taoyuan Chiang Kai-shek Airport?

Typhoon Xangsane was also a strong typhoon arriving on the last day of October. The main cause of the Taoyuan crash was human error, and the airport had little legal responsibility, but given the typhoon weather, the airport could have closed and halted takeoffs and landings, which is perhaps also the most fundamental and safest approach.
