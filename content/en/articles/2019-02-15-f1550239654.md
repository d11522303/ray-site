Only 22 days until the national tour leader and tour guide licensing exam. In the LINE group for the Hsing Wu University (HWU) Department of Travel Management prep class, a student asked a really good past exam question that contains three easily confused legal points. Please take a look.

This question can be analysed in three parts:

1. The traveller and the travel agency did not sign a contract

This is a [violation] by the travel agency itself. The Tourism Administration can fine the agency NT$10,000 to NT$50,000, but the traveller receives no money from it.

(So you can ignore this NT$10,000 in this question.)

2. The travel agency cancelled the trip more than seven days before departure because the group was too small

Because the agency cancelled on the grounds of insufficient group size, the 30% compensation to the traveller that applies when cancelling 2 to 20 days before departure (35000*0.35=10500) does not apply.

(This is where students who overthink it go wrong.)

3. The traveller had paid a NT$5,000 deposit

This part tests the third clause of [Article 249 of the Civil Code on deposits]: if the contract cannot be performed for a reason attributable to the party who received the deposit, that party must return double the deposit received. This is what the question is really testing. I never mentioned it in class, so it is quite understandable if you got it wrong.

(The NT$5,000 deposit is returned, and because the shortfall in group size is the reason of the party who received the deposit, the travel agency, a further NT$5,000 must be paid to the traveller.)
