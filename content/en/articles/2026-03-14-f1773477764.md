Tour Guide and Tour Leader Exam Companion EP101

#LiveWalkthrough (answers assisted by AI, please treat them as a reference only)

1. The country with the largest population in Southeast Asia is:

(A) the Philippines (B) Vietnam (C) Malaysia (D) Indonesia

Correct answer: (D)

Explanation: Indonesia has a population of over 270 million, the fourth largest in the world and the largest in Southeast Asia.

2. Which of the following historically belongs to the Sinosphere (Chinese character cultural sphere)?

(A) Nepal (B) Bhutan (C) Vietnam (D) Mongolia

Correct answer: (C)

Explanation: Vietnam was long influenced by Chinese culture in its history and once officially used Chinese characters (Chu Nho), so it belongs to the Sinosphere in the broad sense.

3. Between the early 15th and 17th centuries, Chinese merchants settled in Malaysia, Indonesia and other places and married local women, forming a group that blends Chinese and local cultures. What is this group usually called?

(A) New-arrival Chinese (B) Peranakan Chinese (C) Hokkien people (D) Chinese nationals

Correct answer: (B)

Explanation: This group is the famous "Baba-Nyonya" (Peranakan), also called locally born Chinese or Straits Chinese.

4. In the past the Indochinese Peninsula was called "Indochina". Which country ruled it?

(A) France (B) Britain (C) the United States (D) the Netherlands

Correct answer: (A)

Explanation: "French Indochina" included today's Vietnam, Laos and Cambodia.

5. In Chinese history, who "dismissed the hundred schools and honoured only Confucianism"?

(A) Qin Shi Huang (B) Duke Huan of Qi (C) Emperor Wu of the Western Han (D) Emperor Wen of Wei

Correct answer: (C)

Explanation: Emperor Wu of Han adopted Dong Zhongshu's advice and established Confucian thought as the official orthodoxy.

6. Which eminent Tang monk arrived in Japan in the 8th century to teach Buddhism?

(A) Jianzhen (B) Xuanzang (C) Huineng (D) Yixing

Correct answer: (A)

Explanation: The monk Jianzhen tried six times to cross the sea to Japan and finally succeeded in arriving and founding the Japanese Ritsu school.

7. India's religious architecture includes Hindu and Buddhist buildings originating on the Indian subcontinent, as well as Islamic and Christian buildings whose decorative styles came from abroad. Among the following religious buildings with their characteristics, which is most likely Islamic?

(A) A temple with figures and elephant statues decorating its roof (B) A tall tower with scriptures and geometric patterns inscribed on its outer walls (C) A cave whose inner wall niches hold half-naked statues of deities (D) A chapel with arched structures decorated with ornate sculptures

Correct answer: (B)

Explanation: Islam strictly forbids idol worship, and its architecture is mostly decorated with geometric shapes, Arabic calligraphy (scripture) and plant motifs.

8. Under which treaty with the Qing court was Hong Kong Island ceded to Britain?

(A) Treaty of Aigun (B) Treaty of Nerchinsk (C) Boxer Protocol (D) Treaty of Nanking

Correct answer: (D)

Explanation: The Treaty of Nanking was signed after the Opium War in 1842, and the Qing court ceded Hong Kong Island to Britain.

9. The National Museum of Taiwan History exhibited underpants made from flour sacks. The theme of this exhibition is most likely:

(A) Taiwanese people during World War II (B) Taiwan's agricultural specialities (C) American aid and Taiwan (D) The history of Taiwan's textile industry

Correct answer: (C)

Explanation: During the American aid period of the 1950s, Taiwan was short of supplies, and people often remade flour sacks printed with the words "Sino-American Cooperation" into clothing.

10. What religious transformation did Angkor Wat in Cambodia undergo in history?

(A) Hinduism → Buddhism (B) Buddhism → Hinduism (C) Hinduism → Islam (D) Buddhism → Islam

Correct answer: (A)

Explanation: Angkor Wat was originally a Hindu temple and gradually became a Mahayana Buddhist temple from the late 12th century.

11. In the 16th century, the Sultanate of Malacca was destroyed by which country?

(A) Britain (B) Spain (C) Japan (D) Portugal

Correct answer: (D)

Explanation: In 1511 the Portuguese commander Afonso de Albuquerque captured Malacca and destroyed the Malacca Sultanate.

12. Which country ruled the Philippine archipelago after Spain?

(A) the United States (B) Britain (C) Portugal (D) the Netherlands

Correct answer: (A)

Explanation: After the Spanish-American War of 1898, the Philippines came under American rule.

13. The ancient Olympic Games were one of the many festivals of the gods in ancient Greece and the most renowned of them, and they also carried a meaning of peace: during the Games the Greek city-states stopped fighting to show reverence for the gods. However, they were once suspended for more than 15 centuries. What was the historical context of the suspension?

(A) After the rise of the Macedonian Empire, it unified the Greek peninsula by force (B) The Roman Empire's eastward campaigns conquered the Antigonid dynasty of Macedon (C) A Roman emperor made Christianity the only favoured religion and harshly suppressed pagan activities (D) The Islamic Ottoman Empire ruled the Balkan peninsula

Correct answer: (C)

Explanation: The Roman emperor Theodosius I declared Christianity the state religion in 393 CE and banned "pagan" festivals, which led to the suspension of the Olympics.

14. In 1940, to show support for the nation's wartime policy, the Japanese baseball league made a special visit to a certain place to hold exhibition games. This was the first time Japanese professional baseball played regular-season games outside Japan proper. Which of the following regions was the place?

(A) Hawaii (B) Manchukuo (C) the Philippines (D) Vladivostok

Correct answer: (B)

Explanation: In 1940, to celebrate the "2600th year of the Imperial era", Japanese professional baseball went to Manchukuo, which Japan was backing at the time, for overseas games.

15. The ancient town of Hoi An in Vietnam is famous for preserving traditional streetscapes from the 15th to early 19th centuries. What is the main reason it has been preserved intact?

(A) The city has never been influenced by foreign merchants (B) The Hoi An area was not damaged at all in the Vietnam War (C) The Hue dynasty designated Hoi An as a main trading port and restricted large-scale rebuilding (D) After the 19th century the harbour silted up and the economy stagnated, so there was no large-scale development

Correct answer: (D)

Explanation: Because silt blocked the harbour and it lost its place as an international trading centre, the economic centre shifted to Da Nang, sparing Hoi An the effects of modern urban development.

16. Among the events of Qing-era Taiwan in which foreigners invaded by force, which was the earliest and most famous?

(A) Wushe Incident (B) Sino-French War (C) Mudan Incident (D) Xilai Temple Incident

Correct answer: (C)

Explanation: The Mudan Incident took place in 1874 and was the landmark event of the first large-scale foreign (Japanese) armed invasion of Taiwan in the Qing era.

17. Which of the following was NOT a policy implemented against the ethnic Chinese under Suharto's New Order in Indonesia?

(A) Restricting ethnic Chinese from doing business (B) Banning public celebration of Lunar New Year (C) Forcing ethnic Chinese to change to Indonesian names (D) Banning the publication of Chinese-language newspapers

Correct answer: (A)

Explanation: Although the Suharto regime suppressed Chinese culture and politics (such as banning Chinese, forcing name changes and banning New Year celebrations), economically it relied on and nurtured certain Chinese business enterprises.

18. Hanoi's Thang Long Imperial Citadel in Vietnam was built in the 11th century under the Ly dynasty. The citadel was not built from flat ground but expanded on the original site of a fortress of which country?

(A) India (B) China (C) Cambodia (D) Korea

Correct answer: (B)

Explanation: The Thang Long Imperial Citadel was built on the site of the "Dai La Citadel" established in the Tang dynasty (a fortress from the period of Chinese rule).

19. Which of the following historical facts best shows the rise of regional powers in the late Qing dynasty?

(A) The Triple Intervention (B) The Hundred Days' Reform (C) The Self-Strengthening Movement (D) The Mutual Protection of Southeast China

Correct answer: (D)

Explanation: During the Boxer Rebellion, the governors-general and governors of the southern provinces refused to carry out the Qing court's secret edict declaring war on foreign powers and reached agreements with the foreign countries, showing that regional authority could already counter the central government.

20. The NHK taiga drama broadcast in 2025, "Berabō ~A Tale of Myriad Splendours Like a Dream~", mentions the early Yoshiwara pleasure quarter. When and where did this pleasure quarter appear?

(A) Kamakura in the Kamakura shogunate period (B) Osaka in the Azuchi-Momoyama period (C) Edo in the Tokugawa shogunate period (D) Yokohama in the Meiji Restoration period

Correct answer: (C)

Explanation: The Yoshiwara pleasure quarter was the licensed red-light district set up by the Tokugawa shogunate in Edo (today's Tokyo).

21. In the early 19th century the Americans actively expanded their territory westward. Which of the following historical events nearly doubled the territory of the United States?

(A) Purchasing the French territory of Louisiana (B) Spain ceding Spanish Florida to the United States (C) The Missouri Compromise settling the question of slavery in new states (D) Annexing the newly independent Republic of Texas

Correct answer: (A)

Explanation: The Louisiana Purchase of 1803 acquired a vast territory at a very low price, nearly doubling the size of the United States.

22. In the 18th century the Dutch East India Company brought Western cultural influence to Japan, known as "Rangaku" (Dutch learning). Which city was the window for "Rangaku"?

(A) Edo (B) Osaka (C) Kobe (D) Nagasaki

Correct answer: (D)

Explanation: During the period of national isolation, Japan opened only Dejima in Nagasaki for trade with Dutch ships, making it the window for absorbing Western learning.

23. Which scholar published the famous short essay "Datsu-A Ron" (Leaving Asia), actively advocating that Japan after the Meiji Restoration abandon Sinocentric ideas and the spirit of Confucianism and learn from and absorb Western civilisation?

(A) Sakamoto Ryoma (B) Fukuzawa Yukichi (C) Saigo Takamori (D) Ito Hirobumi

Correct answer: (B)

Explanation: Fukuzawa Yukichi published "Datsu-A Ron" in 1885. He is regarded as the intellectual mentor of the Meiji Restoration and is the figure on the 10,000-yen note.

24. Ha Long Bay in Vietnam is known as the "Guilin on the water". What natural landscape is it famous for?

(A) Volcano clusters (B) Limestone karst landforms (C) Desert oases (D) Glacial lakes

Correct answer: (B)

Explanation: Like Guilin in China, Ha Long Bay is a well-developed limestone karst landscape.

25. A sea-crossing bridge in China completed in 2018 links two Special Administrative Regions and one Special Economic Zone of China. After it was built, travel between the three places takes only tens of minutes, greatly shortening the distance and strengthening economic and social exchange among them. At the mouth of which river is this bridge most likely located?

(A) the Pearl River (B) the Yangtze River (C) the Hai River (D) the Yellow River

Correct answer: (A)

Explanation: This bridge is the "Hong Kong-Zhuhai-Macao Bridge", linking Hong Kong, Macao and Zhuhai, located at the Pearl River estuary (the Greater Bay Area).

26. The Japanese imperial family's spring cherry blossom viewing tradition, "hanami", spread to the common people, and every spring cherry blossom festivals are held everywhere. Judging by the cherry blossom front dates published by the Japan Meteorological Agency, in which of the four main islands does the cherry blossom season start earliest?

(A) Hokkaido (B) Honshu (C) Kyushu (D) Shikoku

Correct answer: (C)

Explanation: Cherry blossoms open from south to north. Kyushu is the southernmost, where temperatures rise earliest, so its blossom season starts earliest.

27. The tajine is a traditional dish of a certain region of the world, and also the name of a kitchen utensil. Its conical ceramic cooking pot is designed to slow-cook food with a minimum of water, which fits the local environment well. Which of the following is the region most likely?

(A) East Asia (B) South America (C) Western Europe (D) North Africa

Correct answer: (D)

Explanation: The tajine comes from the arid regions of North Africa such as Morocco, and was originally designed to save water in extremely water-scarce environments.

28. The "Date palm, knowledge, skills, traditions and practices", listed as Intangible Cultural Heritage, was jointly nominated by more than ten West Asian and North African countries. Which of the following groups of countries are all among the nominating countries?

(A) Jordan, Egypt, Iraq (B) Kuwait, Turkey, Ethiopia (C) Yemen, Nigeria, Pakistan (D) Saudi Arabia, Afghanistan, Guinea

Correct answer: (A)

Explanation: This was a joint nomination by Arab countries, and Jordan, Egypt and Iraq are all among them. Turkey, Ethiopia and others outside the core Arab cultural sphere were not lead applicants.

29. Which of the following is the modern-style building in Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia with the Islamic eight-pointed star characteristic?

(A) Kuala Lumpur Tower (B) Petronas Twin Towers (C) Merdeka 118 (D) Sultan Abdul Samad Building

Correct answer: (B)

Explanation: The floor plan of the Petronas Towers is based on two overlapping squares, forming the traditional Islamic eight-pointed star.

30. India's rich cultural landscape attracts many visitors. During which of the following holidays is the weather in India driest and most comfortable and pleasant?

(A) Tomb-Sweeping Day (B) Dragon Boat Festival (C) Mid-Autumn Festival (D) Christmas

Correct answer: (D)

Explanation: India has mainly a tropical monsoon climate, and from November to February of the following year (the Christmas period) is the cool season, with a dry and stable climate.

31. The world is divided into 24 standard time zones (15 degrees of longitude per zone). By this theory, Singapore should be at UTC+09:00, but the country now uses the legal time zone UTC+08:00. This is related to a time zone adjustment made by which country or region?

(A) Indonesia (B) Thailand (C) Hong Kong (D) Malaysia

Correct answer: (D)

Explanation: To stay in sync with its important economic and trade partner Malaysia, Singapore followed Malaysia in unifying its time zone to UTC+08:00 in 1982.

32. If you want to ride an ox cart out to sea to work the fields, experiencing the fun of treading the waves to see oyster fields, feeling for clams and dancing with crabs, which of the following places is best for a tour?

(A) Wanggong Fishing Port in Fangyuan, Changhua (B) Magong Marine Ranch in Penghu (C) Waisanding Sandbar in Dongshi, Chiayi (D) Tainan's Sicao Green Tunnel

Correct answer: (A)

Explanation: The "sea ox culture" of Fangyuan, Changhua is a highly distinctive fishing village landscape in Taiwan and has been listed as intangible cultural heritage.

33. The old town of Luang Prabang in Laos was inscribed on the World Heritage list for what cultural heritage?

(A) Ancient royal palaces (B) Colonial architecture (C) War sites (D) Cave systems

Correct answer: (B)

Explanation: Luang Prabang is famous for its well-preserved French colonial architecture, which blends perfectly with traditional Lao style.

34. The cave dwellings (yaodong) of "people half-dig into the mountainside to live, while carts and horses mostly pass overhead" are sturdy and durable, warm in winter and cool in summer, windproof and soundproof, and quake-resistant. In which of the following regions are China's cave dwellings mainly found?

(A) the Mongolian Plateau (B) the Yunnan-Guizhou Plateau (C) the Loess Plateau (D) the Qinghai-Tibet Plateau

Correct answer: (C)

Explanation: The Loess Plateau has thick soil layers with vertical jointing, which makes it suitable for digging cave dwellings.

35. Hawaii's Volcanoes National Park is mainly located on which island?

(A) Oahu (B) Kauai (C) Molokai (D) the Big Island of Hawaii

Correct answer: (D)

Explanation: Hawaii Volcanoes National Park is located on the island of Hawaii (the Big Island) and contains active volcanoes such as Kilauea.

36. The river on which China's Huangguoshu Waterfall lies is:

(A) the Dabang River (B) the Yangtze River (C) the Yellow River (D) the Lancang River

Correct answer: (A)

Explanation: Huangguoshu Waterfall is in Zhenning Buyei and Miao Autonomous County, Guizhou Province, on the Baishui River section of the Dabang River, a tributary of the Xi River in the Pearl River system.

37. The Huanglong scenic area in northern Sichuan Province, China has more than 3,400 travertine coloured pools formed by calcium deposits accumulating in the flowing water, surrounded by dense primeval forest. The emerald travertine pools and golden shoals look like a giant yellow dragon lying in the dense forest. What type of landform are the travertine coloured pools of Huanglong?

(A) Aeolian landform (B) Karst landform (C) Volcanic landform (D) Glacial landform

Correct answer: (B)

Explanation: Travertine landscapes are a special expression of karst landforms.

38. When travelling to Mexico's Yucatan Peninsula, which of the following ancient civilisations' World Heritage sites are you most likely to visit?

(A) Aztec (B) Inca (C) Maya (D) Swahili

Correct answer: (C)

Explanation: The Yucatan Peninsula is the heartland of Maya civilisation, and the famous Chichen Itza is located here.

39. The main immigrants of the tea mountain of Mae Salong in Thailand are:

(A) Cantonese (B) Yunnanese (C) Hakka (D) Hainanese

Correct answer: (B)

Explanation: Mae Salong is a famous settlement in northern Thailand of descendants of the "lost army" (remnants of the Kuomintang), whose ancestors mostly came from Yunnan.

40. The famous Banaue Rice Terraces in the Cordillera mountains of Luzon, the Philippines, were mainly carved by which indigenous people?

(A) the Ifugao (B) the Tagalog (C) the Ivatan (D) the Mangyan

Correct answer: (A)

Explanation: The Banaue Rice Terraces were carved by hand by the Ifugao people and are known as the "Eighth Wonder of the World".

41. What was the original name of "Ho Chi Minh City" in Vietnam?

(A) Hue (B) Da Nang (C) Hanoi (D) Saigon

Correct answer: (D)

Explanation: After Vietnam's reunification in 1975, the former city of Saigon was renamed Ho Chi Minh City to commemorate the founding leader.

42. The Caucasus Mountains were formed by the interaction of which two plates?

(A) Convergence between the Eurasian Plate and the Arabian Plate (B) Divergence between the Eurasian Plate and the African Plate (C) Convergence between the Eurasian Plate and the African Plate (D) Subduction between the Eurasian Plate and the African Plate

Correct answer: (A)

Explanation: The Caucasus was formed by the collision and convergence of the Eurasian Plate to the north and the Arabian Plate to the south.

43. The largest "Chinatown" in the United States is in San Francisco, which is related to the fact that in the 19th century many Chinese entered the US through which port?

(A) Angel Island (B) Alcatraz Island (C) Ellis Island (D) Catalina Island

Correct answer: (A)

Explanation: Angel Island was the main immigration processing centre on the West Coast (1910-1940) and is called the "Ellis Island of the West Coast".

44. Settlements in southern China often have arcades (qilou) or stilt houses. Which of the following climate characteristics is most closely related to this architectural style?

(A) High annual average temperature (B) Heavy rain and humidity (C) Variable wind direction (D) Long hours of sunshine

Correct answer: (B)

Explanation: Stilt houses protect against damp and flooding, and arcades (wujiaoji) provide shade and shelter from rain.

45. The ancient town of Hoi An in Vietnam is famous for what architectural style?

(A) Modernist (B) Purely European (C) A mix of Chinese, Japanese and French (D) Khmer

Correct answer: (C)

Explanation: Hoi An was once a prosperous trading port that blends Chinese assembly halls, the Japanese Bridge and French colonial-era architectural styles.

46. Under which law are Taiwan's geoparks established?

(A) National Park Act (B) Cultural Heritage Preservation Act (C) Wildlife Conservation Act (D) Forestry Act

Correct answer: (B)

Explanation: The Cultural Heritage Preservation Act was amended in 2016 to include geoparks within the scope of preserving "natural landscapes".

47. Regarding the travel advisory levels issued by our Ministry of Foreign Affairs, which of the following is correct?

(A) Level 1: grey, reminder (B) Level 2: green, caution (C) Level 3: red, leave as soon as possible (D) Level 4: black, request protection

Correct answer: (A)

Explanation: Travel advisories are divided into four levels: grey (reminder), yellow (special attention), orange (avoid non-essential travel), and red (not advisable to go, leave as soon as possible).

48. Regarding Visitor Experience and Resource Protection (VERP), which of the following is wrong?

(A) It defines visitor experience and park resource management goals (B) It determines safety engineering standards and activity areas for recreational facilities (C) It applies an impact management plan after visitor use through repeated monitoring and evaluation (D) It sets decisions on limits to visitor use or changes in development

Correct answer: (B)

Explanation: The core of VERP is managing the impact of "people" on "resources", not making decisions about "safety engineering standards".

49. Regarding famous tourist destinations and their countries, which of the following pairings is wrong?

(A) Pattaya - Thailand (B) Boracay - the Philippines (C) Bali (Pulau Bali) - Indonesia (D) Ko Samui - Malaysia

Correct answer: (D)

Explanation: Ko Samui is in the Gulf of Thailand and belongs to Thailand.

50. China's Qinghai Province established the Hoh Xil (Kekexili) reserve mainly to protect which species?

(A) Tibetan antelope (B) Red panda (C) Golden snub-nosed monkey (D) Red-crowned crane

Correct answer: (A)

Explanation: Hoh Xil is the main habitat and breeding ground of the Tibetan antelope and is vital to protecting this endangered species.
