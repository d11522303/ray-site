title: "Doing Little, But Doing My Best: New Taipei Local Revitalisation"
excerpt: "Ray reflects on a NT$160,000 New Taipei local revitalisation project in late 2025, the friends he made, and bringing over a hundred students to Jingtong."
published: true
---

In the second half of 2025, by chance I came into contact with the New Taipei local revitalisation programme. The NT$160,000 budget was used up within a few months, and a lot of things were only just started, superficial and done with little confidence. I did not know what I could do for the locality, but having taken government funds, one must complete the work legally, properly and clearly. After the heavy administrative work, even I could not feel any clear gain, and my role was neither a local youth, middle-aged person nor elder. Writing this, I feel very hollow, very hollow.

Looking at it from another angle, because of the revitalisation project I also met a group of warm and down-to-earth implementers and local friends, and through knowing them I came into contact with many outsiders, but very, very few things that could actually take root came and went. Because of this, I also put down my rosy expectations of local revitalisation and treated it as a chance for mutual growth and acquaintance, drawing a thin thread out of relationships that did not exist before. Perhaps when the opportunity comes, there will still be things the university's resources can do. And through the environmental project I led more than a hundred young students to Jingtong, at the other end of County Road 106 in New Taipei City, so that they could set foot in a small town that is not far away but had never been in their minds, learn a little about the precious tourism resources of their hometown, and about the people and things, such as mining and railways, that already exist yet are nearly forgotten.

I know I did little, but at least I did my best.
