2025.3.5

Southeast Travel vs. HWU Travel Management

In Japan, spring is the job-hunting season. The Department of Travel Management at Hsing Wu University (HWU) got ahead of the game in finding futures for its students. In Linkou, as the cherry blossoms bloom, our students are already drawing their bows for their senior-year internships. Today we invited Associate Vice President Kao and Deputy Manager Li of Southeast Travel, the leading specialists in Japan inbound and outbound group tours in Taiwan, to introduce to our students what a professional and well-paid travel career looks like.

Q: A year after graduating from school, how much do you hope to earn per month in the travel industry?

A: The slides showed alumna Fang-Hsiu Lin of the Department of Travel Management. She has just passed one year at Southeast Travel and was just named Southeast's Best Newcomer of 2024 in the sales department. At her highest she has earned NT$69K a month plus bonuses. In a travel workplace that combines income, a sense of achievement and a shared atmosphere, she is very satisfied with her choice of major and career.

In the misty Linkou classroom, the department's cruise classroom was packed with nearly a hundred students, all passionate about the travel industry. Southeast Travel's dashing Northern Region Sales Division Head, Associate Vice President Kao, gave an encouraging talk on career development opportunities in the travel business, giving the whole audience new expectations for their futures after graduation.

The HWU Department of Travel Management and Southeast Travel have had an internship partnership for more than a decade. Guided by industry needs over the long term, we train travel professionals who are useful to the industry and committed to their work. We also thank the company for providing a sustainable working environment for its dedicated employees. Deputy Manager Li, a veteran HR manager, often communicates one by one with our advising teachers about how suitable each student's placement is. Only such a careful process can achieve an educational link in which students, the company and the school all prosper together.
