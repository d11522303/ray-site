2025.2.26

The Bucket Lists

If you had only half a year left to live, what would truly be worth cherishing?

The protagonist of the film keeps reminding us that, as human beings, having people who love you and whom you love should be the most important thing.

In recent years I have discovered that travel is my way of escaping unhappiness in life. Of course, wanting to see the world is a native desire, but wanting to share the joy of seeing different worlds with other people is an even deeper longing of life. However many places I have not travelled to, none compares to the loneliness of being alone with nobody who cares.

Gaining recognition from others and then turning it into self-recognition: I seem to be like that too, I just don't want to admit it, because I always feel I have too many regrets and have made too many sacrifices, and have lived with too little of myself.

So I still envy those who have money and leisure to travel around the world, who can see the images they saw online appear in real life in front of them, and smell, hear and feel the five senses of that moment, which must be such an anticipated life experience.
