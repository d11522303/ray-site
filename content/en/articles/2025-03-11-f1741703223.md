2025.3.11

Fourteen years ago, I was still working in Hualien. While I was at work at the school one afternoon, we were suddenly notified that work and classes were suspended across the whole county, because a huge earthquake had struck Japan and a tsunami warning had been issued.

I put down my work at the school, got in the car and drove straight to the Dong Hwa University affiliated kindergarten to pick up our little one, and then went back to our home in the Zhixue Five Hundred Households neighbourhood. That was a time when the three of us relied on one another in Hualien. A private-school teaching job let us live a simple life, almost cut off from the world, next to farmland. Between sunrises and sunsets our child slowly grew up, and the two of us, without noticing, passed the golden years of our thirties and forties.

Eight years as a rural teacher in Hualien, living apart from the world, gave us a rare calm in life. Of course, money was very tight, but we raised our child step by step, always within our control. Perhaps it was a pity that I did not rush into a doctoral programme in Hualien back then, but isn't living attentively with my family also a kind of dedication and care in life?

Fourteen years have passed in the blink of an eye, and it turns out life is that short. If I really want to do a doctorate, I should choose one I truly like, and face whatever difficulties come as challenges, not as a helpless situation I could not refuse.
