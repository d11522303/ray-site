Goodbye 2016~

Wishing all my family, teachers, friends, classmates and students a Happy New Year!

They say that if a Facebook post says "Happy New Year", it will set off fireworks!

Let's welcome a wonderful and happy 2017 with greetings from across Asia~

Japan: あけましておめでとう (thanks to Hank Li for this one, let's welcome the new year together)

Korea: 새해 복 많이 받으세요 (Corinna Pai 黃茱蒂, let's enjoy 2017 together)

Philippines: Happy New Year

Vietnam: Chúc mừng năm mới

Cambodia: រីករាយឆ្នាំថ្មី

Thailand: สวัสดีปีใหม่

Malaysia: Selamat Tahun Baru

Indonesia: Selamat Tahun Baru

Nepal: नयाँ वर्षको शुभकामना

Java: seneng taun anyar

Laos: ສະບາຍດີປີໃຫມ່

Myanmar: ပျြောရှငျတဲ့နှစ်သစ်

Sri Lanka: සුභ නව වසරක් වේවා

India: नया साल मुबारक हो

Mongolia: Шинэ жилийн мэнд хүргэе

May all my family, friends, colleagues, classmates and students have a Happy, Prosperous, and Productive 2017.

There will be firework if you or me post " Happy new year" on the Facebook, let us make a global blessing for our fruitful coming year 2017~~
