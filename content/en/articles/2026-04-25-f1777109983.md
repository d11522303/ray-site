25 April 2026

Day 239

Internship visit

To catch the students and their supervisor for a chat before Janfusun Fancyworld opened, I entered the Wild West area through the staff entrance before business hours, amid the morning light and the park's music. Only when there were no visitors was there time, before the shop opened, to talk with the store manager and the students about nearly a full academic year of learning.

The manager said that the staff of her little Wild West burger shop are like a United Nations, with employees from Myanmar, Thailand, Vietnam, the Philippines, Indonesia, Cambodia and elsewhere, and that Taiwanese interns are mostly students living in Guanxi or Longtan, otherwise there would be no one at all to help with sales and shop operations, and the students are the coordinators and managers she relies on most.

Because of the falling birth rate, Taiwan's service industry really has difficulty finding service staff of working age, but tourism demand and supply are still there. When I left the park at 10:30, I saw many adults, children and tour groups queuing at the gate to enter, and Janfusun puts great effort into managing and maintaining the amusement park and its complex animal areas. In the same Hsinchu region of Taiwan, the huge economic gap between the tourism industry and the electronics industry is unsettling.

I have no answer to that unease, but through this internship visit I want to express my admiration for the manager, who has served at Janfusun for 36 years, and for the staff from many countries devoted to service. Their job is to bring everyone joy, and the content of their work is to give visitors who come here real interaction and smiles. Tourism service work deserves even more respect, because we treat travellers with smiles in every setting, hoping to become the providers of wonderful memories for every visitor. Isn't that a beautiful job!
