Tour Guide and Tour Leader Exam Prep, Episode 7

#2025TourLeaderExamQuestionsTourismAdministration

1 In the late fifteenth century, with the unfolding of the "Age of Discovery", European countries began to explore new trade routes and overseas territories. In 1498, which country did Vasco da Gama first reach by sea? ① India ② Japan ③ China ④ Malaysia

Explanation: When you don't know who Vasco da Gama was, think about which country was the first to set sail in the Age of Discovery. And once you consider the order of the sailing routes, you can probably get the answer to this question right away. It is the country with the word "port" in its name: Portugal.

The Portuguese flag is mainly green and red, with the national coat of arms where the two meet, including an armillary sphere symbolising the Age of Discovery and a shield representing the royal family (five small shields and seven castles). Also, the European powers connected with Taiwan, and the harbours they occupied, may all become exam questions, so today's two questions are on Portugal, the country that named the island of Taiwan "Formosa", for practice.

1 In the Age of Discovery, Portugal established one of its earliest colonial strongholds in Asia on the west coast of India, using it as the core base for maritime trade, missionary work and military operations. Today the site still preserves many Portuguese-style churches, convents and colonial districts, and is listed as a World Cultural Heritage site. This site is:

① Victoria Terminus, Mumbai

② Churches and Convents of Goa

③ Ajanta Caves

④ Jaipur City

2 In the Age of Discovery, the first European power to establish a base in Taiwan built a fortress on the coast of present-day Tainan as a hub of its Asian trade network. This country also left colonial buildings and fortress ruins at Malacca, another World Heritage site in Asia. Which European country is it?

① Portugal

② The Netherlands

③ Spain

④ Britain
