2025.7.4

One day before the end of the world, I took part in a very rewarding training course and exam!

Because of the needs of teaching and course training, in the first half of the year I took students to many tour itinerary competitions outside school. Watching my students' excellent performance and eager effort, I often felt that I knew too little and understood too shallowly! Only by personally experiencing a nerve-racking on-site practical exam could I appreciate how tense students are at an on-site itinerary competition, and could I put myself in their place to think about how to offer more appropriate and efficient guidance in teaching in future!

In the two-day itinerary workshop, Chairman Chung (Teacher Arnold) taught two full days of rich content. By systematically linking attractions across Taiwan, together with a highly creative approach to itinerary design, he was truly a model for me, sitting in the audience, to learn from, and it also made me think more about how to improve and develop my teaching design, and about the room for implementing local practice-based research.

The campus design of Lunghwa University of Science and Technology is perfectly combined with the way its logo is presented: refined and simple! The campus buildings and steps built along the hillside are no problem going up, but coming down, my knees really could hardly take it.
