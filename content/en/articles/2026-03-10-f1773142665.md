㊗️ Heartfelt Congratulations ㊗️

#HsingWuTravelManagementClassOf2017Graduate

#白佳文 (Bai Jia-Wen), our senior alumna, passed the exam to become a Taipei Metro station attendant. She has now completed training and works at Sanchong Station. When she was a student she was the joy of the department office, and also the most diligent and lively student. After graduating she worked at the Wulai Yunxian theme park of the Want Want China Times Group, and recently, through self-improvement and hard work, she passed the exam for Taipei Metro.

Right now, Taipei Metro and Taipei Metro Recreation are jointly recruiting talent from all walks of life, with online registration open from #March10 at 14:00 until March 24 at 15:00. This joint recruitment covers a wide range of job categories, with more than 200 openings in total, and also expands the "cross-field training" track to 12 categories with 64 places, where the company trains people throughout to become professionally capable. For the recruitment brochure and registration information, please check the recruitment systems of Taipei Metro or Taipei Metro Recreation.

The highest monthly salary for this round of openings is NT$62,500, and with bonuses the annual pay can reach up to 16.4 months. Employees also enjoy a wide range of family activities and 20 clubs, singles' mixers and a group wedding held every year, and the exclusive "Taipei Metro Childcare Centre" childcare service.
