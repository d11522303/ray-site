The Mutianyu Great Wall in a Rainstorm ⛈️

I booked this Great Wall day tour on KKday, and I only did so because the weather forecast promised good weather. Man proposes, heaven disposes!

When we gathered at 6:50 in the morning the streets were already flooded, and the coach couldn't even pull in. It went around picking up drowned rats from all over the world! Along the way trees were down and roads were under water, and the expressway speed limit had been cut to 60 km/h. I comforted my family that at least we were sitting in the car, cruising the "river" and touring the capital! We could also practise our English listening comprehension with the guide's commentary!

At 9:30 we reached the foot of the mountain in Huairou District, where the Wall is. The rain was still falling! The scenic area's management called to say there was thunder and the cable cars had stopped running. We all waited on the coach for the rain to ease while listening to the guide explain things, and eventually even the guide said that tickets could be refunded, and so could the cable car fares! (I have to praise this local operator, MUBUS, for their professional and thoughtful service. Not only was the young woman guide's English excellent, she thought of our safety at every turn and even arranged seats for guests to rest on.)

A little after ten the rain suddenly eased and the sky gradually cleared. The guide let us get off and suggested we walk up. After a challenging climb of more than a thousand steps, we reached Beacon Tower No. 10 of the Great Wall dripping with sweat, and there to greet us was a beautiful Mutianyu Great Wall with the clouds parted and the mist gone!

I won't say any more about what came after! When the tour ended, the heavy rain started again, the roads were all flooded and the traffic was gridlocked. It even made the news in Taiwan!
