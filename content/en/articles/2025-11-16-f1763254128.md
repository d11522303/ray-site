【Giving Betel Leaf Its Proper Name, Reviving an Industry】

Betel leaf (laoye) is the dark green leaf in the "wrapped betel nut" commonly seen by the roadside. On 30 October 2025, the Taiwan Food and Drug Administration of the Ministry of Health and Welfare sent an official notice to the Agricultural Research Institute and local health bureaus, announcing that dried leaves of the betel vine (Piper betle L.), the betel leaf, may be used as a spice to give foods a special aroma or flavour.

The most crucial driver of this regulation is Professor Chang Yu-chuan of National Taitung University, who was my colleague at Taiwan Hospitality & Tourism College (THT) in Hualien. Having seen his years of effort locally in Taitung, pouring all his energy and funds into research, courses and students, I am delighted that he has finally achieved a breakthrough in the most fundamental regulatory system.

Let me first talk about something from many years ago. When Professor Chang was about to leave Taiwan Hospitality & Tourism College in Hualien for National Taitung University, he didn't just pack up and go. He was concerned about his tourism students, and went from course to course finding suitable teachers to teach them, precisely to fill the gaps in the students' learning after his departure. I was fortunate to be assigned by Professor Chang as one of the successor teachers, which let me get to know many students through the courses, a relationship that continues to this day as a lifelong teacher-student bond. This way of thinking and doing, valuing the teacher-student relationship and the teaching mission, was very inspiring for me, who had only just become a teacher at the time.

I hope everyone will click in and read the reports on Professor Chang Yu-chuan's research and get to know afresh a plant that can be seen all around us. Because of this report I also learned that the betel leaf industry once generated several billion NT dollars in output value in Taitung and supported tens of thousands of local workers. Twenty years ago it even surpassed sugar apples and was Taitung's most important crop. Now, as an ageing society arrives, every local resident still needs a source of income and a focus in life, and every kind of primary industry needs a lot of repetitive manual labour, which makes it just right as small-scale economic activity and a centre of working life for older people. These research results and legal improvements have a very important influence on social stability and diverse development among certain groups.

As his former colleague, watching his research, apart from telling him to drink less and pay more attention to his health, I am truly happy for him. I look forward to the research team's efforts prompting the government to open up regulations further for various uses of the original betel leaf, so that local people can develop more food ingredients and reprocessed products, and we see more uses of it on the dining table and in everyday goods.
