Tour Guide & Tour Leader Exam Prep EP81

#TourLeaderExam18DaysToGo

49 The buildings on the central axis of the Forbidden City in Beijing include ① the Meridian Gate ② the Hall of Preserving Harmony ③ the Hall of Central Harmony ④ the Hall of Supreme Harmony. Please arrange them in order from north to south.

For this question you first need to fix north and south. Most people know that the entrance of the Forbidden City in Beijing is the Meridian Gate, but which direction is the Meridian Gate in?

Because the Meridian Gate is the main gate of the Forbidden City, located at the southernmost end of the whole complex, in the ordering it goes last in the answer. Next, look at the importance of the halls. The Hall of Supreme Harmony symbolises imperial power and has the highest status, so it is at the front. The Hall of Central Harmony is the emperor's rest room, in the middle. The Hall of Preserving Harmony was used for banquets or exams, and is at the back.

#1 The Great Wall was inscribed as a World Heritage Site in 1987. When giving a tour, which pass, regarded as the most complete pass complex in the Great Wall's defensive system, is known as "the First Pass Under Heaven" and located near the eastern starting point of the Great Wall?

(A) Juyongguan

(B) Yanmenguan

(C) Jiayuguan

(D) Shanhaiguan

The answer is (D)

#2 The Qin Shi Huang mausoleum has not only the Terracotta Army; its overall design reflects the idea of "serving the dead as one serves the living". Regarding the structure of the imperial tomb, which of the following statements is incorrect?

(A) The Qin Shi Huang mausoleum consists of an inner city and an outer city, in the shape of a trapezoid that is wide in the north and narrow in the south.

(B) The terracotta army pits are "accompanying burial pits" of the mausoleum, located about 1.5 kilometres outside the east gate of the outer city.

(C) According to the *Records of the Grand Historian*, the underground palace of the Qin mausoleum used mercury to simulate rivers and seas, and had decorations "with the heavens above and the earth below".

(D) The Chinese government has now fully excavated the core underground palace of the Qin Shi Huang mausoleum and opened it to the public.

The answer is (D)
