Tour Guide and Tour Leader Exam Prep, EP30

#TourLeaderExamCountdown69Days

5 Regarding the Zheng dynasty's governance of Taiwan, which of the following statements is incorrect?

① Established Anping Prefecture and Tianxing and Wannian Counties

② Implemented the "soldiers in farming" military-farming policy

③ Zheng Jing renamed the Eastern Capital Mingjing as Dongning

④ Built a Confucian temple and established schools

Explanation:

In 1661 Zheng Chenggong (Koxinga) led his troops to attack Taiwan, and after defeating the Dutch in 1662 he officially ruled Taiwan. His administrative system imitated the Ming model. What was established in the 16th year of Yongli (1662) was Chengtian Prefecture, with Tianxing County and Wannian County under it, as the highest administrative body on the island. Anping was only a military and trade area (around Fort Zeelandia) and was never made a prefecture-level administrative district, so this option is incorrect.

To solve the food problem of long-term troop stationing and to consolidate Taiwan's defence, the Ming-Zheng regime promoted the "soldiers in farming" policy from 1662, ordering soldiers to fight in wartime and to cultivate land in peacetime. This system not only stabilised military provisions but also accelerated Han Chinese development of the western plains of Taiwan.

In 1662, a year after Zheng Chenggong came to Taiwan, he suddenly died, and his son Zheng Jing succeeded him. During his rule (1662-1681), he renamed the "Eastern Capital Mingjing", which carried a strong symbolism of opposing the Qing and restoring the Ming, as "Dongning". This on the one hand maintained the regime's legitimacy, and on the other reflected the regime's shift from a "temporary base" towards more stable local rule.

To strengthen its cultural and political legitimacy, the Zheng regime actively promoted Confucian education. In 1665 (during Zheng Jing's time), Chen Yung-hua promoted the establishment of the system, and built Taiwan's first Confucian temple in Chengtian Prefecture city and set up a school (the "First School in Taiwan"), laying the foundation for Taiwan's later imperial examination and Confucian traditions.

The Ming-Zheng dynasty brought the monarchical system from China, with limited construction in Taiwan. Based on historical sources, try these two questions!

#1 The gate lintel of the Confucian Temple in Tainan bears a plaque reading "First School in Taiwan". Which of the following statements about the origin and historical background of this title is correct? (A) It was established by Liu Mingchuan, the first governor of Taiwan Province during the Qing period (B) Chen Yung-hua advised Zheng Jing to build it in 1665, and it was the first official Confucian institution in Taiwan's history (C) It was built mainly to commemorate Zheng Chenggong's expulsion of the Dutch (D) The building has served only a ritual function from beginning to end, and had no educational function 【Answer】: (B)

#2 During the Ming-Zheng period, an important figure was praised as the "Zhuge Liang of the Zheng regime". He not only built the First School in Taiwan, but also promoted the military-farming system, opened the Laikou salt fields and changed the "boiling method" of salt-making into the "solar evaporation method", seen as the beginning of Taiwan's salt-drying industry, and established a complete administrative and tax system. Who was this key figure? (A) Shi Lang (B) Feng Xifan (C) Chen Yung-hua (D) Zheng Keshuang 【Answer】: (C)
