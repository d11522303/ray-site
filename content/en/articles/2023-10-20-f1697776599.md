If the GSAT and the vocational college entrance exam (the Technological and Vocational Joint Entrance Exam) further sort students into schools and tiers by score, then the students we receive in the future will be the "short-text" type. Whether they are emotionally unstable students still really needs to be studied and proven.

In the future there may be more and more students without the patience to read long texts or watch long videos. But on the other hand, aren't more and more adults and older people behaving the same way? The main reason is the popularity of, and dependence on, mobile media.

Let me ask: have you ever seen an adult on a bus reading a novel in paper or e-book form?

In an ageing society, adults and older people are the backbone. If they become emotionally unstable from watching too many short videos (as the article cited says), the impact on society is far greater than that of students and minors.

But if we can cultivate their speaking and hands-on skills, and even their ability to create short texts and short videos, they may become the most influential group in a diverse society.
