Lots of appointments lately, even more things to say, and quite a few things to confirm. For an introvert, it's truly overload!

Here's the little quiz on the first page of this book. How many do you match? Apart from item 2, I ticked 11 items, but I think if I were travelling solo, there would be no pressure.

I'm curious: does anyone really score below 3?

✅1. I can face my happiest or most blissful moments alone.

✅2. I'm happy once the travel itinerary is decided, but it also puts me under some pressure.

✅3. Even if I don't go out or see anyone for a week, I trust I won't feel bored.

✅4. If I have spare time after errands, I'd rather rest alone in a cafe than look for friends nearby.

✅5. After a breakup or being betrayed, once I've cried my heart out, I want to be alone.

✅6. Gatherings with too many people tire me out; I prefer meeting one-on-one.

✅7. In conversation, if the other person says something interesting, I'm fine listening quietly and don't feel I must speak.

✅8. I often regret what I've said, and remember it for a long time.

✅9. The time I spend alone in my room is the happiest.

✅10. If the other person cancels for unavoidable reasons, I'm not unhappy, and I may even feel glad. But it's not that I dislike the gathering; if I did go, I'd still enjoy it.

✅11. I often suffer from tension headaches.

✅12. I don't like causing trouble, but if I don't properly wrap up something that has already happened, I put pressure on myself.

Matching 10 or more: a typical introvert.

Matching 7-9: leaning towards introvert.

Matching 4-6: somewhere between introvert and extrovert.

Below 3: an extrovert.
