【Cruise Refugee Camps】

The [Ambition Cruise Ship], moored beside King George V Dock on the River Clyde in Glasgow, the capital of Scotland, is a temporary home for Ukrainian refugees. Refugees living aboard can use the restaurants, children's play facilities, shops, cleaning and public spaces the cruise ship was originally designed with, while the Scottish government also provides some healthcare and welfare support services.

The Scottish government has arranged several cruise ships to house refugees fleeing the war between Ukraine and Russia, because the ship's complete living facilities give displaced children and women a place to stay through the cold winter and the burning summer. Ambition and MS Victoria I now house more than 2,000 refugees between them.

【Only with peace can there be tourism】

Scotland has provided more housing for Ukrainian refugees than any other part of the UK. The war in Ukraine is almost a year old, and more than 22,000 Ukrainian refugees have come to Scotland. In 2022 the Scottish government provided a fund of £50 million to turn unused venues into housing and to recruit willing landlords, to increase the housing available to refugees.

After three years of closed borders for epidemic control, plus a back-and-forth conscription system, if war broke out, does Taiwan have a neighbouring country that could take us in and give us refuge? I think not. Taiwanese without a US green card, a Commonwealth visa or Japanese residency have only this land. How to use political wisdom to lower the possibility of war should be what most Taiwanese hope for.
