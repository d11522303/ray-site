Due to the typhoon, check-in originally scheduled for 8/25 (Sat) has been changed to check-in by mail.

The details are as follows:

Colleagues, please help pass this on to new students when you receive their calls. Thank you.

Taiwan Hospitality & Tourism College: Check-in and Registration Instructions for the Independent Admission to the Four-Year and Two-Year Vocational Day Programmes

1. Time of sending the results and admission notices: Thursday, 101.08.23 (2012-08-23) at 5:00 pm, delivered by courier.

2. Check-in instructions:

1. Originally scheduled on-site check-in: Saturday, 101.08.25, 1:00 pm to 4:00 pm.

(Because of Typhoon Tembin, all check-in will be done by mail. Admitted students should mail their check-in documents to the college's Admissions Office by 8/28 (Tue).)

3. Admitted students who wish to apply for dormitory housing should register for beds online starting at 8:00 am on 8/24 (Fri).

4. Uniform fitting can be done on the New Student Orientation Day, Wednesday, September 12, 1:30 pm to 4:00 pm.

5. Documents to bring for check-in:

1. The original graduation certificate or certificate of attendance.

2. Four 2-inch photos (write on the back: independent admission, admitted programme, department, name).

3. Photocopies of the front and back of the ID card.

4. Documents proving special status (supporting documents required, e.g. low-income household certificate, indigenous household registration transcript or disability handbook, for tuition and fee reductions).

6. Complete payment by Friday, September 7.

7. Dormitories open at 08:00 on Saturday, September 8.

8. New Student Orientation Days are Monday to Wednesday, September 10-12, 08:00 to 17:00. New students must attend.
