2026.6.25

Today we sent off the first group of students going to Canada on the MOE "Dreams Come True" overseas internship programme, which also marks the official start of a fulfilling summer for teachers and students.

I asked the students: Grandma, auntie and mum have all come to see you off!

The student said: They're here to play Pikmin!

The families of both students came along to the airport, fussing over every detail for their daughters, while the young daughters' minds had already flown to the other side of the world, their eyes full of joy and anticipation. Just before checking in the luggage, we saw that even the cross-over straps weren't enough, and the two big suitcases had been wrapped layer upon layer in clear film at an extra cost of NT$200.

The student's mother told me: with a transfer, the luggage will be switched in Hong Kong over four hours, so it feels safer to prepare more. As a parent myself, I feel this deeply. A mother sending her child abroad is a mix of worry and expectation. A mother's heart can only be wrapped, in the last moments before departure, round and round the luggage in clear film, packing all her hopes and blessings tightly together for her child to carry far away.
