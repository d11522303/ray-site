[Students, Please Take Statistics]

Yesterday, in our LINE group, I begged my students to take statistics. Besides what the seniors said about needing it for your capstone project, it will also help you in the following ways:

Learning statistics saves you small money

Learning statistics can earn you money

Learning statistics might win you big money

1. Learning statistics saves you small money

Learn to track your daily spending in Excel, and you will discover that the "flesh money" you earn by bowing and scraping goes partly to big meals that grow more flesh, partly to clothes and shoes that cover the flesh, and a small part gets swiped away by the telecom company on your phone! So after learning statistics you will eat out less, buy fewer new clothes and shoes, and use more Wi-Fi. That is saving small money.

2. Learning statistics can earn you money

Learn to run a statistical survey and hand out questionnaires, and you will find that near the school there are only 3 convenience stores, 5 drink shops, 1 pool hall, 1 buffet restaurant and countless breakfast shops, which is lopsided for nearly ten thousand students on campus. You could open a few Chinese-style curry restaurants at the school gate, and a few bubble tea shops where the likes of Xu Taiyu and Lin Zhenxin can study; the campus food court could add a tasty buffet. So after learning statistics, perhaps you will own 3 shops before you even graduate, and earn quite a lot of money!

3. Learning statistics might win you big money

Once you have mastered statistical probability, you can enter the winning numbers from all 72 draws of 2015 into Excel, use the statistical methods I taught you to work out the six most probable numbers, then invest another 100 dollars of flesh money. From the hour of Hai (9 p.m.) today, you might never have to earn flesh money, keep accounts, shake pearl tea or make breakfast again. Because you learned statistics, you would be a billionaire!

And in the end, you could buy a whole row of shops at the school gate, and I will come rent one from you to open a breakfast shop!

So please be sure to add statistics to your schedule. It is about your future!!
