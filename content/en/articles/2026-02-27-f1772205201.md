42 Micronesia lies in the western Pacific and includes the Mariana Islands, Caroline Islands, Marshall Islands, Gilbert Islands and others, with a total land area of 2,584 square kilometres and several hundred coral reef islands and lagoons scattered among them. Which type of coral reef is mainly found here?

(A) Atoll (B) Table reef (C) Barrier reef (D) Fringing reef

The answer is (A) atoll

Micronesia means "small islands" in Greek. The most prominent geographic feature of this sea area is that it consists of a large number of low, flat coral atolls. Coral reefs usually form in the sequence fringing reef -> barrier reef -> atoll. When the original volcanic island sinks with the crust or is eroded until it is entirely below sea level, leaving only the coral reef growing around it that keeps building upward, a ring-shaped structure enclosing a lagoon is formed. The "several hundred coral reef islands and lagoons scattered among them" mentioned in the question is the most representative landscape of atolls. The Marshall Islands and Caroline Islands are among the areas with the densest atolls in the world.

#1 Along the coast of Kenting National Park at the southern tip of Taiwan's main island, and at the offshore islands of Green Island and Orchid Island, what is the most commonly developed type of coral reef, growing tight against the shore?

(A) Table reef (B) Atoll (C) Fringing reef (D) Barrier reef

The answer is C

#2 The waters around the Penghu archipelago have extensive coral reefs. Because Penghu's terrain is relatively flat and its tidal range large, its coral reefs are often exposed over large areas at low tide. Which of the following statements about the features of Penghu's coral reefs is most correct?

(A) The coral reefs are distributed only in deep sea and can hardly be observed from the shore

(B) Like the Pratas Islands, they evolved from large-scale atolls

(C) It has Taiwan's only barrier reef landform and a deep lagoon

(D) They are mainly fringing reefs, widely distributed in the shallow waters around the basalt tablelands

The answer is D
