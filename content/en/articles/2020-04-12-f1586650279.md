The situation of e-go Taiwan Tourist Bus is the reality of many travel and transport businesses. It is not an isolated case but a clear symptom of Taiwanese society, and the country must take it seriously and help. In fact, Taiwan's travel industry can no longer hold on. Most employees in these companies know that business has long since dropped to zero. Many drivers' and back-office workers' families depend on active travel and transport for their livelihood. Many business owners had already done their best in the past to fulfil their social responsibility, and now the country should give something back and help every hard-working family and business owner who is struggling to hold on.

Why do the leaders of Japan, the United States and China always think twice when controlling the epidemic? It is precisely because of the economic damage: the unemployment that follows has an equally huge and far-reaching impact on society and the country. So a full shutdown of work and a closed border would put most businesses in crisis. The surge in unemployment in the United States since March 2020 will have an impact on this powerful country that no past economic analysis anticipated. The same reasoning explains why mainland China, once the epidemic was under control, hurried to resume work and reopen markets.

Taiwan today is not a big-corporation, high-tax, high-welfare state, but an economy of small and medium-sized enterprises in foreign trade and services. Because the epidemic has been well controlled, most industries are still working hard to hold on, but the plight of the travel industry cannot be ignored.

The vitality of Taiwan's small and medium-sized service businesses is like the immune cells in the body of the country. I hope even more that the government and its policies will give something back, so that the whole nation has a chance to regain a healthy society.
