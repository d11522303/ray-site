The Wedding Venue Matters a

Suddenly the open-air wedding banquet versus the star-rated hotel has become a topic of conversation.

I'll stand on the side of the open-air banquet first, because the best wedding feast I ever ate was at a student's wedding in Tainan, held at a community activity centre. The food was lavish, delicious and fresh, the emcee kept the mood great throughout, and we went home with bags and bags of chicken soup, mud crab sticky rice and fruit drinks that we couldn't finish, savouring them again all the way back to Taipei. That 500 km drive was totally worth it.

But I also want to support good restaurants. The best wedding dishes I have ever eaten were at a seafood restaurant next to the Taipei Botanical Garden, where a graduate school classmate held his wedding. There wasn't a dull moment from start to finish, every plate was cleared, we were well fed and well wined, and I still remember it today.

As for why star-rated hotels win the support of a former bride-to-be, the reason is simple: the whole banquet is her showcase. She probably won't take more than a couple of bites of the dishes, which start at NT$20,000 a table, since she has gone hungry for months to fit into the most beautiful, dreamy outfit, hair and make-up. Who cares about the menu? What matters is that perhaps one and only wedding, where everything must be presented perfectly, and she cares even more that no one on stage could possibly be more beautiful than she is, not even the performers. Only five-star decor and pink bubbles are fit for a star-rated bride.

Finally, I realised that our own wedding banquet was held at the National Central Library, right in front of Chiang Kai-shek Memorial Hall, which fits none of the categories above. Perhaps when many teachers and friends came to congratulate us, the topic of conversation at the time was still:

a... You can hold a wedding banquet at the National Central Library?

We drive along Zhongshan South Road almost every week, and we still often wonder what on earth we two were thinking back then 🤣
