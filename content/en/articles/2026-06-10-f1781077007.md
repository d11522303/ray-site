Dreaming Down to Earth, Learning Without Limits

I remember that many, many years ago, in senior high school, I saw high-achieving classmates being selected as exchange students to Singapore, and I was truly envious. Not having good enough grades to be chosen as an exchange student became almost a lifelong regret. In university and graduate school I fantasised even more about studying in the West, thinking it was something that would come true if you had a dream, and I even made a vow: when I go to the US for my doctorate, I must enter on a student visa. The result is that, to this day, I have never been to the USA.

For me the American dream has always remained a dream.

Now, getting the chance to change roles and fulfil dreams for four students is a blessing of being a teacher. I am very grateful to my master's classmate Teacher Shu-Hsien Tsai of the Department of Leisure at the University of Kang Ning, who introduced Canadian partner enterprises and shared her experience writing proposals for the MOE "Dreams Come True" overseas internship programme, and who led me, a complete newcomer to such proposals, step by step to apply properly for the Ministry of Education's programme. I'm delighted that at the start of the month each selected student was notified that she had received NT$100,000 in funding from the Ministry of Education and HWU. The programme is still in full swing, and I hope they work hard on preparing their spoken English and their overseas travel plans, set off happily and safely, return safely and fulfilled, and then come back to share this fantastic journey with us.
