2025.7.21

Two students who studied at Hsing Wu from senior high school through the five-year junior college programme to a four-year bachelor's degree came back to collect their diplomas. After work we had dinner together in Linkou. After finishing their internship with UNI Air at Nangan, Matsu, they found jobs before graduation with a ground handling company at Taoyuan Airport's Terminal 1, continuing their dream in aviation. I hope they keep walking ever more steadily and can fly ever higher.

Chatting with the students, I mentioned that my father and mother have recently remarried and divorced respectively. Coincidentally, the former is marrying a second time and the latter is divorcing a second time, and both are gradually shaping a happy way of living for themselves within their relationships. I actually don't think it is a bad thing. Each generation does what is good for itself in its own way, rather than suffering in silence for a lifetime and complaining for a century. It is good for the whole family, and in fact it widens the picture of happiness.

Lately I have felt the pressure that the five cardinal relationships and five constant virtues impose on life. I see both strengths and weaknesses in my family's lives and in my own. As a teacher, I can truly feel the contradictions within it. Of course, life can't be redone, it only moves forward. What I can be sure of is that if I want to walk the road of life well to the end, I must accept myself and my situation as they are now, and find a balance within it.
