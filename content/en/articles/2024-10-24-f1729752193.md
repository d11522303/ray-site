Why is Mega Insurance the only insurer offering cruise travel insurance?

Mega Bank was formed from the merger of the International Commercial Bank of China (ICBC) and the Bank of Communications, so it counts as a pan-public bank, with the government directly or indirectly holding the majority of shares. Similar banks include Taiwan Cooperative Bank, First Bank, Hua Nan Bank, Chang Hwa Bank and Taiwan Business Bank, plus the 100% state-run banks: Bank of Taiwan, Land Bank of Taiwan and Export-Import Bank of the Republic of China. Altogether these eight are Taiwan's government-owned banks.

So for the insurance industry, cruising is not a low-risk activity, and few privately owned financial holding insurers are willing to design insurance products for it. Therefore Mega's "Comprehensive Cruise Travel Insurance" is the only travel policy in Taiwan designed specifically for cruise travel, but it only covers international cruises with Taiwan as the home port. Other cruises taken abroad are not accepted. It is an integrated policy designed for the special situations that can arise on all sorts of cruises, such as an accident during shore excursions or a lost passport that might make you miss boarding time, or a sudden medical crisis requiring expensive satellite phone use.

A newer version, the "New Comprehensive Cruise Travel Insurance" policy, has recently been released, and I've put the product details in the comments. When you have the chance to join a roundtrip cruise departing from and returning to Keelung or Kaohsiung, you can buy it to protect your rights, instead of, after something happens, turning into a "Tai-min" protesting on a foreign ship or even taking over the ship, becoming a laughing stock at home and abroad.
