2023 Half-Year Report

1. The 1000KM2023 challenge is still under way. In half a year I have run 467.2 km, slow but making progress. I have found that I have number anxiety: if I accumulate the things I want to do as numbers, I have no choice but to do them. So I have switched to using the accumulated daily running distance to motivate myself to keep going, and also to build atomic habits. I'll report back once there are results.

2. The second semester of academic year 111 has ended. I am very grateful that this semester everything I taught was a subject I love. Six courses was not easy, but I was happy throughout both preparing and teaching. The student feedback went into the system last night, and I thank the students for their positive comments.

3. In the first half of the year I handled the department's internship administration. With the strong support of my superiors and colleagues, the partner companies were able to focus, and with the help of considerate students we completed a great many details of the Ministry of Education internship project evaluation and the matching of internship companies. The result is that next week the students will begin work as rookies at well-known companies. Good luck, everyone.

4. After waiting three years I finally caught Covid-19. My body was in great pain and I made my family sick too, so I apologise first. But I always felt this was a process I was meant to go through. There are too many people, and this is Heaven's arrangement; survival of the fittest is the way.

In the second half of the year I have a 10K and a 42K marathon. Having old friends run with me is much more reassuring, but I have still decided to buy travel accident insurance, to be responsible to my family 🥰.

Also, this year I have found that I am less afraid of heat or cold. It must be the result of sweating and metabolising every day. If I could have fewer headaches, that would be even better 😘
