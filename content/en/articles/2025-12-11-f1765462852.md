In the blink of an eye I have been working in the Department of Travel Management at Hsing Wu University (HWU) for eleven years. Zihan, a student from the homeroom class I took on in 2014, the same year I joined HWU, was invited by Teacher Kora to return to her alma mater and share her experiences working at Taiwan High Speed Rail (THSR), bringing frontline practical experience and work insights to the younger students.

The two-hour session was nowhere near enough. Everyone was eager to ask the senior questions, hoping to find their own place in life at THSR just as she once had. They asked, both excited and anxious, about interviews, exams and on-the-job training, trying both mentally and practically to win any internship opportunity to join the Taiwan High Speed Rail family. In fact, the senior had also prepared a secret audio recording together with a junior alumna of HWU's Department of Travel Management who is now a station attendant at Hsinchu THSR Station, wanting to give the younger students even more encouragement. I, Kora Kora's teacher, will surely play it again for everyone in class.

After the session, at the teacher-and-student dinner, I listened to the recording first. In the twelve minutes of the two women's voices, from different years but both familiar, chatting in my ear and sincerely offering words of advice and expectation to the younger students of the Department of Travel Management, I felt a warm, fulfilled and happy feeling that is hard to put into words.
