[HWU Makes the Companies' Favourite University Students List]

On 15 January, Cheers Magazine (CommonWealth Learning) announced its latest "2026 Companies' Favourite University Students Survey" rankings. #HsingWuUniversity ranks tenth among private vocational universities in #CompaniesFavouriteUniversityStudents, and is the #OnlyPrivateVocationalBusinessUniversityListed.

#Cheers was the first media outlet in Taiwan to run this survey. This list, which has continued for 30 years, has long recorded changes in how universities are regarded by employers, and through the eyes of HR managers at 2,000 major companies it offers an important reference for higher education and talent cultivation.

We owe thanks to all the alumni who graduated and are working hard in industry. Without the effort and contribution of your work, the HR managers of 2,000 major companies would not have listed our alma mater Hsing Wu among so many technological universities. Looking at the structure of the top ten private vocational schools in the chart below, most of those listed were originally industrial and technical vocational schools, and Hsing Wu University is the only one in the top ten that focuses on service-industry and business departments. The detailed report is in the comments.

Although the school has already entered final exam week these days, teachers are still discussing next semester's internship partnership plans with the HR teams of China Airlines, Cola Tour, Lion Travel, EVA Air, UNI Air and Taoyuan International Airport Services. We hope every junior student studies hard, and that alumni pass the baton from one to the next, so that students learn and do well in the service-industry roles that suit their strengths and that they love.
