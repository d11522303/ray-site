In Remembrance of My Good Friend, Professor Chen-Hsiung Tsui

If you ask me what a true hospitality professional is, it is someone who thinks of others in everything.

In 2007 I was the liaison and organiser of the Hong Kong and Macau study visit for Taiwan Hospitality & Tourism College in Hualien, and that is how I came to know Teacher Tsui, who was then doing his doctorate in Macau. When I arrived in Macau with the group led by President Lee, Teacher Tsui, the contact I had been in touch with all along, was already waiting to meet us in the lobby of the newly opened Venetian.

As soon as he saw me, Teacher Tsui pulled me aside and handed me a mobile phone with a Macau number, saying: if you need to get in touch these few days, use this phone and call him directly. I did not need to worry about the activities ahead, because he had already coordinated and arranged with all the units.

After that we visited the newly opened Venetian Macao, the Macau University of Science and Technology, the Institute for Tourism Studies, the World Heritage areas and more, and the whole itinerary went smoothly and happily. Of course I never made a call on that phone, but it is hard to put into words how much pressure that little connection took off me as the organiser.

Later, we were fortunate to become colleagues in the Department of Hospitality Management at Hualien's Taiwan Hospitality & Tourism College. He even took over the students of the homeroom class I had led, after I moved to HWU, and with even more love and patience trained a new generation of professionals for the tourism and hospitality industry. Because of this fate, and because we shared the same ideals and outlook as hospitality people, we often kept in touch online to send each other peace and blessings.

At this moment, I remember Teacher Tsui with gratitude. May his merits be complete, may he be free from suffering and attain happiness. Amitabha!
