[Vocational High School or Academic High School?]

Quote: "Chen An-yi said: Take my daughter, for example. Her three years at Taipei Tai-Pei Vocational High School were very solid. Although she wasn't good at academic subjects, and in the GSAT back then she was far below the average in every subject except Chinese, which reached the average standard, she ultimately got into Shih Hsin University with an excellent score on the technical-university entrance exam (the Technological and Vocational Education Joint College Entrance Exam).

By contrast, my relative's daughter did quite well in her studies and attended an academic senior high school, but in the GSAT she missed the average standard in just one subject, English, and as a result she couldn't even apply for the department my daughter is in!"

------ Simplified ------

Tai-Pei vocational student takes the GSAT (hard): not admitted

Tai-Pei vocational student takes the technical-university entrance exam (easy): admitted (Shih Hsin University)

Academic high school student takes the GSAT (hard): not admitted (Shih Hsin University)

Academic high school student takes the technical-university entrance exam (X): not eligible
