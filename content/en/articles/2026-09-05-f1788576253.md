5/695 Campus Legend

I didn't know about this campus legend, and I didn't take a photo at the NTUST gate, but I did take a photo at the NTU gate before the exam (covering my face)!

But during the recommended-admission exam period last September, I went to pray at the Bogong Pavilion near NTU, asking to be admitted to a doctoral programme. The Earth God is very efficacious. I applied to two universities in the nearby Gongguan area, was accepted outright by one and wait-listed by the other. I sincerely thank Bogong for the care!

Because I entered through recommended admission, I sat in on Professor Hou's course half a year early. I feel very lucky to have had the chance to take part in team discussions and to experience more perspectives and practices applicable to teaching settings and research content. I wish I had met them sooner! In class I did reports and discussions with excellent senior students, and actively took part in thinking up more grounded teaching and research. It was a very rewarding process.

This year at this time I went back to the Bogong Pavilion and lit a Wenchang lamp, praying that the Earth God and Earth Goddess light my path of learning. I also took small cards and had them blessed over the incense for the six of us fellow doctoral students in the same cohort, so that together we can clear the levels and advance on the road of education.
