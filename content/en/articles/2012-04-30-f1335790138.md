Register Online

1. First go to the Vocational Training e-Network: http://www.etraining.gov.tw/index.html and join as a member

2. Then register at the Industry Talent Investment Programme website: http://tims.etraining.gov.tw/timsonline/index.aspx

Course Content

1. Introduction to Service Etiquette (1 hour)

2. Work Teams and Teamwork Methods (I) (2 hours)

3. Work Teams and Teamwork Methods (II) (3 hours)

4. Overview of the MICE Industry in the Hualien–Taitung Area (3 hours)

5. Basic Structure and Policy of the MICE Industry (3 hours)

6. Introduction to Conventions, Exhibitions and International Conferences (3 hours)

7. MICE Budgeting and Proposal Writing (3 hours)

8. MICE Marketing Planning (3 hours)

9. Exhibition Event Planning (3 hours)

10. Incentive Travel Marketing (3 hours)

11. On-site Conference Management Skills (3 hours)

12. Practical Drills on Conference Registration and Reception Procedures (3 hours)

13. Practical MICE English (3 hours)

14. Conference Operations Planning in Practice (3 hours)

15. MICE Venue Planning and Use: Audiovisual Equipment and Traffic Flow (3 hours)

16. Exhibition Publicity Materials, Venue Decoration and Human Resource Management (3 hours)

17. Case Analysis of MICE Marketing Events (3 hours)

18. Practicum in International Conference Events and Programming (tentatively APTA) (3 hours)

19. Arranged Visits to an International Conference or Exhibition (3 hours)
