2025.6.7

Taiwan Police College: The Land Administration Agent Exam

A day worth remembering. Although only one person was taking the exam, she walked into the exam room with the blessings of a whole family. For a long time our family has had to fight and move forward through each challenge, and the two of us in particular have rarely had anyone accompany us as we entered an exam room.

But in recent years things have been very different. We changed our mindset and strategy: we prepare hard for exams, choose the field and the exam centre carefully, and treat every challenge as a happy journey for the whole family. Although our son was taking his final exams in Hsinchu, he did not forget to cheer his mother on in the family group chat, and I can feel that Yen-Ling has become different because of our support.

I hope even more that this land administration agent exam will be a victory in the first battle, hitting the target in one shot.

The Police College exam centre is right next to Wanfang Hospital. Over twenty years ago, SARS hospitalisation and our son's birth both happened in this area. Muzha was our family's turning point: we bought our first home here, I moved from industry to teaching, and our child was born and raised here. Twenty-some years later, coming back to the MRT station exit where I used to get off work most often, it feels both familiar and unfamiliar.

I am sitting in the public area of the Taipei Wenshan Sports Center, preparing the course materials for taking my students to Keelung tomorrow while keeping my family company during her exam. Outside, it is the familiar early-summer afternoon downpour. It is very peaceful and very happy.
