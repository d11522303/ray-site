Book Recommendation: 《高等教育的心盲》 (The Blind Spot of Higher Education) by Wei Kuo

1. The definition of a progressive university is the gap between the degree a student receives and the knowledge they actually have: the smaller the gap, the more progressive the university.

2. Eastern societies are used to keeping tuition low, and as a result universities are limited by funding and find it hard to buy equipment or recruit talent. To improve education, funding deserves more attention, and teachers should be given better treatment so that they can teach students well. In the end it is society as a whole that benefits.

3. MIT's department names have barely changed in a hundred years, even though the teaching content keeps changing. University departments in Mainland China, Hong Kong and Taiwan, by contrast, change names often, renaming themselves whenever a new fad appears.

4. University students are in fact not suited to becoming generalists. Universities need specialist education, and businesses need specialist talent. "Most important is teaching students according to their aptitude, rather than insisting on education for all without distinction."

One more note: yesterday I sat at the most comfortable classroom desk and chair. The seat made it easy to concentrate, the desktop was a pleasure to take notes on, and the blackboard was a green one that doesn't reflect glare from the chalk.
