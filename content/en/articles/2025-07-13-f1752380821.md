2025.7.13

Drained

I don't know what happened. When I went home yesterday evening to see my parents, I seemed to catch my mother's malaise. I slept lightly all night, and with indigestion I kept passing wind. I woke up groggy, hurried to buy breakfast and eat, and then went to use the toilet at Carrefour.

Yes, I can't use the toilet at home, because I can never get clean on a sit-down toilet, so I am always looking for squat toilets everywhere. A few days ago the Ministry of Environment, to represent the move towards being an advanced country, even decided to gradually replace squat public toilets with sit-down ones. For me this is terrible news. I can only hope public works move a little slowly; otherwise in my old age I may become that terrible old man pooping under a tree.

In the heavy rain, I took the airport MRT and then switched to the Southern Loop line back to Jingan Station. It took nearly an hour; I watched the scenery and read along the way, and time slipped by unnoticed. When I got back to Jingan, I was greeted by afternoon rain, so I had to wait under the arcade for it to stop. Later my younger brother came to pick me up, and I was very grateful.

Only when chatting with my brother did I learn that the cousin who held a senior post in the Taoyuan City Government at the time of Enchi's wedding has just come out of prison. After reading the report I felt even more moved. How to strive actively in life and learn the best way forward is truly a field of knowledge.

Because of the effects of scams over the past two years, and my two elderly parents living alone, I can feel that the whole home has little life in it. But thanks to my parents' good living habits, the house is still kept very clean and tidy, and the family's finances are being balanced as the bleeding stops. All the way I kept thinking about how to increase my own financial capability and earning power: in the present situation, what should I do to earn more money?

Most books and online commentary stress the importance of investing, but for me, with little surplus between income and expenses, apart from forcibly setting aside living expenses as savings each month, I simply can't find a way to increase income. For the past two years I have thought and worried about this constantly. Taking on extra classes is no problem, but the income only goes up by a few thousand to ten thousand a month, which merely supports living and can hardly improve my financial situation.

Below are my random thoughts along the way, and the thoughts that kept me awake last night. After drinking a cup of coffee, I wrote them down first:

1. I feel that studying investing seems to be one way, but self-study and sitting certification exams have never managed to break through into turning it into money; it is just talk of wealth.

2. Use project funds to develop connections and opportunities, and make good use of these small hundred-odd thousand to turn them into ways of increasing income. What I am thinking of now is planning training courses and tours that could charge fees in the future, attracting potential customers to become groups; training instructors and partners I can work with in the future, so that one person becomes a team and can win more opportunities.

3. Write a blog or make a podcast, to build up my writing and thinking into a website.
