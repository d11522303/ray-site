2024 Half-Year Report

I really didn't want to write this, but Facebook popped up a prompt, so I took a look at the numbers my phone had accumulated. Compared with my notes from last year they are about the same but also a little different. This is the power of atomic habits.

1. The 1000KM2024 challenge is still in progress. In half a year I ran 464.9 km, 2.3 km less than last year, and my pace has slowed. Probably because this year I am busier than at the same time last year, and many times I used fragments of time to find a distance and run and walk. I hope to keep running well for myself in the second half of the year.

2. The second semester of academic year 112 has ended, and grades were submitted last week. Besides that, this semester I tried to apply for the university's outstanding teaching award, but when I organised my teaching performance I found I hadn't coached students for enough competitions and hadn't presented my teaching results well, so of course I wasn't selected. So lately I need to organise my innovative teaching results and records for submission, and plan to take part in an innovative teaching competition in September. I feel that without organising teaching records, you don't know where your blind spots are, and just relying on experience and a good tongue in class makes a course superficial. The core of teaching lies in recording the learning process and student growth. The students we receive are not top students, so they need teaching designs that lay a foundation for them without their realising it, and lift them up through encouragement.

3. Today is the first day of internships for my homeroom students. I'm actually more nervous than they are. Yesterday I was giving orders again and again in the group chat, hoping their first day on the job and every day after goes smoothly, and that every one of them passes the new-hire evaluation.

4. Over the same weekend I seem to have won the lottery and caught Covid again, haha. My throat hurt faintly for 3-4 days and my airways were swollen and uncomfortable. All night, because of joint aches, back pain, headaches and other discomforts, I slept on and off for 10 hours.

In the second half of the year I have still signed up for two races, a 12K and a 21K. I'll skip the full marathon for now. This year I'm switching to the China Airlines half marathon, and I won't join the EVA Air marathon again. Fortunately I've exercised for a long time, so I still have the stamina to work, talk and teach, prepare for exams, attend training and hand in assignments. But I need to rest for a few days first, and let reading, writing and thinking help my soul catch up with me 😘.

Running Ray 2024.7.1
