While university campuses teach marketing and distribution, packaging design, online e-commerce and the like, they are also promoting courses on environmental protection, reducing plastic and carbon, and local revitalisation. When office workers bring their own reusable bowls and chopsticks for lunch, then place an online order on their phones at the same time, isn't it the very same situation?

It is heartbreaking!
