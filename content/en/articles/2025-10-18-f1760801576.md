Why I Started This Education Page

Many people say that education can change a person’s life. Now, I truly feel this myself.

I have worked in vocational education for almost twenty years. At first, my family and I just wanted to change our living environment to raise our child. We left our jobs in Taipei, where we worked in an international company related to tourism information systems. My spouse and I moved to Hualien with our six-month-old baby and our dog, Snoopy. We thought we would return to Taipei after one year, but things did not go as planned.

Over the past twenty years, my life has changed in many ways. My work, family life, and personal growth were all very different from what I imagined. The only thing that never changed was time. Slowly, I noticed that I stopped thinking about the future in a positive way and started counting down the years. I began to wait for certain moments instead of enjoying each day.

I started this education page to change how I think about time. I want to stop counting down to retirement and stop worrying too much about changes in education and student numbers. Instead, I try to focus on meaningful things in my daily life. Sometimes I feel tired or sad, but I still believe in the value of education. Education begins in the family, continues in school, and connects to a sustainable way of living. It is also a way to respect life.

I am a vocational teacher who is trying to live better and make life more meaningful. I know this journey is not easy or perfect. I use the words of American scientist Katherine A. High to remind myself how to face the future.

-Katherine A. High, MD

I would share something that a senior professor told me early in my career: If you are really trying to solve a problem (in my case, that as finding a gene therapy for hemophilia), remember that you own all the problems is not just the problems that you like, or the problems that are most interesting to you, but all of them. If it is in the way, you own it. It has shaped how I tackle difficult problems in my research.
