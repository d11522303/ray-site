Celebrating Christmas Eve through Games and Team Spirit

On Christmas Eve, our team held a warm party in campus. We exchange gifts through interactive games, including Kahoot and a self-made Monopoly game filled with fun and engaging activities. The atmosphere was relaxd, lively, and full of laughter.

Fortunately, Kim, Birdy and I won the third place in a challenging Kahoot competition. Our senior classmates told us that newcomers never won this game before. That made the moment even more surprising and meaningful. What a lucky and unforgettable Christmas Eve.

Merry Christmas to myself, and I am truly happy to have joined a wondrful team at the end of fruitful year.
