2025.6.8

Keelung Port Cruise Tour Leading Micro-Course

"Travelling ten thousand miles beats reading ten thousand books" is too old-fashioned, but today's micro-course gave me, as a teacher, a sense of novelty and focus that talking for a dozen hours in the classroom could not match compared with actually boarding the ship. The smiles in the students' photos say it all.

Many thanks to Secretary-General You and Mr. Yi of the Taiwan International Cruise Association for their great support and help. The Hsing Wu University (HWU) teachers and students all felt they gained a great deal! My deepest thanks also go to Senior Manager Cassie Chou, official ambassador of Star Cruises, for her heartfelt sharing and introduction. The students now have a deeper level of expectation and understanding about cruise work!

Everyone said they want to plan a Star Cruises trip with their families this summer!

Quite a few students also said they look forward to a Star Cruises job for their senior-year internship!
