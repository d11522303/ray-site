2025 Taiwan Cruise Reserve Talent registration form: https://forms.gle/d23YHi86wpSgxxTL6

＃GraduatingStudents

＃GiveYourselfANewChanceToExperienceTheWorld

The Taiwan Cruise Association has been commissioned by one of the world's top five cruise companies to invite people interested in cruise service work to join a database in advance. Starting today we are collecting personal information from interested students. The association has designed the form below, and students are encouraged to fill it in to build the talent database.

Face-to-face and/or online interviews will be arranged separately. Besides meeting the fleet's manpower needs for its expansion over the next 5 years, this also creates a pathway for young Taiwanese to take part in service work in the international cruise industry.

＃TickTheJobOptionsYouAreWillingToDoAndHaveExperienceOrCertificatesFor (multiple choices allowed).

This opportunity focuses on front-of-house work: F&B, Restaurant and Bar service, Housekeeping, and the Guest Service department.

The "Other (please fill in and note your level of experience)" field in the cruise reserve talent data form.

Students with part-time job or internship experience should be sure to briefly list it under "fill in your work experience and note your level of experience". This is a great help in your final chances of being hired.

＃ExpectedCruiseWorkStartDateJuly2025

The company plans to put one 75,000 to 100,000 ton cruise ship into operation each year over the next five years in the Japan, Korea, mainland China and Taiwan region, so it must start building up manpower now. If all goes well, they will come to Taiwan for interviews in April or May next year, with boarding expected in July (if both sides can coordinate, the boarding date may be moved earlier).

＃AboutCruiseCertificates

Cruise work requires a completion certificate recognised by the International Maritime Organization of the United Nations and meeting the standards of the International Convention on Standards of Training, Certification and Watchkeeping for Seafarers (STCW), before the employment relationship between both sides can be completed. If you do not currently hold a certificate, you can still register for the interview selection, but please note it, and later take part in the certification courses and training coached by the association, to obtain the certificate issued by the government of the Republic of China as well as the international certificate issued by the Maritime and Port Authority of Singapore, preparing the first stepping stone for a future in the cruise industry.

This information is used only by the association and the cruise company for possible future interviews, and may not be used for any other purpose.

With respect, Taiwan International Cruise Association
