Have you read Charlotte's Web?

Wilbur the piglet and Charlotte the spider are good friends on a farm. Pigs on a farm are raised to be slaughtered and eaten, but Wilbur refuses to accept his fate. With his friend's help and care, he makes a series of desperate efforts to stay alive.

Charlotte uses all her strength and wisdom to help Wilbur win the fair, and in doing so helps him find a reason and a worth for living. This is how Charlotte describes the days they both look forward to:

"This autumn days will shorten and grow cold. The leaves will shake loose form the trees and fall. Christmas will come, then the snowes of winter. ------ Winter will pass, the days will lengthen, the ice will melt in the pasture pond. The song sparrow will return and sing, the frogs will awake, the warm wind will blow again. All these sights and sounds and smells will be yours to enjoy, ------this lovely world, these precious days..."

In fact, I think the Taoyuan baboon probably imagined that a free life was something like this during these past dozen or so days.

RIP Taoyuan Baboon
