#UCLA

With no class on Friday, I took a trip to the Getty Center museum, near the school. It holds many famous paintings by world-renowned painters, and these four works are all highly representative works by masters of Impressionism and Post-Impressionism in Western art history.

Because the Getty Center is built on a hilltop, this tram is dedicated to carrying visitors from the parking lot and visitor centre at the foot of the hill up to the main museum campus on top. A one-way trip takes about 5 minutes, and as it slowly climbs, passengers can look down through large glass windows at the traffic on Interstate 405 in Los Angeles and the magnificent scenery of the surrounding Santa Monica Mountains. The weather in Los Angeles really is nice, and I hear it gets cool at night.

It isn't an ordinary light rail or train either, but an air-cushion cable railway system, the Hovertrain. When running, the cars float above the track on an air cushion underneath and are pulled by steel cable, so the ride is very smooth and quiet.
