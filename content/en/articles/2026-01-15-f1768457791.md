2026.1.15

Day139

Final grades in progress

This year there may have been too many class sessions, and they are all large classes of 60-80 students. Just marking the assignments and entering the grades for one class takes nearly the same number of hours, not to mention preparing lessons and organising digital materials. Honestly, standing for long periods in class every day, then sitting for long periods at the computer after class to organise materials and mark assignments, is very tiring for my body and eyes. The Apple Watch on my wrist keeps reminding me that I've been sitting too long (twice already today).

Many people think university teachers can have teaching assistants to help, but that's not the case. The real situation is that hiring students as assistants means working around their schedules and also retraining them in office software. Many type with one finger and expect to use voice input, they type slowly, and are unfamiliar with web design and keyboard shortcuts. And after receiving the TA pay they still have to write reports. After several rounds of such help, it would be more fun and pay better for them to work part-time in a clothing shop or restaurant after class.

In recent years I have demanded more of myself in teaching content and assignment design than before, so I expect more of the students. Although most of the teaching period goes smoothly, there are often unhappy or hard-to-handle moments in classroom interaction, but after class I think it over and let them go.

In the final week I asked students to rate and self-assess their own learning on TronClass. Since it is anonymous and ungraded, it was optional, and I only hoped some students would look back on the little moments of learning this semester. When I opened the system, there were dozens of messages. Although I don't know who wrote them, for me, still fighting alone through over 400 final grades, it was a huge encouragement, and it gave me a bit more to think about in being a person and a teacher.
