2026.10.29

Today I took a group of third-year advisee students from the HWU Department of Travel Management to play games at MEG in NTUST's Graduate Institute of Applied Science and Technology. So that we could eat at Taiwan's best student cafeteria before noon, ahead of the students on campus, the activity began at nine in the morning. Although we all live rather far away, the HWU students still showed up right on time at nine o'clock at the gate of the former National Taiwan Institute of Technology, full of energy and listening attentively to a doctoral classmate's explanation of the "1994 Gongguan" empathy game. In fact almost all of us left home before dawn to make the appointment.

Listening to my classmate, Teacher Shih, explain, I realised that in 1994, the year mentioned in the story, I happened to be in my third year of university too, and the students who came with me today are also in their third year. We were all 21, in the prime of youth. We used the empathy game to recognise the differences between us. Teachers and students rode bikes together to the NTU and NTUST campuses, feeling the cool early-autumn breeze and the occasional sunshine, and played an ARG puzzle hunt in the beautiful campuses. Such a coincidence is very interesting.

After noon, once we had eaten our fill at the student cafeteria, I handed the same group of students over to senior Yun-Ching, who ran the two-player game "The Silent Warehouse" in a seminar room in the Lite-On (Yen Yang) building. Sadly, because I had other business, I could not join the students, and I also missed three other wonderful mid-term presentations on gamified teaching. Just now, after a meeting, I was searching for my parking space in the dark campus with mixed feelings, and felt there were a few more memories from today, and the air on the plateau was a bit colder.

Today was truly a day busy from morning until night, and I'm a little cognitively overloaded.
