Three tips for reading academic papers

Leave a hook that makes you want to come back tomorrow

Stop on purpose at a point where you don’t fully understand everything yet, but you already see the overall structure. Then write one sentence:

“Tomorrow, I need to check ______.”

At night, read only the parts that help you judge value quickly

Ask yourself:

– Is the title and abstract worth a deeper read?

– Do the figures and tables really support the claims?

– Does the conclusion offer any real contribution?

Use “spoken recall” as a closing ritual

Before you close the paper, say one sentence to yourself:

“Right now, this paper exists in my mind as ______.”
