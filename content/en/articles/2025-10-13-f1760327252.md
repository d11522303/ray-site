Hsing Wu University, Department of Travel Management

Cruise Travel Practical Experience Activity

Date: 28 to 30 November (Friday evening to Sunday)

Meeting point: Kaohsiung Travel Center (arrange your own transport)

Itinerary: Star Cruises Explorer Dream / Kaohsiung - Laoag, Philippines - Kaohsiung

Prices depend on your choice as follows:

(Inside cabin)

Two per room: NT$10,000 per person

Three per room: NT$8,670 per person

Four per room: NT$8,000 per person

(Ocean-view cabin)

Two per room: NT$12,000 per person

Three per room: NT$10,350 per person

Four per room: NT$9,500 per person

The above are quotations in New Taiwan dollars.

The price includes port fees. It does not include the cruise line's service gratuity of NT$1,200 or the one-day shore excursion in Laoag, Philippines (as in the attached PDF, you may choose one or not take part).

Payment period: before 31 October, ROC year 114 (2025)

Deposit: NT$5,000 per person

Information required for registration: a copy of your passport, valid for at least six months after the return date.

Note: if you don't have a passport, please first submit basic information to allow booking: name in Chinese and English / date of birth / ID number. Please submit the passport copy before 12 November.
