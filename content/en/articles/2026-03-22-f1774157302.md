Our Hsing Wu Graduates Are So Outstanding 🎉✌️

Ying-Shuang Lu (呂映雙), an alumna of the Department of Travel Management, currently works in the travel industry as an OP (operations planner). She is mainly responsible for itinerary planning, arranging accommodation and transport, handling administrative documents, and communicating and coordinating with partners at home and abroad. At work she deals with itineraries and customer needs involving the Japanese travel market. Recognising that professional Japanese language ability is important for improving work efficiency and service quality, and directly affects the satisfaction of companies and customers, she decided after several years on the job to apply for graduate school.

Worth mentioning is that in her fourth year in the Department of Travel Management at Hsing Wu University (HWU), Ying-Shuang did a one-year overseas internship in Akita, Japan, as a front-of-house service staff member, responsible for food and beverage service, customer service and keeping the environment tidy. During that time she not only became familiar with how Japanese is actually used in the workplace, but also deeply experienced Japanese culture, etiquette and service standards, learned how to communicate effectively in a multicultural environment, and strengthened her ability to respond to unexpected situations.

Ying-Shuang holds the JLPT N3 certificate and has Japanese listening, speaking, reading and writing skills for everyday communication. However, she felt that in the real workplace, when it comes to writing formal business letters, giving professional itinerary explanations, cross-cultural coordination and handling emergencies, her accuracy in language expression and her cultural understanding were still lacking. So she wished to improve her language ability through the systematic learning of graduate school, and to combine theory with practice to solve the real problems she meets at work.

Through her hard work and preparation, the results came out for Ying-Shuang on this spring day when the flowers are blooming: she has been admitted to the in-service master's programme at the Graduate Institute of Applied Foreign Languages at Yuan Ze University, as she had hoped. I hope she gains a great deal from her studies at Yuan Ze, and I hope she goes even further and fights for an even better future for herself.
