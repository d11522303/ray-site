【Japan's First Disney Cruise Ship Arrives, Named "Disney Imagination"🚢✨】

Oriental Land Co., Ltd. has officially announced that Japan's first Disney cruise ship, homeported at the Tokyo International Cruise Terminal, will be named Disney Imagination!

📌 Maiden voyage: expected to set sail in the second half of fiscal year 2028.

Ship design: The stern will feature the classic artwork of Ariel from The Little Mermaid and the court composer Sebastian painting the ship's name. The ship will have a theatre with more than 1,300 seats, outdoor deck performances, 3 rotating themed restaurants, about 30 dining venues and exclusive shops, with a children's zone and adults-only spaces.

This "Disney Cruise Japan" project is the third major growth pillar of Oriental Land Company, Ltd. (OLC), after its theme parks and hotels, extending the magic of Maihama from land to sea, and bringing a brand-new immersive experience to Asia's tourism and cruise industries!
