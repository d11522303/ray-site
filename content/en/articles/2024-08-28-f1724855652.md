2024.8.28

Hardly anyone reads newspapers now, do they?

Especially the Commercial Times. Luckily, besides the three major papers, the newspaper rack in my apartment building also has the Commercial Times. I remember that when I worked at China Credit Information Service, senior colleagues drilled into me that to write good corporate and market reports, I should read the Economic Daily News and the Commercial Times every day, and Business Weekly every week, so that what I wrote would have a commercial foundation.

So every time I walk through the building's lounge, I can't help flipping through the Commercial Times and Business Weekly.

Even fewer people may know that China Credit Information Service and the Commercial Times share the same origin. Mi Chang, the founder of China Credit Information Service, and Yu Chi-chung, the founder of the China Times, were once business partners. In 1950 the Credit News was founded, and in 1960 it was renamed the Credit News Daily. In 1961 Chang left the Credit News Daily and founded the Industrial and Commercial Credit News Agency, which was reorganised and renamed China Credit Information Service in 1966, while in 1968 the Credit News Daily was renamed the China Times.

Anyway, today the China Times Group belongs to the Tsai family of Want Want, and China Credit Information Service was acquired by Italy's credit rating company CRIF and became foreign-owned. Their respective glories have become history.
