Such a great deal that even teachers want to join in.

What a pity that I am no longer a youth under 35!

On 29-30 June there will be a two-day, one-night "Youth Hakka Industry Summer Camp", with industry forums and industry visits covering clothing, food, education and leisure. It will broaden your horizons and prepare you early for employment, and you will stay in a four-person suite at the Sanxia Fullon Hotel (completely free).

Eligibility: young people aged 17 to 35; 200 places, while spaces last. Please register on the website of the New Taipei City Hakka Affairs Bureau (see the poster and the registration guidelines). (Please try to register in groups of four.) Thank you! For questions, call Mr. Chen at 29603456-6021.
