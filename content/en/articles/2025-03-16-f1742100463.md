---
title: "If War Comes to the Taiwan Strait"
excerpt: "A discussion about asset allocation after a Taiwan Strait war made me think of my own family, and of my days as a conscript."
published: true
---

On the Facebook page of a scholar living in the US, I saw a discussion about how to allocate your assets if war breaks out in the Taiwan Strait.

I had never even thought about this.

Will war really come?

Where would our family actually flee to?

Would the assets in domestic banks all go to zero?

Would it help to withdraw all our Taiwan dollars?

1949 Chinese Civil War

1958 Kinmen artillery bombardment

1971 Withdrawal from the United Nations

1979 The US establishes relations with China

1996 Taiwan Strait missile crisis

2022 to now: continual military exercises in the Taiwan Strait

Some things are beyond ordinary people's control. I only remember that when I was a conscript in 1995-96, standing guard at Longgang meant full gear and a full rifle magazine; leave was controlled and all leave from the base was suspended; the Sixth Army's third office was staffed 24 hours a day; the logistics command kept coming for equipment inspections; and we could only watch approved programmes on TV.

I've never even been to the US, so I can't keep up with the US-based scholar's discussion. My family are all in Taiwan. If war really happened, and each family only had to send one man, I could go to the battlefield in my son's place.
