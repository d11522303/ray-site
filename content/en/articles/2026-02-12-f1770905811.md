42 The Namib Sand Sea on Africa's Atlantic coast stretches 2,000 kilometres. Which of the following is most favourable to the formation of this desert? (A) Located in the tropics (B) Low, gentle terrain (C) A cool current passes by (D) On the leeward side of the westerlies

The answer is (C)

Namibia is the driest country in sub-Saharan Africa. The Namib Desert is larger than Taiwan and lies in the southwest of the country, a desert landscape extending right to the coast. The key reason is that the cold Benguela Current flows along Namibia's coast. The Benguela Current is a cold water current flowing from south to north along the east coast of the South Atlantic, past the west coasts of South Africa and Namibia, and finally joins the South Equatorial Current. The cool current chills the air above the sea surface, making the lower air cold and stable, so that updrafts do not easily form. Since the air does not rise, it cannot condense into rain.

When you see Namibia's flag, doesn't it look a bit like Taiwan's flag? But their sun is yellow, symbolising life and vitality, and its 12 rays represent Namibia's 12 main ethnic groups. Interesting, isn't it?
