Dear Members of the Society

We again invite you to join the Chinese Society of Tourism Management's general assembly and Cruise Industry-Academia Cooperation Forum next Monday. We have invited General Manager Hsu Ching-Chi of Princess Cruises and the general manager of Carnival Cruise Line (Caribbean) to give keynote speeches. With the support of tourism industry, government and academia on both sides of the Taiwan Strait, we will also hold a summit forum, "Perspectives from Industry, Government and Academia on the Asian Cruise Market."

Summit forum speakers:

(1) Deputy Director-General Liu Hsi-Lin, Tourism Bureau, MOTC

(2) Director Man Hung-Wei, Taipei Office of the Association for Tourism Exchange Across the Taiwan Straits

(3) Chairman Chen Kuo-Sen, Sanfu Travel Service

(4) General Manager Lu Li-Ling, Royal Caribbean Cruises Taiwan

(5) Dean Li Ming-Hui, College of Hospitality and Tourism, Hsing Wu University

Time: 4/21 (Mon) 09:00-16:00

Venue: 3F, Tourism Bureau Travel Service Center, No. 240, Sec. 1, Dunhua N. Rd., Taipei

Please be sure to complete the online RSVP registration form.

If you need any assistance, you are welcome to reply directly to us by email.

We look forward to your participation.

With respect, the Secretariat

The Chinese Society of Tourism Management looks forward to your presence and guidance.
