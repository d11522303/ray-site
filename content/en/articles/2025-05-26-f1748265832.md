---
title: "To My Dearest Homeroom Students"
excerpt: "A farewell message to my graduating homeroom class after four years together, and thoughts on facing an unknown future and welcoming new students."
published: true
---

To my dearest homeroom students:

Being in the same class as teacher and students for four years was truly wonderful.

Because of your thoughtfulness, I was always warmed.

Because of your initiative, the department was full of confidence.

Because of your effort, your careers rose in value.

Congratulations on your university graduation.

I wish every one of you smooth sailing and good luck in everything.

May your future be fulfilling, abundant and happy.

Happy graduation!

With my students about to graduate, a teacher's heart is in turmoil. I always worry: will my future homeroom classes be as thoughtful as you? Will I never again meet such a good class?

Look! About the unknown future we are all very worried, and we all hope that the good things and habits of the present can be kept. But things don't go as we wish. From the moment we receive the graduation certificate, all of us have to choose a direction at the crossroads of life and stride forward, and we must not hesitate in place, clinging to the past.

The teacher also needs to settle his feelings and get to know another group of students, linking up our various bonds of fate, so that layer upon layer of relationships enrich our lives and make us one another's benefactors.
