The Thinker

Auguste Rodin

France, 1840-1917

The Thinker was made in 1880, when Rodin was commissioned by the government to create sculptures for a pair of bronze doors for an art museum. The figure ended up becoming a far more famous work in its own right, The Thinker.

This Thinker, in the collection of the National Gallery of Victoria (NGV), is one of the earliest bronze casts. It was acquired from the private collection of Constantine Ionides, one of Rodin's earliest patrons, who bought the work before Rodin became famous.

Recent conservation treatment has revealed that the coloured patina on the bronze surface is part of the work's original makeup, exactly as Rodin envisioned it at the time. For a 19th-century bronze, it is very rare for the original finish to be so completely preserved.
