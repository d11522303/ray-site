title: "(Ages 45, 44, 11) A Timeline of Taiwan's Ageing Society"
excerpt: "A timeline from 2017 to 2050 of Taiwan's shrinking workforce, ageing society and falling birth rate, with the ages of three family members at each milestone."
published: true
---

(Ages 45, 44, 11) In 2017, the working-age population aged 16 to 64 began to decline, and fewer and fewer people are left to support society.

(Ages 46, 45, 12) In 2018, the share of older people exceeded 14%, and Taiwan officially became an aged society.

(Ages 48, 47, 14) In 2020, the dependency ratio reached 40.7, meaning every 2.5 people must support 1 person.

(Ages 50, 49, 16) In 2022, single-person households are expected to exceed 3 million.

(Ages 53, 52, 19) In 2025, Taiwan's total population begins to decrease.

(Ages 54, 53, 20) In 2026, the share of older people exceeds 20%, and Taiwan becomes a super-aged society. The population with dementia is estimated at about 380,000, with one new patient every 40 minutes on average.

(Ages 56, 55, 22) In 2028, only about 150,000 people will be 18 years old, and universities failing to fill their enrolment quotas will be an everyday occurrence.

(Ages 58, 57, 24) In 2030, 1 in every 4 people will be an older adult, while the child population will be only half the size of the older population.

(Ages 62, 61, 28) In 2034, 1 in every 2 people nationwide will be over 50.

(Ages 68, 67, 34) In 2040, the share of older people exceeds 30%, the share of working-age people falls below 60%, and the dependency ratio exceeds 65.

(Ages 78, 77, 44) In 2050, people under 15 make up less than 10% of the population, and 1 in every 5 older adults is over 80.
