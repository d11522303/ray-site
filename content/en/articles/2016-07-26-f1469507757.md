【A Great Summer Working Holiday Opportunity in Japan】

Dates: 21 August – 3 September 2016

Places: five

Requirements:

Currently enrolled students of public or private universities in Taiwan, with basic Japanese ability

Workplaces:

The Tourism Division of Aso City Hall (city government)

The visitor center at Aso Michi-no-Eki (roadside station)

Well-known inns, hotels and tourist facilities in Aso City

Working hours:

08:00~17:00 every day (this may vary slightly depending on the nature of each workplace, but will always stay at a standard eight-hour working day)

Board, lodging and pay:

The Japanese side provides free accommodation (student-dormitory standard) and three meals a day (ordinary meals)

At the end of the two weeks, the Japanese side will separately pay a thank-you gift of 30,000 yen

How to apply:

By 5 August 2016, please email your résumé and a teacher's recommendation letter as attachments (TXT, RTF, DOC or PDF formats only) to the editorial office of Nippon Café (tencho@nippon.cafe)

The editorial office will select five students from the résumés received and announce the successful applicants on 12 August 2016

Please note

Travel costs to and from Japan must be paid by the participants themselves
