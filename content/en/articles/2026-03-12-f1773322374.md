13 Which of the following is a transnational World Cultural Heritage site?

(A) Sacred Sites and Pilgrimage Routes in the Kii Mountain Range, Japan

(B) The Santiago de Compostela Christian pilgrimage routes in Spain

(C) The historic cities of the Straits of Malacca: Melaka and George Town

(D) The Historic Areas of Istanbul, Turkey

The answer is (B), though strictly speaking it doesn't quite qualify, because the "Route of Santiago de Compostela" within Spain was inscribed as a World Heritage site in 1993, while the pilgrimage routes within France were only added in 1998.

Let's do a few more practice questions on transnational World Heritage. The exam is almost here, so hang in there, everyone.

#1 Regarding the geographical distribution of "transboundary natural heritage", which of the following pairings of heritage site and countries is completely correct?

(A) Victoria Falls: jointly owned by Zambia and Zimbabwe.

(B) Wadden Sea: jointly owned by France, the Netherlands and Belgium.

(C) Primeval Beech Forests of the Carpathians: jointly owned only by Poland and Ukraine.

(D) The Silk Roads: jointly owned by China and Mongolia.

Answer: (A)

(B) The Wadden Sea should be the Netherlands, Germany and Denmark. (C) The beech forests have now expanded to 18 countries. (D) The Silk Roads (Chang'an-Tianshan Corridor) currently inscribed covers China, Kazakhstan and Kyrgyzstan.

#2 Which of the following is the natural heritage site jointly owned and managed by the Netherlands, Germany and Denmark?

(A) Primeval Beech Forests of the Carpathians

(B) The Wadden Sea

(C) Victoria Falls

(D) The Great Barrier Reef

Answer: (B)

#3 A cultural heritage site on the World Heritage List was jointly inscribed by Spain and Slovenia, mainly recognising its contribution to global trade and metal refining. Which of the following is it most likely to be?

(A) The Santiago de Compostela pilgrimage routes

(B) Heritage of Mercury. Almaden and Idrija

(C) Frontiers of the Roman Empire

(D) The Silk Roads

Answer: (B)

#4 Regarding the World Cultural Heritage "The Architectural Work of Le Corbusier, an Outstanding Contribution to the Modern Movement", which of the following statements is correct?

(A) His works are located only in France and Switzerland on the European continent.

(B) It is one of the few transnational serial heritage sites spanning three continents (Europe, Asia and South America).

(C) Japan's National Museum of Western Art is not included in this transnational inscription because it is located in Asia.

(D) This heritage site was jointly inscribed because the architectural styles of the countries are completely different, in order to show diversity.

Answer: (B)
