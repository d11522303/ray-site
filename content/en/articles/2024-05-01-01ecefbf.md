<p>Have you ever had your fortune told or changed your name?</p><p>I remember one year I drove, following an address from my family, to a fortune-teller's place in a private house in Taoyuan for an appointment. After my whole family had just moved to Hualien for work, I also went to have my fortune told on Datong Street in Hualien, recommended by a friend I had just met.</p><p>At that time I mainly cared about my studies and my wishes, so I asked two questions: would I study abroad, and would I pursue a PhD? I won't tell you yet what the fortune-tellers said, but judging by the results, none of my own expectations came true. If you had the chance to ask about something you care about now, what would you ask? What kind of answer would you hope for? Would it affect your whole life?</p><figure class="ph">Photo</figure><p>This book, "Don't Let Fortune-Telling Ruin You" (別讓算命毀了你), I borrowed from the New Taipei City Library lending system. It is not a fortune-telling book. It is rather like the autobiography of Chang Sheng-shu, and it states from the outset that you should read, not be superstitious, and look at the Zi Wei Dou Shu (Purple Star Astrology) chart from a scientific viewpoint as a reference for being human.</p><p>So how do we begin to understand ourselves?</p><p>With Zi Wei Dou Shu.</p><p>Zi Wei Dou Shu is calculated from each person's birth date and time. When I went to have my fortune told years ago, both fortune-tellers first took out a sheet of red paper with a blank table. They solemnly wrote my name, age and zodiac animal, then wrote out the whole chart's star names and positions one by one with paper and brush. This alone would make a first-time client feel it was amazing and admirable, and probably make them respect the fortune-teller a good deal more. Later I found quite a few books to read and learn from, and discovered that the calculation procedure follows fixed formulas. How the stars and palaces are arranged has long been formulaic, and each person's birthday is matched to the positions of the 12 palaces, the 14 main stars and the various auxiliary stars. Rules have existed for thousands of years. It is not that fortune-tellers are magical; it is a skill they have to master.</p><p>So today we just type our birthday into a website and in an instant can generate our very own Zi Wei Dou Shu chart. Having read this far, you can try the website in the comments first, list your own chart, and find the main star in your Life Palace among the 12 palaces.</p><p>Chang Sheng-shu writes in the book that the Zi Wei chart is full of the threads of destiny and fortune, guiding the direction of life and the character of a person. Based on the <b>14 main stars of the Life Palace</b>, he divides personalities into the [Pioneer type], [Leader type], [Support type] and [Cooperator type].</p><p>[Pioneer type] Qi Sha, Lian Zhen, Po Jun, Tan Lang: four stars</p><p>[Leader type] Zi Wei, Tian Fu, Wu Qu, Tian Xiang: four stars</p><p>[Support type] Tai Yang, Tian Ji, Ju Men: three stars</p><p>[Cooperator type] Tai Yin, Tian Liang, Tian Tong: three stars</p><p>The main stars above represent the personality you or I were born with. Pause here for now and think back on whether you are much like your type. If you are, don't conclude that this is simply how you are; if you aren't, then you should believe even less in whatever a fortune-teller says offhand. Because the chart is always fixed and comes out the same for anyone, but a fortune-teller's behaviour varies, and we cannot let his words or writing decide our future development and direction, otherwise it will ruin you.</p><p>A Zi Wei Dou Shu chart should be a reference for understanding the shape of your life. Feed that reference back into your personal traits, family relationships, relatives young and old, and most importantly continued effort. That way you won't have your life cut off by the chart or the fortune-teller, but will take back control of your own future. The chart is a map of life that only points the direction of your destination. It is not your book of life and death.</p><p>When I first got this book from the library system, I honestly didn't even want to open it. But thinking of my own fortune-telling experiences, I sincerely feel that if you are very confused about your future now, don't really know your strengths or weaknesses, or don't know what to work on, you could first generate a chart for yourself on a website, then spend one afternoon reading this book, the success story of a person who switched from the Department of Mathematics at National Taiwan University to the fortune-telling and information industry. Perhaps you will discover something about yourself.</p>
