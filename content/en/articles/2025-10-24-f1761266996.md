published: true
---

Ta Ching Vocational High School in Changhua is one of the few schools in central Taiwan with aircraft maintenance and avionics departments. Dahsing High School in Taoyuan has similar departments, but because Taoyuan has enough students and plenty of jobs linked to the future Aerotropolis, it is still running normally.

From the 2019 school year, Ta Ching had recruited overseas Chinese students from New Southbound countries into work-study classes. The Ministry of Education says that in the 2024 school year Ta Ching had 129 students, 7 full-time teachers and 6 staff. The school has not admitted new students since the 2022 school year, and by the end of the 2024 school year it will have no students left, so no student placement is needed.

You can tell from the Ministry's press release that students' rights come first, while the teachers and staff will exit together with the school. When people leave, the tea goes cold.

The colleges that partnered with Ta Ching face similar situations: MingDao University, Chung Chou University of Science and Technology, TransWorld University and Taiwan Shoufu University have already closed or been dissolved. Only Chienkuo Technology University, in the city centre, is still recruiting.
