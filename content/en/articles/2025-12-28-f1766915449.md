Tour Guide and Tour Leader Exam Prep EP23

#TourLeaderExamCountdown76Days

26 Taiwan currently has several national forest recreation areas. If you want to ride an open-carriage trolley "bengbeng" through the forest, breathe in the forest phytoncides, and then enjoy soaking in a hot spring area, which forest recreation area should you go to?

① Alishan ② Daxueshan ③ Taipingshan ④ Dongyanshan

Explanation:

If the question did not say national forest recreation area, then Wulai scenic area near Taipei and its trolley would be my first instinct for the answer, but it is not among the options, because Wulai District has the Neidong National Forest Recreation Area, which is some distance from the Wulai commercial district, and differs in administrative affiliation and management.

The full name of a "national forest recreation area" is the National Forest Recreation Area of the Republic of China. There are 21 in the country. The competent authority is the Ministry of Agriculture, and they are designated under the Forestry Act and the Regulations Governing the Establishment and Management of Forest Recreation Areas, and operated and managed by the Forestry and Nature Conservation Agency of the Ministry of Agriculture.

Tourism facilities managed by the same authority also include the [Flatland Forest Parks] and the [National Trail System]. Taiwan currently has three main flatland forest parks: #Danong Dafu Flatland Forest Park (Guangfu Township, Hualien County), #Aogu Wetland Forest Park (Dongshi Township, Chiayi County), #Linhou Silin Flatland Forest Park (Chaozhou Township, Pingtung County).

In the Mandarin tour guide exam questions, to clearly distinguish the competent authorities of Taiwan's tourism resources you must systematically understand how the agencies are divided, their powers, and the laws they cite for management. If you prepare this part you will score, but if you take the exam with no concept of it, you will mostly guess wrong.

Here is a simple test on the two:

#1 Which of the following is "not" a main siting consideration for flatland forest parks?

① Elevation mostly below 500 metres

② Area of a certain scale (about 1,000 hectares or more)

③ Located in an area with convenient, highly accessible transport

④ Must be close to the core protected area of a high-mountain national park

#2 What is the main function of Taiwan's "National Trail System"?

① To establish closed nature reserves and restrict public entry

② A long-distance trail network linking natural landscapes, historic old trails and ecological resources

③ Exclusively for professional climbers to undertake highly difficult expeditions

④ With commercial tourism development as the main operating goal
