---

According to Taiwan's population statistics, by 2040 (ROC year 129), as the chart shows, Taiwan will become an Elder Nation just like Japan in 2021, defined as a country where more than thirty percent of citizens are over 65.

There are three causes: #LivingTooLong, #TooFewBirths, #RefusingImmigrants

For Japan this outcome is already irreversible, yet Japan's advanced society is gradually finding solutions, and extreme ageing has even sparked innovation in many areas:

1. Bringing retirees back to work, so that older people become contributors to society rather than recipients.

2. Government policy and education focus on the care industry, encouraging more people to learn self-care skills.

3. City planning emphasises walking and public transport; the more people who can walk, the lower the national health insurance spending.

4. Improving integrated community mechanisms for mutual care, using small communities that combine housing, shopping and care so that residents look after one another.

5. Nursing homes, insurance companies and life parks are being horizontally integrated.

6. Health technology applications are a research and development priority for advanced industries.
