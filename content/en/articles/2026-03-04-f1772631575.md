#1 Xiao Chen's tour guide practising licence has expired, but he privately accepted a commission to lead a group as a tour guide. A tourist reported him and it was verified. Under Article 59 of the Act for the Development of Tourism, how much should he be fined? (A) NT$3,000 or more but not more than NT$15,000 (B) NT$10,000 or more but not more than NT$50,000 (C) NT$30,000 or more but not more than NT$150,000 (D) NT$50,000 or more but not more than NT$250,000

Correct answer: (B)

#2 On penalties for a Chinese-language tour leader practising outside the permitted scope: a person holding a "Chinese-language tour leader practising licence" is employed to lead a five-day group tour to Malaysia (a non-Chinese-speaking region). The tour leader has violated the relevant rules. What should the fine be? (A) NT$3,000 or more but not more than NT$15,000 (B) NT$5,000 or more but not more than NT$30,000 (C) NT$10,000 or more but not more than NT$50,000 (D) NT$30,000 or more but not more than NT$150,000

Correct answer: (A)

#3 Under the Act for the Development of Tourism and the relevant penalty standards, if a travel agency operator disgraces national honour, harms national interests, conducts business outside its registered scope (for example, a Class B travel agency operating outbound tours), or defrauds tourists, how much should it be fined? (A) NT$3,000 or more but not more than NT$15,000 (B) NT$10,000 or more but not more than NT$50,000 (C) NT$30,000 or more but not more than NT$150,000 (D) NT$50,000 or more but not more than NT$250,000

Correct answer: (C)
