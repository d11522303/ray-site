The Best-Value Investment You Can Make in Yourself

At the NCCU Bookstore I came across Master Chen Guo-Zhang's new book, [Practising Qigong at Home]. Let me ask Ah-Gou to speak up and recommend it:

This book really is a rescue for busy modern people like us, who live every day under pressure from time and social obligations. Master Chen introduces basic exercises that work from the outside in, softening the muscles and loosening the tendons. In the least time and space, in just a short while they draw out a body full of sweat and stale breath, replacing them with a clear and carefree physical state. I have personally benefited enormously from it to this day, and on the strength of my own experience and rich rewards I recommend Master Chen's book to readers. I hope more people who love martial arts will make this best-value investment in their own health and future lives, and I hope every reader will follow the illustrations step by step and enjoy the surprising feeling of body, energy and spirit all made new.
