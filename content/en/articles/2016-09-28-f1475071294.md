A bad school is one that is broken, not because of its students!

Educators are their students' career leaders!

Students are shaped by the conduct and expectations of their teachers!

Help students set goals and move forward steadily; give learners dreams, and don't crush their confidence with words! Put love into the classroom so that every student can feel it. This is my tenth year as an educator, and on this unexpected day off I used the time to ask myself again whether I have lived up to this.

I often tell my family that today's students are more polite than I was as a student, work harder to support themselves, and face their own futures more seriously. As I walk into my next ten years, I want to use today to thank my students of the past ten years. Because of you, being a teacher has been such a joy!
