1. Which of the following statements about first aid for stroke is incorrect?

(A) If the patient is conscious, have them lie flat with the head and shoulders raised (B) If there is discharge, turn the patient's head to one side (C) Loosen clothing and keep warm (D) Water can be given immediately and the patient helped to drink it.

Answer: (D)

Explanation: Stroke patients may have difficulty swallowing, and giving water rashly can cause choking or aspiration pneumonia.

2. In which situation is the Heimlich Maneuver appropriate for first aid?

(A) Complete obstruction of the airway by a foreign object (B) Partial obstruction of the airway by a foreign object (C) Cardiac arrest (D) Respiratory arrest.

Answer: (A)

Explanation: The Heimlich Maneuver is used specifically to clear a completely blocked airway. For partial obstruction, encourage coughing; for cardiac or respiratory arrest, perform CPR.

3. In the early stage of injury from a fire burn, which of the following correctly describes the cause most likely to lead to death?

(A) Wound infection (B) Inhalation burns of the airway (C) Dehydration shock from the burn (D) Pain caused by the burn.

Answer: (B)

Explanation: The most deadly thing in the early stage of a fire is inhalation burns of the airway and suffocation caused by dense smoke.

4. Regarding the emergency-care mnemonic for burns and scalds, "Rinse, Remove, Soak, Cover, Send", which of the following statements about emergency care before getting to hospital is incorrect?

(A) Rinse: rinse under running clean water for 10 minutes, without applying too much force (B) Remove: take off jewellery and clothing from the scalded area (C) Soak: if there is no open wound, you may keep soaking in cold water (D) Send: cover with wet gauze or clean wet clothing.

Answer: (A)

Explanation: Rinsing time is usually recommended to be 15-30 minutes, until the pain eases.

5. By which of the following routes is the COVID virus mainly transmitted?

(A) Airborne or droplet transmission (B) Food or water transmission (C) Contact transmission (D) Vector-borne transmission.

Answer: (A)

Explanation: The COVID-19 virus is mainly spread through respiratory droplets and aerosols.

6. During a trip, a group member suddenly has rapid breathing, night sweats, palpitations, nausea, vomiting, and chest tightness and pain. What health problem might this be, and how should it be handled?

(A) Heartburn: suggest taking an antacid (B) Food stuck in the oesophagus: suggest drinking water (C) Heart attack: suggest calling the local emergency number (D) Chest tightness and pain: suggest taking painkillers.

Answer: (C)

Explanation: The question describes typical symptoms of myocardial infarction, which is an emergency that requires immediate medical attention.

7. When a group member is ill and must be taken to a doctor, the condition should be described clearly to the doctor. Which of the following statements is incorrect?

(A) A complete chief complaint can quickly guide the doctor to a correct diagnosis (B) Give the chief complaint in order from top to bottom, starting with the upper part of the body (C) Explain in detail all illnesses previously suffered (D) Relevant family medical history should be reported.

Answer: (B)

Explanation: One should first state "the symptom that is currently most uncomfortable (the chief complaint)", not go in order of body location.

8. Which of the following is correct for hepatitis caused by eating contaminated food through improper diet?

(A) Hepatitis A (B) Hepatitis B (C) Hepatitis C (D) Hepatitis D.

Answer: (A)

Explanation: Hepatitis A and E are mainly transmitted by the faecal-oral route (through food and drink).

9. A tour group has 30 people, including 3 vegetarians and 1 diabetic. When booking the meal, the tour leader gave the following information: the group name, number of diners, a meal of seven dishes and a soup, a meal budget of 20 euros per person, and an expected arrival time of 18:30. Which key piece of information did the tour leader mainly neglect, so that the restaurant could not make proper arrangements?

(A) The name of the tour escort (B) When the deposit is to be paid to the restaurant (C) Whether a private room or special seating arrangement is needed (D) The number of people with special dietary needs.

Answer: (D)

Explanation: The tour leader did not clearly tell the restaurant about the 3 vegetarians and 1 diabetic with special needs.

10. An 8-day eastern Australia tour arrives in Brisbane in the early morning and starts sightseeing right away. In the afternoon it goes to the Gold Coast and checks into a hotel by the beach. The tour leader tells the members to rest and catch up on sleep for 2 hours, and to be woken at 5 pm. How should the tour leader express the request to wake the members to the hotel switchboard?

(A) Collect call (B) Check-in call (C) Follow-up call (D) Wake-up call.

Answer: (D)

Explanation: The hotel's morning call service is called a wake-up call.

11. When a group checks into a hotel, travellers ask the tour leader to arrange rooms near their relatives and friends so they can easily contact each other. Which room type should the tour leader arrange as the more suitable one for this group of members?

(A) Accessible Room (B) Connecting Room (C) Dormitory Room (D) Family Room.

Answer: (B)

Explanation: A Connecting Room has a door in the middle that allows passage between rooms, suitable for relatives and friends.

12. A 12-day tour of Germany, Italy and France holds a pre-departure briefing. The tour leader explains that the group's flight leaves Taipei at 23:25 on 8 February (GMT+8) and flies non-stop to Rome, arriving at 07:15 on 9 February (GMT+1). A member asks how long the flight to the destination takes. What is it?

(A) 13 hours 20 minutes (B) 13 hours 50 minutes (C) 14 hours 20 minutes (D) 14 hours 50 minutes.

Answer: (D)

Explanation: First convert both places to the same time zone (such as GMT+8). The arrival time is 14:15 on 2/9 (GMT+8). From 23:25 on 2/8 to 14:15 on 2/9, the total duration is 14 hours 50 minutes.

13. When the group arrives at a restaurant, it is found that the restaurant did not reserve seats, so members must wait at least 50 minutes. The cause is traced to the tour leader not reconfirming in advance. Which of the following is the most appropriate way for the tour leader to handle it?

(A) Blame the restaurant for its mistake on the spot, and demand immediate seating and compensation (B) Sincerely apologise to the members, settle them in the waiting area outside the restaurant first, then actively coordinate with the restaurant to speed things up, and prepare a compensation plan (C) Since the restaurant cannot respond promptly, lead the members away at once to find another restaurant nearby, with no need to coordinate with the original restaurant further (D) Immediately apologise to the members and explain that it was an internal operational error at the company, unrelated to one's own handling.

Answer: (B)

Explanation: The tour leader should show professionalism and sincerity, prioritising settling the travellers and solving the problem, and should not shift blame or argue with the restaurant in front of guests.

14. When the tour leader leads the group to dine in Venice, Italy, and finds that the restaurant is in an alley with house numbers and street signs hard to recognise, which of the following is the most appropriate approach to prevent members from getting separated and lost?

(A) After arriving, use phone navigation to lead the members to the restaurant as needed (B) Describe the route verbally, let members go to the restaurant freely in groups, and ask each group to report on arrival (C) Assign members to help identify the route, let them move freely, and then go to the restaurant on their own to gather (D) First find out the exact location of the restaurant, and remember the obvious landmarks nearby so members can recognise the restaurant.

Answer: (D)

Explanation: Being familiar with the terrain and finding landmarks is the best professional practice for preventing people from getting lost.

15. When handling group check-out and before leaving the hotel, the tour leader's most important tasks are: (1) Ask members whether personal luggage and valuables are in order, and confirm before boarding that they have seen their own large suitcases loaded on the coach (2) Whether accommodation and personal charges have been paid (3) Count the group luggage to see whether any pieces are missing and whether all have been loaded onto the coach (4) Ask members to board and take a roll call. Which of the following is the more appropriate order of operation for the tour leader?

(A) 4-3-2-1 (B) 2-1-3-4 (C) 2-3-1-4 (D) 1-2-3-4.

Answer: (B)

Explanation: The process is usually: settle the bill (2) -> remind about personal luggage and belongings (1) -> count the group luggage onto the coach (3) -> board and take roll call (4).

16. The Western Roman Empire fell in 476 AD. Which of the following Chinese dynasties corresponds to that time?

(A) Han dynasty (B) Tang dynasty (C) Northern and Southern dynasties (D) Song dynasty.

Answer: (C)

Explanation: 476 AD falls in China's Northern and Southern dynasties period.

17. Which of the following French kings had the shortest reign?

(A) Louis XIII (B) Louis XIV (C) Louis XV (D) Louis XVI.

Answer: (D)

Explanation: Louis XVI was executed during the French Revolution, and his reign was relatively short and his end the most tragic (although Louis XIX reigned for only 20 minutes, among the options, XVI is the answer).

18. Using the ABACUS reservation system, 123NOVSFOTPE11PYVR is entered. Which city is the specified connecting point?

(A) NOV (B) SFO (C) TPE (D) YVR.

Answer: (B)

Explanation: In the command structure, SFO (San Francisco) is the connecting point, and TPE is the origin or destination.

19. When taking group flights abroad, the tour escort should pay special attention to each airport's MCT rules. What does MCT refer to?

(A) Minimum connecting time (B) Maximum connecting time (C) Minimum group size (D) Maximum baggage weight.

Answer: (A)

Explanation: MCT = Minimum Connecting Time.

20. For airline codes in air ticket reservations, which of the following is correct?

(A) Korean Air KA (B) Malaysia Airlines MS (C) Mandarin Airlines AS (D) Qantas QF.

Answer: (D)

Explanation: Korean Air is KE, Malaysia Airlines is MH, Mandarin Airlines is AE. Qantas is correctly QF.

21. For airport codes in air ticket reservations, which of the following is correct?

(A) Tokyo Haneda, Japan HNT (B) Osaka Kansai, Japan KIX (C) Seoul Incheon, Korea ICL (D) New York Kennedy, USA EWK.

Answer: (B)

Explanation: Haneda is HND, Incheon is ICN, Kennedy is JFK. KIX is the correct code for Kansai Airport.

22. In air ticket reservations, regarding seat confirmation status codes, which of the following is incorrect?

(A) KR (B) HK (C) KK (D) KL.

Answer: (A)

Explanation: HK (Confirmed), KK (Confirming), KL (Waitlist confirmed). KR is not a standard seat status code.

23. In air ticket reservations, which code is used for passengers checking in a pet as special baggage?

(A) PEAG (B) PEBG (C) PEBC (D) PETC.

Answer: (D)

Explanation: PETC = Pet In Cabin (or the general pet code).

24. In English invitations, which abbreviation is usually noted at the lower left to mean "please reply"?

(A) ASAP (B) ASST (C) MAAS (D) RSVP.

Answer: (D)

Explanation: RSVP (Répondez s'il vous plaît).

25. What is the charging method called for hotel room rates that cover accommodation only, "without any meals"?

(A) American Plan (AP) (B) Continental Plan (CP) (C) European Plan (EP) (D) Bermuda Plan (BP).

Answer: (C)

Explanation: EP is accommodation only.

26. Regarding the etiquette of presenting business cards, which of the following is more appropriate?

(A) Place the printed side of the card face down so others cannot see it (B) After receiving the other person's card, put it away immediately and look at it later (C) When handing cards to several people at once, the order should be "from far to near" (D) The person of lower rank hands the card first to the person of higher rank.

Answer: (D)

Explanation: In international etiquette, the person of lower rank should present their card first.

27. In traditional English afternoon tea etiquette, various treats are arranged on a three-tier porcelain stand. Which treat should be placed on the top tier?

(A) Sandwiches (B) Quiche (C) Scones (D) Fruit tarts.

Answer: (D)

Explanation: Traditionally, from the bottom up, it goes from savoury to sweet: sandwiches on the bottom tier, scones in the middle, and desserts/fruit on the top.

28. In a nine-seat minibus with three rows of seats in the US, which is the most honoured seat?

(A) Next to the driver (B) The right-hand seat by the door in the last row behind the driver (C) The middle seat in the row behind the driver (D) The middle seat in the last, third row.

Answer: (B)

Explanation: In a minibus, the seat behind the driver on the right by the door (convenient for getting on and off, and safe) is usually the most honoured.

29. When the national anthem is played at an important national ceremony, which salute is appropriate for civilian government officials present?

(A) Hands-clasped salute (B) Hand-over-chest salute (C) Raised-hand salute (D) Handshake.

Answer: (B)

Explanation: Civilian officials perform the "hand-over-chest salute" (right hand pressed to the left chest), while military personnel perform the raised-hand salute.

30. In dining etiquette, when eating Western food, what should you do if a utensil falls?

(A) Pick it up yourself (B) Ask the waiter to help replace it with a new utensil (C) Use another utensil instead (D) Ask someone nearby to pick it up.

Answer: (B)

Explanation: Travellers should not bend down to pick it up themselves, but should signal the waiter to handle it.

31. Which method is most suitable for the tour leader to prevent members from getting lost during a visit?

(A) Report the itinerary to the overseas office so it can provide assistance at any time (B) Give members the local police telephone number in case of need (C) Agree in advance with travellers that they wait in place for the tour leader to come back and look for them (D) Store the main documents such as air tickets, passports and visas in the hotel safe for security.

Answer: (C)

Explanation: "Waiting in place" is the most effective guidance for preventing the distance from growing after someone gets lost.

32. Which of the following is not an item that travel agencies often charge group travellers through surcharges?

(A) Air tickets (B) Airport tax (C) Visa fees (D) Fuel surcharge.

Answer: (A)

Explanation: The air ticket is the main fee, not a surcharge.

33. While leading a group on a cruise, bad weather prevents the ship from docking on schedule, and members start complaining and worrying that the itinerary will be affected. How should the tour leader handle this?

(A) Assure the travellers that the original itinerary will definitely be followed (B) Honestly explain the situation to the travellers, calm their emotions, and coordinate adjustments to the later itinerary at any time (C) Ask the travellers to look up information online themselves (D) Ignore the complaints and wait until the weather improves.

Answer: (B)

Explanation: Honest communication and professional coordination are the core of a tour leader's crisis handling.

34. According to the six steps of the "CRISIS" emergency-handling model, which of the following statements about "Interpretation" is correct?

(A) Obtain relevant documents, such as a police report (B) It is mainly about controlling and grasping the guests' actions and mindset (C) Seek help from all possible people (D) Stay calm and use 5W2H to build a mode of thinking and reaction.

Answer: (B)

Explanation: In this framework, Interpretation means explaining the situation and managing travellers' psychology.

35. When an emergency happens on a coach and neither the automatic door nor the safety door can be opened, and a window breaker must be used to smash the window glass to escape, which of the following methods is correct?

(A) Strike the centre of the glass directly (B) Strike the four corners of the glass (C) Strike the midpoint of the lower edge of the glass (D) Strike to break it first and then kick it open with the foot.

Answer: (B)

Explanation: The four corners of tempered glass are its weakest points.

36. Regarding the "overseas emergency assistance" rider attached to the travel agency's travel agency liability insurance, which organisation provides travellers going abroad with its various emergency assistance services?

(A) Tourism Administration, MOTC (B) Ministry of Foreign Affairs (C) Travel Quality Assurance Association, R.O.C. (D) The insurance institution (company).

Answer: (D)

Explanation: This is part of the insurance contract, provided by the insurance company and its contracted assistance companies.

37. When a Taiwanese national abroad meets an emergency, and the overseas office confirms that the person cannot obtain financial support in a short time and urgently needs to return home, the office may lend funds for basic living expenses while waiting to return. What is the maximum amount, in US dollars?

(A) 500 (B) 600 (C) 800 (D) 1,000.

Answer: (C)

Explanation: When a national abroad meets an emergency, and the overseas office confirms that the person cannot immediately obtain financial help in a short time by the method in point eight, and has an urgent need to return home, it may purchase the most economical return air ticket and provide a loan for basic living expenses of no more than US$800 (or the equivalent in local currency) while the person waits to fly home.

38. Which of the following is emergency assistance that our overseas offices can provide?

(A) Mediating civil, criminal or labour disputes (B) Advancing medical and hospitalisation payments (C) Providing legal opinions on judicial matters (D) Providing information on how to respond to force majeure events and necessary assistance.

Answer: (D)

Explanation: Overseas offices do not intervene in judicial matters or advance money, but they provide information and administrative assistance.

39. During a trip, when the planned itinerary, accommodation or sightseeing items cannot be carried out because of force majeure or reasons not attributable to the travel agency, which of the following statements is appropriate?

(A) Costs of itinerary reductions or additions are all borne by the travel agency (B) After travellers agree to the itinerary change, they may not claim any refund or compensation (C) Savings from itinerary reductions should be refunded to travellers; additional costs caused by force majeure may not be charged to travellers (D) Savings from itinerary reductions should be refunded to travellers; additional costs caused by force majeure may be charged to travellers.

Answer: (D)

Explanation: According to the standard contract, savings are refunded to travellers and added costs are borne by travellers (but this is usually negotiated by both sides).

40. When a group member suffers bodily injury or property loss abroad "not caused by the travel agency's intent or negligence", which of the following statements is most appropriate?

(A) The travel agency should exercise the care of a good manager and pay all of the traveller's medical and related expenses in full (B) The travel agency should exercise the care of a good manager to help the traveller deal with it, with the costs borne by the traveller, who should be told of the possible costs beforehand (C) Since it was not caused by the travel agency's intent or negligence, the agency need not provide assistance, and the traveller must deal with matters alone (D) Since it was not caused by the travel agency's intent or negligence, the agency only needs to provide the resulting accommodation and transport costs.

Answer: (B)

Explanation: The tour leader has a moral and contractual obligation to help, but the travel agency does not foot the bill.

41. If a group member is refused entry by the destination country because of the travel agency's fault (for example, a visa was not properly handled), which of the following ways of handling it is correct?

(A) The travel agency need not bear costs, and the member must deal with returning home or being stranded alone (B) Arrange for the member to join the other members at the next stop, or arrange for the return home, with the resulting costs borne by the travel agency (C) The tour leader only needs to provide contact information and need not arrange the later itinerary or the return home (D) The travel agency only notifies the overseas agent, which is responsible for the costs and handling.

Answer: (B)

Explanation: When the travel agency's fault causes inability to enter, it must bear compensation and return costs.

42. Regarding Overseas Emergency Assistance (OEA), which of the following statements is correct?

(A) It only provides medical consultation and hospital arrangements, and does not include legal or travel-related assistance (B) It is an international rescue service covering multiple emergency support services such as medical, legal, travel and emergency event assistance (C) It is an overseas emergency rescue service provided free with credit cards and is not available to insurance company policyholders (D) Policyholders can use overseas emergency assistance unconditionally when going abroad, regardless of the status of the insurance contract.

Answer: (B)

Explanation: OEA has a wide scope, including medical transfers, legal referrals and so on.

43. A group member joins a 12-day group tour of Northern Europe. A volcanic eruption in Iceland cancels flights, delaying the return home by 8 days. During the stranded period, additional daily accommodation and travel expenses are NT$3,000. Under the additional accommodation and travel expenses rider of the travel agency liability insurance, what is the maximum compensation per tour member?

(A) NT$8,000 (B) NT$16,000 (C) NT$20,000 (D) NT$24,000.

Answer: (C)

Explanation: According to the insurance terms, the compensation limit for overseas itinerary change costs is usually NT$20,000.

44. To reduce the impact of jet lag caused by crossing many time zones on overseas travel, which of the following practices is best?

(A) Schedule important meetings or sightseeing right after arriving at the destination, to speed up adjustment of the body clock (B) Drink more alcohol or caffeinated drinks during the flight, which helps stay awake and reduce jet lag (C) Gradually adjust sleep time before departure, get more sunlight during the day after arrival, and avoid light at night (D) Buy melatonin supplements yourself to adjust the body clock quickly.

Answer: (C)

Explanation: Sunlight is the most natural tool for adjusting the body clock.

45. A traveller is injured in an accident overseas and needs emergency medical care, and does not understand the local language and asks the tour leader to help. Which of the following services is excluded?

(A) Advancing or paying the hospital admission deposit (B) Providing a list of hospitals and professional interpreters (C) Helping to contact family (D) Helping to notify the insurance company.

Answer: (A)

Explanation: Tour leaders and travel agencies have no obligation to advance medical expenses.

46. A flight cancellation leaves the whole group stranded at the airport, and the hotels are full. As tour leader, how should you handle it?

(A) Calm the members immediately, and contact the travel agency and airline to arrange meal vouchers or temporary accommodation (B) Ask the members to sort out accommodation themselves and gather again the next day (C) Let the members vote on whether to switch to another airline (D) Immediately refund the tickets and switch to another airline.

Answer: (A)

Explanation: The tour leader should first calm the members and fight for their rights with the airline through the company's channels.

47. If a tour group encounters a natural disaster (such as an earthquake, typhoon or flood) in which members are injured, which of the following is not the tour leader's job?

(A) Take the members to hospital (B) Notify the travel agency (C) Explain to the media (D) Take care of the members and record how the incident was handled.

Answer: (C)

Explanation: The right to speak usually belongs to the company spokesperson, and the tour leader should avoid commenting to the media privately.

48. When leading a senior-citizen group into a hotel, which is the most appropriate way for the tour leader to get the porter to deliver the luggage to the rooms as quickly as possible?

(A) Ask the porter to finish delivering all the luggage within 20 minutes (B) Ask the members to carry their luggage to their rooms themselves (C) The tour leader delivers the luggage personally, room by room (D) Help the porter write the room number on the luggage tags.

Answer: (D)

Explanation: Clearly marking room numbers most effectively improves the efficiency of luggage delivery.

49. A strong earthquake strikes while the group is travelling in Japan, and local transport is completely cut off. How should the tour leader respond?

(A) Go to the attractions according to the itinerary (B) Ask the travellers to do their own thing (C) Lead the group to evacuate to a safe area and report to the travel agency and the overseas office (D) Wait for media reports before deciding.

Answer: (C)

Explanation: Safety comes first, and the reporting mechanism should be activated immediately.

50. When the group returns to Taiwan at Taoyuan Airport, one member's luggage has not arrived. After the tour leader asks and registers with the airline, it is confirmed that the luggage will only reach Taiwan the next day. What right can the tour leader help this member claim from the airline?

(A) Apply for a baggage delay fee (B) Have the luggage delivered to the member's residence (C) Apply for reimbursement of daily necessities expenses (D) Apply for travel inconvenience insurance.

Answer: (B)

Explanation: When luggage is delayed, the airline is obliged to deliver it to the place the traveller designates (residence or hotel).
