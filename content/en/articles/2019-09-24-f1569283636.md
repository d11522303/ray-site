According to The Guardian, Thomas Cook took on a large amount of debt when it acquired other travel operators in 2007. On top of that, the rise of independent travel and the popularity of booking tickets and reservations online in recent years have seriously tested the traditional travel agency business model. Heat waves in Europe over the past two years have affected the number of trips, and Brexit has weakened the pound's purchasing power and led Britons to travel abroad less, which are also among the reasons that crushed Thomas Cook.
