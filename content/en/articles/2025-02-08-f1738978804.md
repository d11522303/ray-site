2025.2.8

Yesterday, while I was on campus rushing a proposal, Ayou, whom I had messed up so badly in graduate school years ago that he never graduated, suddenly pushed open my office door. He said he had come to see me, and after chatting with me he said he would also go talk to Ya-ling, the counselor at the counselling office. How thoughtful.

In the last few days of the cold winter break, sitting in my empty office, I wondered what this profession has given me. Through more than ten years of role changes, I am still very troubled, and to this day I still have no answer as to whether I am suited to being a teacher. University students are adults, and their reasons for being here are varied and complicated. Some leave once they've paid their tuition and finished class; that distance is farther than the distance from the lectern to the seats, and if a teacher takes on too much responsibility, he feels he can't live up to this thin salary. But some students always treat the teacher as a friend. When they aren't around I miss them too, and after graduation they drop by from time to time to say hello and chat. That warmth is something money can't buy.

Ayou is now a hard-working cook at Haidilao. The hardship and complicated office politics of the job have given him a lot of trouble, and many times when he wanted to quit he would chat with me and share on LINE. My reply was always to ask him to change his mindset and think from another direction. Indeed, if you stand long enough at the opera stage, you'll learn the tune. Slowly he has gained a small management role at Haidilao, with people teaching him and people he leads, and of course the pay has followed, so his loving father and mother can slowly relax. He is a good kid who can support his family and share family affection.

He said that next month he will take his family to Osaka. He has never been to Japan in his life, and he is really looking forward to this family trip. I am looking forward to it too, hoping Ayou's family will meet the most beautiful cherry blossom season in Japan this year.
