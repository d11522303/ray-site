8/692 The library on the second day of term

I felt dizzy right after eating, yet as far as the eye could see nobody in the library was sleeping. Everyone was grinding away. These past years, I have been going back to campus myself while accompanying my child through university, and what I feel is an increasingly competitive and ambitious atmosphere on Taiwan's campuses. Nothing else, just that the hard-working students are getting more and more earnest.

Too embarrassed to nap in the library, I practised again the ARGMaker interface that a senior in the lab had spent three hours introducing to us that afternoon. Using the power of peers to encourage myself to learn a bit more of the system's various features, I also thought about how to schedule the levels and transitions of future teaching games. Before I knew it, rush hour had passed, and I got ready to drive home.

In fact, the two seniors in the lab today have already graduated and have full-time jobs elsewhere, yet they used their days off to teach us how to build the system. They have both finished their doctorates, and still use Claude to organise huge numbers of papers and think through research directions together with us. I find this kind of academic culture very comforting.
