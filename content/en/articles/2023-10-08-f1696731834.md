---
title: "Counting the Friends Around Me, I Feel Deeply Fortunate: From Primary School, Junior High, Early and Late High School..."
excerpt: "I'm grateful for friends from every stage of life, though I forget conversations, which strained my marriage. Becoming a father taught me what it means to love someone."
published: true
---

Taking stock of the circle of friends around me, I feel deeply fortunate: from primary school, junior high, early high school, late high school, university, army comrades, colleagues, graduate school classmates, academic partners and more. I am truly grateful to have met so many people.

Sadly, although my mind remembers surrounding scenery at a glance, my memory of conversations and interactions between people is very vague. Because of this, my partner has been very unforgiving. What she cares about, I don't remember; what she doesn't mind, I feel deeply. This has caused a great deal of unhappiness in our married life.

Until my son came into this world.

Because of him, I felt for the first time that besides myself there was someone in this world I care about enormously, not out of romantic love, and not out of self-love, but simply and purely loving one person, a unique individual.

I think my partner feels the same as I do. Without him, the two of us, such self-centred individuals, would have had a hard time finding common ground, let alone similarities to last a lifetime.
