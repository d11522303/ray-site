Today's English listening content was about work. Is work important to you?

It is very important to me. Work gives me the chance to support my family and fulfil myself, especially as my work is in the tourism industry and teaching. During the two and a half years this industry came to a halt, I discovered how enormous the power of national policy is, and that the people living within it can only accept whatever arrangements are made.

Since the pandemic began, our government has shut off the island and played by itself, and the Taiwanese people who dutifully got their shots and wore masks were closed in along with it. My students, my colleagues and my friends in the tourism world I once worked with have all had to look for other paths and find ways to survive. To this day the government is busy arranging the year-end elections to reduce changes in the electoral situation, ignoring that countries across Asia have gradually been reopening to tourism, and continues to announce the closed island and sealed land at a daily 2 p.m. press conference.

Compare this with how other countries are working hard to plan the huge changes in post-pandemic travel. As the pandemic eases worldwide, if the international travel market is not reopened, the tourism industry and the generation passionate about it will be at a loss.

Take Singapore's Changi Airport as an example. Besides opening its borders early and resuming all kinds of commercial activity, during the two years that Terminal 5 was postponed because of the pandemic, it thoroughly reviewed the design of T5 to make it more modular and flexible, so that in future it can become a new kind of terminal with passenger-flow controls suited to pandemic management, and it has begun to increase manpower training. In comparison, of course we hope our Terminal 3 construction, which is still proceeding as planned, can be completed on schedule. Young students and workers are eagerly looking forward to jobs with real prospects, related to what they have studied, waiting for them in the future.
