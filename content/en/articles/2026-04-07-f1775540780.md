▋EP2 In-person three-player cooperative FPS shooting and puzzle game: FPS Island T Rescue

#About the game

The scientist David is trapped in enemy territory. You and your teammates form a 3-person intelligence unit and slip onto Island T on a rescue mission. Besides facing enemy threats, you'll need to master the terrain and strategy to complete the operation. Note: FPS means First-Person Shooter.

▋Time: If you'd like to sign up together with Rayfong, I'm at NTUST on Thursdays after 3 pm.

▋Place: National Taiwan University of Science and Technology

▋Host: Teacher Chan Hung-yu, Department of Educational Technology, Tamkang University

▋Players: Teams of 3

▋Format: #Game + #FeedbackSurvey, 2 hours in total, in one classroom, split into two groups that play through the experimental procedure together.

▋How to take part: Join the [Wait a Minute, Rayfong] learning games group. Future after-class and other game opportunities will all be announced in the group!
