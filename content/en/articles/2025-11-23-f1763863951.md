[Labor Thanksgiving Day]

I feel strongly about this day every year, because the mainstream mood and media in Taiwanese society all take making money from money, property and financial management, the roiling stock market and extravagance as life goals, making people think that whoever can give money is the boss. In fact, those with the ability to provide services and daily necessities are the most worthy of respect, and only then is life most complete and society full of happiness.

Japan's Labor Thanksgiving Day aims to "respect labour, celebrate production, and express gratitude to one another". In short, this day is for cherishing work, celebrating the creation of life's necessities, and expressing mutual appreciation. Food service, cleaning, shop employees, police, house construction, farmers, fishers, factory workers, electronics engineers, painters, writers and publishers, garbage truck crews, transport drivers, logistics drivers, office workers, hairdressers and beauticians, actors, flight attendants, pilots, doctors, teachers and the hundreds of other trades are all jobs that involve people serving people.

But excessive obsession with financial management, speculating in stocks and crypto, property investors, and even online card games, get-rich schemes and scam rings, which involve no labour and spend all day fantasising about getting rich, retiring and travelling the world, and so on: I can understand that these money games and pleasure activities also require a high level of intelligence and judgement, but that is not a reasonable justification for a way of life!

Work, properly speaking, must relate to other people's needs in life. Without the work of these people we could not live normally, because people working and supporting each other is what makes our smooth lives possible. So today, 11/23, is a day for hardworking people to give themselves a round of applause, and please also express your gratitude when you receive services out and about!
