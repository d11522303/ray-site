If teachers' pay is all the same, then of course they will look for the school where they have the most control over their own time, and use the extra time for a second career to make a profit. The problems of Chinese Culture University were already there when I studied there many years ago: lazy teachers, students who don't work hard, no investment in the campus or facilities, and a management approach that has always been about collecting tuition and accumulating capital.
