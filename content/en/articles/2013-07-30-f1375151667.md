Dear students,

From 8/19 (Mon) to 8/22 (Thu) is the last chance for your juniors to enter our school. Graduates of any track, whether hospitality, business management, information technology or mechanical engineering, can register. The registration website is: http://ibook.tht.edu.tw/newman/

They will then take an online exam, and placement into departments will be based on the exam results.

Most importantly, if you recommended a classmate to register, please fill in the attached application form for the admissions-assistance scholarship as soon as possible. For every independent-admission student you recommend who enrols, you can receive a NT$1,000 scholarship. (Be sure to apply before the results are announced on 8/23. If you are not in Hualien, you can ask a teacher or a classmate to help.)
