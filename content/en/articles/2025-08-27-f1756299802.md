2025.8.27

What a Head of the Household Looks Like

Today I heard that last Thursday Director Ming-I Huang had a motorcycle accident in front of the school gate. He refused to get into the ambulance and didn't tell his family, worried that his parents, wife and children would be too anxious once they knew, and so he bore everything himself. Suddenly I truly admired and respected him. What a strong mindset that takes!

After work I met Dean Huang in the parking lot and he chatted about all sorts of things. Only one thing surprised me greatly: he had recommended Teacher Hsiao-Hua to the Department of Aviation at Vanung University, and the application process was already under way. It seems he let it slip, and only then realised he should not have let me know. I said at once that it was really not appropriate, as it would be very detrimental to Hsing Wu itself.

Both incidents involved people close to me. I feel I should set my mindset right, carry out my work and plans step by step, and use that to expand my network, hoping for broader opportunities in the future, or a proper and hard-working place at Hsing Wu. We only live once, and this school has given our family ten stable and peaceful years. Whether the next ten years are happy and fulfilled depends on my own attitude toward my surroundings and work: engaging with the world, settling my mind, and striving to give myself and my family a meaningful life. That is truly the best way to be the head of a household.
