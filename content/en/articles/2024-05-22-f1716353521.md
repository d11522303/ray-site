Heaven Rewards the Diligent, and the Trade Rewards the Skilled

The green students in the Pingxi Line train carriage in the photo were the first-year class that, three years ago at the height of the pandemic, with global flights grounded and travel banned, chose to study in the Department of Travel Management at HWU. In heavy rain, wearing masks and thin clothes, after a sleepless night they still went with their teacher, full of enthusiasm, on a railway field trip.

Over three years, we got to know each other on campus, learning, mentoring, exchanging and growing together. The students behind those masks have become mature adults and will one by one enter professional workplaces. The last table shows that a class of 39 students at a university of science and technology earned a huge total of 137 professional certificates during their time at school through the efforts of teachers and students (some were still taking Amadeus certification exams at noon on 0522), which is what allowed them to win enviable internship placements in fierce competition with other schools.

As their teacher I am very proud, and I look forward to everyone showing confidence in their internships over the coming year. For the students still on campus, study actively, and, like the wishes written on the sky lanterns, I hope our whole class will ALL PASS.
