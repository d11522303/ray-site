2025.5.16

People often say, "You're so lucky to work with young people." I also think that is a fun side of this job, and it is good for me too. At least when I drive students to other counties for recruitment events or competitions, I let them play songs from my Spotify, which is how I get to hear good songs by artists like 玖壹壹 (Nine One One) and Fujii Kaze.

But in reality, what we have to face is time slipping away day after day and year after year, and we also take on the age-related issues and realisations of family and friends around us. So the 3,000-metre run I stick to every day is the best way to remind myself of my physical condition and my psychological acceptance. Whether I run fast or slow reflects my fitness, and adjusting the distance between my five senses and my mind from time to time is the most important lesson of entering middle age.

In the attached image, Akita Prefecture in Japan has the highest proportion of elderly people in East Asia (37.6%). Hong Kong, Korea and Taiwan also have sizeable elderly populations. Taiwan is one of the fastest-ageing countries in the world, and with its extremely low fertility rate, the area with the most elderly people in Taiwan is Chiayi (20%). Nationally, Taiwan ranks fourth in Asia, after Japan, Hong Kong and Korea. But this is a table of 2022 data, and by 2025 it should be even more severe, because three years have passed and I have become a person in my fifties.
