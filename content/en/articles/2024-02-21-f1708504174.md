Beautiful Mountains and Waters, So Boring

Hualien is left with only two universities. One is National Dong Hwa University, with only 10,350 students (academic year 112), which includes the National Hualien University of Education merged into it some years ago. The other is Tzu Chi University, since Tzu Chi University of Science and Technology will also merge with it from academic year 113.

In fact, given the population and scale of eastern Taiwan, it is reasonable for one public and one private university to remain in Hualien, and the surviving universities include both research-oriented and practice-oriented ones. That is a healthy outcome.

I lived in Hualien for eight years. Rural areas have their own pace of life, and in other words their own distinctiveness. A university's existence should be tied to the local environment and its residents, whether as a university town or a local university. Besides, in modern learning, online courses have no regional boundaries. If top universities or top universities of science and technology opened online degrees and courses so that motivated students could study and earn equivalent degrees, a campus might not even be necessary.

I went to Hualien back then to raise my child. I was very happy that during the child's growth from age 0 to 8, they weren't forced to learn to write, do arithmetic or take up talents. The teachers at the kindergarten attached to National Dong Hwa University guided children with all sorts of fun, life-based methods, learning through play, spending all day playing on the grass and in the fields, and learning community life through interactions with classmates.

It was such a wonderful process and experience, and having the memories is a great blessing. Although we are not believers, I truly felt that God was with us.
