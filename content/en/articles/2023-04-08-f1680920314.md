---

Here is a summary of the problems that keep coming up when I help students with company internship interviews. No need to take it personally or feel hurt; just check yourself against each one and improve.

When making contact:

1. The job application email has no subject line, or only a name

2. The body of the email has no recipient and no signature

3. The CV and attachments are not combined into a single PDF, making the interviewer hunt for the attachments

4. The CV has no photo, or has an inappropriate photo

At the interview:

1. Arriving late or even being a no-show

2. Clothes not ironed

3. Hair needs to be tidy, and not dyed

4. Bring an extra copy of your CV and your own pen

5. Always be ready with a self-introduction in English or another foreign language

That's it for now. Because aviation and travel internship opportunities are plentiful this year, most students have already found the organisation they like. But I want to remind you that the first 1-3 months after you start work are the key, and before starting you should still keep the serious attitude above and remind yourself constantly. Especially for interns going into an English-language environment, please practise your language listening, speaking, reading and writing often before you start, and make good use of your remaining time at school to reach your own language goals.
