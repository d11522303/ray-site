Tour Guide and Tour Leader Exam Companion, EP102

#2026TourLeaderAssessmentTestQuestions #ForeignLanguageTourLeader #OverviewOfTourismResources

#OnTheSpotSolutions (Answers were assisted by AI; please treat them as a reference only)

1. The Beatles come from: (A) Denmark (B) the United States (C) the United Kingdom (D) Italy

Answer: (C). The Beatles were a rock band formed in Liverpool, England, in the 1960s.

2. In the mid-17th century, Koxinga adopted a strategy of "garrison farming" (tuntian). Which of today's Taiwanese place names are related to it? (A) Xinying, Zuoying, Linfengying (B) Wulai, Muzha, Pinglin (C) Tamsui, Linkou, Xinshe (D) Xiluo, Caotun, Jiuzhuang

Answer: (A). Place names such as ying (camp), zhen (garrison) and yingpan (barracks) are mostly related to the military farming system of the Ming Zheng period.

3. Historical records say that in the Tang dynasty, city dwellers who had not returned home after the night drum had to find a hidden place to hide. Which system caused this? (A) martial law (B) the ward system (C) conscription (D) the corvée-service system

Answer: (B). The Tang dynasty practised "separation of wards and markets" and a "curfew system", and residents had to observe the opening and closing times of the ward gates.

4. The Little Mermaid is a work of Hans Christian Andersen. In which city was her statue later built? (A) Oslo (B) Stockholm (C) Helsinki (D) Copenhagen

Answer: (D). The Little Mermaid statue is the most famous tourist landmark in Copenhagen, Denmark.

5. In the 17th-century Edo period of Japan, which city was known as the "Kitchen of the Realm"? (A) Tokyo (B) Nagoya (C) Osaka (D) Kyoto

Answer: (C). In the Edo period, Osaka was the centre of goods distribution for all of Japan, so it was called the Kitchen of the Realm.

6. Which country, known as the "Kingdom of Cacti", once hosted the Maya and Aztec civilisations? (A) Mexico (B) Guatemala (C) El Salvador (D) Nicaragua

Answer: (A). Mexico's flag bears a cactus design, and the country has many ancient civilisation sites.

7. Which is the earliest Christian country in northeastern Africa and also the earliest origin of coffee in the world? (A) Egypt (B) Sudan (C) Somalia (D) Ethiopia

Answer: (D). Ethiopia has an ancient Orthodox tradition, and coffee originated in the Kaffa region within its borders.

8. Which of the following writers is NOT Irish? (A) Oscar Wilde (B) George Bernard Shaw (C) Charles Dickens (D) W. B. Yeats

Answer: (C). Charles Dickens was a famous English writer (a Londoner).

9. Italy produced many artists during the Renaissance. Which of the following was NOT an artist of that period? (A) Giotto (B) Reynolds (C) Leonardo da Vinci (D) Raphael

Answer: (B). Joshua Reynolds was an 18th-century English portrait painter; Giotto was a pioneer of the Renaissance.

10. During Spanish colonial rule of the Philippines, many crops were introduced. Which of the following was NOT introduced by the Spanish? (A) rice (B) corn (C) cocoa (D) potato

Answer: (A). Rice was a native crop of the Philippines, or was brought by Asian migrants.

11. Vietnam was once under French colonial rule. Which of the following foods is unrelated to French influence? (A) bread (B) spring rolls (C) coffee (D) beef dishes

Answer: (B). Spring rolls are a product of Chinese cultural influence. French baguettes, drip coffee and beef pho (Phở) are related to French meat cooking.

12. The Dutch East India Company hired a famous printing family to print nautical charts and required them to keep it secret. What was the main consideration? (A) culture (B) war (C) diplomacy (D) commerce

Answer: (D). Maps involved sea routes and trade resources, which in the Age of Discovery were core commercial secrets.

13. It emphasises symmetry and balance and conveys ideas of order and grandeur. In the 17th century it favoured luxury, drama and strong contrast. What is this style? (A) 13th-century Gothic (B) 15th-century Renaissance (C) 17th-century Baroque (D) 19th-century Impressionism

Answer: (C). The Baroque style that flourished in the 17th century is known for splendour, movement and dramatic light and shadow.

14. What was the main function and symbolic meaning of the ancient Egyptian pyramids? (A) the pharaoh's tomb, symbolising a staircase to the afterlife and the eternity of royal power (B) a sacred space for rituals (C) an administrative centre (D) a military watchtower

Answer: (A). The core function of the pyramids was as the pharaoh's burial place, embodying a religious view of eternal life.

15. Among the members of the "Commonwealth of Nations", which of the following is NOT a member state? (A) Morocco (B) Malta (C) Nigeria (D) Kenya

Answer: (A). Morocco was once a protectorate of France and Spain, not a colony of the British Empire.

16. Who launched the "Cry of Dolores" in 1810 and is regarded as the leader of the Mexican independence revolution? (A) Bolívar (B) Miguel Hidalgo (C) Juárez (D) Agustín de Iturbide

Answer: (B). Father Miguel Hidalgo is honoured as the father of the Mexican nation.

17. What is the geographic location and historical significance of Cuzco, capital of the Inca Empire? (A) the highlands of present-day Peru, a political and religious centre (B) within present-day Colombia (C) northern present-day Chile (D) within present-day Ecuador

Answer: (A). Cuzco is in the Andes of Peru and was the heart of power of the Inca Empire.

18. Haiti was once a colony of European powers. Which of the following countries colonised Haiti for a long time? (A) Portugal (B) Spain (C) France (D) the United Kingdom

Answer: (C). Haiti was France's most important colony in the Caribbean (Saint-Domingue).

19. Which of the following is NOT a representative symbol of the Netherlands? (A) windmills (B) tulips (C) baguettes (D) wooden shoes

Answer: (C). The baguette is a food symbol of France.

20. Regarding the features of New Zealand's agriculture and livestock development, which is correct? (A) led by the Maori (B) exported large amounts of fresh meat before refrigeration technology appeared (C) heavily subsidised by the government (D) based on pasture feeding, and successfully entered the British market thanks to refrigeration

Answer: (D). The invention of refrigerated ships let New Zealand's meat and dairy products be sold as far as Britain, becoming a pillar of the economy.

21. What was the core content and historical impact of Australia's "White Australia policy"? (A) encouraging Asian immigration (B) targeting European immigrants (C) excluding non-white groups through language tests (D) protecting Aboriginal land rights

Answer: (C). The White Australia policy aimed to restrict the entry of people of non-European descent (especially Asians) into Australia.

22. In which city is the "European Parliament" located? (A) Berlin (B) Brussels (C) Amsterdam (D) Paris

Answer: (B). The EU's main administrative bodies are mostly located in Brussels and Strasbourg.

23. Which national park in Taiwan is famous for its "volcanic landforms"? (A) Kenting (B) Shei-Pa (C) Yushan (D) Yangmingshan

Answer: (D). Yangmingshan National Park is Taiwan's only volcanic national park.

24. In which district of Hong Kong is the Hong Kong Convention and Exhibition Centre located? (A) Central (B) The Peak (C) Kowloon (D) Wan Chai

Answer: (D). It is located on the Victoria Harbour waterfront in North Wan Chai.

25. Which climate conditions in the Congo Basin best promote the growth of tropical rainforest? ② large annual rainfall evenly distributed ④ controlled by the equatorial low-pressure belt all year ⑤ high temperatures all year with a small annual range (A) ①②③ (B) ①③⑤ (C) ②④⑤ (D) ③④⑤

Answer: (C). The tropical rainforest climate features high temperatures and heavy rain all year (controlled by the equatorial low).

26. Which place, at 71 degrees north latitude, is the northernmost point of Europe and a place to watch the aurora? (A) North Cape, Norway (B) Rovaniemi, Finland (C) Reykjavik, Iceland (D) Kiruna, Sweden

Answer: (A). North Cape, Norway is regarded as the northernmost point of continental Europe.

27. The Abu Simbel temples were relocated because of the construction of the Aswan High Dam. Which river was involved? (A) the Nile (B) the Niger (C) the Congo (D) the Zambezi

Answer: (A). The Aswan High Dam is on the Nile within Egypt.

28. The formation of the East African Rift is mainly due to which kind of plate boundary? (A) divergent boundary (B) conservative boundary (C) convergent boundary (D) subduction zone action

Answer: (A). The Rift is a fault-subsidence zone formed by crustal extension and plate separation.

29. Moscow's main climate type is probably: (A) polar tundra (B) subtropical monsoon (C) temperate continental (D) temperate oceanic

Answer: (C). Moscow is in the interior of European Russia, with warm, short summers and cold, long winters, a distinctly continental climate.

30. By which agent of erosion was the Matterhorn's distinctive "horn" peak formed? (A) sea water (B) rivers (C) wind (D) glaciers

Answer: (D). A horn is a sharp peak formed when several glaciers eroded toward a central mountain at the same time during the ice age.

31. Which statement about South Asia is wrong? (A) India is the most populous country in the region (B) Bhutan is the only country in the world with Tibetan Buddhism as its state religion (C) Sri Lanka lies in the Indian Ocean (D) Pakistan does not border China

Answer: (D). Northern Pakistan borders Xinjiang, China (the famous Karakoram Highway between China and Pakistan).

32. Which of the following cities is NOT a major city on the island of Great Britain? (A) London (B) Dublin (C) Edinburgh (D) Manchester

Answer: (B). Dublin is the capital of the Republic of Ireland on the island of Ireland.

33. What are the physical geographic features of the Amazon basin? ③ controlled by the equatorial low-pressure belt ④ very abundant river water ⑤ high temperatures and heavy rain all year (A) ①②④ (B) ①③⑤ (C) ②③④ (D) ③④⑤

Answer: (D). The Amazon is a tropical rainforest, with no "vast temperate grassland (②)".

34. In which country is the Rhine NOT located? (A) France (B) the Netherlands (C) Italy (D) Germany

Answer: (C). The Rhine flows through Germany, France, the Netherlands, Switzerland, Austria and other countries, but not Italy.

35. What is the world's longest undersea tunnel, connecting Hokkaido and Honshu? (A) Kanmon Tunnel (B) Seikan Tunnel (C) Yamate Tunnel (D) Torii Tunnel

Answer: (B). The Seikan Tunnel connects Aomori Prefecture and Hakodate (Hokkaido).

36. Which of the following countries does NOT belong to Central Asia? (A) Kazakhstan (B) Kyrgyzstan (C) Pakistan (D) Uzbekistan

Answer: (C). Pakistan belongs to "South Asia".

37. Which statement about Canada is wrong? (A) The capital is Vancouver (B) Lake Louise is in Banff National Park (C) Niagara Falls is on the US-Canada border (D) The province of Quebec has French as its official language

Answer: (A). Canada's capital is Ottawa.

38. What are the main reasons for the ecological threats facing the Great Barrier Reef? ① rising sea temperatures from climate change ② water pollution from agricultural production ④ a surge in the predatory crown-of-thorns starfish (A) ①②③ (B) ①②④ (C) ②③⑤ (D) ③④⑤

Answer: (B). The main threats to coral reefs are bleaching (rising temperatures), pollution and attacks by natural predators.

39. The old city of Marrakech, Morocco, is most closely associated with which religious culture? (A) Buddhism (B) Hinduism (C) Christianity (D) Islam

Answer: (D). Morocco is a typical North African Muslim country.

40. Which best embodies the special way Machu Picchu's culture and nature complement each other? (A) the building complex makes extensive use of local peridotite (B) a last refuge (C) the site lies in the Andes, and the terraces were designed for soil and water conservation on steep mountain land (D) a habitat of rare species

Answer: (C). The terraces show how human wisdom adapts to and uses rugged natural terrain.

41. Which emphasises the special landscape formed by long-term interaction between humans and the natural environment (Cultural Landscape)? (A) the Great Barrier Reef (B) the Rice Terraces of the Philippine Cordilleras (C) Serengeti National Park (D) the Galapagos Islands

Answer: (B). Terraces are a typical landscape combining the man-made and the natural. The rest are mostly purely natural heritage.

42. What are the "three manly sports" of Mongolia's Naadam festival? (A) wrestling, archery, horse racing (B) weightlifting, wrestling, horse racing (C) archery, polo, wrestling (D) horse racing, falconry, archery

Answer: (A). The three sports are wrestling, archery and horse racing.

43. What are the cause and source of the "drift ice" landscape in Hokkaido, Japan? (A) volcanic magma (B) local sea water freezing (C) giant ice from the Arctic Circle (D) water from the Heilongjiang (Amur) River, the border river between China and Russia, flows into the sea, freezes, and drifts over with ocean currents

Answer: (D). The drift ice of the Sea of Okhotsk is mainly due to fresh water from the Amur lowering the salinity.

44. Which are buildings on the Acropolis of Athens? ② the Propylaea ③ the Parthenon ⑤ the Erechtheion (A) ①②③ (B) ①③④ (C) ②③⑤ (D) ③④⑤

Answer: (C). The Pantheon (①) is in Rome, and St. Peter's Square (④) is in the Vatican.

45. Which statement about the geographic features of Jeju Island in South Korea is wrong? (A) It is Korea's second largest island (B) It has unique volcanic landforms (C) It was selected as a World Natural Heritage site in 2007 (D) It is known for its snow green tea theme museum

Answer: (A). Jeju is Korea's largest island.

46. Which pairing of country and capital is correct? (A) Vietnam: Ho Chi Minh City (B) Australia: Sydney (C) Turkey: Ankara (D) Canada: Toronto

Answer: (C). Vietnam's capital is Hanoi, Australia's is Canberra, and Canada's is Ottawa.

47. A restaurant in South America serves roasted guinea pig. What is the most appropriate thing for a tour leader to do? (A) take photos as a record (B) explain the differences in food culture and encourage respect for local culture (C) ask for the meal to be changed (D) report to the travel agency

Answer: (B). Guinea pig is a traditional ingredient in the Andes, and guests should be guided from a cultural-education angle.

48. Which crop was introduced from the Inca civilisation of South America and has been passed down to become a staple food worldwide? (A) rice (B) wheat (C) potato (D) buckwheat

Answer: (C). The potato is native to the Andes.

49. Which statement about Brazil's Carnival is wrong? (A) It is a revelry that takes place after Lent (B) It is also called Shrovetide (C) It is one of the world's three major carnivals (D) It is known for samba dancing and floats

Answer: (A). Carnival is a revelry (with meat-eating) held "before" Lent.

50. Which statement about American tourism resources is correct? (A) Yosemite is the world's first national park (B) The Statue of Liberty represents the centennial of American independence (C) The Grand Canyon was formed by erosion from the Colorado River (D) Wall Street is in the capital, Washington

Answer: (C). (A) Yellowstone was the first national park; (B) the Statue of Liberty was a gift from France; (D) Wall Street is in New York.
