19 Who is the author of the famous Greek epics the *Iliad* and the *Odyssey*? (A) Dante (B) Homer (C) Petrarch (D) Boccaccio

Answer: (B) Homer

I honestly didn't know this one, so I asked Gemini to help answer with the following.

Homer was the greatest bard of ancient Greece. Although scholars still debate whether "Homer" was a single individual (the so-called "Homeric question"), tradition attributes the two great epics to him. They appeared around the 8th century BCE, which corresponds to the early Spring and Autumn period in China, the era of the Maogong Ding, a national treasure at the National Palace Museum in Taipei. If you get the chance, classmates, you should definitely go to the National Palace Museum and see our own culture.

The two works by Homer mentioned in the question:

*Iliad*: an epic about the final stage of the Trojan War, whose central theme is "the wrath of Achilles". How do we describe this kind of wrath?

Recently Taiwan's Judicial Yuan has been promoting "restorative justice", asking victims to face the harm done to them in a more objective and detached way. After reading the story of "the wrath of Achilles", all I could think of was the tears of the father whose son was killed by two teenagers not long ago. That is "the wrath of Achilles".

*Odyssey*: the story of the hero Odysseus, who after the Trojan War drifted for ten years before returning home. The famous Trojan Horse, the wooden-horse trick that ended the ten-year war, was his idea. He is a symbol of wisdom.
