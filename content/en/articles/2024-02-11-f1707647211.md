title: "152 Straight Weeks of Running: Another Year with My Apple Watch"
excerpt: "After 152 consecutive weeks of running with my Apple Watch, here are six updated thoughts, from marathon battery anxiety to its price."
published: true
---

152 consecutive weeks of running means this Apple Watch (AW) is almost 3 years old. I wrote up my thoughts last year, and a year later I have quite a few new impressions:

1. Over these three years, whether the AW can be worn to run a full marathon has always been a question. After running the Evergreen Marathon last year, I truly feel that at my pace the AW is not up to running a marathon. Besides worrying about whether I would have the stamina to finish, the long GPS timing sync gave me battery anxiety while wearing it, not to mention the worry about being swept off the course, aid stations running short of things and later even out of water. With all these anxieties, if I take on another one this year I will have to find another solution.

2. I used to care about whether the AW could sync podcasts and Bluetooth headphones. Now I don't want to listen to anything while running, so my bone-conduction headphones and syncing problems are all gone.

3. The AW can work as an Apple Pay offline credit card, which is very convenient, but since I changed phones it hasn't been able to sync and can't make offline payments. It's still a nuisance.

4. The AW lets you check your heart rate and health indicators at any time. I still highly recommend this part.

5. The battery lasts only one day before it needs charging, which is a hassle for trips since you have to carry a charging cable. On my first trip abroad after the pandemic I wore my original Seiko mechanical watch instead. Not to mention that electronics can't be worn at the TOEIC or other important exams, and an empty wrist during an exam feels very insecure.

6. The biggest drawback is still that the AW is too expensive. Time is priceless.
