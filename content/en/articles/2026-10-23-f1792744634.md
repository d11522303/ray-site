Internship Scaffolding

The attached image is a screenshot of current fourth-year students of the HWU Department of Travel Management in the Tourism Administration's database of travel industry personnel. Most people have had experience travelling abroad or joining tours, and know many friends or classmates in the industry, but they probably do not know that anyone working at a travel agency and holding a professional licence must be publicly listed in a government database where it can be checked (link in the comments).

When high school students enrol in the Department of Travel Management and decide to enter this field, we who train them as travel professionals must, besides making clear that this career comes with standards and standard procedures, also actively guide them through their learning experiences step by step during their studies. It is like putting scaffolding up around a building: putting up levels at the right time, removing them once the students have climbed up, then encouraging students to take part in internships, building a bridge between companies and the school, so that in the end they grow independently and gradually take shape as professional travel-industry people.

When I see familiar student names appear in the travel industry personnel system, I feel my work has left a small mark, and I get a little sense of achievement.
