【Be the Person You Want to Become】

On the evening news I heard that Warren Buffett and Morris Chang are 95 and 94 years old, and both are still doing things that give them a sense of achievement, though they already accomplished extraordinary things long ago.

Their age is 40 years ahead of mine right now. If I had the chance to face myself forty years ago, a 14-year-old in the second year of junior high in Yonghe, what advice would I give him?

Back then, in second year, I had just moved from a B-track class to the A- class and was struggling painfully there. It was nothing but exams and corporal punishment all day. If my semester average did not reach the top 2 in the class, I could not transfer again and could never leave this terrible class with its terrible teacher, and my whole life would truly have ended up like that.

Yet the outcome unexpectedly turned around greatly. In the first semester of second year in the A- class, my grades were mostly 3rd place, like the last half-inning of a baseball game. Something surprising happened in the second semester: the student ranked 2nd in class (or was it 1st? I forget) was deeply involved with a girl from neighbouring Fuhe Junior High, and so I took the opportunity and, at the end of second year, moved up to the A+ class with second place. Suddenly my learning environment changed enormously, and I was put on a different track.

So I ask myself now: if I had another 40 years of effort, what kind of life might I achieve? Would I envy that kind of life?

I took this question with me on a night run, and by the time I returned home I had a clearer conclusion. Forty years ago, these two seniors had the same worries as I do, faced similar pressure as people reaching middle age, and had difficulties they could not resolve. But they strived to live in the present, held firmly to the beliefs in their own lives, and worked selflessly for their companies or society, doing their best to face and fight for things at every moment.

They did not imagine what kind of people they would become 40 years later; they worked hard in the moment to be the people they wanted to be. So whoever I want to be now, I should list the conditions, and put them into practice one by one. By acting I gain a sense of achievement, and accumulated over a long time, I will find true happiness.
