2025.1.1

I woke early in the morning feeling thoroughly world-weary, and on the first day of the year I wrote down the following words:

In 2025 I hope life will have no more sudden incidents, that I can feel my own breathing every day.

If I want to eat, want to sleep, and feel just a little bit happy, that is enough!

But after breakfast I saw that my family had updated the calendars, and I felt a sense of hope and decided to make a small change. I decided to sort out the jumbled cabinets and swap the summer and winter clothes, so that I don't need to buy winter clothes this year. I washed and tidied the electric fans into a group so they won't block the way at home. I cleared the dust from every room and corner, and suddenly the house felt fresh.

Sweeping the dust from my own heart starts with what is near me!

The lucky bell hanging in front of the new calendar was bought next to the primary school in the Old Town of Lijiang. There is a small eatery there, and the lady who runs it blesses every guest from afar with the words: "Good luck for life." We tasted the food and filled our stomachs, and received a string of good wishes and joy, which became a lovely memory of the journey. I had originally bought several lucky bells related to peace and safety to give to students, but in the end I could never give them away.

So we must bless ourselves and our families, wishing ourselves good luck for life, year after year.

In 2025 I still hold on to hope. Happy New Year to myself, and I will work hard to fulfil the following expectations:
