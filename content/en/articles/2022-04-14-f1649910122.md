A Great Listen, Part 1: 46 Minutes

This is the most moving broadcast I have listened to recently.

It made me feel that becoming a "complete" person starts with persevering in small things.

When Mr. Lin first went to the United States at about 19 or 20, he worked as a doorman at the famous Long Island hotel. Thanks to his knack for quickly getting to know people and finding common ground, and because he dealt with others so earnestly and honestly, he went with the same values from doorman to engineer, councilman, mayor and CEO of a multinational company, and so came to have a rich and positive life.

To be honest, of course I envy that he had money and the chance to study, train and even work in the United States. But looking back, if I had been given the same opportunity, with my own abilities and personality, I could not have said many of the things he said when facing racial issues or challenges, let alone stood up and tried to take on responsibility.

Life would have followed a different pattern, and the results might have been different too. Either way, I gained a great deal and am sharing it with friends here. If you can spare your ears, have a listen to this rewarding online interview.
