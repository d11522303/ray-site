#22DaysToTheTourLeaderExam

38 At a famous attraction, you can admire a cave of blue-light glowworms and take a boat to slowly view limestone landforms such as stalactites and stalagmites. In which country can this wonder be seen? (A) New Zealand (B) England (C) Norway (D) Madagascar

The answer is (A)

Luckily Australia is not among the answers to this question, because most Taiwanese tour groups arrange New Zealand's glowworm itineraries. But if you are in Australia you can join a self-guided tour: Springbrook National Park in Brisbane, Queensland, also has similar fungus gnats (Glow Worms) that emit "blue light" and spin silk. If you want to join, click the link below to see beautiful pictures of the glowworms, and if the weather is good that night, you also get stargazing.
