2025.5.8

At noon I strolled off campus for beef soup dumplings. A kind of relaxed, romantic feeling I once longed for and experienced returned to my body, and I could feel happiness and dreams again.

I have always loved fantasy and dreaming. I always hope that, beyond reality, life has a different outlook to answer this ruthless world. Take lunch, for example. Why should it only be about filling the stomach? In the past I would imagine, in a calm and cheerful mood, breathing the gentle air, looking at the flowers and grass beside the path, stepping towards the canteen one step at a time, and ordering a meal that satisfies my taste and all five senses, imagining everything beautiful and every day peaceful.

True, the world isn't like that, but surely I have the right to fantasise. Why should this ability be taken from me, forcing me to face reality at every moment? I don't like it and I am unwilling, so I will practise. I will practise bringing this ability back to myself and keeping it firmly in my soul. Because of this, I can love myself, like the environment and people around me, fall in love with this world again, and walk happily towards the end of my life.
