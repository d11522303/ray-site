I read an article I found really useful. If you are also someone who often says "sorry" for fear of "troubling others", you can take a look at your everyday habits in study and work.

What I found most useful were the last three habits on the list. What I had always understood as the habit of "giving up" is, in Mr. Nakajima's article, a necessary "pause". But these habits should be developed in order: first, in your work and life, keep putting in earnest effort and face the challenges of work and life with energy! Only when you truly cannot go on should you take a break for a while.

If you move the later habits to the front, sighing all day that you are tired with nothing really happening, and keeping your life on pause every moment, then that may not be self-affirmation but self-abandonment!

15 habits to drop and habits to keep for people with high self-esteem

Author: Teru Nakajima

<<Habits for gaining a positive self>>

1. Gladly accept other people's compliments

2. Recognise that critics will always exist: the 2-6-2 rule of relationships

3. Magnify your small joys and happiness

4. Create a home where you can return to your true self

<<Time alone>>

5. Step away from your phone and the internet and set aside time for yourself

6. Do not cling to your original plans

7. Review the things you are doing but don't really like

8. Do things that make both you and the people around you happy

9. Reduce small hesitations like "what to eat" and "what to wear"; accumulated small hesitations drain your energy

<<Habits for firm conviction>>

10. Be a bit selfish, and be humane to yourself

11. Find what you want to do, and keep taking on challenges to do something for yourself

12. Act right away on whatever you want to do

<<What if you are tired and have no motivation?>>

13. Take a break, sleep well, and wait for yourself

14. Stop pushing yourself and let yourself off the hook

15. Time spent doing nothing (spacing out) will strengthen your self-affirmation
