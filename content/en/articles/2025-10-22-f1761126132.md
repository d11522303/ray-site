[HWU Travel Management USR Course x Lion Travel Young Lion Guide and Leader Programme]

Thank you to Vice President Lai and HR Director Lee of Lion Travel for visiting our campus today to promote Lion Travel's [Young Lion Guide and Leader Programme]. In the excellent practical presentation, Vice President Lai also mentioned the outstanding performance of alumnae of our Department of Travel Management within the company, and hopes to find travel elites through this programme.

The Department of Travel Management at Hsing Wu University has always held that learning is not just studying marketing and management in the classroom, but taking students out of the classroom to see Taiwan, experience cruises and railways, and put talent cultivation and environmental sustainability into practice. Today Lion Travel has launched the "Young Lion Guide and Leader Programme", giving the school a chance to experience hands-on tour leading in Taiwan's green itineraries and sustainable railway tourism.

The course held at Lion Travel's headquarters combines 👉 tour guide and tour leader training + lectures by industry instructors + on-site guiding practice, helping students go from "loving travel" to "being able to lead groups" and creating the most competitive new generation of travel professionals ✨

✨Eligibility: students aged 18 or above who are interested in travel and tour leader work

💡Registration period: from now until 2025.12.14

📅Class time: 2026.01.10 to 2026.02.14 (one class per week)

The registration method for the Lion Young Lion programme has been posted in the department's group chat. Students should put their passion into their résumés and personal statements. Travel Management students are encouraged to take part in the micro-credit courses and teacher consultations offered on campus. The teachers will teach you hand in hand how to write a résumé and personal statement. I look forward to seeing everyone enter the travel industry and head out into the world.
