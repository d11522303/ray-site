6 April 1995 @ Ho Chi Minh City University of Agriculture and Forestry Academic Exchange, Day 3

The third day was the main academic day of the exchange, so that no one would think we were just there to play without attending classes or giving reports.

In those days all presentations could only show text on a blackboard, explained through speech and action. That morning our exchange was on culture and education, and the presentations were drawn or written in chalk on the blackboard before being delivered. Many of the Vietnamese female students wore the traditional Vietnamese dress, the áo dài, that day. It is the modern dress of the Vietnamese Kinh people and is very beautiful. Later, some of our classmates had them tailor-made at their own expense, and wearing them they looked extraordinarily elegant.

In the afternoon, the department head arranged for our group of Fu Jen students to visit a Catholic language school. The participants in the exchange were all people of faith, of various ages, and most came mainly to learn English. The department head also mentioned that they had no freedom of religion, so looking back, learning English was an excuse, and pursuing their faith may well have been the main reason. But having the chance to interact with us was an interesting experience for both sides.

In fact, I don't remember the other details of the trip very well. I'm leaving it in writing here also because in a few more years even the words and memories may gradually fade. This is for myself to look at. If any seniors or classmates don't want the group photos shared publicly, send Ruey-Feng a private message!
