[Kaohsiung Hospitality in the South, Hsing Wu in the North]

Someone online was promoting a website to check when choosing a department, so out of curiosity I looked up whether my own department had anything similar (link in the comments). It turns out that only National Kaohsiung University of Hospitality and Tourism and Hsing Wu have a [Department of Travel Management] in the whole country. Parents and students always ask what we study. Taking the Department of Travel Management's teaching goal, "begin with the end in mind, practical and applicable", I'll explain directly with the list of internship hosts for the 47 third-year students we are busy matching, with figures as of 21 May 2026:

#YearRoundWorkplaceInternship. Not a study programme, not a course experience, and certainly not some kind of after-school daycare, but the most practical arena for vocational students. From 1 July, these students will get their mindset in order and their professional skills ready for a process of 11 to 13 months of real professional combat. Along the way there will be assessments, apprenticeships, business trips and customer service, and also the realities of work: not adapting, being unhappy, not getting enough sleep, standing all day, and even being dismissed from training or transferred to another unit.

#VocationalEducation cannot look only at enrolment rates or only at the course list. The same course produces different results under different teaching models. Students and parents should both consider whether their 20-year-old darling has a chance to turn into a mature adult.
