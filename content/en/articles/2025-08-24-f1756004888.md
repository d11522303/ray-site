2025.8.24

Isn't this how I work too? I often worry for no reason, and things end up worse for it.

Professor Tai-Ting Chen taught us how to use sport psychology to learn to encourage and persuade ourselves.

1. Focus on the "process," not the "result"

(1) What do you most want to challenge in your project plan?

(2) Which detail do you hope to keep well under control in the project?

(3) What new strategy do you want to try?

2. Redefine what "success" means

(1) I was calmer in this project presentation than last time!

(2) This time I tried a new approach, and it was creative.

(3) This time the project management and division of labour were more mature!

3. Accumulate "controllable small wins"

(1) Each day, write up one session's worth of one component of the project plan and share my reflections.

(2) After finishing work each day, go for a 3 km run to build fitness and release stress.

(3) Each time a small step is completed, treat myself to a good meal and tell myself I'm great!

Finally, I want to encourage myself with this line the professor wrote: "I'm not just a practice-session player. I can also let go and give it everything in the match, enjoy the moment, and play my best self!" Work hard for yourself, and enjoy the sense of achievement for yourself.
