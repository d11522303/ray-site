2025.7.16

Is it wrong to have no great ambitions and just be yourself?

To lie flat, you need something to fall back on! You open your eyes and see the air conditioner in the room blowing cool air evenly, with the bright sun blazing outside the shut window. What do you think of?

I think of how the air blowing out is bought with money. Three summers ago, perhaps because of the pandemic, our household electricity bill came to more than NT$10,000. Thankfully it was just that once; the months before and after were still in the thousands, which is reasonable and manageable.

Just that one time, it was out of control and there was nowhere to turn for help, so I obediently paid the power company. I truly felt that every resource around us can be quantified, and if you don't pay attention it can suddenly spiral out of control to an unacceptable level.

This bout of illness is slowly ending. I sorted out the dates on my prescriptions and thought it over: this past month began with the pressure of taking over industry-academia cooperation and preparing for the TOEIC. My mouth said it was fine, but I spent the whole month trapped in a struggle between mind and body.

The other day I talked with my father about the competition between myself and my child over studies, work and all kinds of values. His answer was simply that we should be spectators and not care too much. That irritated me a little. When I came of age, he was nearly fifty and spent all day wishing he could retire and not work. The whole family once asked whether he'd like to drive a taxi or learn a small skill like fixing appliances, so that he'd at least have something to do rather than watching TV at home all day. Later he even copied rich people and took up golf. After I had a child, I got so angry that I stopped his monthly filial allowance, and he was unhappy about it, even hiring a car to come to Hualien to scold me.

Spending money is like breathing: no learning required. Lying flat is an even more innate ability. It saddens me that my own family has this mindset, and I don't know why, since childhood I have been rebellious and contemptuous of this pattern, insisting on fighting to the bitter end over everything, though I have stumbled along all the way to today.
