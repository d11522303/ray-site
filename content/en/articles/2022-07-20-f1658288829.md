Today's Most Beautiful: Hua-Hua-Hua made the news, so share it quickly with family and friends!

For the past few years, Hua-Hua-Hua was a student in Teacher Kang Bo-Kai's local-creation special programme at Hsing Wu University (HWU). I was only an occasional flower-arranging teacher (a flower-arranging teacher, not a floral-art teacher!) who took part in some of the courses, and sometimes helped the team think through ideas and shape the tour-planning courses. I truly feel that this five-year programme has accomplished something, some kind of milestone! It seems I can see a small spark of vocational education developing across disciplines.

Every time I joined the exchanges in this class, I always felt the teaching content was somehow unfinished, that the teaching design could add a little more of this and a little less of that, so that students and teachers could exchange ideas and think together. And because HWU has such a diverse, cross-disciplinary educational environment, I have come to know each student's potential better.

Teacher Jiang, Hua-Hua-Hua, graduated from HWU in 2021. In 2022 we began to see her in the Linkou community, in the old street, at the community base and on campus, becoming an integrator, working hard to let her small efforts shine as brightly as they can. Even family and friends who saw the Lelin Association's post (see attached image) in the Facebook group "Linkou Big Family" said how good and thoughtful the course is.

Please give Teacher Jiang a round of applause, and look forward to her continuing to hold on to her ideals. And let me say too, Kang Bo-Kai, I truly admire you.
