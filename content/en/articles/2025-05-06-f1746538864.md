published: true
---

2025.5.6

More than three years ago, at the homeroom teacher orientation for new students, every student reported in wearing a mask. As the pandemic rose and fell, we met sometimes online and sometimes in person, but we only ever saw eyes, with the whole face wrapped in a mask. I also remember the parents' briefing specially held for the new class. That day we received only one set of parents, and their daughter did not show up, but the parents sat beside me with expectation and concern and we talked and talked.

Perhaps because of the masks, looking into the parents' eyes and listening to their expectations, I could feel more and deeper hopes.

I often see people on social media criticising universities for still holding parent meetings!

Yes, we insist on them!

Teaching and education need communication. The teachers of the Department of Travel Management have not forgotten their original intention in devoting themselves to education. Coming from industry, we try even harder to find ways of nurturing and training that suit vocational students. What cannot be made up in class, we teach again after class for free. For the individual differences and personalities among students, we talk heart to heart one on one, encouraging them to try again and again and to move forward bravely.

Licences, competitions, projects, assignments, clubs, the student union and other activities: we train according to each student's characteristics, so that every student can win their own sense of achievement and become the person they most want to be.

The girl in the video is the student who did not attend the parents' meeting that day more than three years ago. She is now an intern at STARLUX Airlines. In early May the company notified the department that her internship performance was excellent and that she would be converted to a full-time employee after graduation. We are happy for the student, and even more proud of having met the parents' expectations at the time.

A few days later, the girl thoughtfully made a short video for her teacher, who was driven half mad by recruitment work, and left a message in LINE: "Thank you, teacher, for your guidance and care. I'm so happy I could help you!"

We teach professional skills, we teach cultivation, we teach manners, and we turn out students we are proud of ourselves.
