2026.8.15 Doctoral Programme 2/1447

This weekend I did two important things, different from my past behaviour.

First, I took the initiative to invite students to dinner and paid for it myself, chatting with students from my three homeroom classes to learn what they and their classmates are up to, and also to understand what young people today are actually pursuing in life. Second, I said no to my family. I really was a bit unwell, but I rarely turn down my family. This time it was truly for my own good: letting myself put things down and fight for some time to rest. It weighs on me a bit, but I think it must have been a good decision.

At UBC I saw this English children's book published in 2026, "Choose Happy". This is really not easy, because habits formed by the world make us accommodate our own unhappiness without realising, and in the end it is always a string of regrets. Happiness disappears through my not choosing it, and over time happiness no longer comes to find me, because I always refuse it.

From now on, in everything I will choose happiness. In mind, action and thought, I will choose happiness from start to finish, try to give myself a smile, let happiness surround me, and let my mind be freer.
