22/678 "1643, the 16th Year of Chongzhen"

# MEG's first grand-strategy decision GAI text game    # Come and rewrite 17th-century history

If one day you became the central decision-maker deciding the fate of a dynasty, how would you change history? In the 16th year of Chongzhen (1643), the Ming dynasty is tossed by storms. Peasant uprisings break out within, the Qing army watches covetously from without, and the court's finances are near collapse. At this turning point in history, you are ordered into the cabinet as a young minister, to deliberate on affairs of state together with the #GenAI character, the famous minister Ni Yuanlu. Faced with crisis after crisis, each of your decisions may affect the course of the court, and even rewrite the Ming's ultimate fate. You can also see the historical progression after the simulated storyline!
