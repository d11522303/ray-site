<figure class="ph">Photo</figure><p>A few weeks ago, a senior alumna from our Department of Travel Management who works at Taiwan High Speed Rail (THSR) messaged me about a summer internship opportunity. I immediately forwarded it to my homeroom class of students about to enter their third year, encouraging anyone interested to apply. Congratulations to the students who have just received notice that they passed the first round of document screening. Here is the latest interview content for the 2023 THSR internship. I hope the last-minute cramming the teacher has gathered for you will help, so hold on tight to it.<br/><br/>The content below is compiled from the written-test experiences of all the intern students, and I'd like to thank the alumna who often provides internship opportunities, as well as the generations of interns who keep learning through their interviews.<br/>------------ This is a divider ----------------</p><p>Round two: Written test (2023.4.16)<br/>Location: THSR Operations Building in Taoyuan, Exit 5 of THSR Taoyuan Station</p><p><b>English:</b> Cloze, grammar, reading comprehension<br/>50 questions, 30 minutes<br/>TIPS: English depends on real ability. If you don't have it, go with your gut feeling or just fill in C.</p><p><b>Logic:</b> Similar to an aptitude test, spatial and figure reasoning<br/>50 questions, 22 minutes<br/>TIPS: You'll be shown four figures and have to choose the fifth. You can practise this beforehand.</p><p><b>Mathematical analysis:</b> Maths word problems (triangle angles, lengths and widths, circles, etc.)<br/>35 questions, 10 minutes<br/>TIPS: Finish the ones you can do first and leave the ones you can't blank. Don't get stuck on hard problems. Use the 10 minutes to find the questions you have a chance of getting right.</p><p><b>Essay topics:</b> Two essays, at least 200 characters each, 15 minutes<br/>Sample topic: How to become an outstanding THSR employee<br/>Sample topic: The value of high speed rail to Taiwan<br/>Sample topic: My understanding of THSR service<br/>Sample topic: The responsibilities THSR bears<br/><b>Scenario questions:</b><br/>If all seats are full over a long weekend, how would you calm the passengers?<br/>If someone in a group misses the train, how would you handle the passengers' complaints?<br/>TIPS: I suggest students first write a 200-character draft based on the company introduction on the THSR <a target="_blank" rel="noopener" href="https://www.thsrc.com.tw/corp">company website</a>, memorise it, and then rewrite it by copying, reciting and adapting. Writing will then go smoothly.<br/><br/>Actually, all we teachers can do these days is, before the THSR document screening, nag everyone about approaching deadlines, help tidy up fragmented résumés and personal statements full of family members and total freedom of style, and send them off once they look presentable. Before the exam we say a cheerful "you're all great", and afterwards we say "it's okay, try again next time", and so on.<br/>Beyond that, teachers are honestly pretty useless. It is these brave students who dare to try who are truly admirable.</p><p>Wishing everyone success in the written test.</p>
