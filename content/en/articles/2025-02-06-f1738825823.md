2025.2.6

First thing this morning I chatted with Ms. Li of Southeast Travel about the details of industry-academia cooperation, and only then could I relax and get ready to head out.

Before the semester began I had planned to ride Fatty Di (my scooter) from Linkou to Hsinchu. Besides saving nearly a thousand dollars in shipping fees and the time cost, the truth is that I wanted a reason to ride and see the north-south trunk road I have long wanted to walk more of.

As soon as I left Linkou I got lost by the mountainside, and the navigation led me wrong all the way to Qingpu. Thinking about it afterwards, the new route was a nice experience anyway. I looked at the map and replanned my direction, and along the way there was the Taoyuan Lantern Festival, ponds, and new roads by the High Speed Rail that I had never been on. It was a real pleasure to ride out alone during the winter break.

I really feel I should find a little time outside work to wander out and feel the wind!

The scooter ride from Linkou to Hsinchu takes only two hours. I had a meal with my son in front of Tsing Hua University. It was a bit rushed, but seeing him always puts my mind at ease. My child has grown up while I still feel like a kid myself, and there is a little ache and reluctance in that, but also a feeling of great happiness. Working to earn money and bring happiness to my family is the biggest source of my sense of achievement.

I hopped on a Kuo-Kuang bus back to Linkou, and next I need to spend more time preparing for work. Remember to stay cheerful and walk step by step in a happy mood! Don't panic! Don't worry needlessly!
