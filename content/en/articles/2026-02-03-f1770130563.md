(A) Gusuku Sites and Related Properties of the Kingdom of Ryukyu (B) Himeji Castle (C) Iwami Ginzan Silver Mine (D) Korakuen Garden in Okayama City (2024 Overseas Tour Leader Tourism Resources exam)

Jomon Prehistoric Sites in Northern Japan (2021)

Sado Island Gold Mines (2024)

In fact, only a small number of the sites above are well-known tourist attractions. But because the definition of World Heritage is not tourism-oriented, rather it is established because the cultural or natural resource holds an irreplaceable position and needs protection. For example, Mount Fuji's World Heritage status has been discussed in recent years because of excessive climbing activity and tourist behaviour, and from 2025 climbing has been regulated.

If a World Heritage site is poorly managed, it is first placed on the "List of World Heritage in Danger", and if there is still no improvement it is formally removed. There are historical precedents: the Dresden Elbe Valley in Germany and Liverpool Maritime Mercantile City in the UK were removed because of over-development.

#1 Located in Kagoshima Prefecture, Japan, with "Jomon Sugi" trees thousands of years old on the island, and said to have inspired the animation master Hayao Miyazaki to create Princess Mononoke, this natural heritage site is: (A) Shiretoko (B) Shirakami-Sanchi (C) Yakushima (D) Ogasawara Islands

Answer (C)

#2 The most recent site that Japan had inscribed on the UNESCO World Heritage List (cultural heritage) in 2024 is: (A) Jomon Prehistoric Sites in Northern Japan (B) Sado Island Gold Mines (C) Mozu-Furuichi Kofun Group (D) Amami-Oshima and Tokunoshima Islands

Answer (B)
