Before the holiday, after the exam, I chatted with my students. The sophomores in my class will face corporate internships in just a year. Watching the juniors keep announcing the companies that have accepted them, I could feel that everyone has a sense of urgency.

Me: Why do you keep working part-time?

Student: Teacher, I need money!

Me: If you work at McDonald's every day after class and on weekends, about how much do you earn a month?

Student: About NT$10,000, depending on how many hours I put in.

Me: McDonald's is very busy, so you have no time to prepare for the self-improvement content from your classes!

Student: Probably, but I still really want to get my certificates and learn useful tools.

Me: Then you need to plan your time, make good use of the school's resources, and come to class and keep up with the schedule.

Student: Teacher, do I have a chance to intern like the seniors do at #StarLuxAirlines #EVAAir #UNIAir #LionTravel #TaiwanHighSpeedRail?

Me: Of course, but opportunities go to those who are prepared. When the seniors intern, they are treated just like full-time staff. Some companies even offer interns NT$28,000, which is much better than the NT$10,000 you're working yourself half to death for. You'd also get regular days off, even two-day weekends, and after graduation you have a chance to be converted to a full-time employee.

Student: Thank you, Teacher. I'll think about it carefully.

Me: Remember, don't just think about it! Plan and take action! I'm always here for you.
