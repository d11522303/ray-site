<p>If a family member suddenly falls ill, and you are at work and swamped, how would you cope? Take leave and go home to care for them? Ask a family member who doesn't work to do it?</p><p><b>The Barthel Index</b></p><p>Step 1: Take your family member to apply for a Barthel Index assessment.<br/>Step 2: Apply for a foreign caregiver through a legal manpower agency.<br/>Step 3: A suitable foreign caregiver arrives at your home.</p><p>The three steps above are a simplified process I put together after making enquiries. Let me first list just the costs, so you can see whether you can afford to hire a full-time foreign home caregiver.</p><p><b>How the Fees Work for an Indonesian Manpower Agency</b></p><p>Domestic agency fee: NT$28,000 (this was obtained by phone; I will update it with the actual amount paid)<br/>Overseas paperwork fee: a little over NT$20,000 (also obtained by phone; I will update it with the actual amount paid)<br/>Scope of service: recruiting and interviewing the foreign worker, communication between both sides about needs and the process of coming to Taiwan, and communication about adjusting after arriving at the home. These are one-off fees collected by the agency.</p><p>Next is the foreign worker's monthly pay: the household pays NT$26,400 (the minimum wage), which includes national health insurance, the employment stabilisation fee and so on, and the worker takes home about NT$21K. So for a family, on average, each foreign worker means an extra NT$50,000 in application fees, plus NT$320,000 a year in salary costs for the worker.</p><figure class="ph">Photo</figure><p><b>After Applying, Expect to Wait Three to Four Months for the Worker to Arrive</b></p><p>From my phone enquiries, it takes three to four months from application until an Indonesian worker arrives in Taiwan. Every family in need should allow this much time for administration and care, in case an urgent need suddenly arises. And this is only the beginning, so I have started recording what I experience, turning what I originally worried about into a process I handle, growing old step by step together with my family.</p>
