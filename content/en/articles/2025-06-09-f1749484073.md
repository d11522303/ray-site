2025.6.9

Today a student asked me, where is your home? I thought about it for a long time. All I could answer was that I live in Linkou now, but I could not say where my home is, because I have moved countless times since childhood.

0 I was born in Taichung. Before military service I lived with my parents and moved through the following places:

1-2 Rented a place in Yonghe

2 Factory dormitory in Guandu

2-4 Rented a place in Guandu

5-12 Apartment in Banqiao

12-22 Apartment in Zhonghe, and a rental on Lane 514 near Fu Jen

23-24 During military service, trained in Chiayi and was assigned to the Sixth Army in Longgang, Taoyuan. After discharge I married right away, and as a young couple we wandered from place to place looking for somewhere to live:

24-25 Rented a place on Tongshan Street, Taipei

25-29 Stayed with relatives in Sanchong

29 Stayed at my mother-in-law's home in Guting

30-34 Bought an apartment in Muzha

34-35 Rented in the Lianhua building in Hualien

35-40 Bought a home in the Zhixue community in Shoufeng

40-41 Bought in a building in Meilun, Hualien

42 Rented in Linkou

43-53 Bought in a building in Linkou

I have changed homes 16 times, but whatever happened I have always lived with my family: first with Dad and Mom, and later with a family of my own, from two people to three. I still don't know whether I will move again, but one thing I am sure of: wherever my family is together, that is where my home is.
