Not long ago the college office held a retirement recognition ceremony, giving professors a little time to share their thoughts. Several of them used staff numbers 079 or 078 to show they had devoted 35 or 36 years to education, and had finally reached mandatory retirement age, full of joy.

The attached picture shows the 1992 (ROC year 81) admission scores for five-year junior colleges in northern Taiwan. Counting up, there were only a few such colleges back then, and our Hsing Wu had only five departments: Accounting, International Trade, Business Administration, Banking and Insurance, and Tourism. Look closely and you will see that Hsing Wu was the only five-year college in northern Taiwan with a Tourism department. The admission score then was not low, and it still often makes senior teachers proud.

Among the schools on the list, Hwa Hsia, Fu Hsing and Ta Han have closed down. By contrast, the national Yilan Agricultural and Industrial college has been upgraded to National Ilan University, and quite a few other schools have been renamed so many times that nobody knows what they are called now!

I took the northern five-year college joint entrance exam in 1987 (ROC year 76). I wonder whether I can find the newspaper from that year?
