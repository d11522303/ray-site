Canada Study Visit Weekly Diary - HWU Travel Management, MOE "Dreams Come True" Overseas Internship Programme EP1-2

It's still a typhoon day off in Taiwan on Saturday, but the wind and rain aren't heavy, so maybe I can go out for a run later. If you're stuck at home, take a look at the weekly diary of our lovely student Ceryna, ten thousand kilometres away. The videos are all made so well, which is truly the strong suit of this new generation of students. The teacher is completely outdone.

----- Ceryna Chen's Weekly Diary -----

On my very first day here I took part in a welcome party hosted by my boss and his wife and their friends. I met many of the local residents and went to Rolly's home to see the game he had hunted, such as bear, deer and leopard. I ate delicious barbecue, cake and cookies. Besides all this, I also tried horse riding here! It was my first time riding a horse, a super cool experience. Although my balance was unsteady and it was a bit scary, this experience made me fall in love with riding, and I hope I will have the chance to ride again!

July 1st is Canada's national day! After work I went to a nearby park and saw some small vendors. In the big pavilion in the centre, everyone was lining up for free hot dogs and pie, then rating which pie was best. There was also a singer performing on stage that day. It let me experience a different country's national-day celebration, and helped me fit in with and understand the local culture faster. Facing the locals' speed of speech and expressions, at first I really had to concentrate extremely hard to get the gist. Later, through continued listening and speaking, I can now understand about seventy percent of the content and reply.

The first thing I learned on the first day of the internship was folding towels and bed sheets. Because there are quite a lot of types, there is a lot to remember. At first my movements were slow, and I would forget how to fold, so too much time was spent on it. When collecting the sheets and towels from rooms, my uncertainty would also stretch something that could be done in 3 minutes to 6 or 7 minutes. From the first day until now, the time I need to tidy a room and fold sheets has dropped greatly! Although I still sometimes fold wrongly, it happens much less. This week I again met many people and have become more and more familiar with the people here. Everyone is truly so good to us! This place has become my favourite place! I'll keep working hard for the next three weeks!
