A few days ago, at my child's invitation, I went online and half-heartedly pressed a few times on the Popcat game. This is the screenshot from that day.

Today my child sent me another Popcat-related story, about the protests in Thailand, and asked what I thought. They said that in a country where nobody is held to account, the vaccines have gone to the powerful and the wealthy, while ordinary people can only accept with resignation that the virus spreads and their families fall ill.

The world has changed enormously in recent days, with the Haiti earthquake, the Afghan coup and so on, and the Thai people have turned to Popcat to win the world's support.

But mountains collapse and people run away.

While the whole world is in chaos, people in every country can only rely on their own efforts to fight for their rights and to watch whether the state machinery is really serving its citizens. When their rights are already being affected, they need ways to resist; otherwise, in the end it will be a contest of fists rather than of heads.
