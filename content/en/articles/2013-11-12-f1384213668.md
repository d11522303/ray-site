---
title: "Guangfeng Farmers' Association Holds a Matai'an Creative Ingredients Travel Itinerary Design Competition"
excerpt: "A call for entries to a Matai'an creative-ingredient tour design contest in Guangfu, Hualien, with NT$50,000 for first prize and a deadline of 5 December."
published: true
---

Guangfeng Farmers' Association, with the theme of developing the Matai'an leisure agriculture area, is holding a Matai'an Creative Ingredients Travel Itinerary Design Competition. The organiser hopes entrants will use ingredients grown and sold locally in Matai'an as the basis for creative products, and combine them with other distinctive local resources in Guangfu Township, innovatively pairing local ingredients and integrating the agricultural resources of the leisure agriculture area and its surroundings, such as agricultural production, farmers' daily life, rural ecology, cultural character and natural scenery. By linking these with agriculture-related activities and bringing out local character, entrants should design a diverse, refined and distinctive form of agricultural tourism.

1. Deadline: 5:00 p.m. on 5 December

2. Anyone interested in this itinerary design competition, whether an individual or a team, may enter. Entries are being accepted from now until 5:00 p.m. on 5 December this year. At 9:00 a.m. on 6 December, the organiser will invite a panel of judges to review the entries on the second floor of the Guangfeng Farmers' Association supermarket. The scoring criteria are theme creativity (30%), creative ingredients (20%), travel itinerary (20%) and overall completeness (30%).

3. The top three entries will be selected. First prize is NT$50,000, second prize NT$30,000 and third prize NT$20,000. The winners will be announced in an advertisement in the Keng Sheng Daily News on 10 December this year.

4. Entrants should submit their work, including an electronic file, to the Extension Section of Guangfeng Farmers' Association, at No. 1-1, Ziqiang Road, Datong Village, Guangfu Township, Hualien County.
