Singapore's COMPASS Framework and Its Top Universities

Singapore has in recent years been the country most wealthy people want to move to, and its passport is the strongest in 2024. But I am very afraid of heat and would rather emigrate to a northern country than choose a place where you can't wear winter clothes. Anyway, I don't qualify as wealthy at all, so I'm overthinking this.

While looking things up, I saw the points table published by Singapore's Ministry of Manpower. For education, the points fall into three tiers: 0 points for high school or below, 10 points for an ordinary university, and 20 points for a top university. I have heard from someone who once worked at a Singaporean agency in Taiwan that Singapore's immigration authorities even keep a list of Taiwanese universities that don't count for points at all. So there are schools that can't even be classed as ordinary universities. Other countries have their own views too.

Only three Taiwanese schools are listed in category A as global "top universities", which seems rather different from what we ourselves assume. Also, Singapore, which focuses on the tourism industry, lists the tourism management and tourism studies students of one well-known university as a Category B designated subject, and I truly feel Singapore's respect for tourism expertise.
