2025.4.19 Third Life Countdown: 834 days

Every spring it is easy to feel vexed. It is too late to apply for a doctoral programme, and when I want to plan a trip I find the air tickets too expensive and my wallet empty. The weather goes hot and cold, and my wardrobe is as messy as my mood. When I look back at my age and my expectations, I am thoroughly unsettled.

Early this morning I read this passage, from the precepts left by Tokugawa Ieyasu, whose full text can be seen at Kunozan Toshogu in Shizuoka. I can't read Japanese very well, so I asked AI to translate it into classical Chinese. By understanding the words over and over, my restlessness eased a little.

Life is like carrying a heavy load on a long journey; do not hurry.

If you take lack of freedom as normal, nothing will feel insufficient.

When desire arises in your heart, remember the times of hardship.

Patience is the root of lasting peace.

Treat anger as your enemy.

To know only how to win and not how to lose is to invite disaster upon yourself.

Blame yourself, not others.

Falling short is better than going too far.

Translating it again into modern vernacular Chinese seems to lose some of the thoughtfulness. I prefer classical Chinese myself, since I can compare it against my own state of mind to interpret what Ieyasu was thinking back then.

人の一生は重荷を負ひて遠き道をゆくが如し。｜A person's life is like carrying a heavy burden along a long road.

いそぐべからず。｜Do not rush; move forward slowly.

不自由を常と思へば不足なし。｜If you take inconvenience as normal, you will not feel any lack.

こころに望おこらば困窮したる時を思ひ出すべし。｜When desire arises in your heart, remember the days of hardship.

堪忍は無事長久の基。｜Patience is the foundation of a peaceful and lasting life.

いかりは敵と思へ。｜Treat anger as an enemy.

勝事ばかり知りて、まくる事を知らざれば害其の身に至る。｜If you only know how to pursue victory and do not know how to accept defeat, harm will come to you.

おのれを責めて人を責むるな。｜Reflect on yourself instead of blaming others.

及ばざるは過ぎたるに勝れり。｜Falling short is better than overdoing it.
