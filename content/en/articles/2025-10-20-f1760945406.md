[International Students] EP2

Although we see foreign service workers and students in daily life, and our department also has more and more of them, in the attached photo are first-year freshmen who arrived in Taiwan just last month. I'm curious about what the real numbers are.

According to Taiwan's Ministry of Education statistics: in academic year 113 (2024-25), the total number of students in colleges and universities was 1,074,365, and the total number of overseas students was 123,188, 11.47% of all students. In academic year 107 (2018-19), when overseas student numbers were at their highest, the total was 129,207 out of 1,244,822 university students, or 10.37%. In 107 the top source was mainland China with nearly 30,000 students (see attached figure). For example, Zhao Lusi, a graduate of Minghsin University, came to study in Taiwan at that time. Next was Malaysia with 16,640, and most of these students are ethnically Chinese.
