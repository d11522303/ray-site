Among the deadliest human wars in the world, ranked by number of deaths, first place goes to the Second World War, followed by the Mongol invasions of Eurasia. Third is the war of the Three Kingdoms of Wei, Shu and Wu after the collapse of the Han Empire. The novel *Romance of the Three Kingdoms* is a tragedy written with 34 million lives. Given East Asia's population then, nearly everyone on the land lived in fear. If you list the later wars too, you will be even more surprised how many bloody wars there were in your own region's history!

5. Qing conquest of China (25 million)

6. First World War (22 million)

7. Timur's westward expansion (17 million)

8. Tang An Lushan Rebellion (13 million)

9. Russian Civil War (10 million)
