1995.4.4 @ Nong Lam University Ho Chi Minh City Academic Exchange D1

During spring break 31 years ago, I went with my fellow fourth-year sociology classmates from Fu Jen and the third-year juniors to Nong Lam University in Ho Chi Minh City for an academic exchange. I remember landing at Ho Chi Minh airport on EVA Air, still wearing the sweater I had set out in, and being hit by the heat coming at me. I asked the driver, and it was 34 degrees Celsius. In the airport, besides the recently launched EVA flight, all I saw were Russian-made combat helicopters parked on the hangar.

After landing at Ho Chi Minh City International Airport, a special car took us straight to the national agriculture and forestry university, where many Vietnamese students had come to welcome us. Unfortunately I had a film camera then and had brought only 3 or 4 rolls of 36 exposures. I remember that in those days a roll of Kodak or Fujifilm cost about NT$100, and developing back in Taiwan wasn't cheap either, so not many photos remain. The ones you see today were converted to digital files from the film at a shop in Gongguan.

Once at the university, we were taken directly to the air-conditioned scholars' dormitory and met the local university students in its common areas. In my memory my English was terrible; I could understand but could hardly speak, and I was flustered. The heat was also hard to get used to, and eventually I got heatstroke and a fever, and missed one of the interactive activities. I still remember how self-doubting and annoying that felt.

If classmates and juniors see the photos, you are very welcome to message me privately to share your memories of those days. These are fragments of my memory, and I welcome you to share our youthful years together.
