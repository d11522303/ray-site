On National Day I didn't feel like watching the parade, so I went online to look at how Kyoto Tachibana University in Japan has developed and arranged its departments, and compared that with the academic classification of Taiwan's National Science and Technology Council (NSTC). In short, universities focus on research, so how a university arranges its departments makes clear what counts as science, and the NSTC classification is much the same, planning similar fields of study.

Below are the NSTC's 698 fields, covering main and sub-categories. It is long and has a lot of entries, but it is a standard worth consulting. The link to Kyoto Tachibana University's department pages is in the comments. After comparing them with the content of Taiwan's higher education departments, I decided to go to the sports field and run 3,000 metres first!

A strong country starts with a strong body! Don't overthink it!
