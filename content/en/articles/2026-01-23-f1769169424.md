Before the Tour Guide and Tour Leader Exams, EP49

#TourLeaderExamCountdown50Days

10. What is "Lawrence of Arabia" famous for?

(A) Leading the Arabs against the Ottoman Turkish Empire

(B) Helping the Arabs build the Suez Canal

(C) Leading the Arabs in opposing the Jewish return to Palestine

(D) Leading the Arabs in resisting British, French and Italian colonial rule

This question is a rewrite of a past exam question. The answer is simply (A). "Lawrence of Arabia" refers to the British officer Thomas Edward Lawrence (T. E. Lawrence). During World War I he was sent by Britain to the Middle East as liaison officer to the Arab Revolt forces. He successfully incited and helped the Arab tribes unite, using guerrilla tactics to harass and strike the forces of the Ottoman Empire (Turkey was then Germany's ally), and ultimately helped the Allies occupy Damascus.

I don't quite understand why the current tour leader exam asks this question. Because of the situation in the Middle East, few tours go there now, and it's hard to see why, even in the days when religious tours and Egypt tours were popular, Lawrence of Arabia would be an exam topic. Perhaps the question-setter had seen this classic film and could not forget it!
