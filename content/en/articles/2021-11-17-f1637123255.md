---
title: "A Bitter Memory of Teaching at Fanji Senior High School"
excerpt: "Looking back on a year of weekly teaching support at Fanji, its bright students, and the falling birth rate that now threatens schools of every kind."
published: true
---

What a wretched industry!

This time last year, I was still going to Fanji every week to support a full day of teaching activities, and it is a very good memory. After finishing four morning classes, I could follow the teachers to the main hall to get the group meal. Students and teachers alike ate four dishes and a soup, and I would take my own reusable bowl, serve myself, and sit in the quiet hall eating with the teachers and staff. I did not have to go out looking for food, and I could finish quickly and rest before the consecutive afternoon classes.

The students were chatty too. Everyone knew I was a teacher from Hsing Wu University, though in fact they were not even taking the technical-college joint entrance exam; they had all finished the General Scholastic Ability Test and were preparing for regular universities. Perhaps because they knew I was there out of bitterness, they were even more considerate and asked about the plight of the education industry. The students felt strongly about the career prospects of university departments and the falling birth rate affecting enrolment. A classmate even joked that across the street Wende Girls' High School has hardly any students left, and if a school is going to close, Wende would go first.

In fact, when I taught the senior-year tourism and travel practice course at Fanji last year, the students were all very motivated, and the Fanji teachers worked hard at teaching. The students did well on the scholastic ability test, and most could get into national universities. The school is also conveniently located on the Neihu MRT line.
