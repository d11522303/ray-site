[Admission Routes for Universities of Technology and Technical Colleges, Academic Year 113]

This morning I received the PDF from the Strategy Committee for Universities of Technology, more than fifty pages, posted publicly on FB for reference.

The Academic Year 113 Unified Entrance Exam for Technological and Vocational Colleges (a bit like the five-year junior college joint entrance exam we had in the past) takes place next weekend. Many students around us will be sitting the exam for two days, so please cheer them on.

According to the Technological and Vocational Education Entrance Examination Center Foundation:

This year, 2024 (Academic Year 113), 69,361 vocational high school students registered for the exam, 4,620 fewer than in Academic Year 112.

There are fewer candidates every year. The four-year universities of technology offer 55,886 places in total, of which national universities of technology account for 13,983. That means about 20% of these candidates will end up at national schools. For group 17, Hospitality and Tourism, which is relevant to HWU's Department of Travel Management, there are 9,127 candidates this year (10,411 last year), a one-tenth drop year on year.

If you've read this far, please also cheer on the admissions teachers at our private universities of technology.
