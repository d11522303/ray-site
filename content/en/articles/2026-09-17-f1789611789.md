At the MEG welcome dinner for new PhD students, five of the six new doctoral students are teachers, spanning junior high school, five-year junior college, general high school, comprehensive high school and university of technology. That fits the research character of our group at NTUST very well.

Research needs to be grounded: starting from practice and moving towards educational practice. It's really wonderful.
