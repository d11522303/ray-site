To the students on the 2015-16 academic year overseas study visits:

Please download the attached template and complete your overseas visit study report according to the following rules:

1. Length: the main text, including photos, must be at least 12 pages, not including attachments.

2. Print in full colour, have it glue-bound, and hand it in to the office on the 6th floor of the college building.

3. Email the electronic file to: h111@mail.hwu.edu.tw

4. Deadline: all reports must be submitted within 2 weeks after the visit ends.

Ningxia group: deadline 12/8

Beijing group: deadline 12/16

Henan group: deadline 12/24 (students who joined the Henan visit last semester are subject to the same deadline)

[Important] Students who submit their report to the college office within 2 weeks after completing the visit can collect a study certificate. Students who wish to have credits exempted should fill in the "Overseas Visit Study Credit Exemption Application Form" and send it to the college office for review. This form will be provided before the start of next semester as the voucher for students to apply for "overseas visit" credit exemption at the Registration Section of the Office of Academic Affairs.
