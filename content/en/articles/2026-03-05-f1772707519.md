Hsing Wu University 2026-27 (ROC year 115) Master's Programme and In-Service Programme Entrance Examination

#MasterOfTourismAndLeisure

Places: #8

Application period: 115.03.02 (Mon) to 115.04.14 (Tue) (2026-03-02 to 2026-04-14)

Application website: #SeeTheComments

Alumnus recommendation: Assistant Vice President Yang Teng-wei

The attached picture shows the soul behind the successful operation of the Wugu "quasi-leisure farm": he is an outstanding alumnus of the Graduate Institute of Tourism and Leisure at Hsing Wu University and is now Assistant Vice President Yang Teng-wei. He spent 20 years in manufacturing and understood that the leisure industry cannot rely on passion alone, so he returned to his alma mater to study and earned a master's degree from the Graduate Institute of Tourism and Leisure.

He especially mentioned that the institute's faculty are rich in learning and the class cases are varied and interesting, which filled the gaps in his industry management knowledge: from case analysis to risk prevention, from on-site processes to customer experience, with an emphasis on being implementable and replicable. He turned the factory's management mindset of efficiency and systems into the farm's management SOPs: on weekdays it focuses on school field trips, large corporate gatherings and social welfare groups, and it opens to individual visitors only on holidays, giving the experience a rhythm and the service quality.
