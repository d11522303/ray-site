2025.2.13 Overcast and rainy, 14°C

Being a family is not easy. My child wanted to travel to Tainan with classmates, so early this morning, clutching my aching stomach, I drove them to the Chang Gung transfer station to catch a Kuo-Kuang bus. My mind went back to 19 years ago and the scene of our family in the delivery room at Cardinal Tien Hospital. From that year on, my wife and I went from depending on each other to being a family of three. Over these dozen-plus years there have been so many drives like this one, me concentrating on the wheel and heading for the destination, while my family sat in the back seat, comfortably enjoying being driven.

Looking at yesterday's photo, although Yen-Ling and I may have eaten too much, or perhaps it was a virus that kept us vomiting and having diarrhoea all night, seeing the picture and thinking of the changes of nearly twenty years moved me deeply. Thank you for being our child. You have grown up now and are preparing to fly higher and farther, with your own friends and life, and your own goals and hopes. But for me, as long as the three of us are safe and healthy, that is enough.

Money really is not easy to earn, but without a healthy body, having anything at all is empty.

Happy birthday to myself.
