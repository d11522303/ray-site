2026.7.19

Listening to someone's show moved me deeply.

Financial freedom is only the beginning; financial confidence is the foundation. Here is the view he shared: you don't have to wait until you've earned a lot and become successful before sharing about money management, because that may leave many people unable to find the details and methods. Instead, he started experimentally with his original intention seven years ago, and he should still be under 30 this year. He shares his expertise and interests with discipline every day, encouraging investors to observe trends and economic figures. I admire him very much.

Freedom in life is the appearance; confidence in your own life is the truth.

I will enter the NTUST Graduate Institute of Applied Science and Technology doctoral programme at 55, as a middle-aged freshman. With full-time work and family responsibilities, how can I maintain my physical strength, brainpower, eyesight, perseverance and mental energy, to achieve trust in life and a sense of its value? Over the next two to four years of study, I will use AI and game-based collaborative learning for coursework, teaching, activities and research. Here I will start sharing the various interesting discoveries I make about technology-applied learning, so that I can find balance among the five powers and have more confidence in life.

"Let the mind arise without dwelling on anything." (the Diamond Sutra)

The heart does not cling to any person, matter or thing, yet can still give and create actively. From now on, make decisions carefully, and as long as what you decide to do is meaningful, give and create actively.
