---
title: "A Chat With Students After Class"
excerpt: "After class, students kept me talking. We covered foreign students, a bad group interview and a legendary colleague, and I told them where teachers' survival instinct comes from."
published: true
---

2025.3.19

A student kept me behind after class and we talked about quite a lot. Looking back, I think I said too much. I shouldn't really have said so much about other people and about myself, but keeping it all inside is hard, and I am dissatisfied with my life and my situation. Exercising or zoning out seems to help, and then again maybe it doesn't.

The student said the school is now full of international students, and that the school could change its name to OO International University of Science and Technology. After he said it, we all laughed.

The student also said that when he went for an interview at Dongnan Travel, the behaviour of his classmates left him a bit stunned. In a one-hour group interview, some people behaved like outsiders, as if the interview had nothing to do with them, so that even the interviewers wondered why they had bothered to come.

The student also mentioned hearing that a certain teacher has been the subject of a lot of controversy recently. All I could say is that this teacher is a legend: in 25 years at the school, something has happened every year. The teacher himself is a legend.

I told the students: you will leave in four years, and this is where teachers have to find their way to survive. In today's storm in higher education, don't underestimate teachers' will to survive. Just graduate obediently and go on to open up your own lives.
