2021 was a year of gratitude

Thank you to my other half, who has accompanied me and put up with my unexplained moods all along

Moved that heaven gave me a handsome son, whose self-discipline and effort have broadened his horizons

Grateful to the senior mentors and superiors who have supported my career and brought me to a new stage

Thankful for the colleagues who work hard alongside me and the thoughtful students

And even more appreciative of every family member and friend: in every way, I can feel your support.

2021 was a year of changing direction, adjusting my focus so that my viewpoint and my heart can be closer to myself. Years of interpersonal overload had suppressed my emotions and spirit. For 2022 I hope to keep running every day, to listen to and read books often, to work hard at starting to write, and to learn how to get along with people. I also hope to have a chance to be admitted to a doctoral programme in science and engineering to learn new things, hoping not only for a change but also for improvement.

Finally, I wish every family member, friend, classmate, comrade, colleague and student clear skies and wishes fulfilled in 2022.
