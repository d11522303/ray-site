#UCLA

It has been a long time since I posted about my child at school, mainly because he says his coursework is very busy and he has homework to write and no time for us. This is probably all an excuse; the main thing is that he wants to be free, left alone, happy and carefree.

In fact, we were the same when we were young, always feeling that our parents were out of touch and kept looking for people all day long. Back then I did not know how to refuse, so I became the family's errand-runner, and over time my parents' behaviour regressed and became irresponsible, while I took on emotional stress, until in my thirties, after having a child, I decided to flee with the whole family to Hualien.

Looking back now, for one's own good one really must find a comfortable space for more than one person. Life is limited and the time we can use is not long. And once physical discomforts slowly arrive, one feels more negative energy about life and no longer has the expectations and imagination of the world that one had when young. The result is that when the environment is best, one has the least time of one's own; when one's own time slowly returns, the body's illnesses and pains gradually catch up too.
