【The 20th Anniversary of Star Princess, the First 100,000-Ton Cruise Ship, at Keelung Port】

This week I finally found these precious images among photos that had been packed up and left unopened through eight house moves. They are all re-photographed from film prints. The people in them are the cruise guide trainees who boarded the Star Princess when she made her maiden call at Keelung in 2002! Thanks to my then classmate Alumi Lu, who recommended this valuable opportunity and let me get on board and broaden my horizons. The deep impressions from that year are still vivid today!

On 25 January 2002, the Star Princess set out from Italy on her brand-new 26-day maiden world voyage, sailing through the Mediterranean, the Suez Canal, the Indian Ocean and the Asia-Pacific region. In the Asia-Pacific she stopped at Keelung Port to promote cruise travel to the business community. It was the first time Keelung Port had welcomed a 100,000-ton cruise ship, which was a big event for the port that year!

In the end the Star Princess moved to her home port of Los Angeles and began a rich sailing career. When the pandemic hit, in 2020 she changed operators, joined P&O Cruises Australia and was renamed Pacific Encounter. She is now based at Brisbane, Australia, cruising along the east coast of Australia in Queensland, around Oceania and among the islands of the South Pacific.

By the way, cruises in many parts of the world have actually already resumed. People are simply still cautious and worried, and don't dare to travel boldly. But if you visit the official websites of the major cruise lines, you can see that tickets are on sale all over the world. I hope Taiwan ends its tourism lockdown soon, so that in the near future we might have the chance to see her in eastern Australia!

Addendum:

My favourite, Audrey Hepburn, took part in the bottle-breaking ceremony of the Star Cruise's Princess ship (since sold and renamed) in 1989 and was its godmother. It's a pity that it wasn't the 2000 Star Princess discussed here, but many godmothers of Princess Cruises ships are members of royal families. For example, the Royal Princess launched in 1984 invited Princess Diana as godmother, and the elegant Kate, Princess of Wales, was godmother of the new-generation Royal Princess in 2013.
