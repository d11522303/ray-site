Before the Tour Guide and Tour Leader Exams, EP65

#TourLeaderExamCountdown34Days

38. Which of the following global geoparks in Japan is the easiest place to observe the differences in geology and landforms between eastern and western Japan? (A) Shimokita Geopark (B) Unzen Geopark (C) Yoko Geopark (D) Itoigawa Geopark

The answer is (D) Itoigawa UNESCO Global Geopark. It is located at the westernmost tip of Niigata Prefecture, bordering the Sea of Japan to the north and the Northern Alps to the south. It was the first geopark in Japan recognised by UNESCO as a Global Geopark, and its core feature is that it sits on the "Itoigawa-Shizuoka Tectonic Line". Because it lies right on the huge fault zone that divides eastern and western Japan, it is a natural classroom for observing the front line of the collision of the Eurasian and North American plates and the squeezing of crustal blocks. After reading up, I also learned that this place is a world-famous producer of jadeite, a mineral whose formation is closely related to the high-pressure environment produced by the collision of the two plates.

In fact, Laonao Mountain behind the Taiwan Hospitality & Tourism College (THT) in Hualien, where I used to work, and the nearby Laoxi River are no less geologically rich than this geopark. They also hold fun childhood memories for our child, playing in the water and looking for Taiwan jade during summer vacations.

So what world geoparks does Taiwan have? Sadly, Taiwan has no geopark formally certified by UNESCO as a Global Geopark. The main reason is political (Taiwan is not a UN member), which means Taiwan's precious landscapes cannot be formally placed on UNESCO's list. However, to connect with the international community, Taiwan has followed the criteria for global geoparks, and as of December 2025 the #TaiwanGeoparkNetwork has #15 members. The competent authority is now the Forestry and Nature Conservation Agency. They are #PenghuMarineGeopark, #NorthernCoastYehliuGeopark, #NorthernCoastBitouLongdongGeopark, #CaolingGeopark, #KaohsiungMudstoneBadlandsGeopark, #TaitungLijiBadlandsGeopark, #MatsuGeopark, #YunChiaNanCoastalGeopark, #EasternCoastFugangGeopark, #LongqiNiupuBadlandsGeopark, #TaoyuanCaotunSandDuneGeopark, #HepingIslandGeopark, #JiufenJinguashiShuinandongGeopark, #BiexiGeopark, #TiezhenshanGeopark. Let's learn through the questions.

Here are some questions on Taiwan's geoparks.

#1 Which geopark is known for its large-scale "aeolian sand dunes", praised as "Taiwan's Sahara", and shows the interaction between strong prevailing winds and coastal landforms?

(A) Yun-Chia-Nan Coastal Geopark

(B) Taoyuan Caotun Sand Dune Geopark

(C) Tiezhenshan Geopark

(D) Heping Island Geopark

Answer (B)

#2 Heping Island Geopark in Keelung is similar to Yehliu, but its most famous feature is the grid-like landform formed by seawater erosion, called what?

(A) Honeycomb rock

(B) Tofu rock (Thousand-tatami)

(C) Candlestick rock

(D) Potholes

Answer (B)

#3 Besides its magnificent mountain and sea views, the Jiufen-Jinguashi-Shuinandong Geopark has a geological origin mainly related to which process, which created its past gold-mining history?

(A) Metamorphism produced by plate collision

(B) Hydrothermal mineralisation accompanying volcanic activity

(C) Placer gold accumulated by ocean currents over time

(D) Pure gold deposits formed by fault compression

Answer (B)

#4 The Tiezhenshan Geopark in Dajia has a geological structure mainly composed of thick gravel layers. It is also an important place to observe which landform evolution similar to the landscape of Huoyanshan (Flaming Mountain)?

(A) Modern active volcanic landforms

(B) Red soil gentle hills and river terraces

(C) Limestone caves

(D) U-shaped valleys left by the Ice Age

Answer (B)

#5 The Biexi Geopark is in Fuli, Hualien. Its most unique feature is that it is Taiwan's first park initiated by community self-governance, and it is known for which special rock landscape and rich freshwater fish ecology?

(A) Mud volcanoes

(B) Dianguangjian layers (Miocene volcanic and sedimentary rocks interbedded)

(C) Columnar basalt

(D) Stalactite caves

Answer (B)
