2025.1.30, Second Day of the Lunar New Year

I tried ignoring LINE and all other social interactions during the festival holidays, putting all my attention into interacting with my family and child. I accompanied my new-driver child on a focused round-the-island trip, driving the freeway from home to Xiluo, where in the packed national highway service areas he pulled off a perfect roadside parking. We also sat in traffic all the way through Kaohsiung's old Yancheng district to Chaozhou in Pingtung. It was night when we passed the Pingdong-Eluanbi highway, and by the time we turned onto the South Link it was completely dark, with only the headlights of oncoming cars rushing by.

After a night drive along 106 kilometres of the South Link Highway, we arrived safely in Taitung. The night and the quiet left us, who had returned to Hualien and Taitung from the city, a little unaccustomed. After driving several hundred kilometres, my child could still keep his mother company chatting at the hotel's late-night snack session. No wonder he always feels lonely in his EE department full of engineering geeks.

Such precious family interaction was made possible by putting down meaningless social media interactions. Let me give myself a holiday first, and leave those overloaded, meaningless online relationships in the void for now!
