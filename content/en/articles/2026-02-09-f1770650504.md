#TourLeaderExamCountdown33Days

39. Which of the following statements about the geographic environment and social characteristics of China's ethnic minorities is correct?

(A) Most are distributed in western and southwestern China

(B) They are concentrated in particular regions, with population concentration even higher in recent years

(C) Most are distributed in mountains and hills and engage in secondary and tertiary industries

(D) They are severely restricted by the one-child policy, with negative population growth

Explanation: (A) Southwestern China is the region with the most kinds of ethnic minorities and the densest distribution. By administrative region it is mainly Yunnan, Guizhou, Sichuan, Tibet, Guangxi and so on. Among the peoples, the Zhuang have the largest population, followed by the Miao, Yi, Tibetan, Dai and others, and Yunnan Province has the most complex ethnic composition in China, with more than 25 ethnic groups. Although ethnic minorities make up only about 8% of China's total population, their distribution is very wide, covering more than 60% of the country's total area.

#1 A tour leader plans a northern ethnic culture study tour moving along the China-Mongolia border and the northeastern forest areas. Which group of ethnic peoples is most likely to be encountered?

(A) Zhuang, Miao, Dai

(B) Mongol, Manchu, Oroqen

(C) Uyghur, Kazakh, Hui

(D) Tibetan, Yi, Bai

Answer: (B)

#2 If leading a group on an in-depth cultural trip to the province with the most complex ethnic composition and the most kinds of peoples in China, where should the tour leader take the tourists?

(A) Xinjiang Uyghur Autonomous Region

(B) Inner Mongolia Autonomous Region

(C) Yunnan Province

(D) Tibet Autonomous Region

Answer: (C)
