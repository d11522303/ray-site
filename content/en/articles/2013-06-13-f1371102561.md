Taiwan Hospitality & Tourism College Opens Independent Admissions for Four-Year, Two-Year and Five-Year Programmes

High school and vocational school graduates are welcome (no hospitality or tourism background needed).

Please register on the online application site and fill in your order of preference:

http://ibook.tht.edu.tw/newman/

If you are a fresh new student:

1. Want to be Chef Aji's apprentice? Choose: Department of Culinary Arts Management

2. Want to work in a five-star hotel? Choose: Department of Hospitality Management

3. Absolutely love fine food and restaurants? Choose: Department of Food and Beverage Management

4. Hoping to become a tour guide or tour leader and follow in Lin Long's footsteps? Choose: Department of Tourism and Travel

5. Passionate about outdoor leisure in Hualien's mountains and sea? Choose: Department of Tourism and Leisure

Whether you come from an industrial, electronics or business programme, or a general high school, you can apply!

Download the admissions brochure: http://www.tht.edu.tw/newspic/20130610.pdf

Register right away here: http://ibook.tht.edu.tw/newman

The college welcomes our new students!!!
