title: "My Heart Goes Out to This Graduate Student"
excerpt: "As both a teacher and a doctoral student, Ray reflects on the heavier pressures graduate students face today and on how to interact with and support students."
published: true
---

My heart goes out to this graduate student.

Although I have no graduate students at HWU, I have capstone project students and competition students, and I am myself a doctoral student.

As a teacher, I can feel the joy of some students who improve because of my requirements, but I also sense some students' reactions and emotions to those requirements, and I often take care to appreciate this and interact carefully.

When I was a graduate student, fortunately my advisor was very warm and understanding that everyone who walks this path has expectations and demands of themselves. And as a graduate student, I can truly feel that the social pressure and expectations borne by students today are greater and stronger than before. The school library and graduate labs are almost always full of students studying hard or doing research, a scene that was hard to imagine when I attended graduate school 25 years ago.

It is the clearest realisation from returning to the role of graduate student after 25 years. So given the chance to be a graduate student again, I keep learning and adjusting how I interact with and support students as a teacher. I don't know how well I'm doing, but this road of education is not easy for either teachers or students.
