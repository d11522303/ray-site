The Year of the Internship:

The internship meant going to faraway places to lead groups, and in fact the days away from home were not long, but homesickness stretched time out. The place where she interned was very busy, often starting before dawn and working until late at night. Her body was tired, and her heart even more so. She stood in an unfamiliar city, learning to read itineraries and table arrangements, and also learning to bear criticisms that came suddenly and sharply. When she was blamed, she did not always understand what she had done wrong; she only knew that at that moment grievance quietly welled up, and she could only swallow it back.

Work at a travel agency seems to be about arranging trips, but is really about dealing with people. Among people there are boundaries and hierarchies: some people cannot sit at the same table, and some demands cannot be questioned. That night, she and her senior colleague arranged the seating until dawn, and the task was finished, yet it still brought a scolding. She stood at the restaurant door and suddenly understood that growth often comes not from being affirmed, but from learning to endure.

She once wanted to leave. The commute was long, life was bumpy, and her heart was weary. But the senior colleagues beside her always gave her strength without meaning to: a word of encouragement, a little company, which made her understand that staying might be harder but was more worthwhile. So she stayed. Her supervisor began to let go and handed her more important matters, no longer just assisting but having to bear the consequences alone.

Later, she began to take charge of large conferences and incentive trips. The process, the details and the timing could not go wrong. Once, the souvenirs ran short, and in the panic she learned to stay calm, making call after call until the gap was filled. At that moment she suddenly found that she was no longer the intern who only knew how to be nervous.

She said that this work carries high risk: if you make a mistake you have to pay for it, and the pressure is heavy. But when a trip is completed and you see the guests' smiles, a quiet and solid sense of achievement arises. Looking back, the internship was not a test but a lesson in life. Those hardships and anxieties later turned into stories, quietly reminding her that she had walked this way and had grown because of it.
