The 2017 WEF (World Economic Forum) Global Competitiveness Index report. Taiwan ranks 15th in the world, ahead of Israel (16), the UAE (Dubai) (17), Australia (21) and South Korea (26).

But the report points out four problems for us that score above 10:

1. Policy instability 20.2

2. Restrictive labor regulations 15.7

3. Insufficient capacity to innovate 15.4

4. Inefficient government bureaucracy 14.6

http://reports.weforum.org/global-competitiveness-index-2017-2018/competitiveness-rankings/

We are not bad, we just need to work harder. That was my biggest takeaway from being immersed in RTM (pan-tourism) on the first working day of 2018.
