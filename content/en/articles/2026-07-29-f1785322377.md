Canada Study Tour Weekly Journal: HWU Department of Travel Management "Dreams Come True" EP3-1

Without realising it, it's already the third week, and I'm getting more and more used to life and work here. This week in the evening I went outside with my boss to look at the stars and learned how to tell the Big Dipper, Polaris and the Summer Triangle apart. With almost no light pollution here, you can see the whole sky full of stars when you look up, and I even saw a faint Milky Way. It was really beautiful! It was my first time seeing the Milky Way and so many constellations and stars. I already liked stargazing, and this made me like looking up at the night sky even more, and left me with an unforgettable, precious memory. I heard that when the weather is good you can see the Milky Way more clearly, and even have a chance to see the aurora, so I've started looking forward to the chance ><.

On Monday I went with local residents to the place where they move hay. I didn't help carry anything and only watched from the side, but I did stand on the big truck loaded with hay, which was a very special experience for me. On Wednesday, I went with my boss and my boss's friends to walk around the nearby town of Ashcroft. We went all around the town and I got to know small-town life here better!

However, a forest fire broke out nearby this week. When I got up in the morning, the view outside the window was almost all haze, the air quality was clearly affected, and you could smell smoke as soon as you went outside. My boss happened to be away from the hotel during this time, and residents who needed to be evacuated because of the fire and firefighters checked into the hotel one after another, making this week's work especially busy. Besides cleaning more rooms, I also began handling check-in, and sometimes I had to confirm check-in information while dealing with other things, and felt my brain was about to run out. On some days I had to clean up to 11 rooms. Although the workload was much more than usual, it also let me see what work is like in special circumstances, and it was a very good and memorable learning experience for me.

The biggest challenge this week was the sudden increase in workload. It was much busier than previous weeks, and it taught me the importance of arranging my work in order so that I could finish within the time. Although I was exhausted every day after work, seeing my own progress made me really happy and gave me a great sense of achievement!

This forest fire was the first time I felt the impact of a fire up close. Recently, whenever I see the grey, hazy sky in the morning, I worry about how the fire is going, and I hope the flames can be brought under control soon and that everyone affected will be safe. This week was harder than usual, but it also taught me how to handle emergencies and to finish each day's work conscientiously.

This week made me realise that the work situation can be different every day, and when emergencies arise, we need even more to stay calm and complete each task step by step. Although it was tough, I learned a lot from it that is not easy to encounter normally.

Written by: Yen-Yun Chen, HWU Department of Travel Management, Class of 2025 (ROC 114)
