[2022 Summer Reading List]

These days people rarely read physical books because of all kinds of screens, but reading still has many benefits. It can calm the mind, spark imagination, prompt reflection and even bring creative ideas. So when students finish their final exams and I can finally nag them a little, I keep reminding them: make good use of the summer to travel and look around, and build a habit of reading and learning. Part-time work to earn money is understandable, but the time to build your abilities is now.

Below is the summer reading list from "Ah Gou's school". Let's read together! If you can't finish all 29, at least pick one and read it properly, and we'll wait to hear your thoughts!

10. Shen Hsin-hung, *How to Become a Man*, Ballad Publishing
