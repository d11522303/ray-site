My students will be transferring in Singapore on their way to Australia, with a whole night to spare. What can they do at Changi Airport?

1. They can take the Free City Shuttle into the city for a look around. The timetable is at this link: http://www.changiairport.com/changi/en/airport_guide/transit/suggested_activities/free_city_shuttle.html?__locale=en

2. They can find somewhere to sleep at the Napping Areas. It costs S$30 for 3 hours: http://www.plaza-ppl.com/sg_en/nap.php. There are more options here: http://www.changiairport.com/changi/en/airport_guide/exp_changi/rest_n_relax/napping_areas.html

3. And of course there are places with big chairs where you can sleep, which should be free: http://www.changiairport.com/changi/en/airport_guide/exp_changi/rest_n_relax/napping_areas.html

All of the information is on the Chinese-language web page below, so have a look!
