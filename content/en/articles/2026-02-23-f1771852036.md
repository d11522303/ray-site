Tour Guide and Tour Leader Exam Prep EP80

#Tour Leader Exam Countdown: 19 Days

Starting from Chicago, we enjoy the lakeside scenery of the Great Lakes region, the largest freshwater lake system in the world and the natural geographic border between the United States and Canada. Which of the following lakes belong to the Great Lakes? (1) Lake Louise (2) Lake Huron (3) Lake Superior (4) Lake Ontario (5) Lake Erie

The answer is 2, 3, 4, 5.

Explanation: Since they are the Five Great Lakes, do you know which one is missing? It is Lake Michigan, which shares its name with the state of Michigan. It is the only one of the lakes entirely within the United States, and the major central US city of Chicago sits on its shore. In addition, Lake Superior is the largest freshwater lake in the world by area, about 82,100 square kilometres, more than twice the size of Taiwan.

#1 Which of the following statements about the Great Lakes of North America is incorrect?

(A) Lake Michigan is the only one of the Great Lakes located entirely within the United States.

(B) Lake Superior is the largest freshwater lake in the world by area.

(C) The waters of the Great Lakes ultimately flow into the Atlantic Ocean via the Mississippi River.

(D) The world-famous Niagara Falls lies between Lake Erie and Lake Ontario.

Answer: (C) is incorrect, because the waters of the Great Lakes flow via the St. Lawrence River past Quebec City, Canada, into the Atlantic Ocean, not via the Mississippi River.

#2 A tour leader brings the group to Old Quebec in Quebec City. Which of the following statements about this area is incorrect?

(A) Old Quebec was listed as a UNESCO World Cultural Heritage site in 1985.

(B) The district's landmark, the Fairmont Le Château Frontenac, is a typical example of French Loire Valley château-style architecture.

(C) It is the only city in North America that has preserved its complete defensive walls (fortifications).

(D) Quebec City is located on the lower St. Lawrence River, and its name means "place of rushing water" in an indigenous language.

Answer: (D) is incorrect. Quebec means "where the river narrows," not a place of rushing water. This is also why the site became a military fortress.
