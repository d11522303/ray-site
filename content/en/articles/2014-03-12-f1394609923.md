The Chinese Tourism Management Association is running an industry-academia collaboration with Royal Caribbean's Taiwan general agent and Lion Travel. For the first time, the 140,000-ton Voyager of the Seas, the largest cruise ship in Asia, will serve as the training venue, with the most in-depth and professional programme on the latest cruise travel trends in tourism and hospitality. On completion, participants will receive three seed-teacher certificates in cruise management, logistics management and gaming management.

Training details are as follows:

(1) Dates: Sunday, May 18 to Friday, May 23, 2014 (Republic of China year 103), six days and five nights in total.

(2) Venues: The Chinese Tourism Management Association's training venue, and Royal Caribbean's [Voyager of the Seas] on its "Keelung, Nagasaki, Fukuoka route".

(3) Fees:

1. Non-members of the Chinese Tourism Management Association: NT$38,500 (four per room).

2. Members of the Chinese Tourism Management Association: NT$37,500 (four per room).

Note: The two-day optional shore excursions to Fukuoka and Nagasaki (NT$5,000) are already included in this fee.

(4) Training certificates:

Certificate of completion and licence for "Cruise Operations Management Seed Teacher, 16 hours"

Certificate of completion and licence for "Cruise Logistics Management Seed Teacher, 16 hours"

Certificate of completion and licence for "Cruise Gaming and Entertainment Management Seed Teacher, 16 hours"

Only 30 places are reserved, and the market has already sold out. If you are interested, feel free to message me privately!!
