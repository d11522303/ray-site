2026.1.1

In 2025 I really got better through my own hard work, so I give myself a round of applause.

On the first day of the year I slept until I woke naturally. On the last class day of the year, students who sincerely wanted to pass their certification exams fought alongside me all the way to the ninth period, which was truly satisfying. After I got home, I went with my family and my child to have a meal and buy clothes. The two of them really enjoyed it, and although I was nearly dizzy from keeping them company, it is truly happy that a child who is already an adult is still willing to eat, chat and talk things over with us.

The first half of this year was spent in minor illnesses and aches. Before a short holiday in Thailand I took the TOEIC, but sadly only the listening reached B2, and my reading still hasn't improved. I'll try again this year and make another push in the summer holiday. At the start of 2025 I made four wishes for myself, and three were fully achieved: running 1,000 kilometres in the year, paying off NT$1 million of the family mortgage, and being admitted to the NTUST doctoral programme. I feel a real sense of accomplishment.

I am truly grateful to my younger brother and my family for urging me to see a psychiatrist in Zhubei. My mood and motivation have improved, I feel healthier and more positive, I love eating and sleep well, and I handle many things with more skill and confidence. I deeply feel how important it is to take the body as the foundation: it turns out people really do need to eat properly and rest properly to fulfil themselves.

The doctoral coursework is the focus of 2026, and I hope to reach the graduation requirements in a short time. So starting this year I must push my English listening, speaking, reading and writing further, earn the TOEIC gold certificate in listening and reading, and stick with a long-term journal reading and writing log for English writing until it becomes a habit. I will try to secure an academic overseas travel budget, and bravely go to international conferences to present on my own.

With a new year begins, a family should bless itself and each other, may we have good luck every year of our lives.

Happy New Year to me. In 2026 I'll keep going, ganbatte:
