[Building the Nation on Vocational Education]

Q: Does everyone know that today is the day of the Technical and Vocational College Joint Entrance Exam?

A: ......

Let's take a look at question 10 of the Chinese-language paper from the exam just finished. The question is very down-to-earth and its design is cute, but do you know the answer?

According to the latest statistics released in the evening by the Technological and Vocational College Admissions Committee, the total number of registrants for this joint exam (including the four-year and two-year technical programmes and the two-year college transfer) is 71,568, which is 1,033 fewer than last year, setting a new all-time low again.

Before 2015 (ROC year 104), the number of candidates in the vocational system's joint exam was always higher than the number in the general high school system's scholastic ability test. But in 2016 (ROC year 105) the situation reversed. In 2015 there were about 136,000 joint exam candidates and about 135,000 scholastic ability test candidates. Ten years later, this year's university scholastic ability test still has 121,535 registrants, nearly double the joint exam number, the largest in three years and close to the number of candidates ten years ago.

For me in the role of a teacher, each student I meet at admission is unique, not a head-count in statistics. Each of you who goes through teaching, counselling, internship and employment shares with me bit-by-bit the bond of teacher and student, and seeing you walk towards your own hopes is the greatest source of our achievement. I have served in the vocational system for nearly twenty years. Although the number of joint exam candidates has clearly been cut in half, to the students who just finished the exam and who may become my students in the Hsing Wu Department of Travel Management, I want to say today: well done, you worked hard. I look forward to meeting you all in the classroom in the future.

Good luck with the exam ✌️
