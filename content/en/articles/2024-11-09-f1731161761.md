2024.11.9

Dr. Lu Chiang-Chuan, my mentor in cruising.

In 2001, when the Star Princess made her maiden call at Keelung, it was Teacher Lu who made it possible for me to board and take part in Chinese and English tour-guiding training and practice for prospective customers.

In 2006, the Tourism Bureau carried out the first survey of the Asian cruise market as a special project. Teacher Lu sent me and three other surveyors aboard the Virgo Star to collect hundreds of paper questionnaires in English, completing the first official market analysis of international cruise passengers.

From 2007 to 2009, at the Hualien College of Tourism, Teacher Lu co-supervised me as I organised integrated cruise routes to Penghu, Singapore, Japan and the United States. We also ran, for the first time as a teaching project, several study camps at sea aboard Star Cruises and Princess Cruises ships, with 300 student participations.

In 2018, when the Hsing Wu University College of Hospitality and Tourism building was in its initial design, I sought Teacher Lu's views and consulted many cruise books, which became the Hsing Wu Department of Travel Management's own cruise simulation classroom.

In the midterm exam week at the end of 2024, Teacher Lu came to campus, to the HWU cruise simulation classroom, to give a talk to 80 newly enrolled first-year students in the Department of Travel Management. The aim is more meaningful practical teaching: breaking through Taiwanese students' tendency to look only at what is in front of them, and analysing future cruise career internship options from an international, big-picture perspective. We hope that in this promising blue-ocean travel market, the teachers and students of HWU Travel Management will be among the leaders, seize the first-mover advantage in cruise tourism and sail toward a more forward-looking future.
