Tour Guide and Tour Leader Exam Prep EP10

#2025TourismAdministrationTourLeaderExamQuestion

28 In ancient times emperors toured south of the Yangtze. Which of the following buildings are the Three Great Towers of Jiangnan? ① Yellow Crane Tower in Hubei ② Misty Rain Tower of Jiangnan ③ Yueyang Tower in Hunan ④ Xunyang Tower in Jiangxi ⑤ Pavilion of Prince Teng in Jiangxi ⑥ Daguan Tower in Yunnan (multiple choice)

Explanation: The Three Great Towers of Jiangnan are three famous ancient towers along the Yangtze River: the Yellow Crane Tower in Wuhan, Hubei; the Yueyang Tower in Yueyang, Hunan; and the Pavilion of Prince Teng in Nanchang, Jiangxi. It is quite reasonable for a question like this to appear in the Chinese-region tour leader exam's tourism resources subject, because when leading groups to mainland China you always need some knowledge of its history and geography, but for the new generation of students who rarely come into contact with Chinese history and geography, it is a very hard question.

The only picture with a modern photo is the Yueyang Tower on the shore of Dongting Lake in Hunan. It is the only one of the Three Great Towers of Jiangnan that has kept its original ancient appearance, and Fan Zhongyan's "Record of Yueyang Tower," with the line "to worry before the world worries, and to rejoice only after the world has rejoiced," is a timeless saying. The other two towers have either been rebuilt or relocated, so I am sharing old photos and paintings instead. Here are a few more questions that might appear. If you don't understand, ask a GAI!

#1 Wang Bo, Li Bai and Fan Zhongyan all wrote works on the Three Great Towers of Jiangnan. What shared function of these towers in Chinese culture does this show?

① A centre of religious worship

② A border defence facility

③ A cultural space for literati to gather and express their feelings

④ A political centre symbolising imperial power

#2 "In ancient times, Yangtze River shipping travelled from the inland to eastern seaports (such as Nanjing and Yangzhou downstream)." If a boat sails in order along the Yangtze through the locations of the Three Great Towers of Jiangnan, which of the following is the most reasonable?

① Yueyang Tower (on Dongting Lake) → Yellow Crane Tower (Wuhan) → Pavilion of Prince Teng (Nanchang)

② Pavilion of Prince Teng (Nanchang) → Yellow Crane Tower (Wuhan) → Yueyang Tower (on Dongting Lake)

③ Yellow Crane Tower (Wuhan) → Yueyang Tower (on Dongting Lake) → Pavilion of Prince Teng (Nanchang)

④ Yueyang Tower (on Dongting Lake) → Pavilion of Prince Teng (Nanchang) → Yellow Crane Tower (Wuhan)
