Wu Tung-Hsing, Distinguished Professor at National Chung Hsing University, says this is common at technical colleges that were renamed universities of technology relatively late. Once the Department of Technological and Vocational Education approves a programme's enrolment, schools can award degrees themselves under the Degree Conferral Act. Under the current rules, a school only has to submit its admission rules and degree-conferral rules to the Ministry for approval, so the members of master's oral examination committees carry a great deal of responsibility.

https://udn.com/news/story/11320/4171083

I am very happy that, in all my years of teaching, the only master's thesis oral examination committee I have taken part in was for two Indonesian international students in the international master's programme of the Graduate Institute of International Business at Chinese Culture University, supervised by the wonderful and brilliant Professor Wen-Yu. They wrote their theses and defended them entirely in English during their studies, and the content was rigorous and professional.

In fact, the National Central Library publishes the names of oral examination committee members online in full. If you are interested, you can look up the names of friends who have graduated with a master's or doctorate in Taiwan.
