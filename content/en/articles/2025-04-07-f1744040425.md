2025.4.7

My career is like this week's tariff war, surging up and washing down, with rumours all around. There is talk of pay cuts, and I see others thinking of leaving. I am also constantly preparing for the doctoral programme at Fu Jen Catholic University and for new plans and considerations.

I record the whispers on campus these days in words, and in doing so let them go.

I tell myself that a career is the ups and downs of life. Only with change is there scenery, and only by striving is there opportunity. I will work hard to train my body to keep up with every change, and strengthen my mental resilience through self-love to face challenges, working only for the happiness and joy of my body and mind.

If I cannot control the quality of my life's time, then having all the money in the world is meaningless. From today, I will no longer be afraid of any threat to my career, because what is most precious is the little golden time I have left. Whether in teaching, research or industry-academia work, the quality of interaction comes first. My interaction with students should be deep and sincere, my research progress focused and professional, and ideally I will also improve my language skills at the same time. In industry-academia interaction, the starting point must be increasing income and results. I won't do free work for any company, and I will invest in my own earning power.
