40 Along the Langqiao-Beinan Ancient Trail (Alangyi Trail), which of the following nature reserves was established to preserve one of Taiwan's few natural coastlines, coastal forest ecosystems, and diverse species and culture?

(A) Xuhai-Guanyinbi Nature Reserve (B) Kenting Uplifted Coral Reef Nature Reserve (C) Taitung Cycad Nature Reserve (D) Wushibi Coastal Nature Reserve

＃Explanation

This question tests nature reserves. Under the Cultural Heritage Preservation Act, the Ministry of Agriculture has successively designated 19 nature reserves: Tamsui River Mangrove, Guandu, Pinglin Taiwan Keteleeria, Hapen, Chatienshan, Yuanyang Lake, Nan'ao Broadleaf Forest, Miaoli Sanyi Huoyanshan, Penghu Basalt, Alishan Taiwan Pleione, Chuyunshan, Taitung Hongye Village Taitung Cycad, Kaohsiung Wushanding Mud Volcano, Dawushan, Dawu Forest District Taiwan Cephalotaxus (Amentotaxus), Wazihwei, Wushibi Coast, Kenting Uplifted Coral Reef, and Jiujiufeng. Among them, ＃GuanduNatureReserve was delisted in 2021 (ROC year 110).

In addition, Penghu County, Pingtung County, Taipei City and Tainan City have each legally designated 4 more nature reserves: ＃PenghuSouthSeaBasaltNatureReserve, ＃XuhaiGuanyinbiNatureReserve, ＃BeitouStoneNatureReserve and ＃LongqiNiupuBadlandsNatureReserve. So the local competent authority for the Xuhai-Guanyinbi Nature Reserve in the answer is the Pingtung County Government.

A society under the rule of law means administering according to law, but when laws and regulations overlap by time and place, many things become complicated, and those who want to get things done feel uncomfortable, while those who understand the law and administration know where the shortcuts are!

So let's start with the legal basis, the ＃CulturalHeritagePreservationAct. Only [natural landscapes] and [natural monuments] are administered by the ＃MinistryOfAgriculture, while all the other categories are administered by the Ministry of Culture of the Republic of China. Cultural heritage can be divided into two major categories, tangible and intangible, and each is divided into further types. Its diverse and colourful content is the greatest focus of tourism resources.

------------- Legal side ------------

Tangible cultural heritage > Natural landscapes > Natural areas > Nature reserves, geoparks

> Natural monuments > 9 categories including special landforms, geological phenomena, and rare plants and minerals

Intangible cultural heritage > Traditional performing arts

> Traditional crafts

> Oral traditions

> Folk customs,

> Traditional knowledge and practices

> Preservation techniques and preservers, 6 categories in all

---------- Tourism practice ---------

I think whichever category of cultural heritage it is, it is the most attractive kind of tourism resource, and the most convenient path to developing local tourism is to find the important cultural heritage around you. Take the recent local creation project in the Pingxi Line area: after taking stock of everything, besides the local communities' efforts, the nearby ancient trails, flora and fauna, the Pingxi Line railway, the Keelung River landscape and the coal mine ruins can all be linked together as resources. But the overall highlights that draw attention are not enough, so soft management is needed, such as the sky lantern event, local food, and even cultural stories. I'll put the Ministry of Culture's National Cultural Heritage website in the comments. When planning a weekend trip, look for related resources to integrate into the itinerary, and it will be deeper and more fun.
