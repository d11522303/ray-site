23/677 "FPS: Rescue on T Island"

The scientist David is trapped in an enemy camp and urgently needs your team to rescue him!!!

This game is the latest work from NTUSTMEG, an educational training game that combines FPS (first-person shooter), AI and puzzle-solving elements. Players form a three-person intelligence team and travel to T Island to rescue hostages trapped in an enemy camp. Team members must not only fight off the enemy but also master the island's terrain to have a chance of completing the mission. During the activity, AI characters will also solve puzzles with the team, and you can talk with the AI characters to get hints and clues.

Game experience exchange session

Time: 18 October 2026 (ROC year 115), 13:00 - 15:00

Venue: TR-1012, 10th floor, Taiwan Tech Research Building (NTUST)

Who can join: Open to all; anyone interested in this topic is welcome (minors must be accompanied by a parent or relative)
