[Completely Free] Taiwan Hospitality & Tourism College New Triathlon Activity Instructor Training Camp

Professional water-sports and land-sports instructors from industry have been engaged to run a training course for instructors of the new triathlon activities. Teachers from the partner schools of the Northern Region Teaching Resource Center are warmly invited to take part.

Dates: 5-6 September 2013 (Year 102 of the Republic of China calendar)

Venue: Liyu Lake in Hualien and Taiwan Hospitality & Tourism College

The course includes basic lessons and hands-on practical experience sessions, 22 hours in total, and a training certificate will be issued on completion.

Participants should wear casual clothing and sports shoes during the event.

There is no registration fee for this training course. You are warmly encouraged to join.
