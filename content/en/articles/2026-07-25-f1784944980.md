2026.7.25 (7.24 BC Canada)

The hotel where the students are on their field trip is at the marked spot in the picture. Because a large number of lightning strikes on 7/21 ignited the nearby forest, and with this year's dry and hot global climate, within a few days it had become a crisis for the whole of northern British Columbia. On the evening of 7/23, the residents of Clinton gathered for an emergency meeting of the whole village, and the next day, 7/24 (7/25 Taiwan time), the local government and related agencies issued an evacuation notice asking all residents to leave. Of course my two students rode back to Vancouver by car with the hotel owner.

They are now on the road back. I, their teacher, am worried from ten thousand miles away, but I know everything will be fine.

This Evacuation Alert is in place to prepare you to evacuate your premises or property should it be found necessary. Residents will be given as much advance notice as possible prior to an Evacuation Order; however, you may receive limited notice due to changing conditions.

What You Should Do:

Locate all family members and designate a meeting area outside the evacuation area, should an Evacuation Order be issued while separated.

Pack essential items for quick departure, such as government-issued ID, medications, eyeglasses, valuable papers (e.g. insurance, credit, and mortgage information), immediate care needs for dependents or pets, and, if time and space permits, keepsakes.

Prepare a grab-and-go bag with clothing, toiletries, food, water, and medications.

Arrange to stay with family or friends (if possible).

Prepare to move disabled persons, children and/ or neighbours, if assistance is needed.

Prepare to take pets with you and move large animals and livestock to a safe area (if possible).

If you are a livestock producer, ensure that you have your Premise ID in the event that you require support should an Evacuation Order be issued.

Arrange transportation for all your household members. Fill the gas tanks of personal vehicles to be used in case of an Evacuation Order.
