title: "The National Dagu Cup Tourism Itinerary Design Competition aims to draw on the creativity of young students..."
excerpt: "The 2015 National Dagu Cup Tourism Itinerary Design Competition invites students to design creative travel itineraries for the travel industry; entries are due by 10 June 2015."
published: true
---

The National Dagu Cup Tourism Itinerary Design Competition is held in the hope that the creativity of young students can help us find more Taiwan travel itineraries worth promoting to the industry.

We hope participants will come up with many creative, high-quality and diverse itineraries for the travel industry to promote, thereby achieving a real link between industry and academia.

Organisers: National Association of Travel Agencies of the R.O.C., Taiwan Quality Tourism Development Association

Co-organisers: Department of Tourism, Taipei City University of Science and Technology; Lianhe Xin Business Management Consulting Co., Ltd.

Sponsor: Hua Li Books Co., Ltd.

Executing unit: Department of Tourism, College of Human Ecology, Taipei City University of Science and Technology

How to register - see the attached guidelines

By 17:00 on Wednesday, 10 June 2015, deliver the registration documents, seven copies of the tour proposal, and one disc containing the written files
(including the tour proposal in Word format and the PowerPoint presentation) (please show the itinerary theme on the cover)
to the Department of Tourism, Taipei City University of Science and Technology
(Address: No. 2, Xueyuan Rd., Beitou District, Taipei City; please note "2015 National Dagu Cup Tourism Itinerary Design Competition").
