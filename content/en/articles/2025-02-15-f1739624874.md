2025.2.15

These past few days I have been keeping in mind the planned discussion with Southeast Travel about a project, and after a few days of sorting things out I have finally reached a conclusion. The most important point is that every professional contribution should come with fair compensation. First I need to know whether the other side truly expects to complete an ESG project, how much the company values it, and how many resources it is willing to invest. It should not be me who names a price, because I am not a consulting firm but a university researcher with expertise in planning and management.

The Ministry of Education's ESG programme starts from a win-win between industry and student internship courses. At every meeting or event, the travel agency managers who attend receive an attendance fee from the programme budget, but for the faculty advisory and consulting portion, we expect the travel agency to provide comparable compensation in return. Specifically, the details are as follows:

Hsing Wu University (HWU) faculty and student team:

1. Opportunities to lead Southeast Asia tours as tour leaders during summer or winter vacation

2. Students can serve as paid tour assistants on domestic tours

3. When taking part in meetings to write and discuss the company's ESG plan, reasonable consulting fees can be paid according to the number of discussion sessions attended.

4. The ESG report should be written and led by full-time company staff, or drafted by a separately hired consulting firm.

5. We will also actively help find all kinds of relevant resources and contacts and bring them into the company to improve its ESG content.

Southeast Travel:

1. Organise ESG training courses for the relevant project staff, with lecturer attendance fees paid from the Ministry of Education project funds

2. Other needs can be set later according to the discussion.

All of the above are my own thoughts. In the end it depends on what is discussed at next Monday's meeting. On the spot, I must never agree to any work that requires free effort. The main role should be advisory and consulting; I can help revise documents and offer suggestions, but I must never do work for nothing. If there is no conclusion at all, so be it.
