2025.4.16

Briefing Session for Recruiting Staff for the NTHU Four-Department Joint Camp

Everyone has goals they want to pursue. Gradually I can feel my child using academic effort and participation in activities to find his own place.

It also makes me recall my own youth, when I wanted to do things but didn't dare, and later was left with regret. Is the work of a teacher now perhaps more suitable for me? There are students to interact with, room for my own learning and growth, and a regular income for my family to rely on. In truth, there is really nothing wrong with that.

Yesterday the school held a university-wide faculty forum, and proposed a minimum enrolment for each department: a day-division department that has fewer than forty registered students will be closed. It also drew definitions of international students versus domestic students. From the discussion and sharing, I confirmed the following conclusions. First, for the short term of the next six to ten years, I can still work in the Department of Travel Management at Hsing Wu, with no need to worry about retiring too early. Second, the supply of international students is too changeable to be treated as a requirement for a department's sustainable development; it can only be an extra bonus.

Finally, I want to set a direction for my work and for increasing my income: stay on the path of a teaching career, and add value to the content and meaning of my teaching.

(In my annual plan I once wrote that I wanted to raise the family's cash reserves. The goal for 2023 to 2025 is the one mentioned above: NT$30,000 more income every month, which means NT$360,000 more every year. I am working hard at writing industry-academia collaboration proposals over the long term, using proposals, teaching, side jobs and so on to increase income.)
