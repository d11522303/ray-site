First, let me say that Ningbo felt wondrous to us, not strange or unlikeable!

Let me talk about the popular local restaurant and our impressions. After we checked into the hotel on our transit afternoon, a super-friendly young lady kept visiting our room, asking us all sorts of questions and giving us little gifts: room slippers, new toothbrushes, and nonstop, outgoing chatter. I decided to turn the tables and ask her about local restaurants within walking distance and city sights. She recommended this famous place: Alar Famous Stove, the Taste of Ningbo People, and even named several must-eat dishes.

After thanking her and catching our breath, we went out. Through the muggy heat we walked past the Moon Lake scenic area. The city is beautifully planned, the roads orderly, and apart from electric scooters darting out from the sidewalks now and then, the whole city is lush and green. On a holiday stroll, it was mainly local residents, with very few tourists.

The Alar Famous Stove Moon Lake branch is right next to the park. It was scheduled to open at 17:00; we arrived at 17:10 and it was already 80% full. What the locals say you must eat is no exaggeration. Every dish is ordered by scanning a code (photo attached). After being seated upstairs, you can look at what you'd like and order whatever you want. The prices and flavours were both good, and the value for money is really high. Restaurants like this would be a good model for Taiwan's seafood restaurants at fishing ports. The prices are transparent too: live seafood has clearly marked prices, and you scan the code and decide for yourself whether to order the delicious food. It inspires real trust.

The three of us ordered 7 dishes, with tableware, for a total of 202 RMB. We sincerely recommend the soul-stirring Dongpo pork, eggplant with rice cake, spicy stir-fried clams, the healthy stir-fry, and stir-fried crown daisy. The portions are bigger than in Taiwan, so if you visit, don't order too much. Those with small appetites really won't finish it all. Next time, I'll definitely go on an empty stomach and order live fish.

Transparent pricing was the most reassuring thing on this trip. Just ask taxi drivers or friendly young men and women and you get plenty of recommendations in no time. We had also learned beforehand how to use the Meituan app, carefully read the transparent online reviews, and asked before entering a shop whether we could pay the group-buy price on Meituan. That kept good food and tasty spots with us all along the way. Between using technology and visiting shops in person to try new things, it was very happy and fulfilling.

While eating, we kept observing the people of Ningbo. It felt that this place is mainly commercial; people speak less loudly and are more reserved. The grandfather and great-grandfather of Taipei's current mayor came from Fenghua District in Ningbo. Thinking about it, the Moon Lake we just walked past really is a bit like Cihu in Daxi, Taoyuan, no wonder it felt familiar all along, from the muggy weather to the green waterside scenery.

After eating too much in the evening, we walked to nearby Tianyi Square for a stroll and some shopping. As we were nearly back at the hotel, heavy rain from a typhoon's outer circulation started again, so we hurried into the hotel and upstairs to pack for our flight back to Taipei.
