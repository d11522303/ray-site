Look Here~~ Look Here~~

Working tour guides who already hold a tour guide practising licence now have a chance to learn Korean, Indonesian, Vietnamese or Thai, so that you can lead foreign groups too.

1. Training period: late April 2016 to 30 April 2017 (ROC years 105 to 106).

2. Eligible trainees: working tour guides who already hold a tour guide practising licence.

3. Languages: Korean, Indonesian, Vietnamese, Thai.

4. Training regions: northern, central and southern Taiwan.

5. Number of sessions (classes): 16 sessions in total across the north, centre and south, with a maximum of 30 people per session in principle; a class will not open with fewer than 12 people.

6. Format and curriculum: 48 lessons of 50 minutes each, including:

(1) Opening ceremony, 1 lesson.

(2) Introduction to the market for that language, 2 lessons.

(3) Practical language training, 42 lessons, including learning basic language skills, self-introductions and airport transfer reception practice, vocabulary, phrases and sentences used in tour guiding at tourist attractions, and general discussion and exchange of views.

(4) Final oral test, 3 lessons.

7. Training fee: NT$5,000 per person.

The Bureau's "2016 Tour Guide Training in Other Foreign Languages (Korean, Indonesian, Vietnamese, Thai)" has been awarded to the Taiwan Tour Guide Association (R.O.C.). For matters concerning the commissioned work, please contact the association: tel. 02-25922207~9, website: http://www1.tourguide.org.tw.
