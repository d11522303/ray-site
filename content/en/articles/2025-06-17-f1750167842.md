17 June 2025

Jingtong Industry-Academia Field Visit

I received this project because Teacher Kang kindly left it to me before moving to Lunghwa University of Science and Technology. And when I got back to school, I heard that Teacher Kuo would also move to Tamkang University this semester.

Colleagues I once worked with on projects earned their doctorates in the meantime, so moving to a better school is only natural. I am still standing in the same place, to this day stuck because my TOEIC score isn't high enough.

I haven't been able to move up, and the school is getting worse by the year. The whole campus is empty and quiet, filled with Vietnamese students there just to make up the numbers, and the working environment is cold, with infighting between departments often heard about. At a time like this, calmly facing my own career is not easy at all.

At the railway souvenir shop in front of Jingtong Station I bought a Taiwan Railway travel passport. In these directionless days, I am first anchoring my own life. Having an income is the most important thing now, and life and health must be kept close. Today I met Teacher Huang from the Department of Digital Design, and it gave me something a little different. I hope that I won't have to walk every step of the future alone.
