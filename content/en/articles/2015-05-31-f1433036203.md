Episode 3 is "Crazy College". Contestants have to experience university life in a single week: learning to tap an RFID card for attendance within the ten minutes before and after each class, handling online course registration that keeps crashing or won't connect at all, submitting assignments on iCan, a platform even Professor Lien can't use, and checking academic warnings and grades in a school system that is not much different from a computer virus.

They are also forced to take part in flipped classrooms where you must watch videos at home, and in group discussions and problem-based learning where you are made to speak even when you don't want to. As for the labour portion, they must act as teaching assistants to a group of juniors who are pretending to be dumb, do ten people's worth of experiments in the lab, and write papers, sweep floors, look after children and help move house for the professor.
