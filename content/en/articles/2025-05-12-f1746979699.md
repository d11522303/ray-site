2025.5.11

Life Is Bitter and Short

This was the final conclusion given by the owner of the Witch Shop in an interview I just listened to. The same four words mean something completely different to me, having grown up in a different family. Her interpretation is that because people are living very happily, they worry that life is too short; mine is that life is bitter and not long either, so why bother coming through it at all? Isn't that negative?

The attached study, on how Japanese people's sense of time differs by age, surprised me a little. It turns out I really do have that feeling now that a whole year passes as if it were only a week, but looking back it is bitter and hard to recall.

I really feel that happiness in this life depends on comparison with those around us. I was born into a very ordinary family, and as a child I even thought we were comfortably off, but compared with the families of my classmates from junior high school onward, my family background really could not have been more ordinary, and this made for a big gap in my later values and perceptions. Add to that a partner who is also very realistic and takes a sceptical stance toward everything in life that requires imagination, and life is truly bitterness on top of bitterness, and whether it is short or long matters even less.
