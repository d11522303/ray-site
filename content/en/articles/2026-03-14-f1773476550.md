1. Xiao-Ying graduated from a vocational high school in tourism and currently works as a sales staff member at a travel agency, also serving as a tour leader. How many years of experience does she need to qualify for travel agency manager training?

(A) 2 years (B) 3 years (C) 5 years (D) 6 years

Correct answer: (D)

Explanation: Under the Regulations Governing Travel Agencies, vocational high school graduates working in travel agency business or as tour leaders need 6 years of experience before they can take part in manager training.

2. Xiao-Li leads a group to the United States. On the way, the bus has an accident, causing 2 tourists to die and 5 to be seriously injured. According to the rules, Xiao-Li should report as quickly as possible, within the time limit, to the travel agency that employs him and to which office?

(A) Taipei Economic and Cultural Representative Office in the United States (B) Tourism Administration, MOTC, New York Office (C) Tourism Administration, MOTC, San Francisco Office (D) Tourism Administration, MOTC

Correct answer: (D)

Explanation: In the event of a major accident, the tour leader should handle it immediately and report to the employing travel agency, and the agency should promptly report to the Tourism Administration, MOTC.

3. Under the Regulations Governing Tour Leaders, for a person who takes part in the pre-service training test for tour leaders and has been approved to postpone the test for a legitimate reason, within what maximum period must he apply to take the test?

(A) 3 months (B) 6 months (C) 1 year (D) 2 years

Correct answer: (B)

Explanation: According to the rules, those approved to postpone the test must apply to take it within 6 months.

4. Da-Ming's tour leader practising licence was revoked while working as a tour leader. At least how many years must pass before Da-Ming can work as a tour leader again?

(A) 1 year (B) 3 years (C) 5 years (D) 10 years

Correct answer: (C)

Explanation: Where a practising licence has been revoked, a licence may not be obtained for 5 years from the date of revocation.

5. Under the Regulations Governing Tour Leaders, after a tour leader has obtained a training completion certificate or practising licence, if he has not performed tour leader duties for how many consecutive years must he retake the training and obtain or renew the practising licence before performing tour leader duties again?

(A) 1 year (B) 2 years (C) 3 years (D) 5 years

Correct answer: (C)

Explanation: Those who have not performed tour leader duties for 3 consecutive years must retake the pre-service training to return to work.

6. What is the range of the fine for performing tour leader duties without obtaining a practising licence under the Act for the Development of Tourism?

(A) NT$3,000 or more but not more than NT$15,000 (B) NT$5,000 or more but not more than NT$25,000 (C) NT$10,000 or more but not more than NT$50,000 (D) NT$30,000 or more but not more than NT$150,000

Correct answer: (C)

Explanation: Those who perform tour leader duties without a licence are fined NT$10,000 or more but not more than NT$50,000.

7. Among the key strategies promoted in the "Tourism 2030 Taiwan Tourism Policy White Paper", which of the following are correct? ① Tourism branding to attract visitors worldwide ② Upgrading the value of smart scenic areas ③ Cross-regional integration of diverse travel ④ Round-the-island highlights driving outbound travel

(A) ①②③ (B) ①②④ (C) ①③④ (D) ②③④

Correct answer: (A)

Explanation: The strategies include brand-led visitor attraction, smart upgrades and cross-regional travel. "Round-the-island highlights driving outbound travel" is not a core strategy (the focus is on attracting inbound visitors to Taiwan).

8. According to the Act for the Development of Tourism, which of the following statements about travel agencies are correct? ① They are required to take out performance bond insurance and liability insurance ② It stipulates fines for illegal operation or violations ③ They must first apply for approval from competent authorities at all levels before operating ④ They are divided into categories (comprehensive, Class A, Class B travel agencies)

(A) ①③④ (B) ①②④ (C) ①②③ (D) ②③④

Correct answer: (B)

Explanation: Travel agencies must apply to the "central" competent authority (the Tourism Administration) to operate, not to authorities "at all levels".

9. Which of the following businesses may a comprehensive travel agency operate? ① Soliciting business entrusted by or on behalf of other comprehensive travel agencies ② Entrusting Class A travel agencies to solicit or receive domestic and foreign tourists and arrange travel, accommodation and transport ③ Entrusting Class B travel agencies to solicit or receive domestic tourists and arrange travel, accommodation and transport for domestic group tours ④ Designing domestic and overseas itineraries and arranging tour guides or tour leaders ⑤ Collecting fees from tourists who leave the group midway

(A) ②③④⑤ (B) ①②④⑤ (C) ①③④⑤ (D) ①②③④

Correct answer: (D)

Explanation: Comprehensive travel agencies have a broad scope of business, including entrusting Class A and B agencies to act on their behalf, but they may not casually charge tourists a "group-leaving fee" (unless the contract provides otherwise).

10. Under the Regulations Governing Travel Agencies, which of the following about the bond a travel agency must pay is correct?

(A) Comprehensive travel agency NT$12 million (B) Class A travel agency NT$1.5 million (C) Class B travel agency NT$1 million (D) Each branch of a comprehensive, Class A or Class B travel agency NT$300,000

Correct answer: (B)

Explanation: The head office bonds are: comprehensive NT$10 million, Class A NT$1.5 million, Class B NT$600,000. Branch bonds are NT$300,000 for both comprehensive and Class A, but NT$150,000 for Class B.

11. What must be stated when a travel agency advertises the tours it organises? ① Name of the itinerary, departure place, date and number of days ② Tour fee ③ Liability insurance taken out and its insured amount ④ Performance bond insurance taken out and its insured amount ⑤ The itinerary is for reference only

(A) ①③④⑤ (B) ②③④⑤ (C) ①②④⑤ (D) ①②③④

Correct answer: (D)

Explanation: Advertisements must be truthful and state the itinerary information and insurance items. "For reference only" easily leads to disputes and does not comply with the rules.

12. Regarding the pre-service training for tour leaders, which of the following pairs (first the number of training sessions, then the proportion of the sessions that may be missed at most) is correct?

(A) 36 sessions, 1/10 (B) 56 sessions, 1/10 (C) 36 sessions, 1/5 (D) 56 sessions, 1/5

Correct answer: (B)

Explanation: The current pre-service training for tour leaders is 56 sessions, and absences may not exceed 1/10.

13. During a tour organised by a travel agency, an accident causes the death of a tourist. If the tourist was married, and the children and parents have all passed away, what is the order of priority for claiming insurance benefits from the liability insurer? ① Grandchildren ② Spouse ③ Siblings ④ Grandparents

(A) ②→④→①→③ (B) ①→④→③→② (C) ③→②→①→④ (D) ②→①→④→③

Correct answer: (D)

Explanation: Under the Civil Code order of succession and insurance payment: the spouse (②) is fixed; since the first-priority children have died, grandchildren (①) take their place, then grandparents (④), and siblings (③) come last.

14. From 1 January 2023, what is the minimum age (inclusive) at which travellers entering Taiwan may bring in alcohol and tobacco?

(A) Alcohol 18, tobacco 18 (B) Alcohol 18, tobacco 20 (C) Alcohol 20, tobacco 18 (D) Alcohol 20, tobacco 20

Correct answer: (C)

Explanation: In line with the lowering of the age of majority in the Civil Code to 18, the age limit for tobacco was raised to 20, but the rules for duty-free alcohol and tobacco on entry currently remain at alcohol 18 (may be carried) and tobacco 20 (may be carried duty-free). *Note: this question involves a recent fine adjustment of the rules, so pay attention to practical operation.*

15. Mr. and Mrs. Chen, nationals without household registration in Taiwan, lose their Republic of China passports by accident while on their honeymoon in Europe and are anxious to return to Taiwan. Which of the following documents can they apply for at our overseas mission to return to Taiwan?

(A) Certificate of Entry (B) Entry Permit (C) Republic of China visa (D) Taiwan Area Resident Certificate

Correct answer: (A)

Explanation: When a passport is lost abroad and one needs to return home urgently, overseas missions can issue a "Certificate of Entry".

16. A group member buys a total of 6 packs (cans) of goods abroad and brings them back: ① ham sausage instant noodles with seasoning packet ② shrimp-flavour dried instant noodles with seasoning packet ③ vegetable-flavour dried instant noodles with seasoning packet ④ canned beef for pet dogs. Which of the above can be brought into Taiwan without declaration?

(A) ①② (B) ②③ (C) ③④ (D) ①④

Correct answer: (B)

Explanation: Dried instant noodle packs (without meat pieces, or with highly processed seafood/vegetables) can be brought in. Ham sausage (processed meat products) and canned meat are generally subject to quarantine restrictions.

17. Which of the following items, in what quantity, may an incoming traveller carry through the green lane at customs?

(A) Gold worth US$10,000 (B) 250 cigarettes (C) 30 cigars (D) RMB 30,000

Correct answer: (A)

Explanation: Items within the duty-free allowance (200 cigarettes, 25 cigars, gold worth under US$20,000) can go through the green lane. Cigars are duty-free up to 25, and 30 must be declared. More than US$10,000 or more than RMB 20,000 in cash must be declared.

18. Mr. Zhang, aged 25, applies for a passport for the first time, and Mr. Huang, aged 25, applies for a replacement because he lost his passport. Which of the following statements comparing the validity of their passports is correct?

(A) Both Mr. Zhang and Mr. Huang get 10 years (B) Mr. Zhang gets 10 years and Mr. Huang gets 5 years (C) Mr. Zhang gets 5 years and Mr. Huang gets 10 years (D) Both get 5 years

Correct answer: (B)

Explanation: A newly issued passport for an adult is valid for 10 years, but a passport reissued because of loss is limited to 5 years.

19. Under the current Regulations Governing the Inspection, Duty Collection and Release of Baggage of Inbound Passengers, what is the duty-free allowance, in NT dollars, for the total value of personal and household baggage items imported by an incoming traveller at the dutiable value?

(A) NT$25,000 (B) NT$30,000 (C) NT$35,000 (D) NT$40,000

Correct answer: (C)

Explanation: Starting in 2024 the rules were adjusted, and the duty-free allowance for personal baggage on entry was raised from NT$20,000 to NT$35,000.

20. Under the current Regulations Governing the Inspection, Duty Collection and Release of Baggage of Inbound Passengers, what is the duty-free limit for personal-use alcohol brought by an incoming traveller?

(A) 1 litre (B) 1.5 litres (C) 2 litres (D) 2.5 litres

Correct answer: (A)

Explanation: The duty-free allowance for alcohol on entry is 1 litre (regardless of the number of bottles).

21. Xiao-Ming is 19 years old and is a conscription-age male who has not yet completed the military service physical examination or served. He wants to travel to Japan with his family. If he leaves the country without approval, which of the following statements is correct?

(A) He only needs to return and complete the conscription examination, without punishment (B) He must obtain approval before leaving the country, and if he leaves without approval he will be placed on a watch list and have his exit restricted (C) If it is a short-term tourist trip, it is an exceptional case and a parent can apply for retroactive approval on his behalf (D) He will only be restricted from applying to leave the country in the future, with no fine or criminal liability

Correct answer: (B)

Explanation: Conscription-age males must obtain approval before leaving the country, otherwise they will be restricted from leaving or placed on a watch list in accordance with the law.

22. Under Article 2 of the Foreign Exchange Control Act, which of the following items are included in "foreign exchange"? ① US dollar banknotes ② Gold and silver ③ Foreign securities, such as stocks and bonds ④ International credit cards ⑤ Foreign bills of exchange, cheques, promissory notes and traveller's cheques

(A) ①②④ (B) ①③⑤ (C) ③④⑤ (D) ②④⑤

Correct answer: (B)

Explanation: Foreign exchange includes foreign currency banknotes, foreign securities and foreign remittance instruments. Gold is not foreign exchange, and credit cards are a payment tool.

23. Under the Foreign Exchange Control Act, foreign exchange receipts, payments or transactions equivalent to how many NT dollars or more must be reported as required, and which agency sets the reporting rules?

(A) NT$500,000, Central Bank (B) NT$1,000,000, Central Bank (C) NT$500,000, Ministry of Finance (D) NT$1,000,000, Ministry of Finance

Correct answer: (A)

Explanation: Transactions reaching NT$500,000 must be reported, and the competent authority is the Central Bank.

24. Under the Foreign Exchange Control Act, a person who illegally buys and sells foreign exchange as a regular business is punished by imprisonment of up to how many years, detention, or a fine of up to the equivalent of the total turnover, or both?

(A) 1 year (B) 2 years (C) 3 years (D) 5 years

Correct answer: (C)

Explanation: Illegally buying and selling in violation of the Foreign Exchange Control Act as a regular business is punished by imprisonment of up to 3 years.

25. Under the "ISO 4217 currency codes", which is the currency code for the euro?

(A) AUD (B) EUR (C) GBP (D) USD

Correct answer: (B)

Explanation: EUR is the euro.

26. Which of the following countries use the euro? ① Germany ② United Kingdom ③ Greece ④ Switzerland ⑤ France

(A) ①②⑤ (B) ①③⑤ (C) ③④⑤ (D) ②③④

Correct answer: (B)

Explanation: The United Kingdom (pound sterling) and Switzerland (Swiss franc) do not use the euro.

27. Under the Foreign Exchange Control Act, which agencies are the administrative authority for foreign exchange control and the agency in charge of foreign exchange business, respectively?

(A) Central Bank, Ministry of Finance (B) Ministry of Finance, Central Bank (C) Executive Yuan, Central Bank (D) Executive Yuan, Ministry of Finance

Correct answer: (B)

Explanation: The administrative authority is the Ministry of Finance, and the agency in charge of the business is the Central Bank.

28. A bank quotes its customers a USD to NTD rate of buy 29.81 / sell 30.48. Which of the following statements is wrong?

(A) Different banks quote different exchange rates (B) The spread between buying and selling is 0.67 (C) The bank is willing to pay NT$29.81 to buy US$1 (D) For a traveller, to buy US$1 requires paying NT$29.81

Correct answer: (D)

Explanation: When a traveller buys US dollars, the bank's "selling rate" applies, which should be NT$30.48.

29. Under Article 514-4 of the Civil Code, before the start of a trip, a tourist may arrange for a third party to take part in the trip in his place. Which of the following statements is correct?

(A) The difference must be refunded regardless of the amount (B) The tour operator may refuse for any reason (C) If costs increase, the third party bears them; if costs decrease, the original tourist can get a refund (D) If costs increase, the tour operator may request payment; if costs decrease, the tourist may not request a refund

Correct answer: (D)

Explanation: When a tourist is replaced, any increased cost must be made up, but decreased costs (such as the hotel price difference) are not refunded by the operator.

30. Under the "Standard Contract for Overseas Travel", the contract states that the tour needs 15 people to go ahead, but only 10 have registered. At least how many days before the scheduled departure must the travel agency notify the tourists that the contract is terminated?

(A) 3 days (B) 7 days (C) 10 days (D) 14 days

Correct answer: (B)

Explanation: When the minimum group size is not met, notice of termination must be given 7 days before departure.

31. Under the "Standard Contract for Overseas Travel", a tourist cancels the trip ten days before departure because of personal illness or similar reasons. What percentage of the tour fee must the tourist pay as liquidated damages?

(A) 10% (B) 20% (C) 30% (D) 50%

Correct answer: (C)

Explanation: For cancellation from the 2nd to the 20th day before departure, damages of 30% of the tour fee are payable.

32. Under the "Standard Contract for Overseas Travel", a travel agency entrusts an overseas operator to arrange the trip. If that overseas travel business breaches the contract or acts unlawfully, causing damage to tourists, what responsibility must the domestic travel agency bear?

(A) None (B) The same responsibility as the overseas operator (C) The insurance company pays (D) An apology is enough

Correct answer: (B)

Explanation: The domestic travel agency must bear the same responsibility for the acts of its performance assistant (the overseas operator) as for its own acts.

33. Under the "Standard Contract for Overseas Travel", a travel agency arranges shopping at specific venues. If a tourist finds the goods defective after buying them, within how long after receiving them may the tourist ask the travel agency to help handle it?

(A) 7 days (B) 14 days (C) 1 month (D) 3 months

Correct answer: (C)

Explanation: Tourists may ask the travel agency for help with defective goods within one month of receiving the purchased items.

34. Under the "Standard Contract for Overseas Travel", if a travel agency deliberately abandons or strands tourists during the trip, besides refunding the fees for the unfinished part of the trip as actually calculated, it must also pay liquidated damages of how many times the amount obtained by dividing the total tour fee by the total number of tour days and multiplying by the number of days of abandonment or stranding?

(A) 1 time (B) 3 times (C) 5 times (D) 10 times

Correct answer: (C)

Explanation: For maliciously abandoning tourists, liquidated damages of 5 times the tour fee for that period must be paid.

35. Xiao-Li joins an overseas group tour with a total tour fee (including a NT$1,000 visa fee) of NT$31,000. The day before departure he is injured in a car accident and notifies the travel agency of his cancellation, but the visa has already been issued. Under the "Standard Contract for Overseas Travel", how much of the tour fee must the tourist compensate the travel agency at minimum?

(A) NT$31,000 (B) NT$15,000 (C) NT$15,500 (D) NT$16,000

Correct answer: (D)

Explanation: Cancelling 1 day before departure incurs 50%. (31,000 - 1,000) × 50% = 15,000. Plus the visa fee already spent, 1,000, makes NT$16,000.

36. Jia pays NT$200,000 for a 10-day luxury tour of northern Italy with a travel agency. On the fifth day, because of an operational error by the agency, the tourists are put in a two-star hotel instead of the scheduled five-star hotel. Also because of a ticketing error, they miss the originally scheduled 12:00 flight home and have to take a flight at 20:00 the next day. The agency calculates the hotel price difference as NT$4,000. Under the "Standard Contract for Overseas Travel", how much must the travel agency compensate Jia in liquidated damages at minimum?

(A) NT$4,000 (B) NT$20,000 (C) NT$24,000 (D) NT$48,000

Correct answer: (D)

Explanation: For a hotel below the specified grade, twice the price difference is payable (4,000 × 2 = 8,000). The itinerary is delayed by 1 day (less than 24 hours counts as 1 day), so twice that day's cost (200,000 / 10 days = 20,000) is payable (40,000). The total is NT$48,000.

37. When a tourist leaves the group on his own during the trip and returns home, which of the following is wrong under the "Standard Contract for Overseas Travel"?

(A) The tourist may ask the travel agency to refund the unused tour fee in proportion to the tour days (B) The tourist may ask the travel agency to refund the costs that can be saved (C) The tourist may ask the travel agency to refund costs that do not need to be paid (D) The tourist may not ask the travel agency to bear the cost of transport home

Correct answer: (A)

Explanation: A person who leaves the group cannot ask for a proportional refund and can only ask for a refund of the costs the operator saves because of his departure (such as meals not eaten).

38. Under the travel section of the Civil Code, Law of Obligations, what is the limitation period for a claim for damages arising from a travel contract?

(A) Extinguished if not exercised within one year from the date of contract (B) Extinguished if not exercised within two years from the date of contract (C) Extinguished if not exercised within one year from the time the trip ended or should have ended (D) Extinguished if not exercised within two years from the time the trip ended or should have ended

Correct answer: (C)

Explanation: The limitation period for compensation claims related to travel contracts is one year.

39. Under the "Standard Contract for Overseas Travel", when a consumer dispute arises between a tourist and a travel agency and cannot be resolved after negotiation, the tourist may apply for mediation to relevant agencies, but which of the following is not included?

(A) The travel agencies' trade association of each county or city (B) The consumer protection officer of each county or city (C) Tourism Administration, MOTC (D) Travel Quality Assurance Association, R.O.C.

Correct answer: (C)

Explanation: The Tourism Administration mainly accepts complaints, and formal "mediation" is usually conducted at the Quality Assurance Association, consumer service centres or trade associations.

40. Xiao-Hua joins a tour of Travel Agency A and pays a tour fee of NT$50,000 before departure, but on the day of departure she finds that she has been transferred by Travel Agency A to a tour of Travel Agency B, and Agency A did not notify her. Under the "Standard Contract for Overseas Travel", how much liquidated damages can Xiao-Hua demand from Agency A?

(A) NT$2,500 (B) NT$5,000 (C) NT$7,500 (D) NT$10,000

Correct answer: (A)

Explanation: Transferring a tourist to another travel business without the tourist's written consent requires liquidated damages of 5% of the tour fee (50,000 × 5% = 2,500).

41. Xiao-Hua joins a 5-day, 4-night tour of Travel Agency A with 20 members in total. After departure she finds that the tour leader has no tour leader practising licence. Afterwards, how much liquidated damages can Xiao-Hua personally demand from the travel agency under the "Standard Contract for Overseas Travel"?

(A) NT$375 (B) NT$500 (C) NT$1,125 (D) NT$1,500

Correct answer: (A)

Explanation: For assigning an unlicensed tour leader, the agency must pay NT$1,500 per day divided by the total number of group members, multiplied by the number of tour days. ($1500 \div 20 \times 5 = 375$).

42. When people of the Taiwan Area and people of the Mainland Area interact, they should pay attention to the restricted or prohibited matters in the Act Governing Relations between the People of the Taiwan Area and the Mainland Area. Which of the following is wrong?

(A) Food from the Mainland Area imported or carried into the Taiwan Area is treated as an import, and its inspection, quarantine, management and the collection of tariffs and other taxes are handled in accordance with the laws on imported goods (B) Chinese antiquities from the Mainland Area that the competent authority has permitted to be brought into the Taiwan Area for public display or exhibition may be taken out again (C) Currency issued in the Mainland Area may not be brought into or out of the Taiwan Area, except in amounts within the limit set by the Financial Supervisory Commission (D) Publications, films, video programmes and radio and television programmes from the Mainland Area approved by its censorship bureau and related agencies may be published, sold, produced, broadcast, exhibited or screened in the Taiwan Area

Explanation: Mainland publications and the like entering Taiwan still require permission from our competent authority (the Ministry of Culture), not just approval from the Mainland.

43. When leading a group as a tour leader, if a group member is a current civil servant entering the Mainland Area for exchange activities, which of the following points of attention is wrong?

44. 45. Explanation: The legal definition of the Taiwan Area includes Taiwan, Penghu, Kinmen, Matsu and other areas under effective jurisdiction. Hong Kong and Macao are covered by a separate act.
