To Our Graduates (Please Recommend Hualien to Your Juniors)

Now that you have graduated, do you ever think back to the days when you left home and came from all over the island to live on the east coast, going through the sweet and sour of adapting and learning? I believe that amid your busy lives in western Taiwan, the beauty of Hualien and the leisurely pace of campus life still linger deep in your hearts. Besides the mountains, the countryside and the starry skies, I believe you also found lifelong friends or partners here. I did too. Because you walked this road with me, my life in Hualien has been so rich and wonderful.

If you are nostalgic and willing to recommend us, and the people and things at Taiwan Hospitality & Tourism College still mean something to you, Teacher Ruifeng hopes you can help recommend your juniors to come and enjoy the good life in Hualien. Our college's independent admission is under way. They can register for the exam online, and the final deadline is Thursday, 8/23. You are welcome to recommend your juniors, or relatives and friends, to study in Hualien. Tell them this is a short study-abroad experience within Taiwan, one that will bring challenges and, even more, plenty of rewards.
