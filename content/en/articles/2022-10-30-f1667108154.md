---
title: "Crushed in the Crowd: A Memory from Seventh Grade"
excerpt: "A news report brings back the frightening day in junior high when I was lifted off my feet and trampled in a packed corridor."
published: true
---

Many years ago, when I was in seventh grade, I was only about 143-150 cm tall. Every morning the school held a morning assembly. There were more than 50 students in a class and 37 classes in a grade, and the school's not-so-big playground had to squeeze in 5,000 to 6,000 people in a short time. So every morning, class after class of students had to move to the playground, passing through several very narrow corridors. Junior high students pushed and shoved their way toward the playground exit. There were truly so many people, and it was miserable, especially for a small seventh grader like me.

Then one day, in the middle of all the pushing and shoving, I was squeezed right off the ground and carried along with the crowd. Then a gap suddenly opened, and I fell and was pinned underneath. Time seemed to stop. Everything was a mass of darkness, and I have no idea how many classmates stepped on me and pushed over me. Suddenly one classmate pulled me up with all his strength, and pushing and pulling, got me out of that terrifying dark corridor. Only when I saw the light of the playground did I regain my senses.

This was the second time I nearly lost my life. I don't know whether it left any after-effects, but when I saw the news, I faintly remembered the dreadful feeling of that day: the darkness, the helplessness, the loss of any sense of time.

It is truly frightening!

May the dead rest in peace and the injured recover safely!
