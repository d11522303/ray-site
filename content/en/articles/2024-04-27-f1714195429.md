published: true
---

Yesterday, while teaching my World Tourism Geography class in the pouring rain, I shared with my students that Thailand and other South and Southeast Asian countries are facing unprecedented heat in mid to late April.

I know I can only explain to a few dozen people the impact of climate change on tourism and many industries. I also reminded the students sitting in air-conditioned comfort that in many parts of Southeast Asia, schools have no air conditioning at all, and governments and schools have done little to prepare for how to keep students learning in such hot conditions.

I hoped that the students, heads down watching the handsome winter outfits of Kim Soo-hyun in "Queen of Tears", could bring their concern for the world around them back from the plot to reality. But I know that is hard.
