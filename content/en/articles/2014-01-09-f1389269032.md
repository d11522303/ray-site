---
title: "Agou Asked: \"Dad, Look at This Book. Which Rifle Did You Use in the Army?\""
excerpt: "A father and his son hunt through books and Wikipedia to work out which rifle he carried during his military service."
published: true
---

Agou asked: "Dad, look at this book. Which rifle did you use in the army?"

Me: "The Type 57 rifle."

Agou asked: "But there's no Type 57 in here?"

Agou's dad: "Then let me find it online and show you: http://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/57%E5%BC%8F%E6%AD%A5%E6%A7%8D (said rather impatiently...)"

Agou: "Oh, so that's the M14 automatic rifle!!"

Agou's dad: "Then see if you can find the .30 rifle, because I remember using the .30 rifle at Chenggong Ridge and for target practice in high school."

Agou: "It's not here either..."

Afterwards: I looked it up myself. It turns out the ".30 rifle" was the 1903 Springfield, a product of the Second World War era. Whether I actually ever used one, I honestly don't remember very well, but judging by its shape, it's hard to forget that rifle's distinctive bolt handle.
