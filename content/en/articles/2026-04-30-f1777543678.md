Game-Based Learning EP2: FPS Virtual Integrated Collaborative Educational Game

Today HWU students came with me to NTUST to find a senior fellow from the doctoral programme and learn while playing.

I hope they all had a great time. Through the need for tight coordination within their groups and completing a rescue mission within the time limit, the game tested emergency decision-making and communication skills, and taught them about the relationship between strategy and cooperation.

🎯 About the game: NTUSTMEG's latest work, an educational training game combining FPS (first-person shooter), AI and puzzle-solving elements. Players form a three-person intelligence squad and head to Island T to rescue hostages trapped in an enemy camp. The squad members not only need to fight off enemy troops, but also need to master the island's terrain to have a chance of completing the mission. During the activity, AI characters solve puzzles together with the team, and you can talk to the AI characters to get hints and clues.

🎯 Organiser: Mini Educational Games (MEG) team, National Taiwan University of Science and Technology

🎯 Who can join: aged 18 or above (basic Chinese ability required).

🎯 Venue: National Taiwan University of Science and Technology (in-person activity)

Session times: can be selected on the registration form. (You can invite friends and family to form a team of three.)

🎯 Duration: 80 to 100 minutes per session (it may end early if you clear it smoothly)

🎯 Notes: This is an in-person activity. You must form a team of three, and at least one member must have FPS experience. You do not need to bring your own computer.

🎯 Participation prize: each member receives a pack of Creative Cards plus a NT$50 gift voucher

🎯 How to join: see the comments
