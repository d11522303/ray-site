Tour Guide and Tour Leader Exam Prep EP53

#Tour Leader Exam Countdown: 46 Days

15 Before its independence in 1903, which country did Panama belong to?

(A) Venezuela (B) Costa Rica (C) Colombia (D) Peru

My guess for this one was that the answer is a neighbouring country, so I could first cross out A and D, then guess one from B and C, and I guessed wrong. The correct answer is Colombia.

Before 1903, Panama was not an independent country but a province of Colombia, then called the Isthmus of Panama. After breaking away from Spanish rule in 1821, Panama joined the "Republic of Gran Colombia" founded by Simon Bolivar. Even after that republic broke up, Panama remained within Colombia's territory.

In the early 20th century, the United States was eager to dig the Panama Canal, but fell into a deadlock with the Colombian government in treaty negotiations. As a result, in its own economic interest, the US turned to support Panamanian independence activists, and in November 1903, under US naval gunboat diplomacy, Panama declared independence from Colombia. Only a few days after independence, Panama signed a treaty with the US allowing it to control the Canal Zone until 31 December 1999.
