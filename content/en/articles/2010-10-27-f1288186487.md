Relaying the press release issued by the Bureau of Consular Affairs, Ministry of Foreign Affairs, on 27 October 2010 (ROC year 99), reminding Taiwanese travellers of points to note when travelling to Thailand and Bali, Indonesia for business or leisure (latest information published on the Bureau's website http://www.boca.gov.tw):

1. 

Thailand: The flooding that has hit the northern, northeastern and central regions of Thailand since the 10th of this month has clearly improved. The travel alert for the whole of Thailand remains at yellow. Before travelling to Thailand, please check local weather reports and traffic conditions first. If your itinerary in Thailand includes a river cruise, the water levels of the Chao Phraya River and rivers in other provinces are high and the currents are swift, so please confirm with the travel agency arranging your trip whether it can go ahead, for your own

safety.

2. Bali, Indonesia: Bali continues to face the threat of rabies, and the outbreak has not been effectively brought under control. If you are travelling there, please take particular care to protect yourself and avoid infection.
