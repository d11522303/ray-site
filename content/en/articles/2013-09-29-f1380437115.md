Strange crab spotted: the documentary "Crabs on the Hot Pot" explores the secrets of the deep sea

China Times [reported by Bing-Jun Lai, Yilan]

The turtle-shell crab (Xenograpsus testudinatus), which lives in the waters off Guishan Island, is a crab species unique to Yilan and can survive in hot springs of 100 degrees. Lin Yu-Hsiang, a 37-year-old young director, and his team spent more than two years diving into the deep sea and enduring the heat to film the crabs. At the moment they captured the crab swarm on camera, Lin said: "It was a wonderful surprise, and so beautiful!"
