2025.4.27

I woke early this morning from a nightmare in which the three of us in my family got separated while travelling. I had also forgotten to book a hotel for the night and hadn't mentioned how we would meet up, and I was terribly worried that my family couldn't be reunited.

Lately I have too many things on my mind. Although they are all trivial matters, they pile up layer by layer and trap me. Career, child, family, economy and health are all self-inflicted worries, but I can't put them down.

The attached image is something I had AI make from two statistical tables. The indicator year ROC 95 (2006) was my first year in the vocational education environment, when there were still more than 257,000 candidates nationwide for the Technological and Vocational Education Joint College Entrance Examination. This year, ROC 114 (2025), the number of candidates has dropped to only 72,601 😱. And what the numbers don't show is that among the candidates there may be several thousand who also sit the General Scholastic Ability Test, and in future they will choose the best admission between general universities and universities of science and technology. This is my turbulent career and source of income. If it were you, wouldn't you be worried?
