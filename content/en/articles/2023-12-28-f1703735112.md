Posting the requirements of a recent quality internship host:

Eligibility: please see the attachment.

Additional notes:

(1) Average grade of 70 or above in all subjects and average conduct grade of 80 or above while at school, with no demerit record.

(2) Familiar with basic computer word-processing systems; positive, proactive, enthusiastic and responsible, patient and careful; willing to try club activities, all kinds of part-time jobs or off-campus service; happy to learn diverse things and accept challenges.

(3) Applicants from non-related departments applying for the OO job category must have taken basic OO-related courses and provide written records (transcripts, etc.).

Translated into student campus language, that means:

(1) Come to class properly; don't sleep through the daytime, don't bully classmates or scold teachers.

(2) Use the computer to write reports more, and read from your phone less. Part-time jobs, clubs and volunteering all matter. In competitions, be the team leader or person in charge.

(3) Certificate exams matter a lot; don't take courses that are too easy.
