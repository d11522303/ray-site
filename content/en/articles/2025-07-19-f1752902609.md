--------3-day, 2-night itinerary-------

Using 13-15 August 2025 as an example

8/13 (Day 1)

After arriving on the red-eye flight, you can go straight to a jjimjilbang (sauna) to rest. After you have slept and eaten well, I suggest checking in at the J-ONE hotel and then wandering around Cheongju, a small city you are visiting for the first time.

8/12 (Day 2)

You can take a taxi to the sights, or take the train to Seoul for a day trip.

8/13 (Day 3)

After sleeping in, enjoy the city and go shopping. If you are a World Heritage collecting fanatic like me, Andong's Hahoe Village, Dosan Seowon and Beopjusa Temple are all worth visiting. Of course, it is worth another visit in late October or early November, when the autumn maples are red and the ginkgo leaves cover the ground in gold.

All of the above was planned online, and if you are interested you can book it yourself. This summer, a self-guided trip lets you flash over to Korea for under NT$6,000 per person, and you don't even need to take leave from a long weekend.
