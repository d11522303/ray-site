Did I forget? Or did I remember too many other things?

Yesterday an old friend brought up something I have absolutely no memory of: the Yonghe Junior High round-the-island graduation trip. It was the era when junior high schools had hair regulations. I opened the graduation yearbook I had not looked at closely in more than thirty years, and the small photos in it proved that the graduation trip really did happen.

I truly have no memory of the last two years of junior high.

All I remember is that I changed classes three times, had 150 different classmates, and went from the B-track class to the A+ class.

All I remember is that when I went back to school after the entrance exams to get my results, the teacher only said to me: "Ruey-Feng Chen, you got into the Affiliated Senior High School of National Taiwan Normal University with the lowest English score in the whole school."

All I remember is

for nearly 1,000 days

the teacher's cane made a sound in the air

falling lightly on my left palm like winter rain

I did not close my eyes

I just watched with open eyes as the burning red lines on my hand

built up one by one into a patch of red

I made no sound

because wisps of pain, one after another, had turned into warmth

and I quietly turned fear into helplessness and let it sink to the bottom of my heart

I kept my eyes open

and looked at my left palm, where the constant bruising of those years is long gone

but when I think of the fright of having nowhere to escape back then, I still shudder
