**2024-9-17**

Threads suddenly suggested I add Gazi (嗄孜) as a friend, and one look was enough to fill me with grief.
The day before yesterday, driving to Taipei, Google Maps routed me right past the place where the accident happened, at just after three in the afternoon.
Gazi has been gone for almost a month. Sometimes I still think she is out there somewhere in the world, working hard, and then I come back to myself and realise the world is missing a soul who mattered a great deal to me.
It is the Mid-Autumn Festival. The moon is full, but we are not all together.

**2024-8-22**

Do you know someone like this?
Talking with them just feels warm; you can chat about anything with no pressure.
When you ask them to do something together, you simply get busy side by side, without even needing to discuss who does what.
Every time you meet, they are full of energy, and life feels positive.
Every so often you check in on each other, and even after a long time apart it never feels awkward.
In many people's hearts, she was an angel.
Yesterday, heaven suddenly took her away,
leaving the rest of us heartbroken.
Gazi, my angel student, your training in this world is complete. Go on to heaven, and I will see you in the next life.
