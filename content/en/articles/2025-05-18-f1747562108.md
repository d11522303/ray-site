2025.5.18

I often see posts in my feed about quick money-making and getting rich. The overall pattern looks unchanged after many years. More than thirty years ago I attended a real estate investment conference in Tianmu and a never-ending briefing on selling aloe vera, and wanting to earn a bit more, I also went to "briefings" for Amway, Nu Skin and Shaklee. But because I am suspicious and stingy by nature, the most I ever did afterwards was open a small account on Yahoo or eBay to sell old stuff, and I never dared to make any quick money at all.

Such "briefings" have moved from in-person to online and are all around us, held everywhere for different groups and audiences. Up to now, none of the classmates or friends who got involved back then has stuck with it to this day, and I haven't seen anyone who became wealthy year after year through quick money.

How do you tell them apart and stay away?

1. Don't attend any briefing.

2. Don't try or taste any health product.

3. Be suspicious of any sales job opportunity that comes looking for you.

4. Put NT$10,000 of each month's salary into a fixed deposit first. If you have no spare money, you won't think wildly.

5. Don't use brand-name goods or luxury cars to make friends.

6. Watch whether family, friends or parents have unusual spending or a new circle of friends.
