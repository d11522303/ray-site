1 in 8 Billion

You could try playing this game, especially someone like me who is already on the return leg of life, and it stirs mixed feelings. The first time I played, I was born in China, and at 1x speed (24 seconds per year) I lived out the life of Song Jun. The first half of the life was plain and pleasant: I earned a lot, lived long, had ability and no burdens, super perfect.

But after 60, for almost 35 years, whenever I hoped for any change or opportunity, the game gave me no options at all. I could only watch many people pass away, friends and family leave, attend gatherings and events that had little meaning, and do things that gave too little sense of achievement. The 1x speed of life felt so slow. After 80 I couldn't stand it, and began speeding life up to 8x (3 seconds per year). I somewhat looked forward to death, but still held on for 15 years until I got cirrhosis of the liver and my life ended.

If you are interested, play it too. Which country will you be born into as a newborn?

How does the newborn you get on?
