2025.4.20 Third Life Countdown: 833 Days

I took my son, who studies at Tsing Hua, for a good meal and chose a Thai restaurant on Nanda Road. The atmosphere and food were both good. Only after posting on IG did I learn it is a restaurant with queues. On a Sunday night we waited only half an hour for a good table, and the toilet was very clean, which shows the owner's care.

My son is tired from studying and almost never gets enough sleep. When I took him back to school I only took him to the study area of the 24-hour library, and watching his back I really felt for him. As he himself said during dinner, life doesn't need to be so tiring, there are many things to pursue, and there is no need to put all one's time and energy into studying. I expressed my support and agreement. At least for me, having him as my son and family, and being able to enjoy the beauty of the world together, is already wonderful. Success and achievements come as fate allows and are not something to be obsessively attached to.

Family has always treated resources and the future as things that cannot be given up. We hold different views and it is hard to agree. She cannot impose her own expectations on us, and we have our own outlook on life. Having more curiosity about the world and pursuing joy is my truth.

Work has been mentally exhausting lately, and because my income may be unstable I have been very anxious. The thought of resting has never stopped. I don't expect much. I just hope to schedule a solo trip and take time to face myself.
