Taking a guided-tour photo my student took for me as an example, the Gemini AI that is all the rage these days can make everything you don't want to see vanish in an instant. But naturally, anything you can think of, people or things, can also be faked and added in, or erased, and unless you look carefully it comes very close to the real thing.

So lately I have begun asking for all fee-related documents to be mailed to me on paper, and every company resists. Recently I have also cut up quite a few credit cards. Besides cutting them up, you have to phone to cancel the membership too, otherwise a brand new one is sent when the old one expires, and they may even add a bonus and send you an extra card, calling you a premium member. Also, I had just paid for my scooter's compulsory insurance, and less than a month later I received an even more expensive scooter insurance bill. When I phoned to ask, I learned it was to encourage owners to buy additional coverage, but it was only "encouraged," not compulsory.

So lately, when I contact colleagues and friends, I try to arrange face-to-face discussions or chats. Otherwise you spend all day looking at photos, and then when you arrive in person you can't recognise anyone at all.
