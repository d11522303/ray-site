[A Guide to Driving on Taiwan's Roads]

1. Taiwan's highway speed limits don't apply to speed cameras

2. If a car is in front of you, flash your high beams at its rear to show your strength

3. Every lane is a passing lane

4. If you drive a BMW, your speed limit is double everyone else's

5. Never let anyone drive in front of you

6. There is no such thing as a safe following distance

7. On the road you're actually playing a GAME; if you die you can respawn

8. When overtaking, get as close to the car ahead as possible to show off your skill

9. Slow vehicles can drive in any lane

10. If the rain is so heavy you can't see your bonnet, there's no need to change your speed or following distance

11. You can turn right at any time without looking left for oncoming traffic, because they must give way to you

12. When turning right on a scooter, there's no need to look left at oncoming traffic, because you're small and light and other vehicles can easily avoid you

13. When turning left, edge slowly into moving traffic until everyone stops for you

14. Helmets are only needed inside Taipei City

15. Flip-flops are fine on a scooter, since it might rain at any moment

16. On a scooter you can strap a baby to yourself with a baby carrier, because nobody sells baby helmets

17. Scooters have no passenger limit; the more people or animals the better. A family of four is fine: the bigger child squeezes between Mum and Dad, another stands in front, and the dog finds a spot on the footboard

18. Put a chair on the front of a scooter and it's a child seat

19. Holding a baby in your arms in the car is love, because babies don't like car seats

20. If your scooter fails its emissions test, just keep riding it

21. If you live on an outlying island such as Green Island, Orchid Island, Kinmen or Matsu, licence plates and insurance are optional

22. When riding or driving, you must smoke, chew betel nut and drink Taiwan Beer

23. Indicators are optional extras

24. Never mind your mirrors. Everyone riding or driving behind you must respect you

25. When stopped at traffic lights on a hot day, always stop in the shade

26. Overtaking is only right when you're in a hurry

27. The Taipei–Yilan highway is a private motorcycle race track

28. On a scooter, the pavement counts as road too

29. If you are forced to stop at a red light, you can ride across the road to whichever lane you want

30. For a short distance, it's fine to ride the wrong way for a bit

31. Give way to ambulances and police cars when it suits you

32. If you drive a bus, you own the whole road. You don't need to care about anyone, especially cyclists and pedestrians.

33. A red light means nothing unless cars in the junction physically stop you

34. Cyclists are like ants. They move slowly and you should run them over.

35. If someone is in your way, sound your horn for as long as possible.

36. If someone honks at you after you stall, get out and have a verbal debate with them

37. If someone gets out to debate you after you honk, take out your baseball bat and threaten to hit their car

38. If they take out a bat as well, run

39. After a minor accident, let it go unless the other driver looks rich

40. Try to settle all accidents in cash first

41. If a lane is about to close, dash through at the last moment or keep driving on the hard shoulder

42. Seat belts are optional unless you're on the motorway

43. If you drive a Tesla, you don't need to pay attention in autopilot mode

44. If you drive a Ferrari, Porsche, Lamborghini, or a Mercedes, BMW or Audi, you can drive however you like

45. If your car is more than 10 years old, you deserve to be overtaken by everyone in a new car on the motorway

46. Parking on red lines is fine in the daytime as long as it's not downtown Taipei. It's much cheaper than those annoying white parking spaces

47. You can park on a red line if it's for less than 15 minutes

48. As long as your hazard lights are flashing, you can park anywhere: on the road, at junctions, double-parked. Come on, everyone knows Taiwan has no parking spaces

49. Park however you like unless there's a police officer nearby

50. Drive as fast as possible unless you see police or a speed camera
