2/698 Secret Manual, Volume 1

Hsing Wu's travel department has set up a new multi-purpose AR/VR board game classroom, and course materials must be designed before the semester starts, which has been giving me a bit of a headache lately. This is when I rely on the secret manuals (journal literature) provided by teachers and senior fellow students to find answers. So for a while now it will be days of laying the foundations and holding the horse stance. I hear there are hundreds of volumes of secret manuals in the scripture library, and today I'll start with this one.

Lin, Y. C., Chien, S. Y., & Hou, H. T. (2025). A multi-dimensional scaffolding-based virtual reality educational board game design framework for service skills training. Education and Information Technologies, 30(8), 11251-11278.
