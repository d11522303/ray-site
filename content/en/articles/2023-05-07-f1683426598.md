🆖【Social Dark Personality Test】🆗

It's a fairly accurate test. Are you willing to see how dark you are? A year has passed; has your Social Dark Personality Test result changed? Friends who took it last year can compare!

🈸If you want to take the test, click the link in the comments 🃏

Taking the median of the six indicators as 300, anyone who scores below 300 is a person whose positive social personality outweighs the negative.

My Social Dark Personality Test results are: Paranoia 64, Domination 46, Suspicion 33, Calculation 33, Indifference 23, Exaggeration 21, with a total score of 220. Each indicator is a relative measure from 0 to 100. In simple terms, taking "paranoia" as an example, if there were only 100 people in the world, I would rank 36th among the paranoid; so if I scored 100, I would be the most paranoid of all.

There is no good or bad here; it is simply a way to get to know your own and other people's perceptions better.
