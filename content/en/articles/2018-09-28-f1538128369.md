Singapore's Ministry of Education will reduce the number of mid-year examinations from primary school through to lower secondary (Grade 10 equivalent), in response to the sweeping changes of a new era.

Going forward, the aim is to steer students towards active learning, with "positive learning orientations" (PLO) such as diligence, curiosity, collaboration and enthusiasm in everyday lessons and learning activities, rather than being driven by academic results.

"On the ground, teachers are on a high-speed train ... rushing, assessments, and preparing the students for exams," he said. "I think it's time to take a pause."

The selection of both awards at these levels will no longer be based on academic scores, said MOE. Instead, the eligibility criteria will be adjusted to recognize "positive learning orientations" such as diligence, curiosity, collaboration and enthusiasm in daily lessons and learning activities.
