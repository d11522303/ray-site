【Notes on "One-Person Unlimited Company"】

I own several books on starting a one-person business. I haven't bought this one, but its content is good and has a few ideas worth referencing.

1. The author suggests four kinds of side businesses suited to office workers, analysed by the money and labour invested, as shown in the attached image. My own takeaway is that in Taiwan, published articles can only be monetised if the content is good, and if you really want traffic and profit at the same time, writing about investment experience offers better value than other topics.

2. Time is money

Instead of wasting time, go earn money. Besides your working hours at your main job, the author stresses reusing the rest of your day, and suggests calculating the value using the basic minimum wage. For example, last week I spent 27 hours on my phone x NT$183 = NT$4,941 (see image, though my LINE is mainly used for work). Most people browse their phones not for work, so if you add up your weekly phone time and multiply by the minimum wage, you can work out how much of your most basic time and money you have simply thrown away.

After forced practice, I have cut it down to under NT$5,000.
