#2025TourismAdministrationTourLeaderExamQuestion

41. A distinctive form of folk dwelling on China's northern coast, the "seaweed house" (haicao fang), has stone walls and a seaweed roof. It is warm in winter and cool in summer, fireproof and pest-resistant, and is known as a living fossil of traditional Chinese folk architecture. In which of the following cities are seaweed houses mainly found? ① Dandong ② Qingdao ③ Weihai ④ Qinhuangdao ⑤ Yantai

You can get the answer by asking AI, but these "seaweed house" dwellings have a long history. They were widespread across the Jiaodong Peninsula from the Qin and Han periods, and they flourished most in the Yuan, Ming and Qing periods. With stone walls and a seaweed roof, their look is rustic and sturdy, with strong local character, like a thatched cottage in a fairy tale.
