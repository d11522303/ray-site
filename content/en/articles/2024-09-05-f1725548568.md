2024.9.5

Today is freshman orientation. Do the new students know why Hsing Wu University of Science and Technology is called "Hsing Wu"? It is written on the big rock at the school gate, so stop and take a look on your way to and from school.

First, a little trivia.

Many universities have changed their names, for many reasons. How many of these did you not know about?

1. Kuang Wu Institute of Technology > North Taiwan Institute of Science and Technology > Northern Taiwan Institute of Science and Technology > Taipei City University of Science and Technology

2. Hsin Pu Institute of Technology > St. John's University of Science and Technology

3. China Junior College of Marine Technology > Taipei College of Maritime Technology > Taipei University of Marine Technology

4. Kaohsiung Institute of Technology > I-Shou University

5. Te Yu Nursing Junior College > Ching Kuo Institute of Management and Health > Te Yu College of Nursing and Health

6. Tamsui Institute of Business and Commerce > Aletheia University

7. Taichung Healthcare and Management University > Asia University

8. Hsing Kuo College of Management > CTBC Financial Management College

9. Tainan Home Economics Junior College > Tainan Women's Technical Institute > Tainan University of Technology > Tainan University of Applied Science and Technology

10. Yunlin Institute of Technology > Huwei Institute of Technology > National Formosa University

11. Szu Hai Institute of Technology > Deh Yu Institute of Technology > Hung Kuo Delin University of Technology

12. Ching Chung Commercial College > Taiwan Tourism College > Taiwan Hospitality & Tourism College (closed)

13. Chih Yuan College of Management > Taiwan Shoufu University (closed)

In the more than fifty years since its founding, Hsing Wu has always been called Hsing Wu. It first had a senior high school and later added the five-year junior college programme, and through every upgrade and restructuring the name never changed.
