The Department of Tourism and Hospitality is running the Northern Region Teaching Resource Center project and is offering the course "Introduction to Mobile Media Concepts and Production". Students and faculty are welcome to sign up. Course details are below, and we would appreciate your help in announcing it. Thank you.

Respectfully, Kuei-Yuan Tsou, Department of Tourism and Hospitality

Course details:

(1) Purpose: To introduce the Android system, and to design and publish an app.

(2) Instructor: Associate Professor Yu-Tseng Lin, Director of the Library and Information Center.

(3) Time: Starting 17 April 2013 (ROC year 102), every Wednesday from 3:00 to 5:00 pm.

Venue: Room 402, Library and Information Building.

(4) Capacity: 20 people.

(The class is kept small to make coaching easier and to improve learning outcomes.)

(5) Registration: Please complete the online registration form before the course begins; places are limited and will close once full. Link:

https://docs.google.com/a/mail.tht.edu.tw/spreadsheet/viewform?formkey=dFVVVDBudW5qNl9yQ1FIdGRzU3BKaWc6MQ

(6) Contact: Project assistant Hsin-Tao Lu (student)

(Email: 10168103@mail.tht.edu.tw)

(7) Notes:

Registration is free, but to maintain a good learning atmosphere a deposit of NT$300 is required.

Participants who miss no more than 3 sessions will receive a full refund at the end of the course, along with a certificate of training.
