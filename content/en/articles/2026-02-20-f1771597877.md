Before the Tour Guide and Tour Leader Exams, EP78

#TourLeaderExamCountdown21Days

39. Which of the following are railway World Heritage sites designated by UNESCO? (1) Semmering Railway in Austria (2) Mont Blanc Tramway in France (3) Albula/Bernina Railway in Switzerland (4) Darjeeling Himalayan Railway in India

The answer is 1, 3 and 4.

This question is easy. There are only four railway areas in the world listed as World Cultural Heritage: two in Asia (Iran, India) and two in Europe (Switzerland, Austria). Also, option (2), the Mont Blanc mountain train in France, is obviously flawed. The question-setter may have pasted the option straight from Chinese Wikipedia. In Taiwan, Mont Blanc is always translated as Bailang Feng, so it should be rendered as the Bailang Feng (Mont Blanc) mountain train.

It is the highest railway in France and the fourth highest in Europe. It is also the only railway in France above 2,000 metres. The line is only 12.4 kilometres long, but climbs from an altitude of 581 metres to 2,400 metres, so 85% of the track has a third, cogwheel rail added.
