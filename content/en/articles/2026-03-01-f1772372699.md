5 The new US$100 bill circulated from 2013 kept three effective security features from the previous version: a portrait watermark, a security thread, and a colour-shifting 100. Which great American is on the $100 bill? (A) George Washington (B) Benjamin Franklin (C) Ulysses S. Grant (D) Alexander Hamilton

The answer is (B)

My first impression of Franklin is that, to prove lightning was electricity rather than a supernatural force, he risked flying a kite to draw down lightning, and this experiment led him to invent the lightning rod. Although he was never president, he was the only person to sign all four of the key founding documents of the United States (the Continental Association 1774, the Declaration of Independence 1776, the Treaty of Paris 1783 and the US Constitution 1787), making him one of the most important figures in America's founding.

#1 When a tour leader takes a group to developing countries in South America or Southeast Asia, they often remind members to bring some US dollar cash just in case. Which statement about exchanging or paying locally is wrong?

(A) At many private money changers, US$100 notes usually get a better rate than US$10 notes.

(B) To ensure authenticity, banks and shops in many countries (such as Cambodia and Myanmar) refuse US notes that are stained, creased or written on.

(C) The new US$100 bill issued in 2013 has excellent anti-counterfeiting features, so it circulates more widely and is more accepted around the world than the old version.

(D) By international custom, when tipping in US dollars locally, using coins (such as quarters) is more polite and respectable than using one-dollar bills.

Answer: (D) Coins are not suitable for tips

#2 When a tour leader takes a group to the United States or the Caribbean, which statement about using US$1 bills best fits international travel etiquette?

(A) When staying at a US hotel, tipping the porter US$1 for each bag brought to the room is the basic going rate.

(B) After a meal in a restaurant, even if a service charge is included, you should still leave at least ten US$1 bills on the table as an extra tip for the server.

(C) If a helpful passer-by at the airport gives you directions, you should immediately pull out a US$1 bill and press it into their hand as thanks.

(D) To save space, the tour leader should suggest members fold US$1 bills into hearts or triangles before handing them to staff, to show sincerity.

Correct answer: (A)
