[Those of Us Who Lived Through the 20th Century Will Realise: The 21st Century Will Bring Great Change]

Within the next hundred years, the populations of developing countries will largely replace those of developed countries. Unless there is very good political planning and implementation, uneven economic development will become the greatest worry, as humans fight over resources and even go to war with each other. Looking back at human civilisation, population has little to do with the development of culture and ideas. The falling birth rate issue appears only in civilised countries, but however hard civilised countries try to have children, they cannot out-produce developing countries, and turning back is difficult.

A prediction: here are possible ways to live stably and avoid the suffering of war over the next thirty years.

Politically, moving to the US or Canada, China, or island countries such as Japan, Taiwan, Australia and New Zealand are all good places, because strong militaries, natural geographic borders and stable national systems are important protections against large populations in developing regions fighting over limited resources. In Europe, if the EU and NATO integrate successfully, there is a chance; if not, it will return to the chaos of the early 20th century, because Arabs and Africans will appear at the borders, mirroring the European history of the past thousand years, which is hard to avoid.

Economically, because of rapid technological development, large enterprises controlling resource allocation will dominate living standards. Every aspect of life will be completely affected by technological resources, and even a different kind of group and lifestyle will develop in the information environment, crossing national borders and races to become another kind of mutually dependent community. I tentatively call it the Weberian.

The Weberians already exist within today's society. For example, groups formed around anime and Cosplay, regardless of nationality, have turned imagination into reality and exist within society, and they are far more active online than they ever were in real-world interactions. If so, conflicts of population, religion and race will have nothing to do with these people, and within these groups there will be completely independent social strata and lifestyles (to be continued).
