[2023 Summer Extracurricular Reading List]

Next week kids everywhere will start their summer holidays. Our family likes to borrow books from the library or browse physical bookshops, looking for books that suit ourselves or the children. Adults should read along too: when you read, the kids will surely learn from you.

Agou's school hasn't yet announced its recommended books for 2024, but I suspect most of the 2023 titles are unfamiliar to many of you. On these hot days, go borrow a book and read together.
