This is my first lunch in new campus.

Today is February 26, Year 114 of the ROC calendar, Thursday, and it is the first class of this semester. Although I am only auditing the course, I still feel very excited. Before going to class, I walked around the campus to experience the atmosphere. This is something I have been looking forward to for 25 years.

It was almost noon, and the class was going to start at 12 p.m. I decided to have lunch at the student cafeteria. The buffet offered a wide variety of dishes at reasonable prices. Most of the food was Chinese cuisine, including both meat and vegetables. I casually chose four vegetable dishes and one meat dish, along with a bowl of rice. The total cost was NT$70, which was very reasonable. I felt happy, satisfied, and grateful.

I hope that during this semester as an auditing student, I can gain insights in my writing and improve my learning methods through practice. I wish my doctoral journey can become smoother. I am also very thankful that my family members are healthy and have not caused me too much worry, allowing me to fully enjoy campus life—especially at the age of almost 55.
