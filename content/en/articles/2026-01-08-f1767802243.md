Tour Guide and Tour Leader Exam Prep EP33

#Tour Leader Exam Countdown: 66 Days

9 Religious belief in Taiwan is very active, and during the Qing dynasty settler era many deities were brought over from the settlers' ancestral homelands. Which of the following deities was NOT brought over by settlers from the ancestral home of Fujian?

① Guangze Zunwang ② Qingshui Zushi ③ Kaizhang Shengwang ④ Sanshan Guowang

Explanation:

A brief description of the deities in various parts of Taiwan follows:

＃Guangze Zunwang: An important guardian deity of the Minnan region of China, whose real name was Guo Zhongfu, a native of Anxi, Fujian, in the Five Dynasties period. His statue often has the right leg raised and the left leg hanging down, because his mother, reluctant to let him go, held on to his left leg, hence the nickname "the Raised-Leg Deity." His worship spread with settlers to Taiwan and Southeast Asia. He is in charge of warding off evil, protecting the locality and disciplining children, and is regarded as a deity of great filial piety and a guardian of teenagers.

＃Qingshui Zushi: Originally an eminent monk from Anxi, Fujian. The greater Taipei area can be said to be where Qingshui Zushi worship is most flourishing; the Qingshui Temple in Monga, the Zushi Temple at Changfu Temple in Sanxia, the Qingshui Temple in Tamsui and Longyan Temple in Ruifang are known as the four great Zushi temples.

＃Kaizhang Shengwang: General Chen Yuanguang was the developer of Zhangzhou, Fujian, in the Tang dynasty, and is widely worshipped by Zhangzhou settlers.

＃Sanshan Guowang: The worship originated in the Chaozhou area of Guangdong. The Sanshan Guowang temple in Jiadong, Pingtung and the Sanshan Guowang temple in Xinzhuang, New Taipei City both enshrine as principal deities the gods originating in Chaozhou, Guangdong, the spirits of the three mountains Jinshan, Mingshan and Dushan, brought to Taiwan by Hakka and Chaozhou settlers.

＃Mazu is the most widespread belief in Taiwan, with Beigang Chaotian Temple, Lukang Tianhou Temple and Dajia Jenn Lann Temple all enshrining the Heavenly Mother, who originated in Putian, Fujian.

＃Baosheng Dadi: Taipei's Dalongdong Baoan Temple also enshrines Baosheng Dadi. The temple is famous for its exquisite traditional craftsmanship, complete temple layout and joint upkeep by the community, and it has received UNESCO's Asia-Pacific Awards for Cultural Heritage Conservation, giving it world-class significance as cultural heritage. Its principal deity, Baosheng Dadi, was Wu Ben, a native of Tong'an, Fujian, revered as the god of medicine, and the medicine lots in the temple are an important source of support for worshippers praying for health.

＃Ling'an Zunwang: The Qingshan King enshrined at Qingshan Temple in Wanhua, Taipei, is the guardian deity of Qingshan in Hui'an County, one of the Sanyi counties of Quanzhou, Fujian, closely tied to local epidemic prevention and town-guardian beliefs.

Feeling dizzy? But worshipping at every temple you see is a habit of many Taiwanese, and rich local belief is the most abundant cultural literacy of the locality. If you can systematically tell international tourists about the myths and miracles, they are among the most promising and attractive tourism resources.

＃1 A tour guide leads a group in Taipei on a themed tour of "Qing-era settler religious culture," visiting in order Qingshan Temple in Wanhua, Baoan Temple in Dalongdong and the Sanshan Guowang Temple in Xinzhuang. Which of the following explanations best fits historical fact and cultural context?

① All three temples enshrine local guardian deities worshipped by Zhangzhou settlers from Fujian

② Qingshan Temple and Baoan Temple both belong to the belief system of Guangdong Hakka settlers

③ Baoan Temple enshrines Baosheng Dadi and received an international cultural heritage conservation award for its traditional craftsmanship and community preservation

④ The Sanshan Guowang Temple in Xinzhuang enshrines Mazu, showing the sea-god worship characteristic of port cities

＃2 When a tour guide takes a group to Qingshan Temple in Wanhua, Taipei, and wants to explain to the visitors the history and function of the deity, which of the following statements is incorrect?

① Monga Qingshan Temple has for many years held its "secret inspection tour" on the 20th and 21st of the tenth lunar month, in southern and northern Wanhua respectively

② The Qingshan King is a mountain god originating from Chaozhou, Guangdong, symbolising Hakka settlers guarding their settlements

③ The Qingshan King is enshrined as Ling'an Zunwang and is closely related to local epidemic prevention and town guardianship

④ In 2010 the Bureau of Cultural Heritage, Ministry of Culture, listed this secret inspection and procession activity as intangible cultural heritage for preservation
