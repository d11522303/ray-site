---
title: "War and Peace Are One Thought Apart"
excerpt: "The civil war after 1945 never formally ended; on the eve of the 2015 Ma-Xi meeting, a reflection on war and peace across the Strait."
published: true
---

When the Second World War ended in 1945, China, which had already lost millions of people, was still not free of suffering. The leaders of the Nationalists and the Communists, sharing the glory of the victors, finally found a moment amid decades of infighting to sit down and talk.

At that time, I imagine people must have hoped for a fresh start for a new China, so that the blood would not have been shed in vain and China would stop fighting itself. But things went the other way. The Nationalists and Communists fought for another four years until 1949. The civil war may have killed close to ten million people, earning it a place in humanity's ugly history of slaughter. It is a pain hard for us, living in peaceful times, to imagine. The few thousand deaths of the 921 earthquake are something we can never forget, yet not so long ago, only seventy years back, the land of the mainland, so closely tied to us, was filled with misery and the cries of the suffering!!

Strictly speaking, this civil war never ended with a ceasefire or a peace treaty, so the two sides of the Strait are technically still at war. Both sides have changed, and whether to fight has become just an option. On 2015/11/7 we have once again reached a historic moment. I care about it a great deal, and I hope my classmates who, like me, were born in peaceful times will care too, because war and peace are only a change of mind apart.
