【Graduation & Commencement】

The school's graduation ceremony was actually held at the end of May, but I'm stubborn and have always believed this ritual belongs in June, so I decided to write my reflections in June.

These two classes spent almost their whole university life in the pandemic, and at least half of it wearing masks. Looking at the photos, I remembered how youthful and innocent everyone looked when they first arrived from high school. It is a beautiful memory I won't forget, and I will keep it in my heart.

Thank you, students, for your hard work during your year of internship. Because of you, we teachers can face all our partner companies with confidence and speak up loudly. Thanks to your outstanding performance, company managers keep asking for Hsing Wu Travel Management graduates by name. I know all these achievements are woven from your tears and sweat. I am only the one gleaning the ears of grain.

Here I wish all the graduates of the Class of 2023 (cohort 108) of Hsing Wu Travel Management a bright future. Excellent as you are, you have already built strong professional wings on your own, and you now fly confidently toward a wonderful future.
