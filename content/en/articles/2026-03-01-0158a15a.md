<p>First a reminder: there are fewer than 2 weeks until the exam. Today, 1 March, you can already look up exam venues. Go and find out where you will be sitting so you can plan transport for the two exam days, especially if you are not an early riser or have transport difficulties.</p><p>Looking at the 2025 tour leader exam paper common to both Chinese and foreign-language tracks, answering 50 multiple-choice questions in one hour should be no problem time-wise. Before we get the exam paper, let's first understand the weightings of the tour leader exam:<br/>1. Tourism regulations<br/>  1.1 Act for the Development of Tourism: 3 questions<br/>  1.2 Regulations Governing the Management of Travel Agencies: 5 questions<br/>  1.3 Regulations Governing the Management of Tour Leaders: 5 questions<br/>  1.4 Tourism administration: 3 questions<br/>2. Entry and exit regulations<br/>  2.1 Entry and exit common knowledge: 4 questions<br/>  2.2 Passport-related: 3 questions<br/>  2.3 Rules for men of conscription age: 1 question<br/>3. Foreign exchange common knowledge: 7 questions<br/>4. Civil Code law of obligations and standard-form contracts: 9 questions<br/>5. Relations between people of the two sides of the Taiwan Strait and Hong Kong and Macao: 9 questions<br/>By weight, tourism regulations and entry/exit regulations account for 48%; foreign exchange 14%; Civil Code obligations and standard-form contracts 18%; cross-strait relations 18%.</p><p>For students with legal knowledge, the difficulty is such that once you understand it you can score high, but if you answer based on ordinary common sense, you mostly won't exceed 50 points, and this will become the subject that drags down the average score.</p><figure class="ph">Photo</figure>
