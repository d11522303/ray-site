[Congratulations to Yen-Ling on Placing Fourth Nationally in the High-Level Civil Service Exam]

Hardworking Yen-Ling, over twenty years of perfect child-rearing and upbringing, raised a sensible and lovely son, and is the foundation of the heart and love of our family of three.

After our son was admitted to university and moved away to study, she finally had a little time of her own to work for herself. Through such sacrifice and difficulty, today she has achieved a small result, writing a beautiful and worthwhile record of her efforts.

Yen-Ling has always lived by demanding too much of herself. Besides staying young and beautiful through exercise and health, her academic qualifications are also the highest in our family: she holds a Master of Laws from National Chung Hsing University and was a graduate student at the Graduate Institute of Law at National Dong Hwa University. Before our child was born, she was a manager at the French-owned model market research firm and a researcher at the Radio and Television Foundation, with professional ability in statistics and law, and has always been our family's brain trust and strategist.

Here, I want to say "I love you" to her with all my heart, and thank her for nearly half a lifetime of dedication. Now please enjoy the third life you have won for yourself, devote yourself to public service in pursuit of personal excellence, contribute what you have learned and are good at to the country, fulfil yourself, and enjoy the wonder of starting work again.
