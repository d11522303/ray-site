Tour Guide and Tour Leader Exam Prep, Episode 21

#TourLeaderExam78DaysToGo

22 A tourist sets out from downtown Chiayi to climb the main peak of Yushan. If the temperature at departure is about 36 degrees Celsius, what is the most likely temperature at the summit of Yushan's main peak at that time?

① -20 ② -10 ③ 10 ④ 20

#2025ChineseLanguageTourGuideExamPastQuestion

Solution

Chiayi city's elevation is close to sea level, while the main peak of Yushan is about 3,952 metres high.

Using the vertical lapse rate: in the troposphere, the temperature falls about 0.6°C for every 100 metres of ascent. To calculate the temperature difference, we estimate at 0.6°C per 100 metres, so 1,000 metres is 6°C less. Yushan, at nearly 4,000 metres, is therefore about 24°C colder, and you can intuitively work out that ③ 10 is the closest answer.

This question is easy, but looking at it the other way around, as air descends, the temperature rises about 1.0°C for every 100 metres of descent. The core reason for the warming is atmospheric pressure, and the most common phenomenon is the foehn wind. When moist air meets a high mountain and is forced to rise, the water vapour condenses into rain on the windward side. When the air crosses the summit and sinks as dry air, it produces hot, dry, strong winds. Let's try questions using the California wildfires earlier this year:

#1 Suppose that on a day in 2025, a powerful air current crosses a mountain range and causes descending warming in both California and Taiwan. Assume the air at both summits is 15°C. California's Santa Ana wind descends from an inland plateau at 1,500 metres to the Los Angeles coast at 0 metres. Taiwan's typhoon outer circulation crosses the 3,000-metre Central Mountain Range and sinks to Taitung City at 0 metres. When the air reaches sea level, what are the approximate temperatures in Taitung and Los Angeles respectively?

① Taitung 30°C, Los Angeles 25°C

② Taitung 45°C, Los Angeles 30°C

③ Taitung 33°C, Los Angeles 45°C

④ Taitung 18°C, Los Angeles 20°C

#2 California's "Santa Ana wind" and the "foehn wind" common in Taiwan's Taitung are highly similar in physical mechanism. Which of the following statements about their causes is correct?

② Both are produced on the western plains by the summer southwest monsoon blocked by the Central Mountain Range
