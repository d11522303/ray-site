Tour Guide and Tour Leader Exam Prep EP86

#Tour Leader Exam Countdown: 13 Days

2. When arranging itineraries and assessing risk for outbound tourist groups, one may refer to the travel warning levels published by the government. Which agency's announcements should a group travelling to Yunnan consult? (A) Bureau of Consular Affairs, Ministry of Foreign Affairs (B) Mainland Affairs Council (3) National Immigration Agency, Ministry of the Interior (4) Tourism Administration, MOTC

Explanation: The travel warnings that Taiwanese travel agencies must consult when sending outbound groups are administered by three different agencies:

1. Ministry of Foreign Affairs (other countries in the following three regions)

2. Mainland Affairs Council (Mainland China, Hong Kong, Macau)

3. Centers for Disease Control, Ministry of Health and Welfare (international travel epidemic information)

The first two agencies' travel warnings have four levels: grey, yellow, orange and red. The Ministry of Health and Welfare has three levels: Watch, Alert and Warning. They serve as a reference for travel agencies when operating tours. As the Ministry of Foreign Affairs website explains, the travel warnings it issues are also advisory only and not legally binding; citizens must decide their own travel plans carefully. The CDC, at the third level, Warning, likewise states wording to avoid all non-essential travel.

So the standard for travel agencies or consumers to cancel a trip is still negotiated between the two parties, and government agencies mostly use administrative publicity to make operators take a passive attitude toward running tours.

#1 Which of the following statements about the agencies issuing Taiwan's travel warnings and the areas they cover is correct?

(A) Citizens who wish to travel to Japan or South Korea should consult the four-level travel warning colour signals issued by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs.

(B) Travel warnings for Mainland China, Hong Kong and Macau are issued uniformly by the Tourism Administration, MOTC.

(C) The levels the Centers for Disease Control, Ministry of Health and Welfare, issues for international epidemics are the four levels "grey, yellow, orange and red."

(D) The travel warnings issued by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs mainly target countries outside the three regions of Asia-Africa, Europe and the Americas.

Answer: (A)

#2 A travel agency organises a tour to Thailand. Before departure, a serious infectious disease breaks out in Thailand, and the CDC lists the country at "Level 3: Warning." If a traveller now wishes to terminate the contract, which of the following handling methods best fits regulations and practice?

(A) Since this is a CDC Level 3 Warning, the traveller may receive a full refund of the tour fee unconditionally, and the travel agency may not deduct administrative fees.

(B) Proceed under Article 14 of the Standard Contract for Overseas Travel, deducting necessary expenses already paid (such as airfare and visa fees) and refunding the balance to the traveller.

(C) Travel warnings are for reference only; if the traveller cancels, liquidated damages should still be paid in proportion to the number of days under Article 13 of the contract.

(D) Proceed under the Ministry of Foreign Affairs' "red" warning, forbidding the traveller from terminating the contract, and the travel agency must announce the cancellation itself.

Answer: (B)
