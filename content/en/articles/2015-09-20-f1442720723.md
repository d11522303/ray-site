Sherry is aged using a method called the Solera System, in which old and new barrels are stacked and blended before the wine is bottled and sold. A winery in Crimea on the Black Sea holds a 1775 sherry from the Jerez de la Frontera area of Andalusia, on Spain's Atlantic side. It probably arrived as cargo sold by Dutch or British maritime merchants.

Around 1700 the Russian Empire forced the Ottoman Empire to recognise Crimean independence, and Russia sent tens of thousands of troops into Crimea. In 1783, after occupying the area, Russia passed a law formally annexing the whole Crimean peninsula. It feels as if the same thing happened again in 2014.
