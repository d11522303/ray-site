---

The list of 30 selected classic small towns:

North:

Zhongzheng District, Keelung City (Haijiao Paradise barrier-free quality itinerary)

Yilan City (city of culture and education)

Toucheng, Yilan (Taiwan's only sea-and-sky 3D travel town)

Beitou, Taipei (enjoy hot spring baths)

Dadaocheng, Taipei (an old street district like a city museum)

Ruifang, New Taipei (the mountain mining secrets of Shuinandong, Jinguashi and Jiufen)

Pinglin, New Taipei (a paradise for tea tasting)

Daxi, Taoyuan (the presidential town's old street district)

Guanxi, Hsinchu (Jiujiang winds and the scent of grass jelly)

Old city district of Hsinchu City (the historic sites of Zhuqian)

Central:

Yuanli, Miaoli (rush weaving)

Gongguan, Miaoli (the home of red jujubes, a golden small town)

Jiji, Nantou (cycling along the railway and the green tunnel)

Xinshe, Taichung (mushrooms, fruit, flowers and firefly watching)

Tianwei, Changhua (the highway garden)

Lukang, Changhua (witness the beauty of craftsmanship)

Beigang, Yunlin (the home of the lantern festival, and a century-old arts town with deep history and culture)

South:

East District, Chiayi City (the Hinoki cultural and creative living area)

Meishan, Chiayi (the beauty of tea plantations and tea fragrance)

Yanshui, Tainan (the beehive fireworks festival)

Houbi, Tainan (the home of rice)

Donggang, Pingtung (the Welcoming the Royal Lords culture and food)

Hengchun, Pingtung (the scenery of an old town at the southern cape)

East Coast and Outlying Islands:

Chenggong Township, Taitung County (unrivalled mountain and sea views and fishing village scenery)

Ruisui Township, Hualien County (hot springs, rafting, and the aromas of tea, milk and coffee)

Huxi Township, Penghu County (island holidays and relaxed fishing villages)

Lieyu Township, Kinmen County (moving between past and present, reliving wartime days)

Beigan Township, Lienchiang County (distinctive Eastern Fujian architecture)
