Why Study Hard and Work Hard

I accompanied my child to his TOEFL course. Before class he felt the same unease I feel in the face of uncertainty. He said so, and I could feel it too. He said that during junior and senior high school he had this feeling every time he came to cram school at Taipei Main Station, because he had given up some things before deciding to study hard.

I shared with him that when I was at the Affiliated Senior High School of National Taiwan Normal University, I was never on the honour roll of Liu Yi's English cram school, while he was always in the top one hundred on the honour roll of Fei Ge English when he was at Jianguo High School. That is because I never truly accepted that studying and working hard for myself was important back then; instead I felt that the me of that time did not want to do it,

This is the dilemma of adolescence. The immature brain simply resists emotionally: learning is hard, so it resists; practice takes real hard work, so it resists. Some people truly resist in action, skipping study and classes and just muddling through; others resist in thought, doing things reluctantly.

The result was thirty or forty years of regret. Apart from the Affiliated Senior High School of NTNU, I have not had many qualifications to boast of since; even though I fought my way upward through thirty years of work and climbed ever higher in position, the regret from my youth always feels as if it can never be made up.

Perhaps most people simply like the brilliant, dazzling moment of a sunset or a rainbow, and those who pursue the plain things that need deep, patient building are always the few.
