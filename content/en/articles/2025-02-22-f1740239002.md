2025.2.22

Yesterday I submitted the Ministry of Education Industry Academy proposal through the system. That morning the network even dropped, and the structure diagram and several carefully thought-out paragraphs vanished completely. Truly Murphy's Law: the busier you are, the more things go wrong.

Lately everything takes double the time and energy to finish, with half the result. These words fully describe my situation since the start of the year. Whether it's waiting at a red light while driving, arranging courses, interacting with students, system databases or talking with companies, everything has a sense of endless spinning in circles and being busy for nothing.

After finishing every chapter and the budget, I know there are many, many flaws that even I can't bear to look at. But every word was written by me, slowly thought through and accumulated by me. After submitting, I wanted to feel happy and encourage myself: at least I didn't run away at the last moment as I used to. Many half-written articles, half-finished registrations and half-thought dreams made me a jack of nothing, half-baked at everything.

I want to truly face every opportunity. This proposal is a beginning. Next comes the plan for part-time work to earn money, because the prime of my professional life has begun its countdown of 12 zodiac years: snake, horse, goat, monkey, rooster, dog, pig, rat, ox, tiger, rabbit, dragon will come very soon. Work hard every day, make the most of every moment, stop chasing unrealistic dreams, do only practical things, and earn the money I deserve.
