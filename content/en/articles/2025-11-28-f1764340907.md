title: "Days Gone By, Days Cherished"
excerpt: "Reflecting on two former senior colleagues I admired, now convicted and imprisoned in 2025, and the sadness of watching paths diverge."
published: true
---
Back when I worked at Y17, they were my seniors, and the vision and management ability of the two of them back then were models for me to learn from. I had just graduated from the Graduate Institute of Cultural Perspectives and Studies and was not yet thirty, so I could work alongside very capable partners, and I still remember the chats we had and the things they said to me.

Later I changed careers and became a teacher. Because I jumped straight off the original track, many people and things gradually drifted away. I never imagined that in 2025 both of them would be found guilty by the courts one after another, and even sent to prison. It really makes one sigh with regret!
