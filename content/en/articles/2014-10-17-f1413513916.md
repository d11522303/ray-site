The selection for the 2014 Chinese Society of Tourism Management Master's Thesis Award is closing soon. Please recommend your graduates to apply.

【Description】

Students who graduate in 2014 from a master's programme in a tourism-related department at a public or private university in Taiwan (including those who have completed their thesis and expect to graduate this year; applicants must be valid members of the Society from the time of application until the award ceremony that year) are eligible to enter if their thesis research is outstanding and has been recommended by their supervising professor.

First prize: NT$10,000 and one certificate.

Second prize: NT$8,000 and one certificate.

Third prize: NT$6,000 and one certificate.

Honourable mention: one certificate.

【How to apply】

1. By 15 November 2014, please email 1. the application form and 2. an electronic copy of the master's thesis (full text) to Pi-Shen Ku, editorial assistant of the Journal of Tourism and Leisure Studies (jts@tourism.org.tw). If you do not receive a confirmation email from the Society within one week, please resend your submission or contact the editor-in-chief or the executive editor.

2. Please also prepare (1) the application form (signed) and (2) one bound hard copy of the master's thesis, and mail them to "Pi-Shen Ku, Graduate Institute of Tourism and Leisure Management, National Chiayi University" for review. Address: Graduate Institute of Tourism and Leisure Management, No. 580 Xinmin Road, Chiayi City 60054.

Thank you for your recommendations, and you are very welcome to share this with students and teachers.
