Tour Guide and Tour Leader Exam Prep EP90

#Tour Leader Exam Countdown: 9 Days

20 Under the Act for the Development of Tourism, which of the following does NOT need to first apply to the tourism competent authority for approval and a business licence, but only needs to complete the relevant registration and obtain a registration certificate before it can operate? (A) Tourist hotel business (B) Hotel business (C) Travel agency business (D) Tourism recreation business

The answer is (B)

Explanation: The Act for the Development of Tourism provides: "Those who operate a hotel business shall apply to the local competent authority for registration and obtain a registration certificate and a dedicated sign before they may operate." But this question refers to the tourism competent authority, that is, the Tourism Administration, MOTC. Different competent authorities mean different laws apply.

＃1 Under the Act for the Development of Tourism, which of the following tourism businesses applies for registration to the "municipality or county (city) competent authority" and may operate once it obtains a "registration certificate" and a dedicated sign?

(A) Tourism recreation business

(B) Tourist hotel business

(C) Travel agency business

(D) Homestay

＃2 After a hotel business or homestay operator obtains its registration certificate, if it must "suspend business" for more than one month for some reason, it must report to the local competent authority within a certain time. Which of the following statements about the reporting deadline and the suspension period is correct?

(A) It must be reported within 15 days before the suspension; the suspension may last at most 1 year

(B) It must be reported within 15 days after the suspension; the suspension may last at most 6 months

(C) It must be reported within 30 days before the suspension; the suspension may last at most 2 years

(D) It must be reported within 30 days after the suspension; the suspension may last at most 1 year

Correct answer: (A). According to Article 28 of the Act for the Development of Tourism, tourist hotel businesses, hotel businesses, travel agencies and tourism recreation businesses that suspend business for one month or more must file within 15 days. The suspension may last at most 1 year, and may be extended once (by 1 year).
