【Pausing the Posts, Not the Running】

Thank you all for keeping me company through 1,084 days of running 3 km every single day. This process took me from barely holding on to holding steady, from unaccustomed to accustomed, from uncomfortable to comfortable, and what I gained in body and mind is hard to measure. So if my daily running posts ever made you want to get up and move, or if you actually did get up and move and didn't find it annoying, please head over to my Instagram daily running log (the link is in the comments below).

I won't be posting running updates on Facebook anymore, and I haven't decided what to record here instead. Perhaps it will still be the little mutterings of my teaching life. If you want to unfriend me, just do so quietly! (I will absolutely never count my friends.)

Thanks to a student who, just as I was worrying about what picture to attach to this post, suddenly messaged me with a photo from their graduation yearbook. The words in the picture were written four years ago: "Give yourself a stretch of clear sky and you will find the heavens" (an encouragement that teacher Lin Ming-chin of Jianguo High School gave his students), and "Give yourself an expectation and you can find your future" (which I think was my reading note from four years ago, left for my students). In the new 2021 semester I will take over a new homeroom class, and I'm still very nervous!
