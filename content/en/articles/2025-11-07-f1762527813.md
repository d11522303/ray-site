[Daily English 50 Words] Day 4 2025.11.7

corridor, (n), hallway, a long passage in a building

essential, (adj), necessary, absolutely necessary or extremely important

provision, (n), supply; clause, the act of supplying or a legal condition

reimbursement, (n), expense reimbursement, repayment for expenses

draining, (adj/v), exhausting; draining away liquid, exhausting or removing liquid

flushing, (v), rinsing, cleaning with a flow of water

enduce, (v), to induce; to lead, to bring about or cause (variant of induce)

evade, (v), to dodge, to avoid or escape from

exaggerate, (v), to overstate, to make something seem larger or more extreme

mopping, (v), mopping the floor, cleaning a floor with a mop

expedite, (v), to speed up, to speed up a process

verification, (n), verification, confirmation of truth or accuracy

strict, (adj), strict, demanding adherence to rules

vandalism, (n), deliberate damage, deliberate destruction of property

vampire, (n), vampire, a mythical creature that drinks blood

vandor, (n), street vendor, a person who sells goods (variant of vendor)

vanishment, (n), disappearance, the act of disappearing

extinguish, (v), to put out, to put out (a fire or light)

ceramic, (adj/n), made of ceramic; ceramic ware, made of clay or a clay object
