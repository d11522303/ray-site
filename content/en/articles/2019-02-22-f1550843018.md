Only 15 days left before the tour guide and tour leader exam.

In the LINE group of the Hsing Wu University (HWU) Department of Travel Management, someone asked about a question that even teachers can get wrong, because since 2016 (ROC year 105) the standard contract for domestic and outbound group tours has been revised quite a lot. Take question 31 in the picture as an example. It asks what happens if a travel agency abandons a traveller abroad (abandoning or stranding travellers). The compensation is the same amount as the total tour fee divided by the total number of tour days, multiplied by the number of days abandoned or stranded, and then multiplied by a penalty factor with three possibilities: intentional (5 times), gross negligence (3 times), negligence (1 time).

In the exam, you just look for keywords. This question says gross negligence, so the compensation is 3 times the average daily tour fee.

For a 5-day Japan tour costing NT$30,000, the average per day is 30000/5 = NT$6,000.

If it is [intentional], the compensation is 5 times; for 2 days the calculation is

6000*2*5=60000

If it is [gross negligence], the compensation is 3 times; for 2 days again, the calculation is

6000*2*3=36000

If it is [negligence], the compensation is 1 time; for 2 days the calculation is

6000*2=12000

In the past it was always 5 times.
