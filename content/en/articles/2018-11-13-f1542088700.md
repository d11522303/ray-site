Italy's Fincantieri shipyard is transferring cruise ship technology to China's shipbuilders, and Chinese manufacturing has taken another step forward. The Majestic Princess, which uses Keelung as its summer home port, was a 2017 product of Fincantieri.

In fact, since 1994 there have also been factories in China building Airbus A330 and A320 aircraft, and their market share has even reached 50%!
