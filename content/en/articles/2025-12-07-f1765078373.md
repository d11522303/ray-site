#2025TourLeaderExamQuestion (Tourism Administration)

15. Borobudur in Indonesia has profound significance in its religious and cultural background. What kind of religious building is it? ① Buddhism ② Islam ③ Christianity ④ Hinduism

You can find the correct answer by asking AI, as usual. This was one of the midterm exam questions in my World Travel Geography course for many years. Hsing Wu University students should get it right. If you got it wrong, you either skipped class or were playing on your phone 🤣.

Here are some new questions that might be derived from this one:

1. In which city in Indonesia is the Borobudur World Heritage Site located? ① Surabaya ② Yogyakarta ③ Medan ④ Jakarta

2. In the same Indonesian city there is another important World Heritage Site, Prambanan. What kind of religious building is it? ① Buddhism ② Islam ③ Christianity ④ Hinduism
